The master carver flashes each part of the bear blank — ears, snout, paws, body, tail — in the exact order it must be cut. Watch closely, then click or tap the same parts back in the same order, using the number keys 1-6 as shortcuts. Get a part wrong and you lose a life — three mistakes and the bear blank is ruined! Each round adds one more part to remember.
