Incoming Calls (1-4)

Drivers (Q W E R T)

How to play: Calls come in on the left, each with a pickup zone on the map and a countdown bar. Click a call (or press 1-4), then click an available driver (or press Q/W/E/R/T) to radio the job to them — the closer that driver is to the call's zone, the more points you earn. Dispatching to a busy driver fails. Let a call's timer hit zero and you lose a life. Calls arrive faster and timers shrink each level.
