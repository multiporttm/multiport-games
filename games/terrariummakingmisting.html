💧 Terrarium Making Misting
Score: 0 Round: 1 Best: 0
Watch the spray-bottle gauge sweep side to side above the leaves. Press Space, click, or tap the moment the marker sits in the bright green zone to release a perfect spritz of mist. A sloppy press either drowns the roots or leaves them dry, chipping away your moisture meter — empty it and the plants wilt. Five perfect spritzes in a row and the sweep quickens for the next round.
