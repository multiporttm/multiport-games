Hold Space, Up Arrow, or tap and hold the canvas to roll the warm wax sheet around the wick. Keep the pressure needle in the green zone — too loose and air gaps form, too tight and it wrinkles or cracks. Finish rolling each candle before its cool-down timer hits zero. Clear the whole tray to advance — pace quickens every tray.
