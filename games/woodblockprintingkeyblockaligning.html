Drag or nudge the paper so both kento marks settle into their circles.
How to play: A sheet of paper floats over the carved key block, with two kento registration marks on the paper and two matching target circles on the block. Drag the paper (or use arrow keys / WASD to slide it and Q/E to rotate it) until both marks sit inside their target circles — the fit meter fills as you get closer. Press "Press Baren" once it is full enough to pull the print; the closer your registration, the higher your score. A misaligned press still counts but scores little, so take your time lining it up before pressing.
