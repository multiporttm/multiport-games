📯 Gramophone Making: Horn Crafting
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
How to play: A brass-smith's marker sweeps back and forth across the shaping gauge beneath the horn bell. Click / tap the gauge, or press Space / Enter, right as the marker passes through the glowing green zone to land a clean hammer pass and shape the horn true. Miss the zone and your finish quality drops; empty it and the horn is ruined. Land 5 clean passes to finish a round — the marker speeds up and the sweet zone narrows with every round after that.
