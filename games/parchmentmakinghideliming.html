How to play: A batch of raw hides waits beside the lime vat, one lowered in at a time. A soak gauge fills as the hide sits in the bubbling lime solution. Press Space, click, or tap the PULL button exactly when the gauge is inside the green ready band to lift the hide out perfectly limed. A good pull advances to the next hide and builds your streak; pulling too early or too late drains your supply meter. Get through the whole batch before supply runs out to finish the round and move on to a bigger, faster-filling batch.
