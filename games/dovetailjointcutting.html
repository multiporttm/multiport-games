Dovetail Joint Cutting
Phase: Sawing Level: 1 Score: 0 Hi: 0 Lives: ❤❤❤
Press Space or tap and drag to saw along the marked line
Mouse/touch: drag the saw along the line. Keyboard: hold Space to saw, use ← → to correct wobble. In the chisel phase use ← → to pick a bay and Space to strike it.
Mark and saw along the angled pin/tail lines without drifting off the line or sawing past the baseline, then chisel out the waste bays (not the solid pins) so the two halves of the joint fit together tight.
