Cider Making Pressing
Juice: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1
How to play: Press and hold (or press Space / Up Arrow) to drive the press screw down and raise pressure; release to let it ease off. Keep the needle inside the glowing green safe zone on the gauge to squeeze juice from the crushed apples filling the batch bar. Drift below the zone and you waste apples; push the needle into the red zone too long and the press frame cracks, costing a life. Fill the batch bar to finish the round — batches get bigger and the safe zone narrows as you go!
