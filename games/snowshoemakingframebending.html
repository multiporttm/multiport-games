STEAM BENDING JIG

Ash strip softened in the steam box. Press Start, then time your bend to the green zone.

A steamed ash frame flexes on the jig. Watch the bending gauge below — when the sweeping needle crosses into the green zone, press Space, click, or tap the gauge to lock in the clamp. A clean hit shapes the curve; a miss stresses the grain and drains your Wood Quality meter. Land 5 clean bends to finish a round and move to a tighter curve — lose all your Wood Quality and the frame splits!
