Stunt Driving Car Flip
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Take 1/8
How to play: Two gauges swing back and forth — the top bar is ramp speed, the bottom bar is launch angle. Press Space, click, or tap the instant both needles sit inside their glowing target zones to launch the stunt car. Nail it and the car flips the target number of times and lands upright for a clean take. Miss either zone and the car crashes, costing a life. Zones get tighter and flip counts climb with every take — lose all 3 lives and the stunt is scrapped!
