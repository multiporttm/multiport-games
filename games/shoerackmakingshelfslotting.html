👟 Shoe Rack Making Shelf Slotting
Score: 0 Shelf 1 Best: 0
Shelf pegs drop down three lanes toward the slot line. Tap the matching lane button (or press 1, 2, 3) the instant a peg crosses the line to slot it in cleanly. Early or late taps - or missed pegs - rattle the rack and drain your Stability meter; empty it and the shelf collapses. Clean slots build your streak and speed up the next shelf.
