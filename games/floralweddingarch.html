Floral Wedding Arch
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
❤❤❤
How to play: A new floral cluster waits at the top of the arch. Choose Left or Right (arrow keys, A/D, click or tap the side buttons) to clip it onto that side of the arch. Keep the total weight balanced — if one side gets too heavy, the arch tips and you lose a life. Fill the arch with every cluster to complete it and move on to a bigger arch. You have 3 lives — good luck!
