Glassblowing Color Addition Rolling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round 1 Heat: Good
Follow the recipe card at the top: roll the hot bubble (drag left/right, or use Arrow Keys) back and forth over each colored frit pile, in order, until its gauge fills — then move to the next color before the glass cools too much. Hold still too long on one pile or keep rolling after a color is full and it muddies the pattern. Complete every color in the recipe before the bubble cools out to win the round — more colors and tighter amounts appear each round!
