Winemaking Barrel Aging
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1/6
How to play: Barrels age quietly in the dim cellar until their cue ring lights up — amber means it needs topping up (evaporation), purple means it needs a rotation (oxidation). Click/tap the barrel the instant its ring glows, or use number keys 1-9/Arrow Keys+Space to select and service it. Acting too early, too late, or on the wrong barrel spoils the batch and costs a life. Each new round adds more barrels and shrinks the window to react. 3 lives — complete all 6 batches to win!
