Drag the pen tip (mouse/touch) along the glowing path, or steer with Arrow Keys: Right/Up speeds up, Left/Down slows down. Keep the needle in the green safe zone — too slow chars the wood too deep, too fast leaves a faint line. Lines get longer and curvier each round!
