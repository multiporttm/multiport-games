Upholstery Stuffing Padding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Cushion 1
How to play: The cushion grid is full of lumps (bright, raised cells) and gaps (dark, sunken cells). Hold your mouse/finger down over a lump (or move the palm cursor with the Arrow Keys and hold Space) to press it flat — the foam you push out spreads into the nearest low spot. Smooth the whole cushion to an even height before the timer bar runs out to pass the round. Press on a spot that's already flat too long and you'll dent it, so ease off once it's level. Run out of time and you lose a life — lose all 3 and the cushion is ruined. Every new cushion is lumpier, the clock is faster, and the tolerance for unevenness gets tighter!
