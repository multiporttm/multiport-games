🪵 Matchbox Making Splint Tipping
Sticks drop toward the chemical dip pool. Tap each one the instant its tip touches the pool line to form a clean match head.
Wooden splints fall down the chute one at a time. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter the moment a stick's tip lines up with the glowing dip-pool bar to tip it cleanly. Tapping too early or too late — or missing entirely — spoils the head and drains your Head Quality meter; empty it and the batch is scrapped.
