How to play: Drag the trimmer along the overgrown hoof edge (orange) to file it down, staying between the safe inner line (green) and the overgrown edge — trim too close to the green line and you'll hurt the animal! Use mouse/touch drag or the Arrow Keys to move the trimmer and Space to trim. Finish the outline, then check the gait meter to release the animal. Harder animals arrive each round!
