Threading: Click/tap the glowing numbered rings on the sheet in order (1, 2, 3…) — or press the matching number key — to thread the new ring through them, just like a 4-in-1 European weave. Threading one out of order costs a life. Closing: once all rings are threaded, press C or click/tap Close Ring before the pliers timer runs out. Three lives — more rings to thread and less time with every level.
