Round 1 — bring all three needles into the green zone
Level a drone on the calibration stand and use the Pitch, Roll and Yaw dials (or Arrow Keys / Q/E + Tap) to bring the flight controller's reading into the green safe zone on each gauge, then lock it in. Overshoot the zone too many times and the calibration fails. Each round starts further off-center.
