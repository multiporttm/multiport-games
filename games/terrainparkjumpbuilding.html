Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Jump 1/8
How to play: Each jump shows a target size category and a rider's takeoff speed. Use Up/Down Arrow to pick the lip, gap, or landing slider, Left/Right Arrow (or drag/tap the bars) to set its value, and press Space or tap Build It to lock in your shape before the clock runs out. Get all three within tolerance to send the rider down safely — miss and you lose a life. Tolerances tighten and the clock speeds up with every jump!
