How to play: The spray nozzle sweeps back and forth across the panel. Press Space/Enter, or tap/click the canvas, the instant the nozzle is inside the green zone to lay down an even coat. A clean pass earns points and fills your round; a bad pass leaves drips and bruises your coat quality meter. Empty the meter and the job is ruined. Every 5 clean passes the nozzle sweeps faster and the sweet zone tightens — how glossy can you finish?
