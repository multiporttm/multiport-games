How to play: Hold Up Arrow / tap-and-hold the furnace to heat the alloy, release to let it cool. Get the temperature into the glowing target band, then press Space / tap POUR to start pouring. While pouring, move your mouse, drag your finger, or use Left/Right Arrow to keep the ladle inside the moving speed lane. Match both the temperature and the pour speed to cast a clean sheet — miss either one and you lose a life. Alloy ratios get trickier every round!
