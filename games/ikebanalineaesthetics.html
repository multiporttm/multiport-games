How to play: Every round hides a target silhouette (dashed) for the Shin, Soe and Hikae stems. Tap a stem button to select it, then use the Angle and Length controls (or arrow keys, with 1/2/3 to switch stems) to match your solid stem to its target before the timer runs out. Click Confirm Arrangement when it looks right. Close matches score big; wild misses cost a try.
