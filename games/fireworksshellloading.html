How to play: A firework shell drifts down from the top of the mortar rack, showing its size as a number. Use ← → (or the buttons / drag) to slide it over the mortar tube with the matching size, then press ↓ / Space / Drop to load it in — or just let it fall, since it auto-loads whichever tube it is over when it lands. Load every tube correctly to clear the rack before showtime. Load the wrong size and you lose a life; lose all 3 and the show is scrapped. Bigger racks with more shell sizes appear every level!
