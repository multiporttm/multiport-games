A pattern wheel spins on the drafting table, and a brass pointer sweeps endlessly around the dial, hunting for the glowing wedge that marks the next piece of the stained glass pattern. Press Space, tap, or click 📐 Trace the instant the pointer sweeps inside the glowing wedge to transfer a crisp, accurate pattern line onto the carbon sheet. Trace too early or too late and the line comes out smudged and off-angle, costing you a life — three bad traces and the pattern sheet is ruined! Finish every wedge on a sheet to move on to a more detailed pattern with a narrower, trickier wedge.
