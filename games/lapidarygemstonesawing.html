Lapidary Gemstone Sawing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Slab 1
A diamond saw blade oscillates back and forth across the rough slab. Press Space, tap, or click 🪚 Feed Blade the instant it crosses the bright cutting band to slice through cleanly. Feed too early or late and the slab chips — and feeding too often overheats the blade, cracking the gem. Finish every cut in a slab to move to the next — three cracked cuts and the rough is ruined!
