Topiary Watering

Guide the watering can over each topiary pot to keep its moisture gauge in the green zone. Too dry wilts it, too wet drowns it!
Use the Arrow keys or WASD (or drag/touch) to move the watering can around the garden. Hover the can over a pot to pour water on it, filling its moisture gauge. Each pot's gauge drifts down over time as the soil dries out — but pour too long and it overflows into the red, drowning the roots. Keep as many gauges as possible in the green healthy zone until time runs out. Press R to restart.
