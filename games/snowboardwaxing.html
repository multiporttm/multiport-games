Snowboard Waxing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Use Up/Down Arrow or the Cooler/Hotter buttons to keep the iron in the green temperature zone, then drag (or use Arrow Keys + hold Space) in overlapping circles to melt wax evenly across the whole board. Let it cool, then scrape off the excess the same way. You have 3 boards — don't scorch or waste them!
