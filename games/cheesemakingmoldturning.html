Cheesemaking Mold Turning
Lives: 3 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Each cheese mold drains on its own clock. When a mold's ring glows and pulses, click it (or press its number key, or tap it) before the glow fades to flip it in time. Miss a cue and you lose a life. Survive as more molds join the rack and the cues speed up!
