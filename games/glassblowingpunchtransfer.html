Glassblowing Punch Transfer
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Transfer 1 ♥♥♥
A swinging needle shows how centered the hot piece is on the blowpipe. Press Space / click / tap to tap the jacks line (cracks it loose) only while the needle sits in the green zone, then press again at the right moment to swing the punty underneath and catch the piece as it drops. Too early or too late and the piece swings off-center or cracks. The safe zones get thinner and faster each round!
