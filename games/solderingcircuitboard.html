Soldering Circuit Board
Score: 0 Board: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Move the iron with your mouse/touch, or use Arrow keys to move the crosshair, over a pin. Hold mouse/touch down or hold Space to heat the joint — a heat ring fills up. Release right when it enters the green zone for a clean, shiny joint. Release too early for a dull cold joint, or too late and it bridges onto a neighboring pin — both cost a life. Boards add more pins, closer together, each round. Three lives.
