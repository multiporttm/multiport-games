Four cues scroll toward the call line — LX (lights), SND (sound), FLY (fly system) and ENTR (actor entrance). Press 1-4 or tap a lane button the instant that lane's cue crosses the call line. Call it too early, too late, or on the wrong lane and you lose a life!
