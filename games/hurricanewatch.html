Click the ocean to drop a buoy and track the storm
Click on the ocean to place buoys (limited each round) and watch their readings as the hurricane passes — the wind arrows point toward the storm's eye, helping you predict its path. Once you've made your prediction, click the coastal zone buttons below the map to issue evacuation warnings for the zones you think will be hit before time runs out. Score points for correct warnings and lose points for false alarms or missed zones — later rounds bring faster, curvier storms.
