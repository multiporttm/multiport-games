Ink Roller Inking
Score: 0 Round: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Drag, or use Left/Right Arrow (or A/D), to roll the brayer over the plate's five sections and keep every ink bar in the green band. Sections dry out over time — too little ink smears the type, too much blurs it. When every bar is green, press Space or tap Pull Print. 3 lives, speed increases each round.
