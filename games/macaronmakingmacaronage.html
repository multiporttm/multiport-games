Macaron Making: Macaronage
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 🤍🤍🤍 Batch 1
How to play: Press Space, click, or tap to fold the almond-meringue batter — each fold nudges the consistency needle up, while the batter naturally settles back down between folds. Keep the needle inside the glowing pink-green safe zone until the readiness bar fills completely to finish the batch. Fold too fast and the batter turns runny (over-folded); stop folding and it stays stiff and lumpy (under-folded). The safe zone narrows and the clock runs shorter with every batch!
