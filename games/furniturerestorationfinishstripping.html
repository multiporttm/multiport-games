Furniture Restoration Finish Stripping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Panel 1
Drag the scrubber, or move with arrow keys and hold Space to scrub.
An old panel is caked in grimy, yellowed finish, and it is your job to scrub every square of it clean before the clock runs out. Click and drag, or touch and drag, the steel-wool pad across the panel to work through each patch of old finish, or move the pad with the Arrow Keys and hold Space to scrub in place. Once a square turns to bare wood, stop scrubbing it — lingering there too long gouges the wood and costs you a life. Clear every square to move on to a bigger panel with a tighter time limit, and gouge three squares and the whole piece is ruined!
