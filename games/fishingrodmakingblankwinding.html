🎣 Fishing Rod Making Blank Winding
Hold the mandrel spin speed inside the green band while the carbon tow winds on. Too slow and resin pools; too fast and the tow snaps.
Drag the Throttle slider (or use the Up/Down arrow keys) to keep the spinning mandrel's speed needle inside the green target band while a carbon-fiber tow winds onto the blank. The target band drifts, so keep adjusting. Staying in-band fills your layer progress; drifting out drains Blank Integrity — if it hits zero the blank is scrapped and the game ends. Complete layers to advance and raise your score.
