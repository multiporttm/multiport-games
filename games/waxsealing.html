Melt the wax
💠
Hold the candle over the letter until the pool fills the green zone.
How to play: Three steps per seal. 1) Press and hold the button (or Space) to melt wax onto the letter — release when the pool gauge is inside the green zone. 2) A power needle sweeps back and forth — click/tap (or press Space) the instant it's in the green zone to stamp the seal. 3) Keep holding steady until the hold bar fills for a clean impression. Miss any step and the seal comes out smudged or ruined. Timing gets trickier every round!
