Mold 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press and hold (mouse, key, or touch) to pour molten lead-alloy into the type mold. Watch the fill gauge: keep the level inside the glowing safe zone as the mold fills. Release too early and the letter is incomplete; overfill it and the metal spills over the mold edge, ruining the sort.
