Mushroom Cultivation Misting
Time: 0sScore: 0Hi: 0
Press Start to open the grow room
Four grow tents each need humidity kept in the green ideal range and stale air (CO2) kept low. Click/tap Mist or press 1-4 for a burst of moisture, and click/tap Vent or press Q/W/E/R to run a fresh-air exchange (both need a moment to recharge). Mist too much and a tent gets soggy; let CO2 build up or humidity dry out and the room's health meter drops. Keep every tent balanced as long as you can!
