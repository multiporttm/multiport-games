Globe Making Meridian Ring Fitting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Stand 1
The brass meridian ring spins freely around the globe on its stand, and a gold notch marks the exact angle where the pivot pin seats into the horizon cradle. Press Space, tap, or click ⭕ Lock Pin the instant the white pointer sweeps into the gold notch to seat the pin true. Locking it off-angle strips the thread — three stripped pins and the stand is scrapped! Seat every pin on a stand to mount a faster, trickier ring next.
