PRECISION
A gauge needle sweeps back and forth over the fletching jig. Click, tap, or press Space/Enter the instant the needle is inside the green zone to glue a vane cleanly onto the bolt. Miss the zone and your precision meter drains — empty it and the batch is ruined. Every 5 clean attaches the needle speeds up and the zone shrinks, so stay sharp!
