Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Instrument 1/5
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or hold the Arrow Keys) to run the polishing cloth across the instrument's bell. Dark tarnish spots turn bright gold once buffed — clear every spot before time runs out. Keep moving: lingering too long on an already-shined spot burns through the lacquer and costs a life. Each new instrument has more tarnish spots and less time. 3 lives — clear all 5 instruments to win!
