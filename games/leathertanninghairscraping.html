Leather Tanning Hair Scraping
Hide: 1 Time: 0s Scraped: 0% Nicks: 0/3 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag the beam knife across the hide (or use Arrow keys to move it and hold Space to scrape) to shave off every tuft of hair. Thin, pale patches on the hide are weak spots — ease off there or you will gouge a nick straight through. Three nicks ruins the hide! Scrape at least 90% clean before time runs out to move to the next hide.
