Comfort
Press and hold (mouse or finger) on the ear cup to inject foam padding — the fill level rises while you hold. Release right when the level is inside the glowing green target band for a comfortable, even cup. Releasing too early or holding too long under- or over-fills the cup and drains your Comfort meter; let it empty and the padding line stops. Each good cup narrows the next target band and speeds up the fill.
