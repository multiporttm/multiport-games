Puppy Obedience Training
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: A command cue lights up above the puppy — press the matching key (S=Sit, T=Stay, C=Come, D=Down) or tap the matching button while the cue is active. Reward the puppy promptly and correctly to build its reliability. Miss the window, or give the wrong command, and the puppy loses confidence (a life). The reaction window shrinks and more commands mix in as your training level rises. Survive 3 mistakes or less across the session to graduate!
