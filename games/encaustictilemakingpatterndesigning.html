Study the full tile pattern while it is shown, then stamp color into the quarter mold on the left — the other three quadrants mirror automatically. Arrow keys move the cursor, Space/Enter cycles the color, or tap a cell directly. Press Submit to pour and check your match.
Encaustic cement tiles get their geometric beauty from a brass divider mold pressed into the mold base, which splits the tile into symmetric quadrants before colored cement is poured in. In this game a finished tile pattern flashes briefly on screen — study its colors and symmetry carefully. Once it fades, you only get to design one quarter of the tile in the small mold on the left; whatever colors you stamp there are mirrored automatically into the other three quadrants, exactly like the real divider mold. Move the cursor with the arrow keys and press Space or Enter to cycle a cell through the palette, or simply tap/click cells directly. When your mirrored design looks right, press Pour & Check to compare it against the original pattern — a high match percentage pours a perfect tile and advances you to a larger, more intricate pattern, while a poor match wastes a batch of cement and costs you points. The preview flash gets shorter and the grid gets bigger as you progress, so train your eye and work fast.
