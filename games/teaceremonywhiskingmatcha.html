Tea Ceremony Whisking Matcha
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Move the chasen in a brisk zigzag — drag/swipe the bowl, or hold A/D or Left/Right Arrows to swing the whisk side to side — to build smooth, frothy matcha before the bowl cools. Keep a steady rhythm: too slow and it clumps, too fast and it splashes out. You have 3 bowls (lives) to reach the target grade!
