Canoe Gunwale Attaching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
The diagram numbers each screw hole along the gunwale rail — click or tap them in that exact order. Once a hole is selected, a driver depth needle sweeps up and down a gauge; press Space, Tap the canvas, or hit DRIVE SCREW right as the needle crosses the green safe zone. Drive too shallow or blow through the wood and you split it — three splits ends the job. Gunwales grow longer (more screws) each round.
