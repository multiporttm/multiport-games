Stage 1 — Twist: Click/tap and drag in a steady circular motion over the fibers (or hold Left/Right Arrow) to twist them. Watch the tension gauge and stop right in the green zone, then press Space or tap the Lock Twist button. Stage 2 — Serve: Drag back and forth across the center section (or use Left/Right Arrow) to wrap it evenly with thread — fill the coverage meter without piling thread in one spot. Twist too little and the string snaps under a shot; twist too much and it frays. Get it right to finish the bowstring — the tolerance tightens with every one you make!
