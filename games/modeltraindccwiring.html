Model Train DCC Wiring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Decoder 1
 
How to play: Click/tap a colored wire on the decoder (left) then click/tap the terminal it belongs to on the locomotive (right) — or press Tab to cycle wires, Arrow keys to cycle terminals, and Enter to connect. A correct connection locks in green; a wrong one sparks and costs a life. Wire every connection before time runs out. Later decoders add more functions — and more similar wire colors — to the board.
