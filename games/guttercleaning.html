How to play: Use Up/Down arrows (or drag/tap) to move the worker up and down the ladder. Move the ladder Left/Right (or tap the arrows) between roof sections. Once positioned at a gutter, click-and-drag across the debris to scoop it out. Don't lean too far — watch the balance meter, if it maxes out you'll wobble and lose time! Clear every section before the timer runs out to advance to a longer, dirtier roofline.
