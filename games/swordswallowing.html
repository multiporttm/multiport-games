Sword Swallowing
Round: 1 Lives: ❤️❤️❤️ Best: 0
Press and hold on the sword handle (mouse or touch), then drag it straight down, slowly and steadily, keeping it inside the narrow glowing corridor the whole way to the bottom. Drifting outside the corridor or dragging too fast causes a slip and costs a life. Reach the bottom safely to complete the swallow — each round narrows the corridor and lengthens the sword.
