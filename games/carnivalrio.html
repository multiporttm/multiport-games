How to play: Dance move icons scroll down the parade route toward the hit-line in time with the samba beat. Press the matching arrow key (Left = sway, Down = spin, Up = wave, Right = high-step) or tap the matching button just as each icon crosses the hit-line. Perfect timing builds your combo streak and score; missed or mistimed moves break your combo. Tempo and move variety increase every round!
