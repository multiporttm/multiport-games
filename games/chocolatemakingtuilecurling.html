Chocolate Making Tuile Curling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Mold 1/10
How to play: A hot chocolate tuile wafer sweeps around the cooling-window gauge. Press Space, click or tap the moment the needle crosses the glowing golden zone to drape the wafer perfectly over the curved mold. Drape too early and the hot wafer tears; wait too long and it hardens flat on the counter. Nail the drape to finish the mold and move on — the gauge spins faster and the golden window narrows with every mold. Lose all 3 lives and the batch is ruined!
