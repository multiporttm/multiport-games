Gingerbread Window Glazing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Window 1/10
How to play: Hold Space, click and hold, or touch and hold to pour hot sugar-glass glazing into the window frame. Watch the gauge below the window — a gold target band shows exactly how much glazing that frame needs. Release before the fill passes the band to lock it in; overshoot past the top and the glazing overflows and cracks the pane, costing a life, while releasing too early leaves it underfilled. Land inside the gold band to seal the window perfectly and move to the next, trickier window — lose all 3 lives and the batch of windows is ruined!
