Sundial Making Bronze Casting
Mold: 1/8 Bronze Left: 14 Score: 0 Best: 0
Hold down Pour Bronze (or hold Space, or press and hold on the canvas) to stream molten metal into the mold, and release right as the level reaches the glowing fill line. Stop short and the casting is thin; let it overflow and the dial base is ruined.
Casting the bronze base is where a handmade sundial truly takes shape, as molten metal from the furnace crucible is poured carefully into a sand mold shaped for the dial's round foot. Each round presents a fresh mold with a glowing fill line set somewhere near the top, and a tipped crucible ready to stream bronze the moment you hold down Pour Bronze, hold the Space bar, or press and hold anywhere on the canvas. The level rises steadily while you hold the pour, so release at just the right instant to stop the flow precisely on the fill line and cast a strong, sound dial base. Releasing too early leaves the casting thin and understrength, while holding even a moment too long sends bronze spilling over the lip and ruins the mold entirely, costing a heavy chunk of your bronze supply. Mold sizes vary and the bronze flows faster with every dial successfully cast, so quick reflexes and a careful thumb will be needed to cast the full run of dial bases before the furnace runs dry.
