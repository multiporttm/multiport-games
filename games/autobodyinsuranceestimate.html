How to play: Click, tap, or use the Arrow Keys + Enter/Space to inspect each highlighted zone on the vehicle photo. Flagged zones marked DAMAGE are real claim items — click them, then pick the correct repair price from the three choices (or press 1, 2, or 3) before the inspection timer runs out. Submit every damaged zone correctly to move to the next, trickier claim — lose all 3 lives and the estimate gets rejected.
