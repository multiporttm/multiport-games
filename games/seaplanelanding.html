Seaplane Landing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Level: 1 | Lives: 3
How to play: Hold Up/W to raise the nose and slow your descent, Down/S to dive faster. Press Left/Right (A/D) to shift between the three water lanes — watch the wave heights to find the calmest one. Touch down gently (low sink rate, calm lane) before your approach distance runs out. Too fast or too choppy and you'll crash!
