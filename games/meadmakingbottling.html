Mead Making Bottling
Round 1 | Bottle 1/6 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Space, Up Arrow, or Tap/Hold to pour mead from the carboy. Release to stop and seal the bottle — the fill level must land inside the glowing target band. Overflow and the bottle shatters; seal it underfilled and the mead is wasted. More bottles — and a faster pour — every round!
