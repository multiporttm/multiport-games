Watch the tracing arm sweep around the dial plate, and press Strike Burin or hit Space the instant it crosses the glowing hour mark. You can also hold Left/Right arrows to nudge the arm's speed slightly if you need to line up your timing. A clean strike right on the mark engraves a sharp hour line; a strike that is early or late skids across the brass and scars the plate.
Engraving the dial plate is the delicate, final art of sundial making: a brass engraver's tracing arm sweeps slowly around the plate's rim, and the moment it crosses each hour position the engraver must drive the burin to cut a crisp, permanent line into the metal. Each round highlights one hour mark on the rim while the tracing arm sweeps around at a steady pace; press Strike Burin or tap Space the instant the arm's tip passes over the glowing mark to engrave it cleanly, and use the Left and Right arrow keys if you need to momentarily slow or hurry the arm's sweep to line up your timing. A strike landed squarely on the mark cuts a sharp, true hour line and earns a strong bonus, a near miss leaves a passable but shallow line, and a badly mistimed strike skids the burin across the brass and scars the plate's integrity. The tracing arm sweeps faster and the hour marks narrow with every line finished, so sharp reflexes will be needed to engrave the full set of twelve-hour markings before the plate is ruined.
