Watch the test swatch darken as the sun shifts, and pull the print the instant it matches the ready card.
How to play: A cyanotype-coated sheet is drying in the sun beside an exposed test strip, and the test strip's blue swatch keeps deepening and sometimes fading as clouds drift past overhead. A small reference card shows the exact bronze-green "ready" shade the swatch needs to reach for a perfectly exposed print. Watch the swatch and the light-strength gauge beneath it, then press Space, click or tap the Pull Print button, or tap the canvas, the instant the swatch color matches the reference card. Pull too early and the print is underexposed and pale; pull too late and it is burnt and overexposed; either one costs you a miss. Nail three misses and the sheet is ruined and the run ends. Each correctly timed pull banks points based on how close your timing was, and the swatch cycles faster the more prints you pull, so stay sharp and pull as many perfectly timed prints in a row as you can to post a high score.
