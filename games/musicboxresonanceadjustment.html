Left/Right or A/D tune frequency · Up/Down or W/S tune clamp pressure · Drag the marker directly
Adjust a music box's resonance board mounting to maximize volume and tone without introducing buzz. Use Left/Right or A/D to tune the sound board's frequency, and Up/Down or W/S to tune its clamp pressure, or drag the marker directly, to keep it inside the glowing sweet-spot box. Stray into the red buzz zone and the board rattles. Target tone profiles shrink and shift faster as you level up.
