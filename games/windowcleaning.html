Window Cleaning
Floor 1 Window 1/3 Score: 0 Best: 0 Time: 45s
Click/tap a dirty pane to spray it wet, then drag the squeegee in smooth, overlapping strokes to wipe it clean. Wipe too fast or leave gaps and you'll get streaks (shown in yellow) that need re-spraying. Clean every pane before time runs out, then hit Next Window. Higher floors add wind sway that pushes your strokes off course!
