Evenness
Tap Brush, click, or press Space when the marker is in the green zone.
How to play: A lacquer brush glides back and forth across the flute body. Press Space, click the canvas, or tap Brush exactly when the marker sits inside the green zone for a smooth, even stroke. A good stroke builds your streak and score; a bad one leaves a drip or bare patch and drains your Evenness meter. Finish enough strokes to cure the coat and move on to a faster, trickier round.
