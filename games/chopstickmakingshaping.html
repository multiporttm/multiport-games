🥢 Chopstick Making Shaping
Round: 1 Blanks Shaped: 0 Score: 0 Best: 0
Watch the marker sweep across the gauge and press Space, click, or tap right as it crosses the glowing green zone to push the plane through a clean, even stroke. A clean stroke shapes the taper and raises your score; a bad stroke gouges the wood and damages your straightness meter. Shape enough blanks to clear the round — rounds get faster and the sweet zone gets tighter as you go. If your straightness meter runs out, the blank is ruined and the game ends.
