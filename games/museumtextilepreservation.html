How to play: First click/tap each glowing support marker (or press Space to place at the highlighted marker) to tuck acid-free tissue padding under every fold point — don't skip any or the fold will crease wrong. Then, with padding done, click/tap each corner in the order shown by the numbers (or press 1-4) to fold the textile closed smoothly. Fold too fast out of order or with a marker still unpadded and tension spikes, risking a tear and a lost life. Lose 3 lives and the piece is damaged beyond repair. Each new textile is bigger, older, and needs more care.
