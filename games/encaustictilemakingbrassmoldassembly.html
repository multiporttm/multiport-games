Encaustic Tile Making Brass Mold Assembly
Mold: 1 Strips Left: 18 Score: 0 Best: 0
Study the brass divider pattern while it glows, then click each grid line where a strip belongs to rebuild it from memory. Click Confirm Mold when done.
Before pigment can ever reach an encaustic tile, a brass mold maker bends thin brass strips into a divider pattern and seats them inside a steel mold frame, forming the little compartments the colors will later fill. Each round, the frame glows and briefly shows you the exact pattern of brass strips laid along the grid lines — study it closely, because the mold then goes dark and blank. Click the grid lines (or tap them, or use the arrow keys plus Space to move a cursor and toggle a line) to bend and place brass strips back into the positions you memorized, then press Confirm Mold. A perfectly rebuilt pattern earns a big bonus and keeps your strip supply intact, while missing or misplaced strips cost you precious brass. The patterns grow more intricate and the memorize window shortens as you advance through molds, so watch closely and rebuild fast and accurate before your strip supply is spent.
