Choose a module, then click an open tile.
Click a module in the palette (Habitat, Solar Panel, Greenhouse, Oxygen Generator), then click an open tile on the Mars grid to place it — rocky tiles can't be built on. Each habitat houses colonists who consume power, oxygen and food; the other modules produce those resources. Keep all three meters at zero or above using your limited module budget, then click Finish Round to score. Each new round brings a tighter budget and trickier terrain.
