Wood Quality
Click, tap, or press Space right as the marker crosses the green zone to make a clean cut.
How to play: A carving-depth marker sweeps back and forth across the gauge below the crossbow stock blank. Click, tap the gauge, or press Space/Enter the instant the marker is inside the green sweet zone to make a clean cut into the stock. A clean cut scores points and advances your cut counter; a miss gouges the wood and drains your Wood Quality meter. Finish enough clean cuts to complete the stock and start a new, slightly faster round. If Wood Quality runs out, the blank splits and the run ends — try to beat your best round and score.
