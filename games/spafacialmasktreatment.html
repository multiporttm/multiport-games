Spa Facial Mask Treatment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: Each client arrives with a skin concern. First Cleanse the skin (click/tap or press C three times). Then pick the matching mask — Hydrating (1) for dry skin, Clarifying (2) for oily skin, Brightening (3) for dull skin. Once applied, watch the drying bar and press Remove Mask (R) right when it enters the green zone for full marks — too early loses a little, too late loses more, and letting it fully harden costs a life. A wrong mask pick also costs a life. Survive with 3 lives as drying speeds up and the timing window narrows each level!
