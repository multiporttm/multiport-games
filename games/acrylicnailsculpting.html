Acrylic Nail Sculpting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Nail 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead travels the outline of the nail shape — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your brush tip inside the pink tolerance tube around it. Drift too far and your form meter drains; empty it and the bead hardens wrong, costing a life. Stay close the whole way to finish the nail and move on. The bead speeds up and the tube narrows every nail — lose all 3 lives and the set is ruined!
