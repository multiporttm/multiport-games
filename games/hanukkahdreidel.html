Hanukkah Dreidel
Round: 1/8 | Target: נ | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold (click/tap and hold, or hold Space) to charge your spin power, then release to spin the dreidel. Watch the power meter — different power ranges tend to land on different letters. Land on the target letter shown each round to score and light a candle. There are 8 rounds, one for each candle!
