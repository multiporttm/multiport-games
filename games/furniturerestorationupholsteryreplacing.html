A fresh sheet of fabric is draped over the chair frame, and the tension needle sweeps back and forth across the dial as you pull the staple gun taut. Press Space, tap, or click 🔨 Pull & Staple the instant the needle lines up inside the glowing tight zone to fire a clean staple that holds the fabric smooth. Staple too loose or too tight and the fabric puckers or tears, costing you a life — three bad staples and the upholstery job is ruined! Finish every staple point around a chair to move on to a trickier frame with a narrower tension zone.
