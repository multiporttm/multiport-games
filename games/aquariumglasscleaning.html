Drag the magnetic cleaner with your mouse, finger, or the Arrow Keys in overlapping passes across the glass to wipe away every green patch of algae before the timer runs out. Missing patches lets the algae regrow and spread. You have 3 lives — panels get bigger and algae regrows faster each level.
