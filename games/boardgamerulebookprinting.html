Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Rulebook 1/6
How to play: Jumbled pages sit on the feed tray. Click the page showing the next page number in sequence (or press its number key, 1-6) to feed it into the press. Once fed, a registration mark slides sideways — drag it, or steer it with the Left/Right Arrow Keys, into the green zone and press Space/tap the press to strike while it's aligned. If the proof camera flags the page red as a misprint, press Space/tap again quickly to reject it before it is bound — printing a flagged page or feeding the wrong page number ruins the signature and costs a life. Finish every page to bind the rulebook and move to the next, longer one. Lose all 3 lives and the press run is scrapped!
