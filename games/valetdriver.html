Valet Driver

Park arriving cars, then retrieve them when their owner returns!
Cars arrive in the queue above the lot. Click a car, then click an open (non-reserved, dashed-border) spot in the lot to park it. Occasionally a car's owner returns — a glowing spot marks a parked car and a ticket icon shows which one; click that highlighted parked car to retrieve it and drive it off the lot. Park and retrieve as many cars as you can before time runs out — busier lots each round!
