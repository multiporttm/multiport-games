Tap or click the tinted cells to carve them away. Leave the rest untouched!
How to play: The block grid shows tinted cells marking the one color area to carve, surrounded by cells you must leave alone. Click or tap a tinted cell to gouge it out, or move the cursor with the arrow keys/WASD and press the "Carve" button or Space/Enter to carve the cell under it. Every correct cut adds to your score; carving a cell that was not marked costs you strokes. Clear all marked cells before you run out of strokes to finish the color block and move to a bigger, trickier one.
