Telescope Making Collimation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
Use Arrow Keys / WASD or the on-screen pad to nudge the mirror's adjustment screws and bring the cyan target ring onto the center crosshair. Tap small, steady nudges — mashing a direction builds momentum and can over-adjust, throwing you back off center. Once the ring sits inside the green tolerance zone, press Space or LOCK to confirm before the timer runs out. Misalignment gets trickier and tolerances tighten with every round.
