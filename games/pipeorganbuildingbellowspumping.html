Pump steadily to keep the wind gauge inside the safe band while the organist plays.
How to play: You work the hand bellows behind the organ while the organist plays above, and the reservoir's wind pressure gauge must stay inside the green safe band the whole piece. Pump the bellows with the Up Arrow, W, Space, the on-screen PUMP button, or a tap/click — each pump adds a burst of wind to the reservoir, which drains steadily as pipes sound and drains faster during loud passages. Watch the small indicator that shows how hard the organist is currently playing: pump more often during loud bursts and ease off during quiet ones. Let the gauge empty and the pipes starve; pump too fast without easing off and the reservoir overflows its weights. Keep the gauge in the safe band long enough to finish each piece and post a high score.
