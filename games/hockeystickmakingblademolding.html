Carbon sheets slide toward the press line in the LEFT or RIGHT mold lane. Tap the matching side the instant a sheet crosses the line to laminate it. Miss the lane or the timing and the blade delaminates!
Carbon-fiber sheets slide down the left or right mold lane toward the press line. Tap the matching side (click/touch the left or right half, or use Left/Right Arrow or A/D) exactly as a sheet crosses the press line to laminate it cleanly. Tapping the wrong lane, or missing the timing window, weakens the blade and drains your Blade Strength meter - empty it and the blade snaps.
