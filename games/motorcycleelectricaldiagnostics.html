Motorcycle Electrical Diagnostics
Score: 0 Hi: 0 Harness: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Click (or tap) each node along the wiring harness, starting from the Battery, to probe it with the multimeter. Good connections read full voltage; one wire in the chain is broken and will read 0V. Click the node right after the break to isolate the fault and repair it. Wrong guesses cost a life — use the readings and the diagram to narrow it down before you touch a wire!
