Snow Sculpting Smoothing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sculpture 1/6
How to play: Sweep your flat smoothing tool over the sculpture with your mouse, finger, or the Arrow Keys — every rough gray patch it crosses turns smooth and bright. Keep moving! If you sit on an already-smooth patch too long you gouge it and have to re-smooth it. Smooth the entire surface before the timer runs out to finish the sculpture and earn a life back; run out of time with rough patches left and you lose a life. Each sculpture is bigger with more patches and less time — lose all 3 lives and the festival display is ruined!
