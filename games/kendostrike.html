Kendo Strike
Watch your opponent's stance. When a zone (Men/Kote/Do) on the opponent flashes red, click or tap that zone fast to score a point. If it flashes blue, they're guarding — striking it lets them counter-hit you instead. Also watch your own zones: if one flashes red on you, click it to block in time! Win rounds by scoring 3 points first. Each round gets faster.
