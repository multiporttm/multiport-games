Curling Sweep
End 1/5 | Hi Score: 0
A stone slides down the sheet each end. Rapidly press Left Arrow / A or Right Arrow / D (or tap the sweep buttons) in front of the stone — sweeping melts the ice, making the stone travel farther and curl less. Sweep more on one side to steer it! Land it in the house center for the most points across 5 ends.
