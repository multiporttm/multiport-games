Glass Bead Lampworking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Bead: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Each bead needs a sequence of colored glass bands, shown at the top. Pick the matching color rod below (click/tap it, or press its number key) and hold it over the torch flame — then release (click again or let the number key go) the instant the heat gauge is in the glowing green zone to wind a smooth, even band onto the mandrel. Wrong color or bad timing melts the bead out of shape and costs a life. Three mistakes and it is game over. Beads need more bands and the timing window gets tighter as you go.
