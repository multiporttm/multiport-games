Fruit Carving Apple Bird
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bird 1/8
How to play: A glowing bead travels along the curved guide outline of a bird carved into the apple half — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your knife tip inside the orange safe zone around it. Straying outside the safe zone drains your precision meter; empty it and the cut is ruined, costing a life. Stay on the line the whole way to finish carving the bird and move to the next round. Each round brings a more intricate bird shape and a tighter, faster-moving zone — lose all 3 lives and the carving set is over!
