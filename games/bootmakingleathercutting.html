Hide Wasted!

A cutting guide sweeps back and forth over the hide. Click, tap, or press Space/Enter the instant it lines up inside the glowing green zone to make a clean panel cut. A clean cut scores points and restores a little leather quality; a bad cut damages the hide. Finish enough panels to start a tougher batch where the blade moves faster and the sweet zone narrows. Run out of good leather and the session ends.
