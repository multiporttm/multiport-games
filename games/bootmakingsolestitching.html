Seam Failed!

A hand-stitching needle swings over the welt and sole seam. Click, tap, or press Space/Enter right as it crosses the glowing green zone to pull a tight, even stitch. Tight stitches score points and keep the seam's strength up; loose ones weaken it. Finish a row of stitches to move to the next, faster row. Let the seam strength run out and the stitching session ends.
