How to play: A layout diagram on the right shows marked slots for this card's elements — the ornate border, the title plate, and the figure illustration. Drag each loose piece on the left (or select with Tab, nudge with Arrow Keys, and press Enter) onto the slot of the matching shape. A wrong drop just bounces back, but once every piece is correctly placed before the timer runs out you finish the card and move to a more intricate layout with more pieces and tighter slots. Let the timer run out and you lose a life — lose all 3 and the sitting is over.
