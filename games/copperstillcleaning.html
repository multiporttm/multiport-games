Copper Still Cleaning
Score: 0 Run: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Clean the still's Head, then its Neck, then its Pot — in that order. Click/tap a grime spot (or use Arrow Keys to select one and Space to scrub) to clean it; most spots need a few scrubs. Touching a spot in a zone you haven't reached yet scratches the copper and costs a life! Clean every zone before the timer runs out — rounds get faster with more buildup.
