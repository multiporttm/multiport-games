Telescope Making Focuser Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Focuser 1/8
How to play: Press and hold Press Focuser (or Right Arrow / tap-and-hold the button) to seat the drawtube onto the tube — the fit gauge moves right. Release inside the glowing safe band, then click Seat Focuser (or Space) to lock it. Then tighten each numbered screw hole in order 1, 2, 3… by clicking it, tapping it, or steering the white cursor with the Arrow Keys and pressing Enter. Tighten one out of order, or seat outside the band, and the mount comes out ruined. Each round adds more screws and narrows the band!
