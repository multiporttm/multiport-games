Pocket Watch Repair: Crystal Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Case 1/8
How to play: Drag the glass crystal (or rotate it with the Left/Right Arrow Keys and move it with W A S D) until its outline lines up with the dashed target marked on the case. Then hold Space or press and hold on the crystal to pump the seating press — the pressure gauge will climb. Let go while the needle is inside the glowing safe band to seat the crystal perfectly. Release too soon and it is not seated; let the gauge spill past the band and the crystal cracks, costing a life. Every case brings a trickier crystal shape and a narrower band.
