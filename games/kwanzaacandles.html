Tonight's order & principles:
Light the candles in order, starting with the center!
How to play: Each night, study the reference guide showing tonight's lighting order and principle (Umoja, Kujichagulia, Ujima, Ujamaa, Nia, Kuumba, Imani). Then click or tap the candles on the kinara in the correct order: the black center candle is always first, then alternate red (left) and green (right) outward. Light every candle correctly to complete the night and move on. A wrong candle costs points but the night continues.
