Rickshaw Pull
Tips: $0 Time: 60 Deliveries: 0 Best: $0
Alternate A / D keys (or the two pedal buttons) rhythmically to run. Use Up/Down Arrows or drag on the road to steer between lanes and dodge carts and pedestrians. Reach the glowing dock while carrying a passenger to deliver them and earn tips. Faster, steadier pedaling = more speed!
