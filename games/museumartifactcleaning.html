Museum Artifact Cleaning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Artifact 1/5
How to play: Each grime spot needs a matching tool — tan dust needs the Brush, brown stains need the Swab, green varnish needs Solvent. Pick a tool with the buttons or keys 1/2/3, then hold the mouse/finger down (or hold Space while moving the cursor with Arrow Keys) and move slowly over a spot to clean it. Wrong tools or fast scrubbing raise the damage meter — fill it and you lose a life. Clear every spot before time runs out. Later artifacts are smaller, more delicate, and faster to damage!
