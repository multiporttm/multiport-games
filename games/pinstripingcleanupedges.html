Pinstriping Cleanup Edges
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Fender 1
How to play: Drag your mouse/finger (or use Arrow Keys) to move the blade over every glowing smudge before the timer runs out. Stay off the gold pinstripe line itself — touching it gouges the paint and costs a life. Clear every flaw to advance to a bigger fender with more smudges and a tighter no-gouge zone.
