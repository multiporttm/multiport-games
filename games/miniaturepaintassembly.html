Drag (mouse or finger) the highlighted part shown in the diagram to its glowing glue point, in the order numbered on the diagram. Once placed, hold it with Arrow Keys or by dragging gently to keep the balance needle inside the steady zone until the glue sets — tilt too far and the part slides off. More parts join each round.
