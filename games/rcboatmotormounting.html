Use Arrow Keys / A-D or drag to turn the wrench
Click (or tap) the highlighted screw, then turn the wrench — drag left/right, or use Left/Right Arrow keys — to bring its torque needle into the striped safe zone on the gauge. Release at the right moment to lock it in. Tighten every screw in the numbered order shown; skipping the sequence or cranking past the safe zone strips the mount and costs you the motor. Mount more motors as rounds advance to beat your high score.
