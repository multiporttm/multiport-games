Bee Smoker Calming
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Hive 1/5
How to play: Press Space, click, or tap to puff the smoker. Keep the mood needle inside the green calm band — if you under-smoke, the bees drift into the aggressive red zone and sting; if you over-smoke, the hive gets stressed and crashes the other way. Hold the needle in the band long enough to finish each hive. Five hives of progressively more defensive bees, 3 lives — lose them all and the inspection is over!
