Cloisonné Torch Firing
Piece 1Fused: 0%Score: 0Hi: 0
Move the torch (mouse, finger, or arrow keys) evenly across the enamel piece. Keep the heat gauge needle inside the glowing safe fusing zone — too cold and the enamel will not fuse, too hot and it bubbles and ruins the piece. Fully fuse every cell before the piece overheats to advance to a more intricate piece!
