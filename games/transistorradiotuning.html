Transistor Radio Tuning
Score: 0 Station: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Use Left/Right Arrow or A/D (hold Shift to fine-tune slowly), or drag the dial with mouse/touch, to sweep the needle through the static. The signal meter rises as you approach the target frequency (shown as a bright peak) — hold the needle inside the green lock zone until it locks to clear the station and score. The peak drifts a little over time, and each station narrows the lock zone and speeds the drift. Lose a life if you run out of time on a station. Three lives.
