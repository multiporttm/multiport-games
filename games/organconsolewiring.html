Organ Console Wiring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
How to play: Each stop tab on the left must be wired to its matching colored relay channel on the right. Click or tap a stop, then click or tap the channel with the same color to connect them — or use Tab / Arrow keys to select and Enter to connect. A correct match locks the wire in green; a wrong match sparks and costs a life. Wire every stop before the timer runs out to advance — consoles get bigger with more stops every round!
