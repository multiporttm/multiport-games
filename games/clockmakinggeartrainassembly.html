Target Ratio — 1 : 1
 
How to play: Each pivot on the bench is fixed in place — only the right gear will mesh with its neighbors there. Click or tap a gear in your parts tray to pick it up, then click a pivot to set it down (click a filled pivot to take its gear back; click it again while holding to swap). Keyboard: ←/→ move the pivot cursor, ↑/↓ move the tray cursor, Enter/Space picks up or places the selected gear, Backspace clears a pivot. Fill every pivot so neighboring gears mesh with no gap and no binding, and the first gear's rotation must reach the target ratio at the last gear. Click Check Train ✓ to test it — a bad mesh or wrong ratio costs a life. More gears, tighter tolerances, and tougher ratios await each round!
