Canoe Rib Steaming
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Watch the steam gauge and press Space or Tap ACT when the needle is inside the glowing target band to pull a pliable rib. Then use Up/Down Arrows or the +/- buttons to bend the rib to match the dashed guide arc, and press Space/ACT to lock it in. Rounds demand tighter curves — crack the wood three times and the steaming is over!
