Balloon Twisting Hat Shaping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Hat 1/8
How to play: The diagram shows numbered loops that must be twisted together in order to build the hat. Click/tap the glowing loop (or press its number key) to grab it, then hold (mouse/finger down or Space) to build twist tension — watch the tension ring around the loop. Release while it's in the green safe band to lock the loop in place. Release too early and it unravels; hold too long and it pops! Lock every loop in the right order to finish the hat and move on to a more elaborate design.
