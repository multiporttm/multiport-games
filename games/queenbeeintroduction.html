After placing the queen cage, watch the colony's mood ring around it. Press 1/tap Keep Watching while bees are agitated (red), and press 2/tap Release Queen only once they're calm and feeding her through the candy plug (green). Releasing too early — or waiting too long — risks rejection. Three hives to introduce, 3 lives, and the window gets trickier each hive.
