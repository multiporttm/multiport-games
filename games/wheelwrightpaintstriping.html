Wheelwright Paint Striping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Wheel 1
The finished wheel turns slowly on the striping lathe, rim scrolling past a fine sable brush loaded with color. A faint pencil guideline wanders up and down as the wheel turns — use ↑ ↓ arrow keys, drag, or the buttons to keep your brush riding right on that line. Stray off the line and the stripe gauge drains fast; stay glued to it and the gauge climbs. Let the gauge run empty three times and the rim gets wiped clean to start over! Fill the gauge to finish striping a wheel and move on to a wheel that spins a jumpier line.
