Fit Alignment
Lower the finished glass bulb into its turned wooden base and press the press right as the depth marker glides into the glowing green zone, seating it snug and true. A perfect seat raises your alignment and your score; seating it too shallow or too deep cracks the glass seal and drains your alignment meter. Lock in 5 perfect seats to finish a round and speed up the next one. Tap/click the press, or press Space/Enter, at the right instant. Empty your alignment meter and the bulb cracks — game over.
