Kite Flying Line Tensioning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold ArrowUp / W or tap REEL IN to tighten the line, and ArrowDown / S or tap LET OUT to loosen it. Wind gusts constantly push the tension gauge around — keep the needle inside the green safe zone. Too tight and the line snaps; too loose and the kite falls. Survive as long as you can as gusts grow stronger each level!
