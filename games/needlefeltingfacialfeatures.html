Needle Felting Facial Features
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Face 1
A felting needle bobs up and down toward the wool face. Press Space, tap, or click 😊 Poke Needle the instant the tip reaches the glowing target depth to pin that feature in place perfectly. Poke too shallow or too deep and the feature sits crooked — and poking too rapidly stresses the wool fibers until they tear. Pin every feature on a face to start the next — three crooked pokes ruins the face!
