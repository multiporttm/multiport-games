Crosswalk Signal
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lives: 3 | Time: 60
How to play: Pedestrians pile up on both sides of the crosswalk. Press Space or tap WALK to let them cross — but only do it when no car is in the striped danger zone near the crossing! Activating the signal with a car too close causes a near-miss and costs a life (3 lives total). Get as many pedestrians across as you can before the timer hits zero.
