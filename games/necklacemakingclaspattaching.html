CRIMPING FOCUS
A steady squeeze of the crimping pliers locks the clasp forever — too early or too late and the metal slips.
How to play: Watch the crimping gauge as the marker sweeps across it. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter right as it enters the glowing zone to crimp the clasp on tight and secure. A dead-center crimp is flawless and restores focus; a bad crimp bends the metal and drains your grip-focus meter. Secure 5 clasps to clear a round — each round the pliers snap shut faster. Lose all your focus and the clasp comes loose for good!
