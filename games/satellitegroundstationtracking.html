Satellite Ground Station Tracking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lives: 3 | Pass: 1 | Signal: 100%
How to play: Steer the ground dish with Arrow Keys/A-D, or drag/tap on the sky, to keep its crosshair on the satellite as it crosses from horizon to horizon. Staying close keeps your signal lock strong; drifting away drains it. If signal hits zero you lose a life. Complete a pass to advance to a faster, lower pass!
