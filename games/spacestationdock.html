Space Station Dock
Score: 0 | Docks: 0 | Lives: 3 | Hi: 0
Use Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag on touch, to thrust your ship toward the glowing docking port on the rotating station. Approach slowly and roughly straight-on (pointed toward the station center) to dock safely — coming in too fast or sideways causes a bounce, and a hard crash costs a life!
