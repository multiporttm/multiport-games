Yurt Making Insulation Packing

Armful of raw wool ready — pack a tuft into the wall only when the gauge marker rides the green band.

How to play: A marker slides up and down the packing gauge. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter the instant it crosses the green band to pack a wool tuft evenly into the wall. An uneven pack leaves a cold gap and drains your focus meter — empty it and the wall fails, ending the round. Five even packs advance a round, where the gauge moves faster and the target band narrows. Pack as many tufts as you can for the highest score!
