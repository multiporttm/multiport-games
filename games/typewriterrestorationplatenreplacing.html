Move the scraper over the old rubber and hold to peel it off the roller.
How to play: This repair has two stages. First, strip the old hardened rubber: move the scraper left and right with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging on the canvas, and hold the action button (or Space) to peel the cracked rubber away under the scraper. Work steadily — holding the scraper in one bare spot too long gouges the metal core, which hurts your score. Once the roller is fully stripped, the game moves to the press stage: line the new sleeve up with the arrows so the alignment meter centers, then hold the action button to press it on evenly. Press while badly misaligned and the sleeve seats crooked, ruining the attempt. Replace enough platens before you run out of attempts to post a high score.
