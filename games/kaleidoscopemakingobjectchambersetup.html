Kaleidoscope Making Object Chamber Setup
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Chamber 1
A clear plastic chamber sits open at the heart of a half-built kaleidoscope, waiting for the loose charms and beads that will tumble and catch the light inside. A recipe of shapes appears above the chamber — tap, click, or use the number keys 1-5 to drop the matching charm from the tray into the chamber, in order, left to right. Drop the wrong shape, or run out of time before the chamber seals, and a crack weakens the glass — three cracks and the chamber shatters! Fill every chamber in a set correctly to move to a trickier recipe with more charms and less time.
