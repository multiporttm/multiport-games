Move the basket cursor with Arrow Keys and press Space to pick, or just tap/click a slot directly. Pick mushrooms once they're fully ripe (big round caps) — picking a tiny unripe pin breaks the cluster and costs a life. Letting a ripe mushroom overripen wastes it. Fill your quota before the flush window closes — each flush grows faster!
