Tarot Illustration Border Tracing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Border 1
How to play: A golden bead sweeps along the ornate border pattern framing the card. Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your pen tip inside the glowing tolerance band around the bead. Wander off the line and your ink meter drains; empty it and the border smudges, costing a life. Trace the whole loop to finish the border and move on — the pattern grows more intricate and the tolerance narrower with every round. Lose all 3 lives and the illustration is ruined.
