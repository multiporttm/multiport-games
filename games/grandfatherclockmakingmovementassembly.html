Building a grandfather clock's movement means stacking the gear train in exactly the right order on the bench: mainspring barrel first, then the center wheel, third wheel, escape wheel and pallet, each meshing with the one before it. At this bench, parts drift down five lanes and you must catch each one in its lane right as it reaches the rail, but only when it is the part the build order actually needs next — catching the wrong part, or letting the right one fall past, wastes a part from your limited supply. Use the number keys 1 through 5, tap a lane directly, or steer a highlighted lane with the Left and Right arrows and press Space to catch, timing each grab for the moment the part crosses the rail. The parts fall faster and the lanes fill busier as your assembled movement count climbs, so watch the build order closely and catch cleanly before your parts run out.
