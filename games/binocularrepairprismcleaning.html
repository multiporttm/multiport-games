Drag the swab across hazy glass to wear it clean, and dab sharply on fungus bloom to lift it.
How to play: A tarnished porro prism sits under the bench lamp, its face covered with dull gray haze patches and spidery green fungus bloom. Click and drag, or touch and drag, the swab back and forth across a haze patch to slowly wear it down, or click/tap sharply on a fungus bloom to dab it away before it spreads to a clean neighbor. Use the arrow keys to move the swab and hold Space to scrub, or tap Space to dab. Scrubbing one already-clear spot too long dulls your swab for nothing, and letting fungus spread costs cleaning progress. Clean the whole prism before your solvent swab dries out to finish the job and move to the next prism.
