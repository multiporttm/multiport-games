Bladesmithing Sheath Making
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sheath 1/8
How to play: The template shows numbered stitch points around the blade outline. Click/tap the points (or use Tab/Arrow keys to highlight and Space/Enter to stitch) in order, starting from 1. Stitching the correct next point in sequence sews the thread; clicking the wrong point unravels a stitch and costs a life. Finish every point in order to complete the sheath and move to a more intricate shape with more stitch points!
