Epoxy Resin Pouring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Mold 1
Hold Space, click, or tap and hold the Pour button to stream resin into the mold. Watch the fill gauge on the right — keep the rising level inside the glowing safe zone and release before it overflows. Stop pouring once the mold is full inside the band to seal the round. Molds get more intricate each round!
