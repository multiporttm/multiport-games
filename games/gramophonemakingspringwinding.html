⚙️ Gramophone Making: Spring Winding
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
How to play: The winding key turns and a bright pointer sweeps around the tension dial of the mainspring motor. Click / tap the dial, or press Space / Enter, exactly as the pointer passes through the glowing green tension zone to take up a clean, even turn. Miss and uneven strain damages your tension quality meter; empty it and the spring snaps for good. Land 5 clean turns to finish a round — the key spins faster and the safe zone narrows every round after.
