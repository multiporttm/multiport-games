🫧 Snorkel Making Mouthpiece Fitting
Drag the mouthpiece (or use the arrow keys) to chase the drifting collar on the tube, then tap Fit the moment they line up.
Drag the mouthpiece left and right (or use the Left/Right arrow keys) to line it up with the collar drifting along the tube, then press Fit (click/tap the canvas, or Space/Enter) the moment they overlap to lock a watertight seal. A close fit scores big and speeds up the drift; a bad fit lets water leak in and drains your Seal Integrity meter — let it empty and the mouthpiece is rejected.
