Cooperage Bunghole Drilling
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrows (or drag) to slide the drill to the marked X on the template, and Up/Down Arrows to tilt its angle into the safe zone. Once aligned, hold Space, Click, or Touch to drill in — watch the depth gauge and stop before you punch through the far side of the stave!
