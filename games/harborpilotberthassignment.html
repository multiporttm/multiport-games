How to play: Ships arrive with a size and a cargo type, each on a countdown clock. Click (or press 1/2/3) a ship in the queue, then click (or press A/S/D/F/G/H) a berth to assign it — the berth must be big enough and must accept that cargo type (a General berth takes anything). Assign enough ships to clear the schedule before three mistakes or three timeouts cost you all your lives. Every new schedule packs in more ships, more berths, and tighter clocks.
