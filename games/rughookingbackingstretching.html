Rug Hooking Backing Stretching
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Frame 1
Burlap backing is tacked to a quilting frame on all four sides, each one slowly sagging loose. Each side has its own tension gauge that drifts down over time. Press 1/2/3/4 or tap the Top / Left / Right / Bottom buttons to give that side a tug the instant its gauge sits in the green zone. Pull too early or late and the burlap puckers; pull when a gauge is already tight and it rips! Keep all four gauges in the green together to lock the frame and move on.
