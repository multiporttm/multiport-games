Use Up/Down Arrows or drag/touch to steer the needle onto the glowing stitch line. Use Left/Right Arrows (or the on-screen hold) to keep your sewing speed inside the green safe zone. Stray off the line or sew too fast/slow and the strain gauge fills — patterns get curvier each round!
