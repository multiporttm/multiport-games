⚙️ Fishing Reel Making Gear Training
Drag left and right to rotate the pinion gear. Line its yellow tooth up with the green slot on the slowly turning drive gear, then tap Engage.
Drag across the gear (or use the Left/Right arrow keys) to rotate the small pinion gear's marked tooth. The green slot on the big drive gear drifts on its own, so keep tracking it. When the yellow tooth sits inside the green slot, tap the Engage button (or press Space/Enter) to mesh the gears cleanly. A clean mesh scores points and speeds up the drift; a bad mesh grinds the teeth and drains Gear Condition — let it empty and the gear set is scrapped.
