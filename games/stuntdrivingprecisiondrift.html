Stunt Driving Precision Drift
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sector 1/6
How to play: Steer with Arrow Keys / A/D, or drag with your mouse/finger, to slide the stunt car down the winding corridor. Watch the drift gauge at the top — keep the needle inside the glowing band to earn style points, but drift too hard or clip a wall and you crash. Each sector you clear narrows the corridor and tightens the required drift band — lose all 3 lives and the chase ends!
