🧭 Compass Making Dial Engraving
Drag the engraving needle from the start to the finish along the groove without touching the walls.
Press down on the needle at the start of the groove, then drag it (mouse or finger) all the way to the finish flag while staying inside the winding channel. Touching a wall jolts the needle and drains your Steady Hand meter; reach the end to etch the dial and start a new, narrower groove. Let the meter empty and the dial is ruined.
