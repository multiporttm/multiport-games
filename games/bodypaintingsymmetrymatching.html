Body Painting Symmetry Matching
Designs Mirrored: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Paint the blank right side to mirror the finished pattern on the left before the symmetry timer runs out.
How to play: The left side of the mannequin already shows a finished painted pattern; the right side is blank and waiting for its mirror image. Move your brush with the mouse or your finger, or steer it with the arrow keys or WASD, and hold it over a cell on the right side to paint it, matching each spot to its mirrored twin across the centerline shown in the middle of the canvas. Painting a cell that should stay bare smears outside the pattern and costs symmetry, while leaving a cell blank when its mirrored twin is painted keeps the design lopsided, so cover the right side carefully rather than scribbling everywhere. Watch the symmetry-timer bar drain at the top of the frame — once it runs out, the design is graded on how closely the right side mirrors the left, with a bonus for a near-perfect reflection. Mirror pattern after pattern, each one busier and faster-draining than the last, to reach the highest symmetry score you can before the paint dries lopsided.
