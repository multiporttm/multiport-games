How to play: Classic ikebana uses three stems of different heights — Shin (tall), Soe (medium) and Hikae (short) — placed off-center for balance. Tap or drag a stem card into the vase's left, center or right slot to place it, matching each slot's suggested height role. Fill all three slots sensibly and click Finish Arrangement to score the round. Three badly balanced arrangements ends the session.
