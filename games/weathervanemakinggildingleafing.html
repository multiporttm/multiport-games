Weathervane Making Gilding Leafing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Panel 1
A fresh coat of gold sizing has been brushed onto a copper weathervane panel, and the tack-gauge needle sweeps back and forth hunting for the glowing window where the size is just tacky enough to grip a sheet of gold leaf. Press Space, tap, or click ✨ Lay Leaf the instant the needle sits inside the glowing band to burnish the leaf down flawlessly. Lay it too early while the size is still wet or too late once it has skinned over and the leaf wrinkles and lifts, costing you a life — ruin three sheets and the gilding job is wasted! Finish every panel to move on to a trickier curve with a narrower tack window.
