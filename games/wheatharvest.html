Wheat Harvest
Wheat: 0Best: 0
Steer your harvester with the Arrow Keys or WASD (or drag/touch on mobile) as the field scrolls past. Drive over golden wheat to cut it for points, and steer around rocks and stumps — hitting one drains your fuel/health. The field gets denser and obstacles more frequent the longer you last. Harvest as much wheat as you can before your fuel runs out!
