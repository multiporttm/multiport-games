How to play: Your closed-circuit rebreather mixes oxygen (O2) with diluent gas. As ambient pressure rises with depth, PO2 = FO2 × pressure. Use the +/− buttons (or Up/Down Arrow keys) to raise or lower your FO2 percentage and keep the yellow PO2 marker inside the green safe zone on the gauge as the dive profile's depth changes. Drifting too high risks oxygen toxicity; too low risks hypoxia — either drains your health. Survive the full dive profile to complete the dive!
