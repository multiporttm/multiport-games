How to play: A glowing golden bead sweeps along the figure's outline on each tarot card. Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your pen tip inside the violet ink tolerance tube around it. Wander off the line and your ink meter drains; empty it and the linework smudges, costing a life. Stay on the path the whole way to finish the card and move on. The bead speeds up and the tube narrows with every more intricate card — lose all 3 lives and the deck is ruined!
