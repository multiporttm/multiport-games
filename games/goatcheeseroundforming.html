Goat Cheese Round Forming
Score: 0 Tray: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Use Arrow Keys (or tap a mold) to select a mold, then hold Space (or press and hold the mold) to ladle curd in. Release in the target fill level shown on each mold — too little or too much costs a life. Once every mold is filled they'll drain; when a mold flashes ready, press Space (or tap) it to unmold a neat round. Clear the whole tray to move on — each tray adds more molds and tighter fills!
