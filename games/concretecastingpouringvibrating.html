Concrete Casting Pouring & Vibrating
Mold: 1 Fill: 0% Score: 0 Best: 0
Phase: Pouring
Press Start Pour, then move the mouse, drag your finger, or use Arrow keys to sweep the pour spout across the mold and fill every cell evenly — avoid leaving dry spots or overflowing any one cell. Once the mold is filled, the phase switches to Vibrating: sweep the poker over rising air bubbles (dark bumps) before they harden into voids. Even fill plus a clean bubble sweep scores the most, and each new mold is bigger and faster-setting.
