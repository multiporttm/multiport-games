🐦 Cuckoo Clock Making Bird Carving
Score: 0 ❤❤❤ Combo x0 Best: 0
Carving marks appear on the wooden cuckoo bird one at a time, each shrinking inside its ring - tap or click the mark (or press 1/2/3 when one is highlighted) before its ring vanishes. Hit it clean for combo points; miss or let one vanish and you lose a life. Marks appear faster and shrink quicker as your combo grows - lose all 3 lives and the carving splits.
