Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Build 1/8
How to play: The concept art thumbnail (top-left) shows where each part belongs on the vehicle. Drag (or tap to pick up, tap again to drop) each part from the tray onto its matching outlined slot — or press Tab to cycle parts and 1-9/Enter to send the selected part to its correct slot. Drop a part on the wrong slot and a life is lost. Place every part correctly before the shoot-ready timer runs out to wrap the build. Vehicles get more parts and the timer gets tighter every round!
