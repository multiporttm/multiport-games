Wheel HeatShine
Click and drag (or touch) back and forth over the silver ring to buff away the dull scratch marks. Keep moving to build shine, but watch the Wheel Heat gauge — holding still in one spot too long scorches the metal and costs a life. Clear every scratch before the timer runs out. Arrow keys also jog the buffing point. Three scorches and it's game over!
