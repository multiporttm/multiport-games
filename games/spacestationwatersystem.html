Each batch of wastewater must pass through the shown filtration stages in order — click them in sequence. A correct full sequence routes a batch of clean water into the quota bar; a wrong click resets your progress on that batch and fills the backup meter. Hit the day's water quota before the backup meter maxes out to advance — each new day adds more decoy stages and raises the quota.
