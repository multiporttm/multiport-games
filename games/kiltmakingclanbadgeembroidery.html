Kilt Making Clan Badge Embroidery
Badge: 1 Stitch: 1/10 Score: 0 Best: 0
Move the needle with your mouse, finger, or arrow keys toward the glowing next stitch point on the clan crest outline, then press Space or tap/click to punch the stitch through. Landing the needle tip close to the point before the thread tension meter drains scores higher and keeps the thread taut; wander too far and the stitch pulls loose. Complete every stitch point in order to finish the badge and move on to a more elaborate crest.
