Globe Making Varnishing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Coat 1
The lacquer brush glides back and forth over the painted globe, laying down a shiny wet sheen that thickens and thins as it passes. A gold zone marks the exact amount of varnish that dries clear and even. Press Space, tap, or click ✨ Lift Brush the instant the sheen gauge crosses into the gold zone to lift off clean. Lifting too soon leaves a dry patch, lifting too late pools into a drip — three bad patches and the coat is ruined! Finish a whole coat to move on to a glossier, tighter coat.
