Drag each bent rib (orange) onto its target angle (dashed green line).
Pump the spring! Click Pump when the moving bar lands in the yellow zone.
Each bent rib (orange line) needs to be dragged back to match its target angle (dashed green guide) — click and drag the tip of a rib around the hub until it lines up, then release. Once all ribs are aligned, the spring pump appears: click Pump each time the moving bar lands inside the yellow zone to build tension — do it enough times to fully tension the spring. Finally click Test Umbrella to open and close it. Any rib that's still misaligned will make it fail the test. Each round adds more bent ribs to fix!
