❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Rig 1
A freshly cut vane arrow sits on the test spindle in front of a wind tunnel fan, and gusts keep shoving its tail off true north. Use ←/→ arrows, tap the on-screen buttons, or swipe left/right to slide the little counterweight along the arrow's shaft and hold the pointer inside the glowing true-north zone while the gust meter blows. Let the arrow drift out of the zone for too long and it stalls out, costing you a life — stall three times and the test rig shuts down! Hold steady through each gust cycle to move on to a stronger, trickier wind.
