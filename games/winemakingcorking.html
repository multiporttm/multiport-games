Winemaking Corking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Line 1/6
How to play: A bottle sits under the hand press with a cork loaded. Hold Space / Mouse / Touch to pump the lever — the pressure needle rises on the gauge. Release while the needle sits inside the glowing green safe zone to seat the cork perfectly. Release too early (needle below the zone) and the cork stays loose; hold too long and the neck cracks — either way you lose a life and the bottle is ruined. Each new bottling line moves faster and shrinks the safe zone. 3 lives — clear all 6 lines to win!
