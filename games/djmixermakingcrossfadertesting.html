🎚️ Stress-Test The Crossfader
Drag the crossfader left and right. Line it up with each falling pulse's zone as it reaches the test line.
Drag the crossfader handle (mouse or finger) left and right along the bottom track. Colored pulses fall down the lane, each marking a target zone on the track — when a pulse reaches the test line, the crossfader must be sitting inside that zone. A catch builds your Combo and tops up Durability; a miss breaks your Combo and damages Durability. Survive as many pulses as you can: they fall faster and the zones narrow each round, and Durability hitting zero ends the test.
