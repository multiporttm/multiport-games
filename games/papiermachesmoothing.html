Papier-Mâché Smoothing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/8 Time: 24
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to sweep the sanding block over every rough, bumpy patch of the piece until it turns smooth and pale. Work fast — the clock is ticking! But don't park the block in one spot too long: if you over-sand a patch it gouges through the paper layers and ruins that spot. Smooth the whole piece before time runs out. Pieces get larger every round — rack up 3 gouges or run out of time 3 times and the piece is wrecked!
