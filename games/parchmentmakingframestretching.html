Click, tap, or press 1-8 on a peg to pump it when its tension drifts out of the green band.
How to play: Eight pegs hold a wet hide laced to a stretching frame, and each peg's tension drifts up or down on its own over time. Click or tap a peg, or press its number key (1-8), to pump its cord tighter. Let a peg's tension sag below the green band and the hide wrinkles there; let it drift above the band for too long and the cord tears. Keep all eight pegs inside the green band at the same time until the stability meter fills to finish stretching the hide, then move on to a bigger frame where tension drifts faster.
