Ride up a wall and press TRICK right at the peak!
The skater rides back and forth across the halfpipe, building speed with every pass. Press Space, click, or tap the TRICK button right as the skater reaches the peak of a wall — the closer to the very top, the bigger the trick score and the longer your combo chain grows. Mistime it or bail and your combo resets, but speed keeps climbing the longer you last.
