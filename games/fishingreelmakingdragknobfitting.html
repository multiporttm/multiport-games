🎣 Fishing Reel Making Drag Knob Fitting
The tension needle drifts on its own. Hold Tighten or Loosen to steer it into the green band and keep it there to calibrate the drag knob.
The drag tension needle wanders on its own. Hold the − (Loosen) or + (Tighten) button — or the Left/Right arrow keys, or press anywhere on the left/right half of the dial — to counter the drift and steer the needle into the green calibration band. Keep it inside the band long enough to fit the knob and move to the next round; drifting out for too long drains your Knob Fit meter. Each round the drift gets wilder and the band gets tighter.
