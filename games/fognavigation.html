Fog Navigation
Course 1 Safe crossings: 0
How to play: Steer your vessel with Arrow Keys / WASD or by dragging on mobile. Thick fog hides most of the course — watch for brief radar pings that flash nearby hazards and the compass direction to the harbor marker. Avoid rocks and other vessels and reach the marker to complete the course. Each new course brings thicker fog and rarer pings.
