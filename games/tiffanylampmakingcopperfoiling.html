Tiffany Lamp Making Copper Foiling
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Edge 1
Each glass piece's edge wobbles gently back and forth as you wrap sticky copper foil tape around it. Use ←/→ or A/D, hold the ◀ ▶ buttons, or drag on the canvas to keep the foil applicator riding the glowing edge line. Staying on the edge fills the wrap meter; drifting off it for too long tears the foil and costs you a life — three torn wraps and the piece is ruined! Fill the meter to finish foiling that edge and move on to a trickier, faster-wobbling edge.
