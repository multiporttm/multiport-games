🪔 Wall Light Making Bracket Mounting
Keep the bubble level centered, then drive the screw the instant it's steady to mount the sconce bracket dead level.
Use the ◀/▶ pads (or Left/Right arrow keys) to nudge the bracket's bubble level back to center as it drifts, then tap ⚙ (or press Space/Enter) while the bubble sits inside the green zone to drive a screw home. Driving off-level screws cracks the drywall and drains Wall Integrity — let it empty and the bracket falls.
