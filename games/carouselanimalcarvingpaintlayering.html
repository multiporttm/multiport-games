Click the glowing zone's next coat color below: base, then mid-tone, then highlight.
How to play: A carved wooden carousel animal stands on the paint stand with several body zones, each needing three coats in order: a dull base coat, a mid-tone shade, and a bright highlight. Click or tap the glowing zone to select it, then click the correct next-coat color chip (or press the number key shown under it) to apply that layer. Painting the right coat advances that zone and builds your streak; painting out of order clumps the paint and costs drying time. Finish layering every zone before the lacquer timer runs out to complete the animal and move on to a trickier paint scheme with more zones.
