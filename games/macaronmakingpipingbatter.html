Macaron Making: Piping Batter
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 🤍🤍🤍 Sheet 1 Round 1/6
How to play: Each marked circle on the template is your next piping spot. Press and hold Space, click, or tap on the highlighted spot to pipe — the batter round grows while you hold. Watch the size gauge and release inside the green target band. Release too early and the shell is under-piped; hold too long and it over-spreads. Every sheet adds more spots and a tighter target band!
