💎 Chandelier Making Crystal Hanging
Drag the trolley left and right, then let go to drop a crystal onto the left, center or right arm. Keep the three arms evenly loaded or the chandelier will tip!
Drag anywhere on the frame to slide the trolley, then release to drop the crystal onto the arm below it (left, center or right). Watch the breeze arrow - it drifts falling crystals sideways. Keep the three arms balanced: if one arm gets far heavier than the others the chandelier tips over and the game ends. Arrow keys move the trolley, Space or Enter drops the crystal.
