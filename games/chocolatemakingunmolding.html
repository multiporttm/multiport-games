Chocolate Making Unmolding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Mold 1/8
How to play: Each mold shows numbered flex points. Click/tap them in ascending numeric order (or press the matching number key, 1-9) to flex the mold and pop each chocolate piece free. Tap a point out of order and the piece cracks — you lose a life and the mold resets. Clear every point on a mold to move on to the next, trickier shape with more cavities packed closer together!
