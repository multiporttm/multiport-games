Ballet Pointe Shoe Breaking In
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Shoe 1/3
How to play: A needle sweeps back and forth across the gauge below the shoe. Press Space, click, or tap the instant it is inside the glowing target zone to complete each prep step — softening the box, bending the shank, and sewing on the ribbons and elastic. Press outside the zone and you lose a life. Finish all 5 steps on a shoe to move to the next, trickier custom-fit shoe with a smaller zone and a faster needle. Lose all 3 lives and the fitting is ruined!
