🥾 Rain Boot Making Sole Treading
Tread rings open up at random spots on the sole blank and shrink down fast. Tap, click, or press each one while it's still inside its green sweet spot to stamp a clean tread lug.
Shrinking stamp rings appear at random positions on the sole blank. Tap or click each ring while its shrinking edge is inside the green band to press a clean tread lug — too early (ring still too big) or too late (ring collapsed past the band) ruins that lug. Clean stamps raise your score and speed up the press; ruined ones drain the Tread Quality meter — empty it and the sole is scrapped.
