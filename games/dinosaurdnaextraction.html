How to play: Eight lab-process steps are shown in a shuffled grid. Click/tap them (or press the matching number key 1-8 for that grid position) in the correct scientific order — Sample Collection first, then through Lysis, Removal, Precipitation, Washing and Resuspension, finishing with the Quality Check — to isolate a usable DNA sample.
