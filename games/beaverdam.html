Beaver Dam

Select a material — Log (strong, costly), Stick (cheap, weak) or Mud (seals gaps) — then click (or tap, or use number keys 1-3 + arrows + Enter) cells across the dam to stack layers. Each cell needs enough combined strength to beat the rising current. Stay within your material budget, then click Release the River — weak cells spring leaks! Survive to bank the round; the current gets stronger each round.
