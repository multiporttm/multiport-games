Space Cargo Unloading
Capsule: 1 Hull: ❤❤❤ Time: 60 Stowed: 0 | Hi: 0
Tap or click a floating cargo bag to select it (ring shows selection), then press the Arrow Keys or the on-screen D-pad to push it. In microgravity it slides until it hits a wall, another bag, or station equipment — bumping equipment damages the hull! Guide every bag into its matching colored storage zone before time runs out to clear the capsule.
