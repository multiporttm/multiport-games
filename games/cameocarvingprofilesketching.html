Keep your line inside the shaded corridor around the dashed guide.
Click or touch and drag along the dashed guide curve to sketch the profile (arrow keys nudge the pencil, Space toggles drawing). Staying inside the pale corridor marks guide segments as traced in dark pencil; wandering outside it too far breaks your line and costs a mistake. Trace enough of the curve accurately before you run out of mistakes to finish the sketch — later profiles are longer and curvier!
