Strike the beats as they reach the target zone!
Beat markers slide across the bench toward the glowing target zone on the cut edge. Press Space, click, or tap the moment a marker is inside the zone to land a clean bevel strike. Good timing smooths the edge and builds your combo while keeping mallet pressure inside the safe band; mistimed or rushed strikes spike the pressure gauge and crack the leather if it overflows. Finish the whole beat pattern with the edge intact to move to the next hide — edges get longer with more beats each round!
