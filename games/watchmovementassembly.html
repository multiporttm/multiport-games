Watch Movement Assembly
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag (or tap a tray part, then tap its glowing slot) each watch part onto the plate in the correct build order — mainspring barrel, then the gear train, then the escapement, then the bridges that pin it all down. Place a part in the wrong slot or order and you lose a life. You have 3 lives. Finish a movement to advance to a more complex one.
