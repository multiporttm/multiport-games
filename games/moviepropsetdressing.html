How to play: The continuity photo (top-left) shows exactly where every prop belongs on set. Drag each prop from the tray onto its matching marked spot on the floor — or press Tab to cycle props and 1-9/Enter to send the selected prop to its correct mark. Placing a prop on the wrong mark costs a life. Match the whole set before the "Rolling!" timer runs out to lock the take. Sets get busier and the timer gets tighter every scene!
