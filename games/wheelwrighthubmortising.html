Wheelwright Hub Mortising
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Hub 1
A round elm hub is locked in the boring jig, and a chisel needle sweeps slowly around its rim looking for each spoke socket. Press Space, tap, or click 🔨 Strike the instant the sweeping needle crosses the glowing marked socket — too early and the chisel bites into solid wood, too late and it skates past the mark entirely. Mis-strike three times and the hub splits for good! Cut every socket evenly around a hub to move to the next hub with tighter, more crowded sockets.
