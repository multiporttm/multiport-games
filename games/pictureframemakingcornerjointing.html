📐 Corner Jointing
Drag the saw arm around the protractor to match the target angle shown at the top, then tap Lock Cut to join the corner. The closer you match it, the cleaner the joint!
Drag your finger or mouse across the protractor to swing the miter saw arm until it matches the target angle shown above the dial. Tap "Lock Cut" (or press Space/Enter) to join the corner at your current angle - the closer to the target, the higher your score. A bad miss costs you a frame; three ruined frames and the workshop closes for the day.
