Tap or click the Crank button (or press Space / tap the coil) in steady pulses to wind the coiled mainspring tighter. Watch the tension needle climb around the dial — each pulse adds tension but it also bleeds off slowly between cranks, so keep a steady rhythm. Once the needle sits inside the glowing green safe band, press Lock Spring (or hit Enter) to ratchet it in place and bank points — the closer to the center of the band, the bigger your bonus. Overwind past the red zone and the spring snaps; let it idle too long and it goes slack. Each new spring narrows the safe band, so stay sharp!
