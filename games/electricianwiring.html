Electrician Wiring

Wire every panel correctly — avoid shorts!
Drag from each colored wire on the left junction box to the terminal of the same color on the right wall panel. Crossing an already-completed wire or dropping on the wrong-colored terminal causes a short and fails that connection — you'll need to retry it. Wire every terminal to finish the panel; denser panels with more wires appear each round.
