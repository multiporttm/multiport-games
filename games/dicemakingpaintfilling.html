Dice Making Paint Filling
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Pip 1
An engraved pip has been flooded with bright paint, and the squeegee sweeps back and forth, pulling the coat thinner or thicker as it drags across the face. Use the Left/Right arrow keys or tap ◁ Thin Coat / Thick Coat ▷ to shift how the squeegee drags the paint, watching the coat thickness swing across the gauge, and press Space, tap, or click 🎨 Wipe the instant the gauge swings into the glowing target band to wipe away the excess and leave the groove perfectly filled. Wipe too soon or too late and the pip comes out patchy or smeared across the face, costing you a life — three ruined pips and the die goes back to the engraver! Finish filling every pip to move on to a trickier die with a narrower target band.
