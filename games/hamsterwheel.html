Hamster Wheel
Score: 0 | Hi: 0Treats: 0
Alternate Left/Right Arrow (or A/D), or tap the two pedals, in a steady rhythm to keep the hamster running at the target speed shown on the gauge (green zone). Too fast and it flies off the wheel; too slow and the wheel stops. When a treat appears on the wheel, tap/press either key right as it passes the top to grab a bonus without losing too much rhythm. You're scored on longest sustained run and treats collected before falling off.
