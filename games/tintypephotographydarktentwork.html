Tintype Photography Dark Tent Work
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Plate 1
Working entirely by feel inside the darkroom tent, you must reach for the right bottle at the right moment — collodion, then silver bath, then developer, then fixer — while the plate stays wet. Tap the bottles, use keys 1-4, or touch in the correct order before each timer bar runs out. Grab the wrong bottle or run out of time and the plate fogs; three fogged plates and the sitting is lost. Each new plate adds another careful step to the sequence.
