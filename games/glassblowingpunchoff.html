Glassblowing Punch Off
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/10
How to play: Press Space or Tap to score a clean line at the neck with the sharp tool — timing matters: score while the scoring needle sits in the gold zone. Then a tap indicator swings back and forth; press Space, Up Arrow, or Tap again exactly when it crosses the gold zone to punch the piece off cleanly. Miss either window and the piece cracks, costing a life. Pieces get more delicate and windows narrower each round — lose all 3 lives and the session ends!
