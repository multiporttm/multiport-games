🧵 Blender Making Motor Winding
Hold the throttle to spin up the spindle, release to let it coast down. Keep the RPM needle inside the green band to wind copper wire onto the motor coil without snapping it.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/↑) to spin the spindle faster; release to let it coast down. Keep the RPM needle inside the glowing green band to steadily wind wire onto the coil and fill the Winding Progress bar. Spin too fast and the Wire Integrity meter takes damage from snapping strands; let it empty and the motor is ruined. Complete each layer to advance to a tighter, faster band.
