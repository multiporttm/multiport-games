Disassemble the engine in order
 
How to play: A jet engine is mounted on the stand in six stages: Cowling, Fan Blades, Compressor, Combustor Seal, Turbine Blades, Exhaust Nozzle. Click or tap the glowing component (or press 1–6) to remove parts in that exact order. Once disassembled, read the maintenance clue and click the faulty component it describes. Then reassemble everything in reverse order before the countdown clock reaches zero. Three wrong moves ends your shift — repair as many engines as you can!
