Needle Felting Fiber Layering
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Pad 1
A wisp of wool sweeps back and forth across the pad, switching direction each layer — horizontal, then vertical, then back again. Press Space, tap, or click 🧵 Lay Tuft the instant the wisp crosses the glowing target band to lock a clean criss-crossed layer. Miss the band and the fiber lies loose; lay tufts too fast with no gaps and the pad overlofts and collapses. Finish every layer in a pad to start the next — three loose layers ruins the whole pad!
