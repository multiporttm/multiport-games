Ferris Wheel Loading
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Guests wait at the platform, each wanting a colored gondola. Press ACT (or Space / click the wheel) the moment a gondola sits in the loading zone at the bottom: an empty gondola boards the next guest, a full, finished gondola (ring glowing) unloads. Acting on the wrong gondola jams the wheel — 3 jams or 3 stranded guests ends the ride. Speed rises each round!
