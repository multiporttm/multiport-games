Fireworks Mortar Tube Placement
Score: 0 Hi: 0 Field: 1 ❤❤❤
Drag each mortar tube onto a marked firing slot, then use Left/Right Arrow (or drag sideways) to rotate it to a safe angle away from the wind and audience. Press Space or tap the tube to lock it in once the gauge is green. Lock every tube before the timer runs out — mis-angled or too-close tubes cost a life. Clear bigger fields with trickier wind to raise your score.
