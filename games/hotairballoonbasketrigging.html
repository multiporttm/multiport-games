Hot Air Balloon Basket Rigging
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Basket 1/5
How to play: Click, tap, or move the highlight with Arrow Keys and press Enter/Space to pick the rigging point numbered 1, then 2, and so on, in order. After picking a point, a tension gauge rises and falls — click, tap, or press Space the instant the marker is inside the glowing green band to lock the connection. Lock all points on the basket before moving to a bigger one. Picking the wrong point or locking outside the band costs a life!
