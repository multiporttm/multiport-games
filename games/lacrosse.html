Lacrosse
Goals: 0 | Time: 60 | Round 1
Move your attacker (green, with the ball) using WASD / Arrow Keys, or drag on touch. Click or tap anywhere on the field to shoot toward that spot when you have a clear lane. Avoid the red defenders — if one catches you while you're carrying the ball, they'll check it away. Beat the goalkeeper and find the open net to score. Score as many goals as you can before time runs out — defenders get tougher each round!
