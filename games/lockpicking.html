Lock Picking
Lock 1Score: 0 | Best: 0
Hold Tension steady, then click each pin in order (left to right) to pick it.
Press and hold Hold Tension (or hold the T key) to apply steady pressure to the lock. While holding tension, click each pin in order from left to right — a pin flashes green and gives a soft click cue when it's set correctly. Applying tension too hard before any pin is picked, or picking out of order, springs the pins back and you must start the lock over. Pick every pin before the timer runs out to open the lock. Each round adds more pins and a tighter timer!
