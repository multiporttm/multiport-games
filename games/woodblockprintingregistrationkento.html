How to play: Each printed color layer needs the paper's registration notches seated exactly against the block's carved kento corner mark and kagi straight mark. Drag the sheet with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with the arrow keys / on-screen arrows, until both orange notches line up with the blue kento marks before the ink-dry timer runs out. Click "Lock Registration" once aligned closely enough to print that layer — the closer the fit, the higher your score, and each successful layer makes the next notch tolerance a little tighter.
