Sailboat Rigging Trim
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Leg 1/10
How to play: Hold Left/Right Arrow (or drag/tap the left and right halves of the canvas) to ease or trim the sheet, keeping the sail's power meter inside the green power band as wind angle and strength keep shifting. Luff (too loose) or over-trim (too tight) too long and you lose a life. Hold the band through the leg's full time to finish it — wind shifts get faster and the power band narrows every leg. Lose all 3 lives and the race is over!
