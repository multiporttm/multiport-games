Leather Tanning Oiling

Drag the oil dauber across the hide (or use Arrow keys to move it and hold Space to apply oil) to work conditioning oil evenly into at least 85% of the hide before time runs out. The dauber empties with use — swing back to the oil pot in the corner to refill — and lingering too long in one spot over-soaks it into a stain, so keep moving and spread it evenly!
