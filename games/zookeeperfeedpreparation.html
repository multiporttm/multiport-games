Zookeeper Feed Preparation
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Read the prep sheet at the top for each animal's diet. Click/tap a food tray item (or press its number key) to scoop it into the bucket — click the same food again to add more scoops, or a different food to swap. Press V or tap the Vitamin toggle to add the supplement if the sheet calls for it. When the bucket matches the sheet exactly, press Enter/Space or tap Load Cart. Wrong loads cost a life — the sheet stays so you can fix it. Three lives, and food choices, quantities, and the clock all get tougher each level.
