Bladesmithing Sharpening
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Blade 1/8
How to play: Drag your mouse/finger up and down (or use Arrow Up/Down) to sweep the blade back and forth across the whetstone. Keep the angle needle inside the glowing orange safe zone on the gauge, and keep your stroke rhythm steady — swinging too fast, too slow, or letting the needle roll outside the zone burns through your edge meter. Empty it and you dull the blade, costing a life. Fill the sharpness bar to finish the blade and move to a sharper, trickier target. Lose all 3 lives and the blade is ruined!
