Butter Sculpture Carving Tool
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Sculpture 1
A chilled butter block sits on the turntable, and the carving tool sweeps back and forth across the marked notch line waiting for a steady hand. Press Space, tap, or click 🔪 Carve the instant the sweeping blade icon lines up with the glowing target notch — too early or too late and the blade skids across the surface. Land every notch in a sculpture to finish it and move to the next, trickier sculpture with a narrower, faster-moving target. Miss three notches and the block is ruined for the day.
