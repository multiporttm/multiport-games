Stitch markers slide toward the needle line. Tap the instant each one arrives to sew a clean, even seam.
Stitch markers slide left toward the needle line on the mat. Tap (click, tap, or Space/Enter) the instant a marker crosses the needle line to sew a clean stitch. Early or late taps, and markers you let pass untouched, loosen the seam and drain Thread Tension; keep a combo going to speed up and raise your score. Let Thread Tension run out and the seam rips.
