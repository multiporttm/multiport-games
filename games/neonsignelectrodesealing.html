Neon Sign Electrode Sealing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Tube 1/6
How to play: A flame sweeps around the rim of the glass tube. One glowing seal point is active at a time — press Space, click, or tap the moment the flame reaches it to fuse it evenly around the electrode. Seal too early or let the flame sweep past and the seam cracks, costing a life. Seal all the points around the tube to finish it — six tubes get narrower and the flame sweeps faster each time. Lose all 3 lives and the electrode is ruined!
