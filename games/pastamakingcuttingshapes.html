Pasta Making Cutting Shapes
Precision — drag the cutter along the glowing line
Round: 1 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space to auto-grab the nearest point) then drag along the glowing guide line to cut the pasta sheet. Staying close to the line fills your precision meter; wandering too far from the line too many times ruins the sheet. Finish each shape to advance to a trickier one!
