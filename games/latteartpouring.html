Move your pour with Left/Right Arrow (or drag/tap a lane) to match the falling pour cue, then press Space or tap right as it reaches the cup line to lock in a clean wiggle. Match enough cues in the right lane to finish the customer's pattern — a heart, rosetta, or tulip. Miss too many and the cup is spoiled. 3 spoiled cups and the café line is out the door!
