Millinery Felt Stiffening
Hats Stiffened: 0 | Hi: 0
Move the brush with the Arrow Keys or by dragging on the canvas, and hold Space (or keep your finger down while dragging) to apply stiffening solution to the felt. Keep the coverage needle inside the glowing safe zone — too little leaves bare patches, too much pools and ruins the felt. Click Finish Hat (or press Enter) when ready. Each round brings a bigger hat shape and a tighter target band.
