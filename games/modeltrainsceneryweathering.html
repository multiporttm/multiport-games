Model Train Scenery Weathering
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Car 1
 
How to play: Press and hold the pointer (or Tab to select a zone and hold Space) over a numbered zone on the train car to build up weathering grime. Watch each zone's gauge — release once it sits inside the glowing target band. Push a gauge past the top of the band and that zone is ruined, costing a life. Click Finish Weathering once every zone is in range. Later cars add more zones with narrower, trickier target bands.
