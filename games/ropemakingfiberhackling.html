Drag the fiber hank through every comb until it is combed clean, not tangled and not worn thin.
How to play: A hank of raw fiber lies at the start of a row of hackling combs, each a plank studded with sharp iron teeth. Drag the hank with the mouse or your finger, or move it with the arrow keys or WASD, pulling it past every comb in the row so its teeth rake through the strands. Each comb needs a few careful passes to reach a good clean combing shown in golden straw color; rush past too quickly and a comb stays pale and still tangled with shives, but linger on one comb too long and it frays past the mark into overworked, wasted tow, which costs you cleanliness. Watch the fiber-strength bar drain at the top — once it runs out, the hank is graded on how many combs landed in the good combing range. Hackle hank after hank, each with a shorter time limit, to build the highest score you can before a hank frays apart.
