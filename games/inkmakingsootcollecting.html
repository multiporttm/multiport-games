Lampblack ink is made by scraping soot off a plate held over burning oil lamps. Slide your collecting plate left and right along the top of the screen with the arrow keys, A/D, or by dragging/touching, to catch the curling grey soot puffs rising from the lamps below. Watch out for bright orange sparks mixed in with the soot — catching one scorches your plate, and three scorches ends the run. The longer you survive, the faster and thicker the soot rises, and the higher your score climbs!
