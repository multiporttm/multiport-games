Fish Quarantine Treatment
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Look over the new fish's symptoms, then click/tap (or press 1-3) the matching medication: Ich-X for white-spot ich, Fin-Heal for fin rot, Para-Clear for parasites. Keep dosing the same medication until the dosage meter fills into the green zone, then click/tap Clear for Exhibit (or press Space/Enter). Wrong medication or an overdose costs a life — three lives, and the clock gets shorter and symptoms harder to tell apart each level.
