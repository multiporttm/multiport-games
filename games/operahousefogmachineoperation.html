Hold Space or press and hold the TRIGGER button to pump atmospheric fog, and release to let it thin. Keep the density gauge inside the glowing target band for the whole scene — too little fog ruins the mood, too much blinds the audience. Bands get tighter and scenes get trickier as the show runs on. Three ruined scenes and you're pulled from the fog desk!
