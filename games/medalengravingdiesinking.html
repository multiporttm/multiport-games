Cutting pressure
Hold and sweep the graving tool (mouse, finger, or arrow keys) along the glowing outline to cut the steel die. Hold Shift or use the on-screen slider to keep cutting pressure inside the green safe band — too light leaves it unfinished, too heavy damages fine detail. Finish the whole outline to sink a new die and move to a more intricate design!
