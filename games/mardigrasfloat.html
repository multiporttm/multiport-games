Mardi Gras Float
Score: 0 | Lives: 3 | Time: 60 | Hi: 0
Use Arrow Keys / WASD or drag to move your catch basket along the parade street. Catch the beads, coins, and trinkets thrown from the float for points — but don't bother with confetti, it doesn't count! Missing a valuable trinket costs a life. Throws get faster and more frequent the longer you last!
