How to play: Move with Arrow Keys / WASD or the on-screen pad. You carry a lit taper — walk into the numbered torches in the correct order (1, 2, 3...) to light them. Your taper burns down over time; if it goes out, return to the glowing brazier to relight it before you can light any more torches. Light every torch before the timer hits zero to win the level!
