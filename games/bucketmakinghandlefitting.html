🪣 Bucket Making Handle Fitting
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
A wire handle end swings back and forth above a row of clip sockets. Tap, click, or press Space the instant the swinging end lines up over the lit socket to snap it in. Fit both ends of each handle to finish the bucket; a bad snap bends the wire and costs you a Grip point. Run out of Grip and the line stops - keep your fits clean to chase a new best score.
