Leather Craft
Punch the glowing hole, in order!
Pieces: 0 | Best: 0 | Accuracy: 100%
Step 1: PUNCH
Each leather piece has a ring of numbered stitch holes. First, click/tap the glowing hole in order all the way around to punch every hole. Then stitch it: click or drag from each hole to the next in the same order to pull the thread through. Punching or stitching out of order costs accuracy. Finish before the timer runs out — pieces get bigger and more complex (wallets, bags) as you go!
