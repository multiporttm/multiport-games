The engine saw hums as a long blank of bone-and-ebony stock feeds beneath its blade. A marker sweeps back and forth across the blank, and a highlighted target zone shows exactly where the next domino tile needs to be cut. Press Space, click the canvas, or tap Cut! the instant the marker lines up inside the target zone to slice a clean tile into your tray. Cutting too early or late produces a ragged tile and wastes a chunk of your blank stock. The target zone shrinks and the marker speeds up as you advance through more blanks, so sharpen your timing to cut a full run of tiles before your stock runs out!
