Kaleidoscope Making Lens Polishing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Lens 1
Move/drag in small circles over the smudge
Every optical lens for the kaleidoscope arrives from the grinder covered in cloudy smudges that have to be buffed away by hand before it is clear enough to let light through cleanly. Move the mouse in small circles over the canvas, or drag your finger on touch, or use the arrow keys to nudge the cloth — keep the polishing speed inside the green zone on the gauge below. Buff too gently and the smudge barely fades before your turn runs out; buff too hard and fast and you scratch the glass — three scratched lenses and the workshop closes for the day! Clear every lens in a set to move to a cloudier, trickier set.
