⚙️ Yoyo Making Weight Balancing
Round: 1 Score: 0 Best: 0
A gauge needle sweeps back and forth as you seat tiny weight rings onto the yoyo halves. Click, tap, or press Space/Enter the instant the needle is inside the glowing green zone to lock in a clean, balanced adjustment. Miss the zone and your balance accuracy meter takes damage. Every 5 clean adjustments the round gets harder as the needle sweeps faster and the zone narrows. Keep your accuracy above zero to keep building!
