Billiards Chalk Application
Round: 1Score: 0Lives: ❤❤❤Hi: 0
A twist indicator sweeps around the cue tip. Press Space, click, or tap right as it passes over a gray (uncovered) sector to chalk it blue. Some worn sectors need two twists. Pressing on an already-chalked sector wastes a twist. Cover every sector before your twist budget runs out to pass the round — run out early and you lose a life. Tips get more worn and the sweep gets faster each round.
