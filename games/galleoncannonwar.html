Galleon Cannon War
Hull: 100 | Sunk: 0 | Hi: 0
Move your galleon between lanes with W/S (or the Up/Down buttons) to line up with incoming threats. Press A to fire your Port (left) cannons or D to fire Starboard (right) cannons at enemy ships approaching from that side in your lane — each side has its own reload timer. Sink ships before they reach your hull. Survive and rack up as many sinkings as you can!
