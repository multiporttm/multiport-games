Arctic Expedition Frostbite Treatment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Case 1/5
How to play: Drag up/down on the water basin (or use the Up/Down Arrow Keys) to set the warm-water dial, which pulls the finger's temperature up or down over time. Keep the temperature inside the green safe rewarming band to fill the healing gauge — stray outside it too long and the safety meter drains; empty it (or scald the tissue by overshooting into the red zone) and you lose a life. Finish healing all five teammates, each with a colder start and a narrower safe band, before your 3 lives run out!
