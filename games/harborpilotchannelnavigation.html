Harbor Pilot Channel Navigation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Leg 1
How to play: Keep your ship inside the dredged channel as it winds between the shallow banks. Use ← / → (or drag left/right on the water) to steer and dodge crossing harbor traffic. Touch the shallow sand or another vessel and you lose a life — 3 groundings ends your run. The channel gets narrower, curvier and busier with every leg.
