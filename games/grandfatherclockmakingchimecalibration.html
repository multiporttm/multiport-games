Watch the bell rods glow in sequence, then click, tap, or press number keys 1-5 to strike them back in the exact same order. Press Replay Chime if you need another listen before striking.
Calibrating a grandfather clock's chime train means proving the five tuned bell rods ring true, in the right order, every single hour. Each round, a hammer sequence rings out across the bell rods, each one glowing and chiming as it's struck — watch and listen closely, because the sequence grows by one more note every round you clear. Once the demonstration ends, click, tap, or press the number keys 1 through 5 to strike the rods back in the exact same order from memory; if you need a second listen before committing, press Replay Chime to hear the sequence again at the cost of a small time penalty. A correct sequence advances you to a longer, trickier pattern and earns a solid score bonus, while a wrong strike costs a life and shows you how far your memory carried you. Lose all your lives and the calibration run ends, but every round survived pushes the chime pattern — and your score — further than before.
