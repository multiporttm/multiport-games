Mending

Stitch the tear closed, point by point.

A torn seam is marked with numbered stitch points. Click/tap each glowing stitch point in order along the tear. After each stitch, a tension bar appears — click/tap (or press Space) right when the marker is inside the green zone to pull the thread taut. Miss the order or the tension window and the mend weakens. Finish items for points — longer tears and finer stitch spacing follow each round.
