Needle Felting Armature Wiring
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Figure 1
Watch the glowing arrow sequence flash across the wire armature, then repeat each bend in order using the arrow keys (or tap the pad) before the pattern locks. Match every bend to twist the limb into shape — miss a bend or run out of time and the wire kinks. Complete all bends in a limb to wire the next one, but three kinks ruins the whole armature!
