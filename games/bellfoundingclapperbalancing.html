Bell Founding Clapper Balancing
Bell: 1 Arm: 100% Score: 0 Best: 0
A bronze clapper swings on a chain inside the bell. Use the Left/Right arrow keys (or drag/tap the left and right halves of the canvas) to shorten or lengthen the chain, steering the clapper so it strikes the glowing sweet spot on the bell wall at the peak of each swing. A clean strike on the mark scores big; a dull strike against bare bronze costs you arm strength. Survive enough clean strikes to cast the next, bigger bell!
