Press and hold (mouse or touch, or Space) to curl the mat over the roll, and keep holding as you roll it forward. Watch the pressure gauge — stay inside the glowing safe zone the whole way. Too little pressure and the roll falls apart; too much and filling squeezes out. Fillings get more intricate, and the safe zone narrows, each round.
