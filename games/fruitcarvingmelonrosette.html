Fruit Carving Melon Rosette
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Rosette 1/6
How to play: A glowing bead sweeps along a spiral rosette path cut into the melon's rind — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your curved knife tip inside the tolerance ring around the bead. Drift too far and your carving precision drains; empty it and you cut too deep, breaking the petal and costing a life. Stay close the whole spiral to finish the rosette and move to the next melon. Every round adds more petals and tightens the tolerance — lose all 3 lives and the rind splits!
