Metro Fare Turnstile Operation
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Passengers approach three turnstile lanes. Paying passengers (green ticket icon) should be let through — click/tap their lane or press 1/2/3 to open the gate. Fare evaders (red icon, jumping or squeezing) must be flagged — press Q/W/E (or the ⚠ button under that lane) to stop them before they pass. Letting an evader through, or blocking a paying rider, costs a life. Three lives — passengers speed up and evaders get sneakier every level.
