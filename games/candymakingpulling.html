Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Session 1/8
How to play: Press Left/Right Arrows (or click/tap the left and right hooks) to alternate pulling the taffy toward each hook. Keep your stretch inside the glowing safe zone on the gauge — pull too far past it and the taffy tears, losing a life; stall too long without alternating and it cools solid. Reach the target number of good pulls to finish the session. Sessions get longer and the safe zone narrower each round!
