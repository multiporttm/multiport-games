A basket of molted goose feathers sits on the workbench, and only the ones with just the right amount of spring in the shaft will hold up as a writing quill — too limp and the point will wobble, too stiff and the shaft will crack. Use the Left/Right arrow keys, drag, or tap ◀ Flex / Flex ▶ to flex the feather's shaft back and forth along its flex meter, and press Space, tap, or click 🪶 Select the instant the needle swings into the glowing target band to pick that feather for the bundle. Select too early or too late and the feather gets tossed back in the basket — three bad picks and the batch is wasted! Fill a whole bundle to move on to a trickier batch with a narrower target band.
