Brass Instrument Dent Removal
Level: 1 Lives: ♥♥♥ Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press when the rod crosses the glowing zone
Watch the dent rod swing back and forth across the gauge inside the tube. Press Space, Enter, click the button, or Tap the canvas right as the rod crosses the glowing target zone to push the dent out clean. Land in the bright center for a perfect pull, the wider zone for an okay pull, or miss it entirely and you'll over-stretch the brass and lose a life. Work through more dents per tube and trickier, faster-swinging tube curves as you level up!
