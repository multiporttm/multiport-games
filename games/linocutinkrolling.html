Linocut Ink Rolling
Block: 1/8 Lives: 3 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Hold Space (or press and hold the brayer on screen) to roll ink onto the block. Watch the coverage gauge and release right when it reaches the target band — stop too early and bare patches will show in the print, roll too long and the ink fills in the carved recesses, smudging fine lines. Ink every block in the run to rack up points!
