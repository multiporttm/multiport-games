🏮 Torchier Making Shade Fitting
Drag sideways to spin the shade. Line up the needle with a gold clip mark, then tap Fit to lock it in place before the shade slips off the pole.
Drag left or right anywhere on the shade (or use the Left/Right arrow keys) to spin it around the pole. When the needle at the top lines up with a gold clip mark, press the Fit button (or Space/Enter) to lock that clip. Lock every clip to finish the shade. Stability drains over time and refills a little with each successful fit - if it runs out the shade falls and the round ends.
