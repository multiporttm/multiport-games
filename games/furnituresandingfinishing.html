Furniture Sanding Finishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Grit 60/5
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or hold the Arrow Keys) to glide the sanding block across the whole panel, moving mostly left-right along the wood grain. Even horizontal strokes fill the coverage meter; sanding too steeply across the grain scuffs the finish and drains your quality meter. Cover the entire panel before quality runs out to advance to the next, finer grit — lose all 3 lives and the piece is ruined!
