Hockey Puck Drop Ceremony
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Drop 1
How to play: The two captains' sticks swing toward center ice while a timing needle sweeps back and forth below. Press Space, click, or tap the Drop Puck button the instant the needle is inside the glowing center zone to release a clean puck drop. Land it dead center for a perfect drop, land it near the edge for an ok drop, or miss the zone entirely and you lose a life. The zone narrows and the needle speeds up with every successful drop — lose all 3 lives and the ceremony is ruined.
