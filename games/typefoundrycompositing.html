Typefoundry Compositing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Line 1
How to play: A reference card shows the line of text you must set. Click or tap the next needed letter sort from the type case tray below, and it is set into your composing stick in reading order (left to right). Pick the wrong letter and you lose a life and the stick clears — every sort is cast facing the right way already, so just keep your sequence correct. Press Backspace to undo your last sort. Finish the line to advance; lines get longer and the tray gets more crowded each round. Lose all 3 lives and the forme is lost.
