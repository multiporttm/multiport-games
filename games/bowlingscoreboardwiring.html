Bowling Scoreboard Wiring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Board 1
How to play: Click or tap a lane sensor on the left, then click or tap the overhead display on the right it belongs to, to wire them together. On keyboard, use Arrow Up/Down to move the highlight, Enter or Space to pick a terminal, and Esc to cancel a pick. A wrong match costs a life. More lanes get added, and later boards drop the color hints, so you'll need to read the numbers.
