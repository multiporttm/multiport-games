Beekeeping Winter Wrapping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: A cold front is coming! Each hive shows numbered strap slots — the pattern card at the top tells you the correct order to click/tap the straps for each hive. Click slots in the exact order shown (or press number keys 1-4 for the focused hive's slots) to wrap and secure it with good tension. A strap clicked out of order snaps loose and costs a life. Finish wrapping every hive in the row before the cold front timer runs out. More hives and trickier patterns each round!
