How to play: A shaggy, uneven eave hangs along the bottom of the roof with a dashed trim line marking the intended cut height. Use the arrow keys/WASD, the on-screen buttons, or drag with the mouse/touch to move the shears across the eave from left to right while matching the blade's height to the trim line. Staying right on the line slices the straw cleanly and fills your tidy meter; drifting away from the line leaves stubble or gouges the coatwork and drains your precision meter. Reach the right-hand end with the eave fully trimmed before precision runs out to finish and move on to a longer, wavier eave.
