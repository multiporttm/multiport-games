Lapidary Gemstone Grinding

Hold Space, press and hold the gem, or press and hold ⚙️ Hold to Grind to run the rough stone against the spinning wheel and shape its dome. Watch the progress bar — release right as it reaches the gold target line! Grind past it and the cabochon is ground flat and ruined. Shape every cabochon in the batch — three ruined stones and the batch is lost!
