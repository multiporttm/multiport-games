Senet Board Game
You are the white pieces, the AI plays black. Tap "Cast Sticks" to throw the casting sticks and get a move value. Tap one of your glowing pieces to move it that many squares along the snaking path (1→30). Land on a lone enemy piece to swap it back to your old square (capture); the green House of Rebirth (15) and gold House of Beauty (26) protect a piece from capture; landing on the blue House of Water (27) sends a piece back to square 1. Throws of 1, 4, or 6 grant another cast. Bear all 5 pieces off past square 30 first to win!
