Quill Pen Making Nib Cutting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Shaft 1
A cured quill shaft is clamped to the bench, and the penknife has to slice through at precisely the right slant or the nib tip will split or write too fat. Use the Left/Right arrow keys, drag, or tap ◀ Swing / Swing ▶ to swing the knife's blade angle back and forth, and press Space, tap, or click ✂️ Cut the instant the blade swings into the glowing target wedge to slice a clean nib. Cut too early or too late and the shaft splinters — three bad cuts and the shaft is ruined! Finish a whole set of nibs to move on to a trickier shaft with a narrower target wedge.
