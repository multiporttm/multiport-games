A raw quill shaft is buried in a pan of hot sand over the fire, and it has to come out at exactly the right temperature or the barrel stays soft and papery, or worse, scorches black and brittle. Use the Left/Right arrow keys, drag, or tap ◀ Stir / Stir ▶ to sweep the temperature gauge up and down, and press Space, tap, or click 🔥 Pull Out the instant the gauge needle swings into the glowing target zone to pull a perfectly cured shaft. Pull too early or too late and the shaft is ruined — three ruined shafts and the batch is lost! Cure a whole batch to move on to a hotter bath with a narrower target zone.
