Bagel Making Toasting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1/5
How to play: The browning gauge's golden safe zone drifts around the dial as the halves toast. Use the Left/Right Arrow keys or drag around the dial to steer the heat needle and keep it inside the safe zone. Drift outside too long and your toast meter drains toward pale or burnt; empty it and that half is ruined, costing a life. Hold steady for the full toast cycle to pop a perfect golden half and move to the next round. The zone drifts faster and narrows every round — lose all 3 lives and the tray is ruined!
