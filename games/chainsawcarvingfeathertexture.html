Chainsaw Carving Feather Texture
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Barb 1
A wood eagle's wing needs crisp feather-barb texture cuts radiating from the shaft. A highlighted wedge shows the exact angle the next barb cut needs. Drag your finger or mouse around the dial to swing the chainsaw tip — or press ← / → (or the rotate buttons) to turn it — then release, tap 🪚 Cut, or press Space while lined up with the wedge. Line up closely enough and the cut lands clean; miss the angle and you gouge the wood. Three bad gouges and the feather is ruined!
