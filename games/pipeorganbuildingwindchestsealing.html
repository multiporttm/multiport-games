Move the dauber over a hissing leak and hold seal to press glue into the seam.
How to play: A pressurized windchest sits on the bench, with tiny leaks hissing air out of its seams at random spots. Move the sealant dauber left and right with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging on the canvas, and hold the Seal button (or hold Space, or press and hold the on-screen button) to work glue into the leak directly under the dauber. Several leaks open up across the chest at once — plug them all before the chest bleeds down to zero pressure. Keep sealing on a spot after it is already sealed and you risk gumming a pallet valve, costing points; leave a leak open too long and pressure keeps draining. Seal enough windchests before pressure runs out to post a high score.
