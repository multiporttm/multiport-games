📏 Ring Making Sizing

Mount the band on the sizing mandrel. Press when the gauge marker sweeps into the green zone to lock the exact size.

A marker sweeps across the sizing gauge as the mandrel stretches or compresses the band. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter when it lands in the green zone to lock in the exact size. Missing over- or under-sizes the band and drains your precision focus - empty it and the ring must be scrapped. Five perfect sizings in a row speed up the next round.
