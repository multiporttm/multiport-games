Cooperage Rivet Hammering
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Rivet 1/10
How to play: A needle sweeps back and forth across the power gauge. Press Space, click, or tap to swing the hammer the instant the needle is inside the green safe zone — strike too weak or too hard (outside the zone) and the rivet bends, costing a life. Land 3 good strikes to fully seat each rivet and move to the next one. The needle sweeps faster and the safe zone narrows every few rivets, across 10 rivets per hoop band.
