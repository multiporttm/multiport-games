🥏 Frisbee Making Rim Weighting
Tap the left or right side of the rim to nudge tiny calibration weights, level the balance needle inside the green zone, then lock it in before time runs out.
Tap or click the left/right half of the scale (or use Left/Right arrow keys) to nudge calibration weights and level the tilting rim. When the needle sits inside the green zone, press Space/Enter or tap the needle hub to lock it in. Lock in off-center or let the timer bar expire and you waste calibration stock; run out and the shift ends.
