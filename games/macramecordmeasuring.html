Macrame Cord Measuring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤
Drag the cutter along the ruler (or use Left/Right Arrow, hold Shift for fine control) to match the target length from the project plan, then press Space or Tap the cord to cut. Cutting closer to the target wastes less cord and scores more — projects get longer and tolerances tighter each round!
