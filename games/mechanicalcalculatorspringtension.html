How to play: Drag the slider, click the + / − buttons, or press Left/Right arrows to wind each worn return spring to its hidden safe tension window. When you think you are close, press Pull Test Key (or Enter) — too loose and the lever droops back slowly, too stiff and it rattles and binds, just right and it snaps back clean. A bad test costs a life, and every new spring shrinks the safe window and shortens your clock.
