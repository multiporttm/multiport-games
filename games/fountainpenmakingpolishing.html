Fountain Pen Making Polishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Pen 1/10 Time: 20
How to play: Move the polishing wheel with your mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys, and hold Space / tap-and-hold / click-and-hold to buff. Sweep over every dull gray patch on the pen until it shines gold before the timer runs out. Don't linger too long in one spot — the heat gauge rises while buffing and if it maxes out the lacquer scratches, costing a life. Keep moving, cover the whole pen, and work faster each round as pens grow more intricate!
