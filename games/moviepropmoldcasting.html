Movie Prop Mold Casting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Mold 1/8
How to play: Hold Space, click, or touch-and-hold the canvas to pour resin into the mold — keep the white temperature needle inside the green band while pouring. Bubbles rise out of the resin as it fills; click/tap each bubble (or steer onto it with Arrow Keys and press Enter) to pop it before the cure timer runs out. Pour too hot or too cold, or let a bubble survive, and you lose a life. Clear every bubble and fill the mold to finish it — the molds get bigger and trickier as you go!
