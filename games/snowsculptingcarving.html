Snow Sculpting Carving
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Block 1/6
How to play: A glowing carving edge travels along the outline of the shape — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your saw tip inside the icy-blue tolerance band around it. Drift too far and your control meter drains; empty it and you cut too deep, breaking off a section and costing a life. Stay close the whole way to finish the block cleanly. The outline gets more intricate and the edge moves faster every block — lose all 3 lives and the sculpture is ruined!
