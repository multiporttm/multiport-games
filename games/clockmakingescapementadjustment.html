Clockmaking Escapement Adjustment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Watch 1/6
How to play: The top pendulum is the reference metronome ticking the true beat; your escapement's anchor below drifts off that beat over time and gets knocked by random jolts. Use ← / → Arrow Keys, click/tap the Slow / Fast buttons, or hold Space for a quick nudge toward center, to walk the needle on the gauge into the glowing safe zone and hold it there until the progress bar fills. Wander into the red too long and you lose a life. Each new watch narrows the safe zone and jolts you harder!
