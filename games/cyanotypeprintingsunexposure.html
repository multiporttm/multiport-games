Cyanotype Printing Sun Exposure
Prints Exposed: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Chase the sun patch until the exposure meter sits in the green band, then pull the print indoors.
How to play: A coated sheet rests on a board outside while a bright sun patch drifts across the ground and clouds occasionally slide through to dim it. Slide the board left and right with the arrow keys, A and D, the on-screen buttons, or by dragging the board directly, to keep the paper sitting in the brightest light so the exposure meter at the top climbs as fast as possible. Once the meter's pointer sits inside the green target band, press the Pull Indoors button (or hit Space or Enter) to lock in a perfectly exposed print; wait too long after entering the band and the exposure keeps climbing until the sheet is bleached out and overexposed, ruining the print. Expose as many good prints as you can — each round the sun patch moves faster and clouds roll in more often — to set a new high score.
