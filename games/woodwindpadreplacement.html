Select a key, then center and size the pad
Select a key cup by clicking/tapping it or pressing its number key. Drag the felt pad (or use Arrow keys) to center it over the key cup, and use the Pad Size slider to match the tone hole's diameter. Press Seat Pad (or Enter) to glue it down and test the seal. Replace the pads on every key before you run out of attempts — across more keys needing pad replacement as you level up!
