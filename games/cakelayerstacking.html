Cake Layer Stacking
Score: 0 Layer: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Three steps per layer: press Space or tap to stop the leveling needle inside the green zone; click/tap grid cells (or move with Arrow Keys and press Space) to spread filling until coverage is in range, then press Space again to finish spreading; finally press Space to stop the moving layer centered on top. Miss and you lose a life — topple three times and it's game over!
