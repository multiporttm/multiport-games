Encaustic Tile Making Pigment Pouring
Tile: 1 Pigment Left: 16 Score: 0 Best: 0
Hold Pour (or hold Space / press and hold on the canvas) to stream pigment into the compartment. Release right as the fill line enters the glowing target band — too little or too much wastes pigment.
Once a brass mold is assembled, each small compartment it forms must be filled with liquid pigment slurry to exactly the right depth — too shallow and the fired tile shows a pale, washed-out patch; too deep and the pigment floods over the brass divider and bleeds into the neighboring color. A pigment hopper hangs above each compartment on the mold table; hold down Pour (press and hold Space, or press and hold the Pour button or the canvas itself) to open the spout and watch the fill line climb, then let go the instant it reaches the glowing target band for a perfect, even pour. The compartments get narrower and the safe band tightens as you work through more tiles, so watch the rising pigment closely and let go at just the right moment before your limited pigment supply runs dry.
