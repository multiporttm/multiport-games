How to play: Each customer's cone order appears above the cone as a row of colored dots, in order. Click a flavor (or press 1-6) to scoop it onto the cone — scoops stack bottom to top matching the order shown. Press Backspace or Undo Scoop to remove the top scoop, then Enter or Serve Cone once it matches. Scoops melt if you dawdle, and customers leave if the line waits too long — keep it moving!
