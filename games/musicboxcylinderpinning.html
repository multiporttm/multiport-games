Music Box Cylinder Pinning

Place pins to match the melody below
Left/Right move beat marker · Up/Down pick tooth row · Space/Tap place pin · Enter/double-tap to play cylinder
Place tiny pins into a music box cylinder at the exact positions needed to play a target melody when the comb teeth are plucked. Use Left/Right or A/D to move along the beats, Up/Down or W/S to pick the tooth row, Space or Tap to place/remove a pin, and Enter or double-tap to spin the cylinder and check your melody. Melodies grow longer and busier each level.
