Luthier Sound Post Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Fitting 1/10
How to play: The small diagram at top-right marks exactly where the sound post must stand inside the violin. Drag the post (or use Arrow Keys — Left/Right slides its foot, Up/Down tilts it) to line it up over the marked spot, standing as close to upright as the dashed safe zone allows. The green balance bar drains while you lean too far — let it empty and the post topples, costing a life. When you are inside the box, click Set Post (or press Enter) to glue it in. The target gets tighter and trickier every fitting!
