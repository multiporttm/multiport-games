Cones: 0 | Hi: 0
Batter bakes on the press automatically. Watch the cooling-window gauge rise as the cone cools — press Space, click, or tap the button to twist it into a spiral exactly when the needle is in the green zone. Twist too early and it tears, wait too long and it hardens flat. Target shapes get trickier each round!
