Gingerbread Candy Decorating
Score: 0 House: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Each glowing spot on the house shows which candy it needs. Pick that candy from the tray below (or press 1-4), then click/tap the spot — or move the highlight with Arrow Keys and press Space — to press it into the wet icing before the timer bar empties. Wrong candy or running out of time costs a life. Fill every spot to finish the house — the next house is busier and faster!
