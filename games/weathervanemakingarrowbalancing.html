🧭 Weathervane Making Arrow Balancing
Nudge the arrow left and right to counter gusting wind and keep it balanced on its pivot pin as long as you can.
Press and hold the left/right edges of the frame (or A/D, or Left/Right arrow keys) to nudge the weathervane arrow against the wind. Keep the tilt needle near center — if it swings all the way to either edge the arrow topples off its pivot. Survive each gust to advance; gusts grow stronger and gustier each round.
