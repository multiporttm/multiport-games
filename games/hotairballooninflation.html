Hot Air Balloon Inflation
Level: 1 Lives: 3 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Hold F or the FAN button to cold-inflate the envelope with air. Once it is full, hold Space / the BURNER button in short bursts to heat it upright — watch the heat gauge, too much burner scorches the fabric! Keep the balloon from tipping in the wind. Inflate across trickier wind rounds to raise your score.
