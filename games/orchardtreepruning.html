Orchard Tree Pruning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Tree 1
How to play: Drag your shears (mouse, finger, or Arrow keys) slowly over the glowing marked branch wood to prune it away. Keep a steady hand — moving too fast ruins the cut — and never drag over the thick brown trunk, or you will lose a life. Clear a tree to move on to a more tangled one. Press Space or tap to begin.
