Pick a spotlight with Tab or click/tap its column, then drag a slider (mouse/touch) or use Arrow Keys (Left/Right picks the slider, Up/Down adjusts it) to set Angle, Color Temperature, and Brightness. Keep every spotlight inside the safe green range — too hot/bright causes glare, too cool/bright builds up damaging UV — then press Enter/Space to confirm the case before the exhibit opens.
