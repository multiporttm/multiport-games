Touch Tank Monitoring
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Watch the three touch-tank zones. When a visitor flashes red (rough touch!), click/tap that zone — or press 1/2/3 — to coach them before the warning runs out; ignore visitors who are already gentle (green). Also keep an eye on the water quality bar: click/tap it (or press T) to treat the water whenever it drifts out of the safe band. Missing a rough touch or leaving the water out of range too long costs a life. Three lives, and incidents get faster and the water drifts quicker every level.
