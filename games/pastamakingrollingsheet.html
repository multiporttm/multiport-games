Thickness Gauge — hold the needle in the glowing target band
Round: 1 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Hold Space or press and hold / tap the canvas to crank the machine, release to slow down. Keep the thickness needle inside the glowing target band as the sheet feeds through — crank too fast and the sheet tears, go too slow and the dough balls up and jams. Clear the sheet to advance to the next, thinner round!
