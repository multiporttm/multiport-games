Clockmaking Mainspring Winding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Movement 1/6
How to play: A movement is loaded on the bench with its mainspring slack. Hold Space / Mouse / Touch to turn the winding key — the tension needle climbs the gauge. Release while the needle sits inside the glowing green safe zone to store maximum power. Release too early (needle below the zone) and the clock is left underpowered; hold too long and the spring snaps — either way you lose a life. Each new movement winds up stiffer and faster, and the safe zone shrinks. 3 lives — wind all 6 movements to become a master clockmaker!
