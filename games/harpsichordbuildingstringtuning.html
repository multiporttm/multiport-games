A pitch needle sweeps across the tuning gauge as the hammer turns the wrest-pin, pulling the string sharp then letting it drift flat again. A gold band marks true pitch for this string. Press Space, tap, or click 🎵 Lock Pin the instant the needle crosses into the gold band to set the pin and hold the string dead in tune. Locking too sharp or too flat leaves a jangling string — three bad strings and the course is ruined! Tune every string in a course to move on to a tighter course with a narrower sweet spot.
