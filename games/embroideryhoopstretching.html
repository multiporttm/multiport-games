Embroidery Hoop Stretching
Lives: 3 Level: 1 Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Click the glowing pin to begin tightening
Click or tap the pins around the hoop in the glowing cross-pattern order (never two neighbors in a row) to tighten the fabric drum-tight. Watch the tension meter — a pin pulled too hard or too late warps the weave and costs a life. Across larger hoops and trickier fabrics!
