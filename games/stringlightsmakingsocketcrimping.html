🔌 String Lights Making Socket Crimping
Press and hold the pedal to build up crimp force, then release the instant the needle sits inside the gold target band.
Press and hold (click, tap, or Space/Enter held down) to charge the crimp press. The force needle rises steadily — release exactly while it is inside the gold target band to seat a clean socket. Release too early and the crimp is loose; let it rise past the top and the wire is crushed. Either mistake drains your Wire Integrity meter — let it empty and the strand is ruined.
