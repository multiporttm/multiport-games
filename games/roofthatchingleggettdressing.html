Tap SWING, click, or press Space when the marker is in the green zone to dress each clump.
How to play: A fresh course of loose straw runs along the batten, with rough clumps marked out one at a time. A swing meter sweeps back and forth above the active clump. Press Space, click, or tap the SWING button exactly when the marker sits inside the green zone to bring the leggett down square and flatten that clump flush with the course below. A good strike advances you to the next clump and builds your streak; a mistimed strike leaves the straw ragged and drains your focus meter. Dress every clump in the course before focus runs out to finish the row and move on to a longer course with a faster-moving meter.
