Use Arrow Keys / WASD or drag to carry your tray of root beer mugs through the festival hall. Reach the glowing golden table to deliver your mugs before time runs out — but don't bump into the crowd or barrels, or you'll spill! Deliver enough mugs to advance to the next, busier round.
