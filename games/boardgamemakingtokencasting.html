🪙 Board Game Making Token Casting
Press and hold (click, tap, or Space) to pour molten metal into the mold. Release the instant the level sits inside the gold target band to cast a clean token.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space) to pour molten metal into the token mold — the level rises while you hold. Release right when the level is inside the gold target band to cast a clean token and score points for accuracy. Release too early, too late, or let it overflow and the cast is ruined, costing you a mold; run out of molds and it's game over. The target band narrows and the pour speeds up each round.
