Tintype Photography Developer Pouring
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Plate 1
Hold the tray steady and press and hold Space, or press and hold 🫗 Pour Developer (mouse or finger), to wash iron developer across the exposed plate — a faint image rises out of the silver as it works. A gold band marks the moment the image is fully built with rich, correct contrast. Release the instant the fill lands in the gold band to rinse it clean at its best. Stop too early and the image stays faint and thin; let it run too long and the plate fogs into solid black — three ruined plates and the darkroom session ends! Develop every plate in a session to move to a trickier session with a narrower window.
