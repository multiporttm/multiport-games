Hold Space or the ACCELERATE button to power the trolley forward — release to coast and brake. Press B or tap BELL to warn pedestrians and cars near crossings ahead (unrung crossings cause a crash!). Bring the trolley to a full stop with its front doors inside the green platform zone at each station for a Perfect stop. Clear every stop on the route to advance to a busier route.
