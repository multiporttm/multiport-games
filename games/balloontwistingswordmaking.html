Balloon Twisting Sword Making
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sword 1/8
How to play: Hold Space, your mouse, or your finger down to pump air into the balloon — keep the pressure needle inside the lit safe zone. Too little pressure leaves it floppy and weak; too much and it pops! While the pressure is safe, white twist markers light up along the blade — click/tap each one (or press the matching number key) exactly when it glows to lock a sharp, precise twist point. Lock every marker to finish the sword and move on to a longer, trickier blade.
