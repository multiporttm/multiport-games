Music Box Lid Hinge Fitting
Lives: 3Level 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Nudge the selected hinge pin into its socket
Left/Right or A/D nudge the pin · Tab or click a hinge to select it · Drag a pin directly · Space/Tap to seat it
Fit and align small brass hinges onto a music box lid so it swings open and closed without binding. Click or Tab to select a hinge, then use Left/Right or A/D, or drag the pin, to line it up with its socket, and press Space or tap to seat it once aligned. A badly seated pin will bind the lid and cost a life. More hinges and tighter tolerances appear as you level up.
