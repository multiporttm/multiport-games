Quiver Packing
Quiver 1
Packed: 0/0 | Best Total: 0
Drag each arrow from the tray on the left into the quiver on the right (mouse, touch, or drag with keyboard Tab+Arrows+Enter to place the selected arrow). Longer arrows need more depth to fit and wide fletching needs side clearance — arrows glow red when their fletching would overlap a neighbor; release outside the quiver or on a collision to send it back to the tray. Click Finish Quiver once you've packed what you can (or when the quiver is full) to score and move to the next, trickier assortment of arrows.
