Dog Park Behavior Coaching
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Each play group's meter climbs from green (healthy play) to yellow (tension) to red (scuffle). Click a group (or press its number key) only while it's yellow to redirect it. Stepping in during green is an overreaction, and letting one reach red starts a scuffle — both cost a life. Three lives — more groups appear and tension rises faster each level.
