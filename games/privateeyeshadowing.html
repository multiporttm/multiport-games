Private Eye Shadowing
Hi: 0 Suspicion Sight
Use Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag / tap on mobile, to move. Stay close behind the target to keep them in sight. When they glance back (watch for the eye icon), duck into a shaded cover spot or blend into a crowd cluster — stay exposed too long and the suspicion meter maxes out, blowing your cover. Follow them all the way to the destination to win the case!
