🛡️ Shield Leather Covering
Stretch: 1 Score: 0 Best: 0
Click, tap, or press Space/Enter the instant the sweeping roller marker crosses the glowing green zone to press the hide down cleanly. A clean press boosts your score and the bond meter; a mistimed press leaves a bubble and damages the bond. Land 5 clean presses to finish a stretch and start a faster one. If the bond meter runs out, the leather tears and the run ends!
