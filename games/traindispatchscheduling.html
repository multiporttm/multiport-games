Train Dispatch Scheduling
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Goal: Each train waiting on the left shows its destination track. Click/tap a waiting train to select it, then click/tap one of the 3 tracks (or press 1, 2, 3 to select a train and Q, W, E to send it) to dispatch it onto that track. Only one train may occupy a track at a time — sending a train onto an occupied track or letting the junction clog costs a life. Clear trains before they pile up; more trains arrive and tracks clear slower each level. Three lives.
