Pinstriping Paint Mixing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1
How to play: Each color card shows how many drops of every base paint to mix. Click or tap a paint can (or press its number key) to add one drop to the cup, Backspace/swipe-down to undo the last drop, and Enter or the Pour button to pour once you think the ratio is exact. Pour with the wrong count — even one drop off — and the batch is ruined. Match the color before the clock runs out to move to a trickier recipe.
