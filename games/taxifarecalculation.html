Taxi Fare Calculation
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Add up the base fare, the per-mile charge, the wait-time charge and any surcharge shown on the receipt. Type the exact total with the on-screen keypad or your number keys (. for the decimal, Backspace to erase), then press Enter or tap the total to print the receipt. A wrong total costs a life. Three lives — the clock gets tighter and surcharges appear more often every level.
