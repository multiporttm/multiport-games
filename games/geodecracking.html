Position the chisel
Drag along the faint crack line to position the chisel, then lock it in.
How to play: Drag left/right (or use Arrow Keys) to slide the chisel along the faint dashed crack line to the correct spot, then click Lock Chisel (or press Enter). A power meter needle then sweeps back and forth — click Strike! (or press Space) exactly when it's in the green zone. Get both position and timing right to split the geode cleanly and reveal the crystals inside. Miss either one and it shatters! Crack lines and timing get trickier every round.
