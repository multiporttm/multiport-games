Bladesmithing Forge Welding
Round: 1 Layer: 1/2 Lives: ❤❤❤ Score: 0 Hi: 0
How to play: Hold Pump (or Space, or press/hold on the gauge) to heat the layered billets in the forge. Watch the vertical heat gauge — get the glow into the green weld band. Then hit Strike (or Enter) exactly when the moving marker on the timing bar is inside its highlighted safe zone. Underheating gives a weak bond, mistiming wastes the heat, and overheating burns the steel — any of these costs a life. Weld together more layers each round as the band narrows and the timing window tightens!
