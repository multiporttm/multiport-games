🥄 Ladle Making Handle Welding
Drag your torch along the glowing seam line to weld the handle to the bowl. Stray too far and you'll scorch the metal or leave a gap.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter+arrow keys) to move the torch up and down, keeping it on the glowing weld seam as it scrolls past. Staying close to the seam builds a strong, continuous bead and raises your score; drifting too far scorches the handle and drains your Weld Integrity meter — let it empty and the weld fails.
