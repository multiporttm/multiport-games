Click/touch-and-hold near the start dot, then drag your cursor or finger along the glowing engraving line to the end dot — or hold Space and use the Arrow keys to steer the graver by hand. Staying close to the line fills your progress; drifting too far raises the Slip gauge. Finish the full scroll pattern before slipping completely off three times!
