Yacht Anchor Setting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Cove 1/10
How to play: Each cove shows the water depth and forecast wind. Use −/+ (or Left/Right Arrow) to set your anchor rode length to the right scope ratio for the conditions, then click Drop & Back Down. Hold Hold Reverse Throttle (or Space/Down Arrow) gently — not too hard, not too soft — to confirm the anchor digs in and the tension gauge fills. Pick bad scope or throttle too hard and you'll drag and lose a life. Conditions get trickier each cove — lose all 3 lives and the cruise ends!
