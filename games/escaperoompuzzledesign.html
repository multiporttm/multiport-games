Escape Room Puzzle Design
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Click/tap two puzzle cards to swap them, or use Arrow Keys to move the selector and Space to pick up/drop a card. Arrange the chain left-to-right so each puzzle's unlock requirement (shown below it) is satisfied by a puzzle already solved earlier in the chain — no dead ends, no skippable steps. Press/tap Test Chain (or Enter) to check it. Mistakes cost a life — three lives, and chains get longer each level.
