STAMINA
Alternate LEFT / RIGHT paddle strokes (Arrow keys, A/D, or the on-screen buttons) as fast and evenly as you can to sprint down the lane. Even, alternating strokes build the most speed — but paddling drains your stamina, and running dry slows you way down, so pace yourself! Beat the AI racers over three sprint distances to rack up the most points.
