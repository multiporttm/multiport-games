Linocut Carving
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Design 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead sweeps the carve channel that hugs the raised design — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your gouge tip inside the brown channel around it. Drift inward onto the red design line and you gouge it by mistake, costing a life instantly. Drift outward past the channel and your clearance meter drains; empty it and the waste linoleum is left uncarved, also costing a life. Clear the whole channel to finish the design and move on. The bead speeds up and the channel narrows every design — lose all 3 lives and the block is spoiled!
