The current stick (shown at top) needs a slot with enough airflow to dry before the sun timer runs out. Click an empty rack slot (or press its number key) to place it — more fan icons means faster drying.
Freshly rolled incense sticks need the right amount of airflow to dry evenly before nightfall. Each slot on the wooden rack shows one, two or three fan icons for its airflow level, and the stick waiting at the top of the screen is labelled light, medium or heavy — heavier sticks need more airflow to finish drying in time. Click an empty slot, or press the matching number key, to place the current stick there; it will slowly turn golden as it dries, faster in high-airflow slots. Watch the sun timer bar at the top: when it empties, the day ends and any stick that has not fully dried is wasted. Fully dry enough sticks in a day before your supply of fresh sticks or the sun timer runs out to move on to a longer, busier day with a bigger rack!
