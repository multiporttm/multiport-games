Birdhouse Entry Hole Drilling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Hole: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Drag/tap or use W A S D to move the drill tip onto the marked target circle (the species guide shows the exact spot and size). Hold Space / HOLD TO DRILL to drill — while drilling, keep the wobble needle inside the green safe zone using Left/Right arrows (or the Tilt buttons). Drift too far off-target for too long and the wood splits!
