Time: 0.0s
Drag the green print plate (mouse or touch) or nudge it with the arrow keys until it sits over the yellow registration crosshair, then press the Print button (or Space/Enter) before the countdown hits zero. Aligning closely and printing in time keeps your Print Quality meter healthy and scores points; a misaligned or late print smudges the board and drains it. The registration window narrows and the timer shortens each round.
