Cheese Aging Cellar Flip
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Lives: ❤️❤️❤️
Each shelf holds a cheese wheel aging week by week. Watch the ring around each wheel — when it fills and glows, that wheel is ready to flip: click or tap it (or use number keys 1-9 for shelf position) right away. Flip it too late and the wheel spoils, costing a life. More wheels join the cellar as weeks pass!
