Percussion Drum Head Tuning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Head 1/5
How to play: A drum head's tension rods are numbered in the correct star-crossed tuning order. Click the glowing rod (or press the matching number key, 0 for the 10th rod) to tighten it a step toward the target pitch — do them in order, round after round, until the whole head's pitch sits evenly inside the target zone. Tighten a rod out of order and the head slips, costing a life. Run out of time or lives and the head is ruined!
