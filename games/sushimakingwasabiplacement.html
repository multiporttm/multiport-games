Press and hold (mouse or touch) on the marked spot on the sushi to load wasabi onto your pick — the amount gauge fills while you hold. Release within the glowing target band, with the dab centered on the mark, then click Apply Wasabi to confirm. Too little or too much wasabi — or missing the spot — spoils the piece. More pieces arrive, and the target band narrows, each round.
