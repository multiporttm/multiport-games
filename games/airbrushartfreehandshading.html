Airbrush Art Freehand Shading
Piece: 1 Paint: 100% Score: 0 Best: 0
Move the mouse (or drag with your finger) over the stencil shape to spray gradient shading — hold the trigger with mouse-down or touch, and use Up/Down arrows (or pinch) to change the airbrush distance, which controls how soft or dark the spray lands. Fill the shaded zone evenly: too close and too long leaves dark blotches, too far leaves it bare. Watch your paint gauge — release the trigger (lift finger / release mouse) to stop spraying and save paint. Finish each shape within the target shading band before the canvas runs dry!
