A chasing tool slides back and forth over the pitch bowl. Press Space, tap the circle, or click 🔨 Tap Hammer the instant it crosses the bright target band to raise a smooth bump. Tap too early or too late and the metal tears. Raise every bump in the design to finish it — three torn spots and the sheet is scrapped!
