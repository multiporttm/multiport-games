Leather Carving Swivel Knife Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Cut 1/8
How to play: The swivel knife automatically advances along the design line — drag your mouse or finger up/down (or use the Up/Down Arrow Keys) to steer blade depth so the needle on the side gauge stays inside the green safe zone. Cut too shallow and the line will not hold; press too deep and the leather is sliced clean through — either drains your cut integrity. Empty it and you lose a life. Finish the whole cut while still holding together to move to the next hide. Cuts get longer and the safe zone narrower each round — lose all 3 lives and the hide is ruined!
