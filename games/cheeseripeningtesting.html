Cheese Ripening Testing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Click/tap the wheel (or press Space) to probe it with the tasting iron and reveal its texture and aroma readouts. Then decide with the buttons (or keys 1/2/3) whether the cheese is Under-ripe, Ready, or Over-ripe before the clock runs out.
