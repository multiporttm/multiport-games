How to play: Orders appear at the top with a shrinking timer — click or tap the matching flower bucket on the cooler shelves before it expires (number keys 1-9 also work). When two buckets of the same flower are on the shelf, always grab the older one first (lowest age number) — that's FIFO stock rotation, and grabbing the wrong one costs a life. Fill orders correctly to score, and watch out — the shift speeds up! You have 3 lives — good luck!
