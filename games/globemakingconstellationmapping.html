Before engraving the celestial globe you must memorize where each constellation's stars connect. Watch the gold lines trace a path from star to star in order, then click or tap the stars yourself in that exact same order to engrave the pattern. Each round adds one more star to the chain. Clicking the wrong star scratches the brass — three scratches and the globe is ruined! Click 👁 Watch Again to replay the pattern, or use number keys 1-9 to pick stars in the order they appear left-to-right, top-to-bottom.
