Eid Celebration
🌙 Round: 1/5 💰 Budget: 0 😄 Joy: 0 🏆 Best Total: 0
Pick items in every category within your budget!
How to play: Pick decorations, food, and gifts to plan the best Eid celebration you can afford. Each item has a Cost and a Joy rating. Select items by tapping or clicking cards; selected items are added to your basket and their cost is deducted from your budget. Click Celebrate! once you are happy with your picks (or when your budget runs out) to score the round. Budgets shrink each round — plan wisely!
