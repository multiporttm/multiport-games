Satellite Thermal Blanket Wrap
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lives: 3 | Shape: 1 | Phase: Wrap
How to play: Click/drag or use Arrow Keys + Space to paint gold multi-layer insulation over every highlighted cell of the satellite outline — paint outside the outline and you lose a life. Cover it completely with no gaps to move to the Tape phase: click (or Arrow Keys + Space) every seam marker before the timer runs out to seal it. Shapes get bigger and trickier each round!
