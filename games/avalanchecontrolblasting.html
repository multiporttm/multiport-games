Click the unstable slope faces (red) to place a charge before time runs out.
🟩 Safe — leave it   🟧 Caution — leave it   🟥 Unstable — charge it!
How to play: Each slope face shows a stability reading. Readings in the red danger zone are unstable — click or tap them to place a charge and safely release the snow. Leave green (safe) and orange (caution) faces alone — charging them wastes one of your 3 charges and costs a life. Clear every danger zone before the timer hits zero to clear the slope and move to the next, harder one!
