Playing Card Ink Registration
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Layer 1/2 — Round 1
How to play: Drag with your mouse/finger, or steer with the Arrow Keys, to slide the colored ink plate until it sits inside the white registration crosshairs. Press Space or tap the canvas to stamp the layer once it is aligned — stamping while off target costs a life. Finish every layer before the cycle timer empties to move on. Each round adds another color layer and shrinks the tolerance!
