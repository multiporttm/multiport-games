Ring Sizing Resizing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Step 1: press Space / Tap when the moving saw lines up with the marked cut spot on the ring. Step 2: use Left/Right Arrow or drag to stretch or compress the cut shank to match the target size gauge, then press Space / Tap to solder it closed. Harder metals punish overshoot more. Three lives, more demanding rings each round.
