Pilot your submersible with Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag / touch to steer. Descend through the ocean layers collecting glowing treasures and creatures for points while dodging rock formations and anglerfish. Watch your battery — it drains as you go and drains faster if you idle at the surface. Score is based on depth reached and items collected before the battery runs out or you're hit!
