Pitch Focus
A pitch needle sweeps as the tuning file scrapes the reed's tip, raising and lowering its note. A green zone marks the exact pitch for this harmonica's key. Press Space, tap the reed, or click the big button the instant the needle is inside the green zone to stop filing and lock the reed in tune. Stopping too sharp or too flat leaves a sour reed and drains Pitch Focus — let it run empty and the set is scrapped! Tune enough reeds to move to a trickier key with a narrower sweet spot.
