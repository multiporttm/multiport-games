🪏 Shovel Making Handle Fitting
The socket drifts side to side on the conveyor. Drag the handle so it lines up with the socket, then insert before it drifts away.
Drag the handle left/right (mouse, touch, or Left/Right arrow keys) to follow the drifting socket on the conveyor below it. When the handle overlaps the socket's green zone, tap "Insert Handle" (or press Space/Enter) to drive it home. A clean insert scores points and speeds up the next socket; inserting off-target or letting the socket drift too far rattles the whole line and drains Line Stability.
