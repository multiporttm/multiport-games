Hockey Referee Penalty Call
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Each round shows a live infraction on the ice. Read the play, then call the correct penalty — Tripping, Slashing, Too Many Men, or Icing — by clicking a button, tapping, or pressing keys 1-4 before the whistle window runs out. Call it right to keep the game fair and move to the next round; call it wrong or let the clock expire and you lose a life. The window gets shorter every few correct calls — lose all 3 lives and the game ends.
