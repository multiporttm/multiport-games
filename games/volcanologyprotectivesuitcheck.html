Volcanology Protective Suit Check
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lives: 3
Inspect every zone of the heat-resistant proximity suit before a team member approaches active lava. Click/tap or use arrow keys + Enter to flag each damaged zone or unsealed seam you find. When you have flagged every defect, click Approve Suit if it is fully sound, or Reject Suit if any defect remains unresolved. Missing a defect, flagging a sound zone, or giving a wrong verdict costs a life. Checks get more detailed and stricter each round.
