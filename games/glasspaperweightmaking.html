How to play: The target pattern (top-right) shows colored canes/frit pieces in layering order, bottom layer first. Click or tap the highlighted next piece in your tray, then click or tap the matching empty slot in the dome (or use Left/Right Arrow to pick a tray piece and Enter to place it in the next open slot) to build the design in the correct order and position. A wrong slot costs a life. Complete the layered pattern to finish the paperweight — designs get more intricate each round. Lose all 3 lives and the session ends!
