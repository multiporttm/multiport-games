Ice Climbing Ascent
Height: 0m | Best: 0mStamina
Alternate Left Axe (A / ←) and Right Axe (D / →) — or tap the left/right halves of the screen — to swing your ice axes and climb. Time each swing to a solid patch of ice: hitting a crack or getting clipped by falling ice costs stamina. Run out of stamina and you fall. Climb as high as you can!
