Butcher Bone Saw

Lives: ❤️❤️❤️
Hold the mouse/finger down (or hold Space and steer with arrow keys) to drive the bone saw forward along the marked cut line. Keep the blade on the line — drifting off-line too long binds the blade. Reach the end of tougher bones at trickier angles to advance.
