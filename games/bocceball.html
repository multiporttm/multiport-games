Drag down from your ball and release to roll
Drag down from your ball then release to roll it toward the pallino (small white ball) — pull farther for more power, angle your drag for curve. The court has slopes that bend your roll and rocks that block it. Closest ball to the pallino scores a point per opposing ball it beats; try to knock the AI's balls away too. Best of 4 rounds wins!
