Aerial Firefighting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Water: 100% | Lives: 3
How to play: Steer with Arrow Keys/WASD or drag. Fly low over the blue lake to scoop up water, then hold Space (or tap DROP) over a wildfire hotspot to douse it before it spreads. Avoid flying through thick smoke and the hottest fire cores with an empty tank — the heat will damage your plane!
