Trampoline Dodgeball
Lives: 3Score: 0Hi: 0
Hold Space (or your trampoline) to charge a jump, release to bounce!
Hold Space (or press-and-hold your trampoline at the bottom) to charge a bounce, then release to jump — the longer you hold, the higher you go. You must be airborne when an incoming dodgeball reaches you or you lose a life. Tap/click an opponent's trampoline to throw a dodgeball at them — it only lands while they're grounded. Land 3 hits on all 3 opponents before your lives run out to win!
