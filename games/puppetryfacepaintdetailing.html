Puppetry Face Paint Detailing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1 — Feature 1/3
How to play: A glowing bead travels along each facial feature's stroke path — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your brush tip inside the amber tolerance guide around it. Drift too far and your wet-paint meter drains; empty it and the stroke smears, costing a life. Finish the feature while the paint is still wet to move on to the next one — eyebrows, eyes, then lips. Every round adds more features and the guide lines get finer. Lose all 3 lives and the puppet head is ruined!
