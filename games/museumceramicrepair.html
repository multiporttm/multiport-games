How to play: Drag the next highlighted shard (lowest number first — the correct join order) from the tray onto its matching gap in the vessel, or press Tab to auto-select the next shard and Arrow Keys+Enter to place it. Once seated, hold the mouse/finger down (or hold Space) to squeeze out adhesive and release right when the gauge bar is in the green zone — too little leaves it loose, too much drips and weakens the bond, either way costing a life. Lose 3 lives and the vessel can't be saved. Vessels shatter into more shards with tighter adhesive gauges each round.
