Press and drag up or down on the spinning blank (mouse or finger) to slide the lathe chisel along the scrolling target profile. Keep the chisel inside the glowing taper band to shave the wood smoothly and fill your Smooth Finish meter; stray outside it and the wood chatters, filling the Chatter meter. Let Chatter max out and the blank cracks — the band narrows and scrolls faster each round, so stay locked on the curve.
