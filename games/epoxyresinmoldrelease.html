Epoxy Resin Mold Release
Mold: 1 | Hi: 0
Hold Space or press and hold on each glowing edge point to flex the mold. Keep the pressure needle inside the green safe zone until the point releases — release too early (needle too low) and nothing happens, hold too long or press too hard (needle too high) and the piece cracks. Clear every point on trickier mold shapes each round!
