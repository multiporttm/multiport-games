Scrimshaw Ink Rubbing
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Lives: 3 | Hi: 0
Drag the ink dauber with your mouse or finger (or move with Arrow Keys and hold Space to rub) back and forth over the engraved lines to fill them with dark ink. Keep off the bare bone — straying off the line too long causes a smear. Fill every line before the ink dries. Engravings get more detailed each round!
