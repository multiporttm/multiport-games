Lighthouse Keeper Foghorn Operation
Level 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Ships are hidden in the fog, each blasting its horn in its own rhythm. Watch the pulsing ring around each ship and press Space (or tap the button / ship) the instant a ring fills — matching the ship's horn timing calls it off before it reaches the rocks. Miss the timing or let a ship get too close and you lose a life. More ships and faster rhythms every level!
