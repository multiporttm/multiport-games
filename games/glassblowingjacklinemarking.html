Glassblowing Jack Line Marking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Piece 1 ♥♥♥
The pipe is spinning the piece on the bench. Hold Space / mouse / touch to press the jack blades inward and mark the line — release to lift off. Keep your pressure meter inside the green target band the whole time the line marker sweeps across the target zone shown on the piece, while the rotation keeps it turning. Too light and the line will not hold; too hard and the glass pinches off or cracks. The target band narrows every level!
