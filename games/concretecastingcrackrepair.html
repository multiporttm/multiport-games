Concrete Casting Crack Repair
Slab: 1 Filled: 0% Score: 0 Best: 0
Press and drag the trowel (mouse, finger, or arrow keys to nudge it, Space to apply filler at its position) along the jagged crack lines on the slab. Stay close to the crack to pack it with patch compound — straying too far wastes compound and time. Fill every crack above the target percentage before the skin-over timer runs out to seal the slab. Later slabs add more and longer cracks.
