Select a pipe, then select another to swap them, until the whole rank runs tallest to shortest.
How to play: A fresh rank of pipes sits on the windchest in scrambled order. Move the selector across the row with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by tapping a pipe directly, and press Select (Enter, Space, or the on-screen button) to pick up the pipe under the selector. Move to a second pipe and press Select again to swap the two pipes' positions. Arrange the whole rank from tallest at one end to shortest at the other before the round timer runs out. A pipe locks in and glows once it and its neighbor are in correct relative order; swapping two pipes that are already correctly placed costs you time, so plan your swaps. Sort the entire rank correctly to post a high score.
