How to play: A glowing bead sweeps along the target arch profile of the violin's top plate — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your gouge riding right on the gold bead. Carve too deep below the line or leave a high spot above it and your finish meter drains; empty it and the plate is ruined, costing a life. Stay close the whole pass to finish the board and move to the next one. Every board brings a more intricate arch and a tighter tolerance — lose all 3 lives and the carving session is over!
