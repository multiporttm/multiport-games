Miniature Paint Washing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Miniature 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead creeps along the miniature's recessed detail line — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your brush tip inside the dark tolerance channel around it. Linger too long in one spot and the wash overflows onto the raised surface, draining your flow meter; empty it and the wash is ruined, costing a life. Drift away and you miss shading the recess. Follow the whole recess line to finish the miniature and move on. The wash dries faster and the channel narrows every miniature — lose all 3 lives and the model is ruined!
