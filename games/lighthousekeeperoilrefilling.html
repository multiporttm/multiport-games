Lighthouse Keeper Oil Refilling
Level 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Select a lamp reservoir (tap it, or press Tab to cycle), then hold Space (or press and hold the Pour button / that reservoir) to pour oil from your can. Release while the fill level sits inside the glowing target band — too little or too much wastes the can. Every reservoir drains slowly, so fill them all before any one runs dry. More reservoirs and narrower target bands each level!
