Jigsaw Puzzle Quality Inspection
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1 (25 to go)
How to play: Puzzle pieces roll past on the conveyor belt. Click or tap a piece with a crack, smudge, or misaligned print to pull it off before it reaches the box — or use Arrow Keys to move the selector and Space to pull. Pulling a flawless piece, or letting a defective one reach the box, costs a life. Process 25 pieces to clear the shift; the belt speeds up and defects get harder to spot as you go. Lose all 3 lives and quality control shuts the line down!
