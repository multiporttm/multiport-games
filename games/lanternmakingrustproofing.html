🏮 Lantern Making Rustproofing
Hold the trigger as the nozzle sweeps across the lantern body to coat every panel. Keep every panel in the green coverage band before time runs out.
A spray nozzle sweeps left and right across five panels of the lantern body. Press and hold (click, tap, or Space) while the nozzle crosses a panel to add rustproof coating to it — release to stop spraying. Each panel needs to land inside its green band: too little leaves bare rust-prone metal, too much causes drips, and both drain your Finish Quality meter. Finish each round with every panel in the band before the timer runs out to advance; run out of time or Finish Quality and the job fails.
