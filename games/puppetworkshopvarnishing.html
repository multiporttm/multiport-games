Puppet Workshop Varnishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Coat 1/3 — Puppet 1/6
How to play: Drag your brush (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys + hold Space to paint) back and forth across the puppet to coat every cell with varnish. Shiny drip blobs form on finished cells — click, tap, or press Enter while near one to wipe it before it runs; let 3 drips run on a single puppet and you lose a life. Finish the coat, then wait out the dry timer before the next coat begins — brushing wet varnish smears it. Puppets get bigger and drip faster with every round — complete all coats on every puppet to win!
