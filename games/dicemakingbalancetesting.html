Dice Making Balance Testing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Die 1
A finished die is floating in a tall salt-water test tube, and tiny differences in how deep each pip was drilled make it keep tipping and drifting off-level instead of hanging perfectly balanced. Use the Left/Right arrow keys, drag, or tap ◀ Nudge / Nudge ▶ to tap the tube and counter the drift, keeping the die's tilt needle inside the glowing centered band to fill its balance meter. Let the needle drift too far off-band and the meter drains and you lose a life — three failed dice and the batch is scrapped! Hold a die level long enough to pass the test and move on to a trickier die with sharper, faster drift.
