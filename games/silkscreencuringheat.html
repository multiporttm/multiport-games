Sweep the gun across the whole print to cure it evenly
Move the heat gun with your mouse, finger, or the Left/Right arrow keys back and forth across the fabric. Every segment needs time inside the green safe temperature band to fully cure (shown by its progress bar filling). Lingering too long over one spot pushes it into the red scorch zone and ruins the print — keep the gun moving and cure the whole fabric before the timer runs out, across larger prints each round!
