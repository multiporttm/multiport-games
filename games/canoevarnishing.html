Canoe Varnishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press and drag the brush (mouse or finger) back and forth across the hull. Each patch has a thickness gauge — sweep over it enough to bring the fill into the glowing target band. Linger too long and varnish pools (patch ruined); never touch a patch and it stays bare. Finish every patch inside its band before the coat skins over (timer) to clear the round. Hulls grow larger (more patches, less time) each round.
