Exotic Animal X-Ray Diagnostics
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lives: 3
How to play: Compare the patient x-ray (left) to the normal reference (right) for that species. Click or tap the area of the x-ray with the abnormality — a fracture, foreign object, or growth — then choose the correct next step from the menu. Use Arrow Keys + Enter if you prefer keyboard. Wrong spots or wrong next steps cost a life. Diagnose correctly to advance through tougher rounds!
