How to play: Copy the botanical arrangement shown on the left Reference page onto Your Page. Click or tap a leaf in the tray to pick it up, then click or tap an empty slot to place it (works the same with mouse, touch, or keyboard Tab + Enter). Fill every slot to match the reference exactly, then click Close Press and wait for the press timer. A wrong arrangement lowers your score — more leaves and bigger layouts appear each round!
