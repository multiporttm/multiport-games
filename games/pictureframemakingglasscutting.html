🔷 Glass Cutting
Two stages per pane: first tap when the cutting wheel crosses the target line, then press and hold to build snap pressure and release right in the green zone to break a clean edge.
Stage 1: watch the cutting wheel sweep back and forth and tap, click, or press Space exactly when it crosses the glowing target line to score the glass. Stage 2: press and hold to build up snap pressure on the scored line, then release right as the needle passes through the green zone to break off a clean edge. Succeed at both stages to finish the pane; a bad score or a bad snap cracks the glass and costs you a pane. Lose all three panes and the order is scrapped!
