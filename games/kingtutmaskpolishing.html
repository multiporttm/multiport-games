King Tut Mask Polishing
Shine: 0%
Gem risk: 0%
Best: 0
How to play: Click/tap and drag in small circular motions over the tarnished (dull, dark) gold areas of the mask to buff them to a shine. Restore the whole mask's shine to 100% to win. Be careful over the striped beard and blue-and-gold eyes — those delicate gem inlays crack if you scrub them too hard, and cracking three of them ends the restoration. Move gently and deliberately, not fast strokes, over the gem sections!
