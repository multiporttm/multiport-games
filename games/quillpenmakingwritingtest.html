Quill Pen Making Writing Test
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Line 1
The finished quill gets a test run on a scrap of parchment, scrawling a flowing practice line to prove the nib writes smooth and true. Use the Up/Down arrow keys, drag or move your finger/mouse up and down over the parchment, or tap ▲ Lift / ▼ Press to steer the nib dot so it rides the glowing ink stroke as it scrolls past. Wander too far off the stroke for too long and the ink blots and smears — three bad blots and the quill fails its test! Ride a whole set of lines cleanly to prove the quill worthy and move on to a faster, wavier line.
