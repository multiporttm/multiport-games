Cloisonné Powder Application
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press and hold (or hold Space after clicking a cell) over a wire-framed cell to sprinkle enamel powder. Release once the gauge needle sits in the glowing green safe band. Release too early and it is underfilled; hold too long and it overflows into neighboring cells, ending your round. Finish every cell to advance — more cells appear each round.
