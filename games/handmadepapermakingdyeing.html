Handmade Papermaking Dyeing
Batch: 1 Match: 0% Score: 0 Best: 0
Build the vat color with the drop buttons (1/2/3/4 keys work too), then hold Dip (Space or click the sheet) to soak your paper.
Study the target swatch, then add drops of red, yellow, blue dye and water to your vat to mix that exact color — each drop shifts the vat's hue and strength. Once the vat color is close to the target, hold the Dip button (or Space, or touch-and-hold the sheet) to lower your blank sheet into the dye. Release before the sheet over-saturates into a muddy blot, but dip long enough to reach full color, or the sheet will come out pale and unsellable. Match closely and time your dip well to pass each batch — three ruined sheets and the batch is lost!
