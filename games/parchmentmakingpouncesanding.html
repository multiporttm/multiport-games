Drag, swipe, or use the arrows to move the pounce bag over each rough patch.
How to play: A sheet of scraped hide is divided into a grid of rough patches. Drag with your mouse or finger (or steer with the arrow keys / arrow buttons) to move the pounce bag onto a patch and hold it there to sand it smooth. Each patch has a target band shown by its color: too rough and it still needs work, sanded too far and you will rub straight through the hide, losing focus. Bring every patch into its smooth band at the same time, before focus runs out, to finish the sheet and move on to a bigger, trickier one.
