Weaving Warp Threading
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Draft 1
How to play: A threading draft shows the order the warp ends must pass through the heddles. Press the matching number key (1-5) or tap/click the matching colored heddle button for the glowing current step, before its timer runs out. Each correct thread advances the draft; a wrong heddle or a missed timer costs a life. Finish the draft to move to a longer, trickier one with more heddles.
