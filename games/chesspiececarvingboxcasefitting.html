Drag each piece into the felt cutout that matches its shape and size.
How to play: Pieces wait in the tray along the bottom of the case. Drag a piece (mouse or touch) and drop it onto the felt-lined cutout that matches its shape and size — a good match snaps it into place and locks it in with a satisfying click. Dropping it on the wrong cutout bounces it back to the tray and burns a little time. On a keyboard, use TAB (or the CYCLE PIECE button) to select the next unplaced piece, arrow keys to nudge it over the case, and Enter to try dropping it on the slot under it. Pack every piece before the clock runs out to finish the case.
