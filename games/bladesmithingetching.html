Bladesmithing Etching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Blade 1/8
How to play: Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) to pour acid etchant onto the blade. A needle sweeps along the duration gauge while held — release inside the glowing target time band to reveal a clean hamon pattern. Release too early and nothing forms; hold past the band and the steel pits. Hit the band to score and move to a trickier, narrower target. Miss three times and the blade is ruined!
