Round 1Ships: 0 | Best: 0
Drag a part into the bottle neck, then to its target spot.
Drag each part from the tray below, thread it carefully through the bottle's narrow neck, and drop it onto its glowing outline inside the bottle. Move slowly — if the part touches the bottle's glass walls it breaks and that piece is lost! Finish every part to complete the ship. Each round adds more parts and a narrower neck. Keyboard: Tab to a part and press Enter to pick it up, use the Arrow keys to guide it, Enter to drop it in place, and Escape to cancel.
