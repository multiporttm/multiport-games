Harpsichord Building Jack Slot Fitting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Slot 1
A file gauge sweeps back and forth across the width of the jack as the fitting scale reads out below. A gold band marks the exact clearance where the jack will slide freely through the register slot without binding or rattling. Press Space, tap, or click 🔧 Fit Jack the instant the gauge crosses into the gold band to pare the jack to a perfect fit. Fitting it outside the band leaves the jack too tight or too sloppy and chews up a slot — three ruined slots and the register board has to be rebuilt! Fit every jack in a register to move on to a register with tighter tolerances.
