Grab the golden dot and drag (mouse, finger, or Enter + Arrow keys) all the way along the dashed fold line to the finish flag, staying inside the lighter crimping band. Each mark you cross is a crimp — the closer you stay to the line, the better the crimp. Complete every crimp to seal the empanada, then take on more crimps and a tighter band next round!
