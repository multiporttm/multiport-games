Vegetable Carving Tomato Rose
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Rose 1/8
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger up and down (or use the Arrow Keys) to steer your peeling speed on the gauge at the right — keep the needle inside the glowing green safe zone to peel the spiral strip of tomato skin without it tearing. Drift too far above or below the zone and the tension meter drains; empty it and the strip snaps, costing a life. Once the strip reaches its target length it automatically coils into a rose and you move to the next, trickier target size — lose all 3 lives and the rose falls apart!
