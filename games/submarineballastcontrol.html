Submarine Ballast Control
Time: 0sDepth: 0mBest: —
Hold Up/W (or BLOW) to push water out of the ballast tanks and rise, or Down/S (or PUMP) to flood them and sink. Currents and shifting density layers constantly push your depth — keep the sub inside the green safe band as long as you can. Drifting outside it strains the hull; run out of hull integrity and the dive ends.
