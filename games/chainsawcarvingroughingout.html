Chainsaw Carving Roughing Out
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Log 1
A raw log sits on the bucking stand with a bright waste zone marked on it. The chainsaw bar sweeps back and forth across the log. Press Space, tap, or click 🪵 Rip Cut the instant the bar is inside the waste zone to blast a chunk free. Cut outside the zone and you gouge the good wood. Cutting too fast overheats the saw until it bogs down and stalls — three stalls and the blank is ruined! Clear every chunk to move to the next log.
