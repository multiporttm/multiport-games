Gold Leaf Gilding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag the gold leaf onto the glowing zone
How to play: Each section of the book cover has a glowing embossed design. Click and drag the fragile gold leaf sheet slowly and gently onto that zone — moving too fast tears it. Once it's placed, click Burnish and press/hold at a steady rate to seal it down perfectly. Rush it and the leaf wrinkles or the burnish scorches it. Complete every section before you run out of leaf sheets.
