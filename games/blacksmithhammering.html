Blacksmith Hammering
Heat: 100%Combo: 0Score: 0Hi: 0
Strike the beats as they reach the target zone!
Beat markers slide across the anvil toward the glowing target zone. Press Space, click, or tap the moment a marker is inside the zone to land a perfect hammer strike. A well-timed strike keeps the horseshoe hot and builds your combo; a mistimed or missed strike cools the metal. If the heat meter empties, the horseshoe cools solid and is ruined. Finish the whole beat pattern while still hot to forge the horseshoe!
