Topiary Spiral Carving

Move your mouse, finger, or arrow keys to keep your shears on the bright marker as it spirals outward.
A glowing marker travels along a spiral groove carved into the hedge, winding outward and speeding up. Move your shears (mouse, finger drag, or arrow keys) to stay inside the narrowing tolerance ring around the marker. Straying too far fills your Strain meter — if it reaches 100% the cut is ruined. Finish the full spiral on each of six hedges for the best score.
