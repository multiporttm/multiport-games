Move the stake peg with Arrow Keys or by dragging/tapping, and press Space or tap the glowing stake point to drive it in — stake the numbered guy lines in order (1 → 2 → 3 → 4) so tension stays even. Wind gusts push the tent; staking out of order or too slowly lets it collapse and costs a life. Storm arrives faster each level!
