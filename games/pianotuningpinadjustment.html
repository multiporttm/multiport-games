Piano Tuning Pin Adjustment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag the tuning hammer left/right — or use the Left/Right Arrow keys — to turn the pin and bring the string's pitch to match the target note shown by the needle. Press Space (or tap Lock In) once every string in the unison reads in tune. You have 3 lives; locking in out of tune costs one. Targets get trickier and unisons grow to 3 strings as you progress.
