Yurt Making Door Frame Carving

Chisel in hand, strike only when the gauge glows green to carve each notch of the door frame true.

How to play: A marker sweeps across the carving gauge. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter the instant it crosses the green zone to land a clean chisel pass. A clumsy strike dulls your carving focus meter — empty it and the frame splits, ending the round. Land five clean passes to advance a round; the gauge sweeps faster and the sweet zone narrows each round. Carve as many passes as you can for the highest score!
