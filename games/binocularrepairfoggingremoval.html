Binocular Repair Fogging Removal
Housings: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Hold over the fogged haze to clear it — ease off before the heat meter maxes out.
How to play: A prism housing is clouded with condensation haze. Hold the mouse button down, keep a finger on the glass, or hold Space while steering with the arrow keys to sweep the heat gun over the haze and drive off the fog cell by cell, filling your clearing progress. The heat gun's meter at the top climbs while you hold it on and cools only while you ease off; let it reach the top and the glass cracks outright, ending the round. Ease off too much, though, and stubborn haze patches silently condense back across cells you already cleared. Clear the whole housing of fog before the heat meter maxes out to finish the repair and move on to a damper housing.
