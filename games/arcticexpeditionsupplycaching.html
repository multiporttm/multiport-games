Arctic Expedition Supply Cache
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Route 1/6
How to play: A planned route stretches across the ice — click, tap, or move the cursor with the Arrow Keys and press Space to bury a supply cache. Your team can only travel so far between caches before running out of rations, so space every leg (start→cache, cache→cache, last cache→finish) within the safe range shown. Caches cannot be buried inside the striped crevasse fields. When your plan is ready, press Enter or click Confirm Route. A bad gap costs a life; efficient, well-used spacing scores higher. Reach the finish on six increasingly long routes before your 3 lives run out!
