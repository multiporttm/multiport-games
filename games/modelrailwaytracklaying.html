Model Railway Track Laying
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Layout 1 Pieces left: 0
How to play: Click a track piece in the tray at the bottom, then click an open slot on the blueprint grid to place it — click Rotate (or press R) first to turn it so its rail ends line up with its neighbors. Straights, curves and switches all connect only when their open ends face each other with no gaps. Fill every slot on the blueprint correctly to complete the layout and move to a bigger, trickier one. Placing a piece with a mismatched connection costs a life — lose all 3 and you have to start the layout over!
