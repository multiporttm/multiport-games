Fountain Pen Making Converter Installing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Pen 1/10
Drag to slide • Arrow keys or A/D to rotate • Space to lock in
How to play: A piston converter sits beside an open pen barrel with a gold marker showing exactly where its threads must seat. Drag the converter (or use the Arrow Keys) to slide it toward the marker, and use A / D (or Left/Right) to rotate its threads into alignment. Press Space or tap the barrel to try seating it — if the position, angle and rotation are all within tolerance, it clicks home perfectly. Misjudge it and your fit meter takes a hit. Shapes get trickier and tolerances tighten with every pen — lose all 3 lives and the batch is ruined!
