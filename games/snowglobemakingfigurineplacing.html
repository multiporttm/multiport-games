🧍 Snow Globe Making Figurine Placing
Drag the little figurine through the drifting snow-dust and set it down inside the glowing base ring before time runs out.
Press and drag (mouse or finger) the figurine from where it appears over to the glowing ring on the base, then let go. A gentle snow-dust drift keeps nudging it as you carry it, so steer against the drift. Landing inside the ring scores points and shrinks the ring for next time; dropping outside it, or running out of time, costs Workshop Patience — lose it all and the shift ends.
