Aquarium Feeding Schedule
Score: 0 Round: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Each tank shows a schedule card with the species, its food type, and its portion size. Pick the matching food icon from the tray, then pick the matching portion size, and deliver it to the tank — click/tap each choice, or press 1-3 to pick food, 1-3 again to pick portion once food is selected, and Enter to deliver (use Tab/arrows to move between tanks). Wrong food or portion costs a life. Feed every tank in the gallery before the timer hits zero to open on time — rounds add more tanks and less time. Three lives.
