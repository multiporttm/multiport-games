Balloon Twisting Flower Making
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Flower 1/8
How to play: The diagram shows petals numbered around the center, plus a final stem. Click/tap the glowing petal (or press its number key) to grab it, then hold (mouse/finger down or Space) to build twist tension and release while the needle sits in the green safe zone to lock it. Release too early and the petal unravels; hold too long and it pops! Twist every petal in order, then lock the stem to complete the flower and move on to a round with more petals.
