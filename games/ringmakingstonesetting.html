💎 Ring Making Stone Setting
Round: 1 Prongs Set: 0 Score: 0 Best: 0
Watch the setting-pusher needle sweep around the dial and press Space, click, or tap right as it crosses the glowing green zone to squeeze a prong down onto the gemstone with precise pressure. A clean squeeze seats the prong firmly and raises your score; a bad squeeze chips the stone and damages your stone stability meter. Set enough prongs to clear the round — rounds get faster and the sweet zone gets tighter as you go. If your stability meter runs out, the stone pops loose and the game ends.
