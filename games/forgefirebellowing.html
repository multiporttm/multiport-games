Forge Fire Bellowing
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Hold Space, click, or touch the canvas to pump the bellows and heat the fire; release to let it cool. Keep the fire inside the glowing working band to fill the progress bar and clear the round. Let it drop to a dead coal or spike into a white burn-through and you lose a life. The band narrows and the fire responds faster every level. Three lives.
