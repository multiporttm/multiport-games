Teapot Seasoning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press Space, Up Arrow, or tap/hold to pour hot tea into the new clay pot, then release right as the needle sweeps into the glowing steep window to pull it off the heat. Brew enough good pots at the correct steep time to season the clay before you run out of leaves — pour too short or too long too many times and the pot cracks. You have 3 pots (lives)!
