Base Camp Oxygen Rationing
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Each climber needs a minimum number of oxygen bottles for summit day and descent. Press Up/Down (or tap the ▲/▼ buttons) to adjust bottles for the selected climber, Tab or Left/Right (or tap a climber card) to switch climbers, and Enter/Space (or tap "Depart") to confirm once every climber has enough and you haven't exceeded the bottle supply. Leftover bottles earn a bonus. Three lives — more climbers and a tighter bottle budget arrive every level.
