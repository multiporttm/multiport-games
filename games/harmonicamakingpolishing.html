Buffing Focus
A buffing wheel sweeps across the cover plate, carrying a smear of polish back and forth over the metal. A gold zone marks the exact pass that leaves a bright mirror shine. Press Space, tap the plate, or click the big button the instant the wheel is inside the gold zone to lift the polish and reveal the gleam. Buffing too early or too late leaves swirl marks and drains Buffing Focus — let it run empty and the plate is scrapped! Shine enough plates to move to a harder batch with a narrower gleam zone.
