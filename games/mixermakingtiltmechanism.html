📐 Mixer Making Tilt Mechanism
Swipe left and right (or use arrow keys) to tilt the mixer head toward the target angle marked on the protractor, then hold it steady while the hinge wobbles.
Drag left/right across the frame (or press Left/Right arrows) to tilt the mixer head. Bring the pointer into the target zone and hold it there while a random wobble keeps nudging it away — fill the hold meter to lock the hinge. Letting the angle drift too far too often drains Hinge Stability; empty it and the mechanism jams.
