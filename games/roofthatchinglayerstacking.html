Slide the yealm and lay it to overlap the course below by the right amount.
How to play: A new bundle of straw hangs above the rafters, ready to be laid as the next course. Slide it left and right with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging on the canvas, then press Lay Course (Space, the button, or a tap) to drop it onto the roof. Each course should overlap the one below inside the highlighted target zone — land squarely in the zone for a perfect, watertight course, drift too far either way and the course is loose, weakening your stability meter. Keep laying courses up toward the ridge before your stack becomes unstable and collapses.
