Vending Machine Restock
Round 1 Score: 0 Hi: 0 Time: 30
Select a tray item, then click the matching manifest slot.
How to play: Each slot in the vending machine needs a specific snack from the supply manifest (shown faded in the slot). Click a snack in the tray below to select it (or press its letter key), then click the slot you think it belongs in (or press its number key). Correct matches score points; wrong ones cost points and the item stays in the tray. Fill every slot correctly before the timer runs out to advance to a harder round!
