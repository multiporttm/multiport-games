Blizzard Navigation
Bearing: N 0°Find the shelterHi: 0
How to play: Use Arrow Keys / WASD (or drag/tap on the canvas) to walk through the whiteout. Visibility is near-zero except during brief flashes — use those moments plus the compass bearing readout to stay on the rope-line toward the shelter. Stepping into a red danger zone (cliff edge or open water) ends the hike instantly. Reach the shelter before the hypothermia meter fills up!
