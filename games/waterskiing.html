Water Skiing
Score: 0 | Best: 0
Use Arrow Keys / A-D or drag to steer the skier left and right, weaving through the slalom buoys. When you cross a boat wake, press Space (or tap the jump button) right at the ramp to launch a trick for bonus points. Missing buoys or riding rough water knocks your balance down — if it hits zero for too long, you wipe out. Clear buoys and land tricks to rack up your score before you fall!
