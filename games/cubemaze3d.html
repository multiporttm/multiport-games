🧊 Cube Maze 3D
Roll the cube across the grid to reach the glowing goal tile.
Roll the real 3D cube one tile at a time with Arrow Keys, WASD, the on-screen D-pad, or a swipe. Each move tumbles the cube face-over-face across the grid. Reach the glowing goal tile — but roll off the edge or onto a dark hole tile and it's game over. Five levels, each bigger and trickier than the last.
