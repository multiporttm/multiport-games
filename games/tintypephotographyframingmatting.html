The mat cutter's blade glides back and forth along each edge of the mat board. A gold mark shows exactly where the guide line sits for that side. Press Space, tap, or click 🖼️ Cut the instant the blade crosses the gold mark for a clean, square cut. Cut too soon or too late and the mat corner tears — ruin three cuts and the mat is wasted! Finish all sides of a frame to move on to a smaller, trickier mat.
