Candy Making Wrapping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1/8
How to play: Tap the left and right sides of the twisting candy in alternation (or press Left/Right Arrows) to sweep the wrapping needle back and forth. Keep each sweep inside the glowing safe zone on the gauge and land a steady rhythm — too fast and wild tears the cellophane, too slow leaves it unwrapped. Wrap a full batch of candies to clear the round. Target speed climbs every round, so stay sharp!
