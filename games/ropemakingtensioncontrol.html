Rope Making Tension Control
Lengths Laid: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0 Ruined: 0/3
Hold each brake to keep its strand's tension in the green band while the hook twists the rope.
How to play: Three fiber strands run from bobbins down the ropewalk to a spinning twisting hook, and each strand has its own tension gauge on the dial above the canvas. A strand that runs slack loses its twist and a strand pulled too tight will snap, so you must hold its brake lever to slow it whenever the gauge drifts toward the tight (red-high) side, and release the brake whenever it drifts toward the slack (red-low) side, keeping the needle in the green middle band. Hold brake A for the left strand, S for the middle strand and D for the right strand on the keyboard, or press and hold the on-screen Brake A / Brake S / Brake D buttons with mouse or touch. The length-of-rope bar at the top fills as the hook keeps turning; if any gauge sits in the red for too long that length of rope is ruined, costing you a strike, but if you keep all three gauges steady until the bar fills, that length is laid up cleanly and banked to your score. Lay up as many clean lengths as you can — three ruined lengths ends your day at the ropewalk — and each new length twitches the strands a little faster than the last.
