Special Effects Makeup Wound Simulation
Level 1 Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤
How to play: The reference strip at the top shows the exact layer order needed to build today's fake wound. Click the matching layer button (or press its number key 1–6) — correct picks paint onto the wound and advance the sequence; a wrong pick costs a life and resets the layer order. Finish before the timer runs out. Later levels add more layers and less time.
