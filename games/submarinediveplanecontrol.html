Submarine Dive Plane Control
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Maneuver: 1 Lives: ●●● Time: 45
Use Left/Right Arrow or A/D, or drag the wheel / swipe left-right, to angle the dive planes and steer the pitch needle. Follow the target zone as it drifts through each maneuver — drift out of the safe range too long and you lose a life. Later maneuvers move faster and swing wider.
