Brush base coat over the whole figure evenly before the paint supply runs dry.
How to play: A stylized mannequin outline stands ready for its festival body paint, and the whole figure needs an even base coat before anything else can go on top. Drag your brush across the figure with the mouse or your finger, or move it with the arrow keys or WASD, sweeping color over every part of its body-shaped outline. Each zone needs a few careful passes to reach a good even coat shown in bright color; rush past too quickly and a zone stays pale and patchy, but linger on one zone too long and it turns gloppy and overworked, which costs you evenness. Watch the paint-supply bar at the top drain as you brush — once it runs out, the base coat is graded on how even and complete your coverage was across the whole figure. Coat mannequin after mannequin, each with a shorter supply, to build the highest score you can before the paint runs dry.
