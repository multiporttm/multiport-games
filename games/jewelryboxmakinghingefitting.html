Tiny brass hinges need to be fitted into the jewelry box lid. Watch the glowing marker sweep across the gauge and tap, click, or press Space the instant it lands in the bright green zone for a clean fit. A clean hit raises your fit-quality meter, a miss damages it. Fit five hinges to move to the next round - the sweep gets faster and the sweet zone narrows as you go.
