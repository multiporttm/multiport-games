Chocolate Tempering Process
Batch: 1 | Lives: 3 | Hi: 0
Press Up/Down Arrow or drag up/down to heat or cool the chocolate. Guide it through each stage's target temperature range (Melt → Seed → Work), hold steady as the bar fills, then test the sample. Hit Space / Tap to pour when the final gauge is locked in range. Miss the range too long and you lose a life!
