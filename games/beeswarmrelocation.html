Plan each relocation in three steps — press 1/2/3 or tap a button to pick the best travel timing, ventilation, and route for a calm colony. Then perform the transfer: press Space/tap each time the box flashes "TRANSFER NOW" to pour the swarm smoothly into the new hive. Wrong picks or a jostled swarm cost a life — rounds get trickier as you go.
