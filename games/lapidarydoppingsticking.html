The wax marker rises and falls over the alcohol lamp as it softens. Press Space, tap the tube, or click 🖊️ Press Stone the instant the marker sits inside the glowing tacky band to seat the gem perfectly on the dop stick. Press too early and the wax is still hard; too late and it is runny — either way the stone slides off. Dop every stick in the set — three slips and the batch is lost!
