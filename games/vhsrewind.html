The tape counter races backward as the VHS rewinds. Press STOP (click, tap, or the Space bar) the instant the white needle crosses into the green target zone marked on the gauge. Stopping too early or too late (overshooting) costs points — the closer to the center of the zone, the higher your score. Land enough good stops and the target zone gets narrower each round.
