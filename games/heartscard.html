How to play: Each hand you're dealt 13 cards against 3 AI opponents. First, click 3 cards to select them and Pass 3 Cards in the direction shown. Whoever holds the 2 of clubs leads the first trick. Click a card in your hand to play it — you must follow the led suit if you can; hearts can't be led until they've been "broken", and hearts/the queen of spades can't be played on the first trick unless that's all you have. Whoever plays the highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads next. Hearts are worth 1 point each and the queen of spades is worth 13 — you want the lowest score! Take every point card in one hand to "shoot the moon" — everyone else gets 26 points instead. First to 100 points ends the match; lowest total wins!
