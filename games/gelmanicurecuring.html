Gel Manicure Curing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Nail 1/10
How to play: Each of the client's 10 nails needs a base coat, a color coat, and a glossy top coat. For every coat, a pointer sweeps back and forth across the UV lamp meter — press Space, tap, or click Cure Now the instant it sits inside the glowing green zone. Stop it outside the zone and you scorch the coat, costing a life. The sweep speeds up and the zone narrows as you work through the set — lose all 3 lives and the appointment is ruined!
