🎛️ Calibrate The Knobs
Drag each knob around its center to spin the indicator onto the dashed ghost target. Match every knob before time runs out.
Press and drag in a circle around each knob (mouse or finger) to rotate its indicator. Every knob has a dashed ghost target angle — line your indicator up with it and the knob glows green and locks. Line up every knob on the panel before the Time meter empties to clear the round. Clear rounds add more knobs, tighten the matching tolerance and shorten your time, so three missed rounds in a row ends calibration.
