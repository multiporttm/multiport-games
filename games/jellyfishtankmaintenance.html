Jellyfish Tank Maintenance
Score: 0 Round: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Flow: 50
Adjust the water flow rate with the Up/Down Arrow keys, W/S, or by dragging the slider (touch works too) so every jellyfish in the tank drifts gently in its own happy zone — too little flow and they sink and pin against the bottom/walls, too much and they get slammed into the overflow screen at the top. Keep all species happy at once to fill the round meter. Three lives — each round adds a species and narrows the safe flow overlap.
