ColdMolten
Hold Space, the up arrow, or the 🔥 Pump Bellows button to heat the crucible. Release to let it cool. Get the temperature needle into the green pour window and press Enter or Pour Ingot to cast — but overheat past the pink line and the silver scorches. Three ruined pours and the forge goes cold for good!
