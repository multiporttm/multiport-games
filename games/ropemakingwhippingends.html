Rope Making Whipping Ends
Ends Whipped: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0 Combo: 0
Tap when the needle crosses the green zone to lay down a tight turn of twine.
How to play: A fraying rope end sits waiting to be whipped tight with waxed twine, and a needle sweeps back and forth across a dial above it, marking the rhythm of a good, even turn. Press the space bar, click the Wrap Turn button, or tap the screen each time the needle passes through the narrow green zone in the middle of its sweep, and a clean, tight turn of twine lays down around the rope. Tap outside the green zone and that turn comes out loose or bunched, which weakens the whipped band and breaks your combo of clean turns. Once the band is built up to full, one last well-timed tap locks off the final tuck-under and finishes the end; a bad final tap risks the whole whipping unraveling. Each new rope end sweeps its needle faster and shrinks the green zone a little more, so see how long a streak of clean whippings you can chain together before one finally frays apart.
