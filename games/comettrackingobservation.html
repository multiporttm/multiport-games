Comet Tracking Observation
Level 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
How to play: The comet drifts across the eyepiece as Earth rotates. Use Arrow Keys / WASD or drag/tap to nudge the telescope and keep the comet inside the crosshair circle. Every few seconds an observation is logged automatically — the closer to center, the more points. Drifting off-screen costs a life. 3 lives, speed increases each level.
