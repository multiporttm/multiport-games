Press and hold the grind button (or Spacebar) to hold the blade against the spinning wheel. Holding builds both progress and heat — release before the heat gauge enters the red danger zone or you'll overheat and ruin the blade! Grind the left blade, then the right blade, for each pair of scissors in the batch. Later batches heat up faster and demand tighter control.
