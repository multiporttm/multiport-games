Round 1 — First to 5 wins the battle
How to play: Each round, five loading steps appear in shuffled positions. Click/tap them (or press number keys 1-5) in the correct order — Sponge, Powder, Cannonball, Ram, Ignite — before the enemy crew's bar fills and they fire first. Clicking out of order resets your progress for that round. First crew to win 5 rounds wins the battle!
