Salt Flat Race
Run: 1Speed: 0 mphTop: 0Best: 0
How to play: Hold Space, Up Arrow, or press-and-hold the throttle button to accelerate your wagon across the salt flat. Faster speeds heat the engine up quickly — release to let it cool. Push the engine too far into the red and it overheats, ending your run early. Reach the far marker or overheat to end the run, then try again to beat your top speed record over several runs!
