Select an ink color, then tap the bath to drop it. Drag to comb the colors into swirls.
Pick an ink color from the palette (or press 1-5), then tap/click the size bath to drop ink, or click-drag across it to comb the colors into swirls — keyboard players can move the crosshair with Arrow Keys, drop ink with Space, and hold C while moving to comb. Match the small target pattern as closely as you can, then press Transfer (or T) to stamp the endpaper. You have limited ink drops and comb strokes each round, and three ruined sheets (lives) before the session ends — patterns get more intricate as you advance.
