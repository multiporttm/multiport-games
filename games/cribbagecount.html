Your Hand
How to play: Each hand you and the AI are dealt 6 cards. Click two cards to select them, then Send 2 to Crib — the crib goes to whoever is dealer that hand. A starter card is cut. During the play, take turns clicking a card from your hand to lay down, trying to score points: reaching exactly 15 (2 pts), making a pair (2 pts), or completing a run of 3+ consecutive ranks (1 pt per card) — without the running total passing 31. If you can't play without going over, click Go. After the play, hands (and the crib) are scored for 15s, pairs, runs, and flushes. First to 121 points wins the match!
