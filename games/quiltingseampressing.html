Quilting Seam Pressing
Score: 0 Round: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Use Arrow Keys or drag to slide the iron over the glowing seam, then hold Space or press and hold to heat it up. Release once the heat gauge sits inside the green safe zone to press it perfectly. Release too early and the seam stays unpressed; hold too long and you'll scorch the fabric. Press every seam before time runs out, then move on to a round with more seams and a tighter zone.
