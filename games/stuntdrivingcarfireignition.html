Stunt Driving Car Fire Ignition
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Take 1/10
How to play: A stunt car rig speeds toward the burn zone while a timing needle sweeps across the meter. Press Space, click, or tap to ignite the car-fire rig the instant the needle is inside the glowing green window. Ignite too early and you hurt the driver; too late and you miss the shot — either costs a life. The window narrows and the rig speeds up with every take. Survive all 3 lives through 10 takes to wrap the sequence!
