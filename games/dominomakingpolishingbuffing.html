The final step before a domino tile is ready is a hand-buffed shine. Drag the cloth back and forth across the tile's surface — with your mouse, finger, or the Left/Right keys — to spread an even coat of polish across every section, filling the shine meter to complete the tile. But friction builds heat wherever the cloth lingers: watch the heat bar beneath your cloth, and keep moving across the whole tile rather than scrubbing one spot, or you will scorch the finish and ruin the tile, losing a cloth. Finish tiles efficiently to save cloths for bonus rounds, and chase the highest score by buffing fast, even, and cool across as many tiles as your cloths allow.
