How to play: You are planning the color layers of a soap bar before it is carved open. A temperature gauge rises as molten wax is held ready above the mold. Press Space, click, or tap the POUR button exactly when the gauge is inside the bright ready band to lock in a clean colored layer that matches the planned pattern shown along the mold. A good pour advances to the next layer and builds your streak; pouring too early or too late lets the layer bleed and drains your wax supply. Plan and pour every layer in the mold before your wax runs out to finish the bar and move on to a taller, faster mold.
