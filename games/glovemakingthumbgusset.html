Glove Making Thumb Gusset
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Glove 1/8
How to play: A thumb gusset piece sits loose on the bench — drag it with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with the Arrow Keys, and rotate it with Q / E (or the rotate buttons), to match the dashed marker drawn on the palm opening. Watch the two small seam-alignment dots on the gusset's edges: they glow gold when they sit within the tight tolerance ring of their matching palm marks, and turn red when you drift off position or hold the wrong rotation. Press Space or tap the piece once both dots glow gold and the angle is true to stitch it into place — a sloppy fit with any gap or misalignment costs a life. Fit all 3 lives' worth of gussets correctly to advance — every new glove brings a trickier gusset shape and a narrower tolerance, so a sharp eye and a steady hand are needed to the very last round!
