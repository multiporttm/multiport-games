✨ Ring Making Polishing

Hold the ring to the buffing wheel. Press when the shine marker sweeps into the green zone for a flawless mirror pass.

A shine marker sweeps across the buffing gauge. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter when it lands in the green zone for a flawless mirror pass. A missed pass scuffs the metal and drains your polish focus - empty it and the ring is ruined. Five clean passes in a row speeds up the wheel for the next round.
