Umbrella Making Fabric Stretching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Canopy 1/8
How to play: Click or tap an open rib tip to pin the canopy fabric there. Pin opposite and alternating points around the ring to keep the tension gauge centered and green — pinning several points in a row on the same side pushes the needle into the red and strains the fabric. You can also use Arrow keys to move the highlighted rib tip and Enter / Space to pin it. Let the strain run too high for too long and the fabric tears, costing a life. Pin every rib tip to finish a taut canopy — canopies grow larger every round.
