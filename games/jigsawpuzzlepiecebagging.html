Jigsaw Puzzle Piece Bagging
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bag 1/8
How to play: Pieces keep pouring onto the scale and the weight needle climbs. Press Space, click, or tap to hit STOP and seal the bag the instant the needle is inside the glowing target zone. Stop too early and the bag is underweight; wait too long and it overflows — either one costs a life. Seal 8 good bags to finish the shift; the line speeds up and the target zone narrows with every bag. Lose all 3 lives and the line gets shut down!
