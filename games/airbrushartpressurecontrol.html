Airbrush Art Pressure Control
Pattern: 1 Fill: 0% Score: 0 Best: 0
Hold down the mouse button, your finger, or the Space bar to squeeze the trigger and raise the compressor's pressure gauge; release to let it drop. Keep the needle inside the glowing green target band shown for the current pattern — paint only fills the canvas meter while you are in the band. Drift too high and the line spatters (losing progress and health); drift too low and nothing sprays. Fill the canvas meter all the way before the pressure tank runs dry to finish the pattern!
