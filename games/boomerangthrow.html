Boomerang Throw
Score: 0 | Boomerangs: 3 | Hi: 0
Click/tap and drag back from the boomerang to aim and set power, then release to throw it at the targets. When it curves back near you, click/tap to catch it for a bonus and reuse it — miss the catch and it's lost. You get a limited number of boomerangs per round.
