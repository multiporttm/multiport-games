Tile: 1 Drill Heat: 0/6 Score: 0 Best: 0
Match the template's pip pattern. Click/tap the grid spots on the blank (or use number keys 1-9 as a 3x3 grid, top-left to bottom-right) to drill exactly the marked spots, then press Confirm Tile.
Each domino half is identified by its pips — the pattern of drilled spots from blank (0) to double-six (6). A template card shows the pattern this tile half needs. Click or tap the spots on the 3x3 drilling grid printed on the blank (or press number keys 1-9, read top-left to bottom-right) to bore exactly the spots the template shows, then press Confirm Tile. A perfect match scores well and cools the drill bit; drilling the wrong spot count or missing a required spot overheats the bit. Let the drill overheat completely and you have to stop and let it cool before the next tile — work precisely to keep the line moving and rack up the highest score.
