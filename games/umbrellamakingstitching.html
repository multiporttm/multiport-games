Umbrella Making Stitching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Seam 1/8
How to play: A glowing guide path marks the seam. Press and drag the needle (mouse or touch) along the path from start to end, staying inside the safe zone around the line. You can also hold Right Arrow to feed the needle forward exactly on the line, or Left Arrow to back it up. Stitches drop automatically as you move — keep a steady pace so they land evenly spaced. Wander too far off the guide for too long and the thread snaps, costing a life. Finish the seam to bank it — seams grow longer, with sharper turns, every round.
