🧙 Garden Gnome Making Ceramic Painting
Press and hold on the glowing start dot, then drag your brush along the glaze line all the way to the end without straying outside the painted channel.
Press and hold near the glowing dot at the left edge, then drag (mouse or finger) smoothly to the right, keeping your brush inside the glowing glaze channel as it winds up and down. Straying outside the channel chips the glaze and drains your Steady Hand meter. Reach the right edge to finish a pass and start a new, trickier line; let the meter empty and the paint job is ruined.
