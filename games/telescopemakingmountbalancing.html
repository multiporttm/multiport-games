Telescope Making Mount Balancing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
Hold Left/Right Arrow Keys or the Slide buttons to push the counterweight along the mount's declination axis. It has momentum — holding too long builds speed and can send it sliding past the target zone, so ease off early and let friction settle it in place. When the balance needle rests inside the green zone, press Space or LOCK before the timer runs out. Heavier telescope tubes and tighter zones arrive every round.
