Grandfather Clock Making Dial Painting
Dial: 1 Paint Left: 18 Score: 0 Best: 0
Study the gilded spoke pattern on the brass dial while it glows, then click each hour-mark spoke where gold paint belongs to repaint it from memory. Click Confirm Dial when done.
A grandfather clock's face is only as good as the dial painter's steady hand: gilded numerals and scrollwork spokes have to be brushed on in exactly the right places around the brass dial, by eye and by memory, with no room for a wandering line. Each round, the dial glows and briefly shows you a pattern of gold spokes radiating out from the center hub — study it closely, because the dial then goes dark and bare. Click the spokes (or tap them, or use the arrow keys plus Space to rotate a brush cursor and lay down paint) to repaint gold onto the spokes you memorized, then press Confirm Dial. A flawlessly repainted dial earns a big bonus and keeps your paint supply intact, while missed or misplaced spokes waste precious gold paint. The patterns grow busier and the memorize window shortens as you advance through dials, so study closely and paint fast and accurate before your paint supply runs dry.
