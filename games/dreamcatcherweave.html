Weave the web by clicking points in order!
Study the small reference pattern's numbered sequence, then click the matching anchor points around your hoop in the same order to weave the web threads. Clicking a point out of order resets your current web (try again). Once the web is fully woven, click the marked points to hang a bead or feather there to finish the dreamcatcher. Each round adds more anchor points and a longer sequence.
