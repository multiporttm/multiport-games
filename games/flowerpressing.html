Flower Pressing
Score: 0 | Hi: 060s
Drag every flower flat onto the paper without overlap
How to play: Drag (or use Tab + arrow keys) every flower onto the tan paper so none overlap and none hang off the edge — the glow turns green when a flower is placed correctly. Once all flowers are placed, click Start Pressing and click the four corner screws (or keys 1–4) to tighten them evenly to 100%. Let any corner get too far ahead of the others and a flower crumples — tighten evenly to finish the batch!
