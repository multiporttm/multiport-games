Planetarium Projector Calibration
Round 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
How to play: Drag the star field (or use Arrow Keys) to align it with the target constellation outline, use A / D (or the on-screen knobs) to tune rotation speed, and W / S to adjust brightness until the readout turns green. Click/tap Lock Calibration or press Enter when aligned. You have 3 lives — a bad lock costs one.
