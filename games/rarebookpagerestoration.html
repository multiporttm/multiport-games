Rare Book Page Restoration
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Page 1/10
How to play: A jagged tear runs across the page. Drag the Japanese tissue patch (mouse, finger, or nudge it with the Arrow Keys) so it lines up exactly over the tear, then hold it still over the tear — press and hold Space/click-and-hold, or just keep it steady while dragging — so the wheat starch paste can set. Drifting off the tear or jiggling the patch too much drains your steadiness meter; let it empty and the repair fails, costing a life. Every page after is more fragile, with a thinner tolerance and a faster-drying paste — lose all 3 lives and the page is lost!
