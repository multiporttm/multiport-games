Quill Pen Making Slit Piercing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Nib 1
The freshly cut nib is held steady under a fine piercing needle that swings on a little pendulum arm, and it has to be jabbed dead-center through the very tip of the nib or the ink slit will split off-axis and ruin the point. Watch the needle swing left and right over the nib, and press Space, click, or tap 📍 Pierce the instant it crosses the glowing target zone right at the tip to drive a clean, straight slit up the shaft. Pierce too early or too late and the hole opens crooked, cracking the nib — three cracked nibs and the batch is scrapped! Open a slit cleanly in every nib on the rack to move on to a smaller, faster-swinging rack.
