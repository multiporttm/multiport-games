Raku Ceramics Tongs Handling
Saved: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Move the tongs over a glowing pot and squeeze Grip to lift it, then carry it to the can.
How to play: Hot raku pots are scattered inside the open kiln. Move the tongs left and right with the ◀ ▶ buttons, Left/Right arrow keys, A/D, drag, or touch. Line the jaws up over a glowing pot and press Grip (or the Up arrow / Space) to clamp down and lift it — hold Grip while you carry the pot across to the reduction can on the far side, then release over the can to drop it in safely. Each pot cools as it waits, and a held pot slips from a shaky grip if you jostle it too much, so move smoothly. Save enough pots into the can before your kiln session runs out to complete the firing.
