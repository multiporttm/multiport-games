Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/8
How to play: The spinning blank's target profile glows across the piece — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to feed the gouge tip inside the tan tolerance band around the profile line as it travels left to right. Drift too far and your control meter drains; empty it and the tool catches, ruining the piece and costing a life. Stay steady the whole pass to finish shaping it. Every piece spins faster with a tighter profile — lose all 3 lives and you're out of blanks!
