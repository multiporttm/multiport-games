Anvil Shaping Technique
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
The target piece needs a recipe of shaping steps shown at the top. For each step, click/tap the matching anvil part — the Horn (curving), the Face (flattening) or the Hardy Hole (punching) — or press 1, 2, or 3. Hit them in order before time runs out. Wrong feature or a stalled clock costs a life. Recipes get longer and the clock tighter each level.
