A cast metal base is clamped under the shade, and the torque wrench needle sweeps back and forth across its gauge, hunting for the narrow green zone that marks the perfect clamping force for the next bolt. Press Space, tap, or click 🔩 Tighten the instant the needle swings inside the glowing zone to snug that bolt down perfectly. Tighten too early and the bolt stays loose and wobbly, too late and you strip the threads — either way you lose a life, and three ruined bolts means the base falls off! Snug down every bolt in the star pattern to finish the base and move on to a heavier base with a narrower, trickier torque zone.
