Glassblowing Marvering
Level: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrows or A/D, or drag on mobile, to roll the glowing glass glob back and forth across the marver table. Keep your rolling speed inside the green safe zone to shape it evenly — roll too fast and it flattens unevenly, too slow and it cools lumpy. Fill the evenness meter before time runs out!
