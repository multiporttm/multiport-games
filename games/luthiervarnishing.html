Luthier Varnishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Coat 1/8
Coverage: 0% · Pooled: 0
How to play: Drag your brush (mouse or finger), or steer with the Arrow Keys and hold Space to brush, over the violin body to build up an even varnish coat. Every patch must land inside the gold target thickness band shown by its color — too little brushing leaves bare wood, too much makes it pool and ruin the finish. Cover enough of the body within the band before time runs out to move to the next coat. Bigger bodies, finer grain, and tighter bands arrive every coat — lose all 3 lives and the finish is ruined!
