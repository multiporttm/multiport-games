Ship in a Bottle Cork Sealing
: 0 : 0 Hi: 0
How to play: Phase 1 — hold Space (or the button) to charge the push meter, release inside the glowing zone to seat the cork at the perfect depth. Phase 2 — a wax needle sweeps around the rim; tap Space (or the button) each time it passes through the glowing wedge to lay an even seal, all the way around. Miss too many wedges and the seal fails.
