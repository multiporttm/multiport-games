⚫ Domino Making Pip Drilling
A socket on the 3x2 drill grid lights up — tap it before the drill bit plunges to bore a clean pip hole. Miss the window and the tile cracks.
Watch the 3x2 grid of sockets on the domino blank. Whichever socket glows is about to be drilled — tap or click it (or press the matching 1-6 number key, numbered left-to-right, top-to-bottom) before its ring collapses to bore a clean pip hole. Tapping the wrong socket or letting the ring collapse cracks the tile and drains your Tile Integrity meter; chain clean holes to speed up the drill and raise your score.
