Embalming Ritual
Stage 1 — Canopic Jars: Tap an organ, then tap the jar of the god who protects it, to match all four correctly. Stage 2 — Natron Drying: Tap "Sprinkle Natron" the moment the red marker crosses the green zone, several times, to dry the body properly. Stage 3 — Sacred Anointing: Watch the glowing sequence of anointing points, then tap them back in the same order. Complete all three stages to guide the deceased into the afterlife. Works with mouse, touch, or tap.
