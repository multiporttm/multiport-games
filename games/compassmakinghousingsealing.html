🧭 Compass Making Housing Sealing
Press and hold on the glowing seal point, then drag your finger or mouse around the rim, keeping up with it as it circles the housing.
Press down on the glowing bead point on the rim of the compass housing, then keep dragging (mouse or finger) in a circle, staying close to the moving point as it travels around the ring. Staying close fills your Seal Integrity meter and scores points; drifting too far or lifting off drains it. The point speeds up and the tolerance narrows each round — let the meter empty and the housing seal fails.
