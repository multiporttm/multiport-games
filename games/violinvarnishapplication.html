Violin Varnish Application
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Coat 1/3
 
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger over the violin body (or move with the Arrow Keys and hold Space) to spread a thin, even coat of varnish across every panel. Linger too long in one spot and it pools into a drip — catch the falling drip with your brush before it reaches the bottom edge! Once the coat looks even, wait for it to fully dry (don't brush while drying) before the next coat begins. More coats and trickier color layers each round — lose all 3 lives and the finish is ruined.
