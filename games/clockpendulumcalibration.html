Clock Pendulum Calibration
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Clock 1/6
How to play: This clock's hands are drifting out of sync with the reference clock above it. Press the Up/Down Arrow keys, drag the slider, or tap the ▲/▼ buttons to slide the pendulum bob — raising it (shortening the pendulum) speeds the clock up, lowering it slows it down. Watch the rate-error readout and nudge the bob until the error sits inside the glowing tolerance band, then hold it steady there for a few seconds to lock the calibration. Run out of time before locking in and you lose a life. Six clocks await, each starting further off rate and needing a finer touch.
