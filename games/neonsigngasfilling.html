Neon Sign Gas Filling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Tube 1/10
How to play: Each tube's spec card shows which gas it needs and the target pressure band. Pick Neon or Argon (keys N / A), then hold Space, click, or press-and-hold Pump to raise the pressure — it leaks down slowly when you let go. Get the needle into the glowing green band and press Enter or Seal Tube to finish. Seal outside the band, with the wrong gas, or let it overfill and burst, and you lose a life. Ten tubes bring narrower bands and faster leaks!
