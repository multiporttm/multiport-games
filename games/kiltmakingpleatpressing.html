Kilt Making Pleat Pressing
Row: 1 Pleats: 0/0 Score: 0 Best: 0
A hot iron sweeps back and forth across the pleat line — press Press Iron, tap the canvas, or hit Space/Enter the instant the iron's needle crosses the bright target zone on each pleat. A perfectly timed press scores big and sharpens the pleat; miss the zone and the pleat scorches. Press every pleat in the row before your fabric heat runs out to move to a faster row.
