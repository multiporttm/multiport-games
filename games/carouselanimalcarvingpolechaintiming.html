Watch the swinging chain and press CLAMP right as it crosses the glowing notch on the spinning pole.
How to play: A rough wooden carousel animal blank spins on the center pole while a steel chain swings back and forth on its hook above it. Press and hold (mouse, finger, Space, or tap the canvas) right as the chain swings over the glowing notch on the pole to clamp it tight and lock that blank steady for carving. Clamping right on the notch fills your precision streak and tops up your grip strength; clamping far off the notch tires your grip fast. Lock enough poles in a row before your grip strength runs out to finish the carving shift and move on to a faster-swinging chain.
