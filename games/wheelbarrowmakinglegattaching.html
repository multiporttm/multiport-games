🔩 Wheelbarrow Making Leg Attaching
Four bolts hold each support leg. Tap bolts in the numbered order shown, and tap the glowing bolt exactly when its spinning needle crosses the green torque zone.
Each leg bracket has 4 bolts, numbered in the order they must be tightened. Tap (click or touch) the glowing numbered bolt the instant its spinning needle sweeps through the green torque zone. Tapping the wrong bolt, or tapping the right one off-zone, damages Bracket Stability — let it empty and the leg comes loose. Finish all 4 bolts to move to the next leg.
