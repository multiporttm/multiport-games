Subway Tunnel Maintenance
Score: 0 Night: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Each night the tunnel shows several equipment stations — Track, Signal, and Light — each marked with a number showing the required repair order. Click/tap a station (or press its number key) when it's lit as the next one due; repairing it out of order or too late costs a life. Clear every station before the shift clock runs out to open the line on time. Three lives — more stations and a tighter clock arrive each night.
