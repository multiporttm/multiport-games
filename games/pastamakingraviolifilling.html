Pasta Making Ravioli Filling
Fill Gauge — release inside the glowing target band
Round: 1 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Click/tap a glowing marked spot on the pasta sheet (or press Space to target the nearest one) and hold to squeeze out filling. Watch the fill gauge rise — release while the needle sits inside the glowing target band. Too little filling and the ravioli falls flat; too much and it bursts. Fill every marked spot to advance to a round with more dollops!
