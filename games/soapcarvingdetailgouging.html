Soap Carving Detail Gouging
Details: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Press and hold on the glowing point, release when the depth needle is in the gold band.
How to play: A glowing detail point marks the next spot that needs carving on the soap figure. Press and hold the HOLD GOUGE button, click, tap, or hold Space to drive the gouge in — a depth needle rises as you hold. Release exactly while the needle sits inside the gold target band to carve a crisp, clean detail. Release too early (too shallow) or hold too long (too deep) and the soap chips out, costing you one of your limited chip-outs. Finish every detail point on the figure before you run out of chip-outs to complete it and move on to a denser figure with a tighter depth band.
