Furniture Restoration Wood Grain Touch-Up
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Patch 1
A scratched patch of wood needs restaining to disappear invisibly into the grain around it, but the swatch you mix never looks quite right until it is actually blended in. Drag the slider, or use the ◀ Lighter / Darker ▶ buttons, the Left/Right arrow keys, or swipe on the canvas, to move your stain mix toward the target shade sampled from the surrounding grain. Press Space, tap, or click 🖌️ Blend In when you think the mix is right — a perfect match disappears invisibly, a close match still shows a faint halo, and a bad mismatch ruins the patch and costs a life! Blend in every patch on a piece to move on to trickier wood with a narrower tolerance for error.
