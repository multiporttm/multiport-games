How to play: You're dealt 7 cards against 3 AI opponents. Click a card in your hand that matches the color, number, or symbol of the top discard card to play it (Wild cards can be played anytime — pick a new color). Skip, Reverse, and Draw Two trigger their special effects on the next player; Wild Draw Four also forces the next player to draw four and pick a color. If you have no valid card, click the Draw Pile — if the card you draw is playable you may play it immediately, otherwise click Pass Turn. Empty your hand first to win the round — rounds won are tracked across the match!
