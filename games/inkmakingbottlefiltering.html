Fresh ink must pass through a cloth filter before it is bottled, straining out grit and bubbles. Drag the filter funnel left and right along the rack (or use the arrow keys, or drag with your finger on touch) to catch falling dark ink drops in your bottle below — but dodge the brown grit chunks and pale bubbles, which spoil the batch if you catch them. Fill the bottle with as much clean ink as you can before it overflows or time runs out, then cap it to lock in your score.
