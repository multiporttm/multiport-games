How to play: Put the suit on in order: Boots → Jacket → Gloves → Veil. Press 1,2,3,4 or Tap the matching gear icon to equip the next piece. Equip the wrong piece or leave a gap too long and the hive's buzz grows louder — fill all 4 pieces before the buzz meter maxes out to clear the round. Rounds get faster as you go. Three mistakes and the bees win.
