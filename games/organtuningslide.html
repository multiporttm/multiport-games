Organ Tuning Slide
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
How to play: Drag the tuning collar with your mouse or finger, or press Up/Down Arrow, to slide it along the pipe and change its pitch. Watch the needle and listen to the tone — slide it into the glowing target band, then press Space / tap LOCK to lock the tuning while the timer runs. Lock inside the band to tune the pipe; miss it and you lose a life. Target pitches and pipe lengths get trickier every round!
