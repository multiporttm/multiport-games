Rug Hooking Border Finishing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Rug 1
A needle glides clockwise around the rug's edge, passing through a bright target notch on each lap. Press Space, tap, or click 🪡 Stitch the instant the needle is inside the glowing notch to lock in a tidy whip-stitch. Stitch outside the notch and the binding pulls crooked, costing you thread. Finish enough stitches to close the loop and bind a fresh rug.
