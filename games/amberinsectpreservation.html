How to play: Press and hold on the amber (mouse, touch/tap-and-hold, or Space) to polish it — clarity rises. Release once the insect inside is visible but before clarity reaches the red crack zone, or the specimen is ruined. Once clarity is at least 40%, pick the matching species button (or press 1-5) to identify it. Six specimens per batch — preserve and identify as many as you can!
