Press and hold (mouse or touch, or Space) and drag the knife left-right across the whetstone in a steady rhythm. Keep the angle needle inside the glowing safe zone on every stroke. Rolling the angle outside the zone, or straying off-rhythm, dulls the edge. The target sharpness and safe zone get tighter each round.
