Roof Thatching Reed Bundling
Yealms Tied: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Catch the falling reed with your gathering arm, then tie the bundle once it is full.
How to play: Water reed stalks fall from above onto the gathering bench. Move the gathering arm left and right with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging/tapping on the canvas, to catch the falling stalks and pack them into your yealm (bundle). Watch the bundle meter fill as you catch good reed — but catching a spoiled, broken stalk (shown darker and bent) spills some of your bundle instead. Once the meter is full, press Tie Bundle (or hit Space) to cinch it tight: tie too early and the bundle is loose for fewer points, tie too late and stray stalks spill over the edge of the bench, ending your round. Tie enough yealms before you run out of bench room to post a high score.
