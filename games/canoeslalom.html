Canoe Slalom
Gates: 0 | Lives: 3 | Time: 60
Steer with Left/Right Arrow or A/D (or the ◀ ▶ buttons) to guide your canoe between the gate poles. Green gates just need a clean pass through the gap. For red reverse gates, hold the R key (or the on-screen R button) as you pass through. Avoid the rocks! Missing a gate or hitting a rock costs a life — clear as many gates as you can before time or lives run out.
