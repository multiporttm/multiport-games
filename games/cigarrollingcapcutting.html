Cigar Cap Cutting
Cigar: 1 | Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrow Keys, drag, or swipe to slide the round cutter back and forth over the draped cap leaf. Press Space, Click, or Tap to slice the moment the cutter lines up with the glowing seal zone where the leaf overlaps the cigar head. Cut too far left and the cap tears loose; too far right and the leaf bunches up. The seal zone shrinks each round!
