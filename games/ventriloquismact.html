Lip Stillness
Each round is a script broken into words. Click Say Next Word right when a word lights up yellow (the cue) to speak it smoothly through the dummy. Click too early, too late, too fast, or too erratically and your own lips move — draining the Lip Stillness meter. Keep it above zero and land your cues to score; scripts get longer and faster each round.
