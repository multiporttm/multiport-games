Your Button — click holes in order

Click the reference pattern's first hole to begin.
How to play: Study the reference pattern on the left — it shows an X or square stitch order for the button's holes. Click the holes on your button (right) in the same order to begin a stitch. After each pair of holes, a tension bar appears — click Pull Needle (or press Space) while the yellow needle marker is inside the green zone to keep the stitch tight. Complete every stitch in the pattern to sew the button. Wrong-order clicks or loose pulls weaken the button's attachment strength — if it drops to zero, the button falls off and the round ends. Sew buttons correctly to score; each round adds more holes and trickier patterns.
