Pasta Making Drying
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Each pasta shape hangs on the rack with a glowing dial counting down to its next flip. Click/tap a piece (or press its number key, 1–9) exactly when the dial fills to rotate it and keep both sides drying evenly. Flip too early or too late and the piece cracks, costing a life. Keep every piece rotating correctly before the humidity bar at the top runs empty — more pieces join the rack and the timing gets tighter each round!
