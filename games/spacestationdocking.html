Goal: dock your capsule with the slowly rotating station. Use Left/Right (or A/D, or the ⟲/⟳ buttons) to fire RCS thrusters and match your capsule's spin rate to the station's. Use Up/Down (or W/S, or ▲/▼) to control your approach speed. Reach the station with your spin rate matched and your approach speed slow for a soft dock — mismatched spin or excess speed means a collision with the solar panels. Watch your fuel and the clock!
