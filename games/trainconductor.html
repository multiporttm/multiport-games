Route the incoming train to its scheduled platform!
Check the schedule board for which platform each incoming train needs, then click the matching Switch button before it arrives to route it there. Once it's stopped, wait for boarding to finish and click Depart — not too early (passengers still boarding) and not too late (schedule delay). Wrong routing or bad timing costs a life.
