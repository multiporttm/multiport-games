Candle Wick Trimming
Score: 0 Level 1 Lives: 3 Time: 60 Hi: 0
Use ←/→ (or ◀/▶) to pick a wick, then hold Space (or ✂️) to charge the scissors — a short tap snips a little, a longer hold snips more. Release to cut. Get every wick's length into the green target band before closing time. Too short and the wick is ruined — too long keeps smoking. 3 lives.
