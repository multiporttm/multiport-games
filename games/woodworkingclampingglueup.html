Woodworking Clamping Glue-Up
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1/6
How to play: For each seam, first watch the glue spreader swing back and forth and press Space / click / tap the instant it lines up inside the gold target zone to lay glue evenly. Then press and hold to tighten the clamp as the pressure gauge rises, and release right when it's inside the gold band — too loose leaves a gap, too tight squeezes out all the glue. Miss too badly and your flatness meter drains, costing a life when it empties. More seams and tighter windows arrive every round — lose all 3 lives and the glue-up is scrapped!
