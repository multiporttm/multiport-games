How to play: A stylized mannequin figure is marked with a faint glowing target outline for its festival body-paint design, and a shaped stencil floats above the canvas waiting to be laid into place. Drag the stencil with the mouse or your finger, or move it with the arrow keys or WASD, and rotate it with the Q and E keys (or the rotate buttons) until its shape and angle line up with the glowing target, then press Space or the Drop button to lock it in. A close, well-aligned drop fills the target brightly and scores high, while a sloppy drop leaves a smudged, off-center mark and scores low. Watch the countdown bar at the top drain — place as many stencils as you can, each one trickier than the last, before time runs out, to build the highest score possible.
