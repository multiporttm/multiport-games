Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Use W/S (or the +/- buttons) to control Temperature, and A/D (or the +/- buttons) to control Steam Flow. Keep both gauges in the green zone to fill the collection meter, then press Space or tap COLLECT the instant it flashes "READY" to bottle the oil. Miss the window, or drift out of range too long, and you lose a batch. You have 3 lives.
