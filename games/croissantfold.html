Watch the temperature gauge — dough warms as you work it. Click Chill to pop it in the fridge, then once it's in the cold zone (left side), click Fold Dough to fold it in thirds. After folding, drag the rolling pin left-and-right across the dough to roll it back out flat, then chill and fold again. Match the exact number of folds shown in the recipe — too few or too many, or folding overheated dough, ruins the batch! Each new batch demands more folds.
