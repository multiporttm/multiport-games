🌿 Trellis Making Lattice Weaving
Watch the glowing strips cross the lattice, then tap the four directions in the exact same order to weave it correctly.
A sequence of strips will light up pointing up, down, left or right across the lattice — watch closely. Then repeat the same order by tapping the matching arrow quadrant (or using the arrow keys) before the tension timer runs out. Each correct round adds one more strip to remember; a wrong tap or a timeout drains your Weave Tension — let it empty and the lattice falls apart.
