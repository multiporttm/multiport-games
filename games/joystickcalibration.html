A throw indicator bounces along the gauge for the direction shown. Press Arrow keys (or tap the on-screen pad) to lock the actuator screw the instant the indicator is inside the green sweet spot — too early is a dead zone, too late is a double-trigger. Calibrate all 4 directions each round. The sweet spot shrinks and the indicator speeds up every level.
