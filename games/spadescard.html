How to play: You and your AI partner (across the table) play as a team against two AI opponents. Each hand, everyone is dealt 13 cards and bids how many tricks their team will take — click a number to bid. Then take turns clicking a card from your hand to play: you must follow the led suit if you can; if you're void, any card may be played, and spades are always trump (they beat any other suit but can't lead until broken, unless that's all you have). Highest card of the led suit — or the highest spade if any were played — wins the trick. If your team makes its combined bid you score 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 point per extra trick ("bag") won; falling short costs 10 points per trick bid. Ten bags accumulated costs your team 100 points! First team to 300 points wins the match.
