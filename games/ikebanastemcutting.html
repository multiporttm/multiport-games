How to play: A fresh stem lies on the cutting board with a target mark. The shear blade swings back and forth above it — press Cut (or Space / tap the board) exactly when the blade lines up with the target mark to make a clean cut. The closer to the mark, the more points you earn. Miss too far 3 times and the arranging session ends.
