Lighthouse Keeper Lens Rotation
Level 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Space (or press and hold the Crank button / canvas) to turn the heavy lens mechanism. Mechanical drag and gusting weather constantly slow it down — keep the speed needle inside the green safe zone on the gauge to earn points. Let it stall or spin out of control too long and the beam fails. Gearing gets heavier and the safe zone narrower every round!
