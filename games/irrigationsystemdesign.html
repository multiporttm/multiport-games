Irrigation System Design
Straight: 6 Elbow: 6 Tee: 3 Cross: 1 Hi moves: 0
Select a pipe, place it, click a placed pipe to rotate it.
How to play: Click a pipe type in the toolbar, then click an empty cell to place it. Click a placed pipe of the same type to rotate it, or select Remove to pick one back up. Connect the blue water source to every green planted bed using your limited pipes, then click Check Flow to test the design. Keyboard: 1-4 select pipe type, R remove, arrow keys move the cursor, Space/Enter act on the cursor cell, F checks flow.
