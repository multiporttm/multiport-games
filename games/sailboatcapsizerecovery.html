Sailboat Capsize Recovery
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Attempt 1
How to play: Use Left/Right Arrows (or the on-screen buttons) to swim to the centerboard and to shift your weight. Press Space (or the center button) at the right moment to climb on, then to climb aboard, and to grab the tiller. Stage 1: swim onto the centerboard. Stage 2: lean to keep the balance meter centered while the hull rights itself — don't flip it back over. Stage 3: tap the action button when the climb prompt is green. Stage 4: steer clear of the rocks. The current gets stronger with every attempt — lose all 3 lives and you're adrift!
