🍞 Toaster Making Crumb Tray Molding
Hold to pour molten metal into the crumb tray mold. Release right inside the glowing fill zone — too little leaves gaps, too much overflows.
Press and hold (click, tap, or Space/Enter) to pour molten metal into the crumb tray mold — the fill level rises while you hold. Release while the level is inside the glowing fill zone for a clean tray. Releasing too early leaves gaps and releasing too late overflows the mold, draining your Mold Quality meter — let it empty and the tray is scrapped.
