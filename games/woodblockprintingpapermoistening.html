Hold the button to brush on water. Release inside the pale target zone.
How to play: Hold the "Hold to Brush Water" button (mouse, finger, or the Space bar) to brush water onto the dry washi sheet and watch the moisture gauge climb. Release right as the gauge needle sits inside the pale target zone to stack a perfectly dampened sheet. Release too early and the sheet is too dry to take ink; keep brushing past the zone and it oversoaks into pulp. Each stacked sheet narrows the next target zone, so your timing has to stay sharp.
