Silver Mine Excavation
Silver: 0ozBeams: 🪵🪵🪵Best: 0oz
How to play: Click or tap a tile to dig it. Safe tiles reveal a number showing how many unstable rock tiles touch it (an ore-vein hint) plus any silver found there — a 0 auto-clears neighboring tiles. Right-click / long-press a tile to mark it with a support beam if you suspect it's unstable. Digging an unstable tile costs one of your 3 support beams — lose all 3 and the shaft collapses! Clear every safe tile to complete the excavation and bank your silver.
