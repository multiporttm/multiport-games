Luthier Scroll Carving
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Scroll 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead travels along the spiral scroll pattern — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your chisel tip inside the amber tolerance tube around it. Wander outside it and your control meter drains; empty it and the cut slips, costing a life. Track the bead all the way to the eye of the scroll to finish and move to the next headstock. The spiral grows tighter and faster with every scroll — lose all 3 lives and the block is ruined!
