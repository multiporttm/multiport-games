How to play: Press Up/Down Arrows or tap + / − to raise or lower drill speed. The readout on the right shows the hardness of the rock layer ahead — soft layers drill fast and safely, but hard layers overheat the drill bit if you go too fast. Watch the heat gauge: let it max out and the bit breaks, ending the run. Balance speed against hardness to extract a complete core sample from the seafloor!
