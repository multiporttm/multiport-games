Sign Channel Letter Bending
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Letter 1/10
How to play: A bright bend-point travels around the outline of each channel letter — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your bending tool inside the glowing tolerance tube around it. The tube narrows sharply at every corner, so ease off there or the metal kinks. Drift too far and your clean-bend meter drains; empty it and you lose a life. Finish all 10 letters, which get more complex and faster, before you run out of lives!
