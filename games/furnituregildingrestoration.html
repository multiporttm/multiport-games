Furniture Gilding Restoration
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Spot 1/10
How to play: Each damaged spot needs three steps. A needle sweeps across the bar — press Space / Click / Tap when it's inside the glowing zone to apply the gesso and bole, then again to lay the gold leaf. Finally, rub back and forth with the Left/Right Arrow Keys or by dragging across the canvas to burnish the leaf until the meter fills before time runs out. Miss a timing window or run out of burnishing time and the spot is ruined, costing a life. The zones narrow and the needle speeds up with every spot — restore all 10 spots to finish the frame!
