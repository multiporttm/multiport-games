Silversmithing Annealing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Sheet 1
Each zone needs a cherry-red glow — watch for white-hot!
Move the torch with the mouse, arrow keys, or touch drag to sweep flame evenly across every zone of the sheet. Hold it still too long and a zone turns white-hot and slumps; keep moving to spread the heat. Once every zone glows cherry-red, press Enter or 💧 Quench to cool the sheet soft and ready — but quench too early and it is still brittle!
