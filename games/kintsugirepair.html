Drag each scattered shard (mouse or finger) onto its matching pale outline to rebuild the broken pottery — a piece snaps into place when dropped close enough. Once every piece is placed, click along each glowing seam to apply gold kintsugi lacquer and finish the repair. Score comes from correct placements and completed seams; each round adds more pieces to the vessel.
