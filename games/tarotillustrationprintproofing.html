How to play: The reference proof sits top-left — every other card on the tray should match it exactly. Click or tap the PASS or FAIL button under each printed card (or use P / F and Arrow Keys / Tab to move the cursor) before the batch timer runs out. A card fails if its colors are shifted, it has a smudge, a missing border, or a faded patch — look closely, because defects get subtler each round. Wrong calls and missed cards cost a life — lose all 3 and the press shuts down.
