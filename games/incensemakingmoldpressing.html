The mold slides left and right. Line it up over the paste blob below, then press SPACE (or tap the canvas) while also holding the press gauge in the sweet spot — not too light, not too hard — to stamp a clean cone.
Shaping an incense cone takes a steady hand with the brass mold. The mold swings left and right above a row of soft paste blobs, and a separate force needle rises on its own as you hold the press. Line the mold up directly over the lit blob and release your press (SPACE, the Press button, or a tap) when the mold is centered on the blob AND the force needle sits inside the gold sweet-spot band — too little force leaves the cone unformed, too much squashes it flat. A clean press stamps out a perfect cone for points based on both your alignment and your force accuracy. Press enough clean cones before your tray of paste runs dry to move on to a faster tray with a narrower sweet spot!
