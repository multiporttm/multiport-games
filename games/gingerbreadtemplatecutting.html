Gingerbread Template Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead travels the outline of the wall, roof, door or window template pressed into the rolled dough — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your cutter inside the amber tolerance tube around it. Drift too far and the dough-warmth meter drains; empty it and the piece warps out of shape, costing a life. Stay close the whole way to cut a crisp piece and move on. Templates get more intricate and the bead speeds up every piece — lose all 3 lives and the batch is ruined!
