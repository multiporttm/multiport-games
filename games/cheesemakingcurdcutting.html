Cheesemaking Curd Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: A fresh vat of set curd is marked with faint guide lines. Press and drag (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys + Enter to confirm a cut) a straight stroke along each guide line, as close to it as you can, to slice the curd into an even grid of cubes. Clean cuts score big; wobbly or missed cuts damage the curd. Finish the whole grid before the timer runs out and before the curd overworks (3 bad cuts) — each round adds more lines and less time!
