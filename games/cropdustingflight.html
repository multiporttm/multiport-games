Crop Dusting Flight
Row 1/6 | Spray: 100% | Lives: 3 | Hi: 0
How to play: Hold Space (or the SPRAY button) to spray the row beneath you — keep it held across the whole row for even coverage. As you near the power line or trees at the row's edge, press Up Arrow (or PULL UP) at the right moment to clear the obstacle; too early or too late and you'll crash. Finish all rows before your spray tank runs dry!
