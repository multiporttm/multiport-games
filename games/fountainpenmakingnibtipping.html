Fountain Pen Making Nib Tipping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Nib 1/8
How to play: Hold down your mouse, finger, or the Space Bar to apply the torch and raise the heat gauge; release to let it cool. Keep the needle inside the glowing gold fusing band long enough to fill the fusion progress ring and bond the iridium ball to the nib. Push past the red line at the top and the nib overheats and melts instantly, costing a life. The safe band narrows, sits higher, and the heat swings faster with every nib — lose all 3 lives and the batch is ruined!
