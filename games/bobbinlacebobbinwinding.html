Hold Space or Tap and hold to wind thread onto the bobbin. Release while the gauge needle sits inside the safe zone — release too early or too late, or let it overwind, and the thread snaps. Wind a full set of bobbins each round; safe zones narrow and winding speeds rise as you progress!
