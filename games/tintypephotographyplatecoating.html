Tintype Photography Plate Coating
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Plate 1
A black lacquered tin plate waits in the darkroom, held level in your hand. Press Space, tap, or click 🫙 Pour to start pouring thick collodion onto one corner, then hold (press and hold, or tap and hold) to tilt the plate and let the syrupy liquid flow in a slow, even wave across the surface — release right as the pool reaches the target edge, no sooner, no later. Pour too fast and it floods off the corner in ropy streaks; pour too slow and it skins over before it spreads — three ruined plates and the session is wasted! Coat every plate in a batch to move to a trickier batch with a thicker-setting mix.
