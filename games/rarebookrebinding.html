Rare Book Rebinding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Book 1/8
How to play: Each damaged book has scattered pieces — loose signature pages, spine lining, boards and cover — each labeled with a step number. Click (or tab with Arrow Keys and press Enter/Space) the pieces in their correct structural order, from the first signature sewn to the cover reattached, to rebind the book. A wrong click costs a life. Every book that follows has more pieces and a tighter time limit — lose all 3 lives and the restoration is abandoned!
