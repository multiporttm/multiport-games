Mycelium Growth Tracking
Coverage: 0% Batch: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0%
Mycelium spreads cell by cell across the substrate grid over time. Select a tool — N for Nutrient or M for Moisture (or tap the buttons) — then click/tap a grid cell to apply it there, boosting its growth. Unattended patches can turn moldy (contaminated) and stop spreading, costing a life; too much of one input can also spoil a cell. Reach full healthy coverage before the spoil timer runs out to clear the batch. Each batch raises the grid size and spoil speed — you have 3 lives.
