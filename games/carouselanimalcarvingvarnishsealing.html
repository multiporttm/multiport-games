Hold down and sweep the brush at a steady pace to seal every patch before the varnish dries.
How to play: Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) and move the brush over the carousel animal's surface, or use the arrow keys to steer it while holding Space, to spread an even coat of varnish across every patch. Sweeping at a steady, moderate pace builds each patch's coverage cleanly; moving too fast skips patches and leaves them dry, while lingering too long in one place pools the varnish into a drip that damages that patch and costs you dry time. Seal every patch to a smooth gloss before the varnish skins over and dries to finish the animal and move on to a bigger, trickier one.
