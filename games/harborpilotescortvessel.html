Harbor Pilot Escort Vessel
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Leg 1
How to play: Steer your little escort tug left and right with the Arrow Keys or A/D, or drag/tap on either side of the canvas. The big tanker drifts sideways on its own — pull your tug up against whichever side it is drifting toward to push it back toward the center of the channel. Let it drift into a channel wall and you lose a life. Currents get stronger, channels get narrower, and tankers get bigger with every leg — survive six legs to dock safely!
