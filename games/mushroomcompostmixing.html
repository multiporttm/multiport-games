Each batch shows a target recipe (ratios of Straw / Manure / Coco Coir / Gypsum). Press 1-4 or tap an ingredient to add a scoop to the mixing bin — watch the bars match the target zones. Press Space to pasteurize once the mix looks right (heats the bin — watch the temperature gauge). Then press Enter or tap Check & Inoculate to verify pH and temperature are both in range and finish the batch. Get it wrong and lose a life — ratios get stricter and gauges drift faster each batch.
