How to play: A bisque pot waits for its glaze coat. Hold the mouse/touch down and drag the brush across the pot, or use the arrow keys/WASD/on-screen buttons while holding Space (or just tapping them) to paint glaze onto the clay. Staying on the pot fills in the glaze coverage meter; letting the brush stray outside the pot's silhouette drips glaze and drains your steady-hand meter. Cover at least 90% of the pot before your hand slips to finish the coat and move to the next, trickier pot shape.
