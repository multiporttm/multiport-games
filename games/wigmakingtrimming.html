Wig Making Trimming
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Wig 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead travels along the target length line across the wig — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep the scissor tip inside the golden tolerance tube around it. Drift too far from the line and your precision meter drains, but drop below the line and you cut the hair too short — a much faster drain. Empty the meter and the trim is ruined, costing a life. Each finished wig unlocks a more intricate trim pattern with a faster bead and a tighter tube. Lose all 3 lives and the trimming session ends!
