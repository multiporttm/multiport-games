Four clamps grip the corners of the canoe's canvas skin. Each one slowly loses tension over time; tap or click a clamp (or press 1/2/3/4) to tighten it a notch. Keep every clamp's gauge inside the green band at once to rack up Even Stretch score — but tap a clamp too many times and it overtightens past the red line and the canvas tears there, draining your Canvas Condition.
