How to play: Click Verify (or press 1-6) on every system before the clock hits T-0. When a HOLD CALL pops up, read it fast and click GO (or press G) if it's a false alarm, or HOLD (or press H) if it's a real problem — a wrong real-problem call scrubs the launch.
