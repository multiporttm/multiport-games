Marionette Joint Carving
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Joint 1/10
How to play: A dotted target outline shows the joint shape to carve — elbow, knee, or ankle. Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) along the outline to shave wood down to the gold line. Stay inside the safe band and your wood meter holds; stray past the outline into the thin red zone and the joint weakens, eventually snapping and costing a life. Finish tracing the whole outline before the meter breaks to move to the next joint. Shapes get trickier and the safe band narrows every joint — lose all 3 lives and the blank is ruined!
