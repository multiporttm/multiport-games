Amusement Park Operator
Score: 0 | Hi: 0Overflows: 0/3
Guests keep arriving at the gate. Click a ride's queue (or press 1-5) to send the waiting guest there — pick whichever line is shortest to keep the park flowing. Each ride drains at its own pace and stops accepting guests once its queue is full; overflow a queue three times and the park closes for the day.
