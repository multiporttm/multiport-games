Bagel Making Schmear Spreading
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bagel 1/5
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or use the Arrow Keys) to sweep the schmear knife back and forth across the whole bagel half. The side gauge shows your stroke speed — keep the needle inside the green safe zone. Sweep too slowly and cream cheese pools too thick; too fast and spots are left bare. Cover every patch of the bagel while keeping your coverage meter above zero to finish the half and move on. The safe zone narrows and the bagel gets bigger every round — lose all 3 lives and the rack is ruined!
