Glassblowing Blowpipe Shaping
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Space or press and hold the canvas to blow short puffs of air into the pipe, inflating the glowing bubble. The pipe rotates automatically while you blow. Release to stop inflating and let it cool slightly. Stop with the bubble's edge inside the target ring — overinflate and it pops! Rounds get thinner-walled and trickier.
