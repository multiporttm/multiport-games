An engraver is laying out the decorative cartouche border for a fine globe. Watch the colored ink dabs light up around the ring in order, then click or tap them back in that exact same sequence to engrave the design correctly. Each round adds one more dab to the pattern. A wrong dab smudges the ink — three smudges and the cartouche is ruined! Use 1-6 on the keyboard as a shortcut for the dabs in clock position order, or click 👁 Watch Again to replay the pattern.
