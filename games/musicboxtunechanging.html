Music Box Tune Changing
Lives: 3Level 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Rotate the changer to line up the requested tune
Left/Right or A/D rotate the changer · Drag the wheel · Space/Tap to engage the comb
Shift a multi-tune music box's cylinder changer to the requested song, then engage the comb only once the slot is exactly aligned. Use Left/Right or A/D, or drag the wheel, to rotate the changer, and press Space or tap to drop the comb onto the aligned slot. Engaging off-target skips a beat. More tunes to choose from and tighter alignment windows as you level up.
