Move the stencil with Arrow keys, rotate with Q/E, resize with -/= (or use the on-screen buttons) to line it up with the client's placement marks — matching position, size and angle to the dashed target. Press Enter or tap PRESS to transfer the stencil once it's aligned. The tolerance window shrinks every round.
