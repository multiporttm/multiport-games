Kayak Seam Sealing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Seam 1/8
How to play: A glowing bead of sealant travels along the hull seam — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your nozzle tip inside the tolerance tube around it. Staying very close to the bead builds pressure toward overflow; drifting too far drops pressure toward a gap. Keep the pressure meter in the green safe zone the whole way to finish the seam. Seams get longer and the tube narrows every round — let the pressure bottom out or max out 3 times and the hull leaks!
