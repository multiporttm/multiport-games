⚓ Anchor Making Shackle Fitting
Press and hold to lower the hydraulic ram. Release right as the pin reaches the glowing seating zone for a tight fit — too shallow and it's loose, too deep and it jams.
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space/Enter) to drive the hydraulic ram and lower the shackle pin into the anchor eye. Release the moment the pin's tip reaches the glowing seating zone to lock it in with a tight fit. Releasing too early leaves it loose and releasing too late jams and cracks the shackle — either drains Shackle Condition. Land several tight fits in a row to speed up the next pin and raise your score; let Shackle Condition empty and the shackle is ruined.
