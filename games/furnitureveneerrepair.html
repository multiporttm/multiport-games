Furniture Veneer Repair
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1/10
How to play: Move your presser (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys) over a glowing, lifted veneer bubble, then press and hold (click, tap, or Space) to inject glue and flatten it. Watch the glue meter — release once it is in the golden safe zone to bond it cleanly. Hold too long and the veneer cracks, costing a life! More bubbles appear and the safe zone narrows every round. Repair all 10 rounds to restore the piece.
