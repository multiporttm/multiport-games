Umbrella Making Canopy Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1/8
How to play: A glowing guide outline marks the umbrella canopy panel. Press and drag the scissors (mouse or touch) all the way around the outline back to the start, staying close to the glowing line. You can also hold Left/Right Arrow keys to nudge the scissors forward or backward exactly along the line. Wander too far off the guide for too long and the cut is ruined, costing a life. Finish the cut to bank the panel — shapes get more intricate, with sharper notches and tighter curves, every round.
