Dice Making Corner Sanding
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Blank 1
A sharp-edged acrylic blank is spinning slowly on the sanding jig, and one corner at a time needs just enough of a rounded bevel to feel smooth in the hand without softening the pips underneath. Press and hold Space, tap, or press and hold 🧽 Hold to Sand to grind the bevel meter upward, then let go the instant it sits inside the glowing target band to leave that corner perfectly rounded. Let go too soon and the corner stays sharp, let go too late and you over-sand into the face — either way you lose a life, and three ruined corners means the blank is scrapped! Round off every corner on a blank to move on to a tougher blank with a narrower target band.
