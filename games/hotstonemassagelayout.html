Held stone heat
How to play: Each empty zone on the back shows the stone size and temperature it needs. Click or tap a stone from the heated tray at the bottom to lift it, then click or tap the zone that needs it — or use Arrow Keys to highlight a tray stone or zone and Enter/Space to pick up or place, Escape to put a held stone back. A lifted stone cools fast: place it while its heat bar is still high, on the zone that matches both size and temperature. A wrong match or a stone gone cold costs a life. Fill every zone to clear the round — more zones and faster cooldowns follow!
