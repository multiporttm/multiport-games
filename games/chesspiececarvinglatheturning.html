Hold the gouge and move up/down to match the glowing target radius as the blank spins.
How to play: A wooden blank spins on the lathe while a faint target silhouette for the chess piece shows the radius you need to cut at each height. Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) then drag up and down, or use the Up/Down arrow keys, to move the gouge along the spinning blank, matching the target radius band by band. Cutting close to the target fills your precision score and tops up your wood budget; cutting too deep snaps the blank, and drifting far from the target wastes wood fast. Finish every band before the wood budget runs out to complete the piece and move on to a sharper profile.
