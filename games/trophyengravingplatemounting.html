Trophy Engraving Plate Mounting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Trophy 1/8
How to play: Drag the gold name plate (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys + Enter) onto the dashed marked position on the trophy base. Once it snaps into place, screw holes appear each labeled with a number — click or tap them in ascending order (1, 2, 3...), or use Tab to cycle the highlighted hole and Enter/Space to drive it. Mount every screw correctly to finish the trophy. Plate shapes get trickier, screw counts rise, and the target zone shrinks each round — lose all 3 lives and the job is scrapped!
