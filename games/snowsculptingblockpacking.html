Snow Sculpting Block Packing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Block 1/6
How to play: Hold Space, hold the mouse button, or hold your finger down to tamp the snow into the wooden form — the density needle on the gauge rises while you tamp and settles back down when you let go. Keep the needle inside the glowing green safe zone long enough to fill the packing meter. Let it sit too low too long and the block collapses (loose snow); push it too high and it turns to hard ice that ruins the sculpture — either way costs a life. Pack all six blocks, each bigger with a trickier safe zone, before you run out of lives!
