Steer with Arrow Keys/A D or drag on the trail. Hold Space, Up Arrow, or the PUSH button to gallop faster — it drains stamina. Rest to recover, but the bandit gang closes in while you rest. Dodge rocks, potholes and wagons and reach town before they catch you!
