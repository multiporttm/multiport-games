Dragonfly Catch
Time: 40s
Caught: 0
Best: 0
The dragonfly darts around the pond in quick bursts, then pauses briefly. Click or tap it while it's paused to net it — clicking while it's mid-dart misses. Or use Arrow Keys to move the reticle and Space/Enter to swing the net. It gets more erratic over time. Catch as many as you can before time runs out!
