Violin Body Carving
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Plate 1
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger across the wood (or move with the Arrow Keys and hold Space) to carve the violin top's arching down toward the dashed target curve. Watch the caliper readouts — green means in tolerance. Carve past a caliper's minimum and you gouge the wood, costing a life. Get every caliper in tolerance, then click Check Calipers (or press Enter) to finish the plate and move to a trickier arching shape.
