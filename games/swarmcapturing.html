Swarm Capturing
Score: 0 Swarm: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrows or drag to slide the bait box under the swarm cluster on the branch. Press Space/tap to brush-shake the bees in — time it while the box is lined up and holding steady, not while swinging. Scatter too many bees or lose the queen and you lose a life. Capture the whole cluster to advance — swarms move faster each round.
