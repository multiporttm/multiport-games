Bracelet Making Bead Threading

Press Space, click, or tap in the green zone to thread each bead cleanly.

Keep the rhythm steady - a jewelry cord forgives nothing out of place.
Watch the marker sweep across the cord gauge. Press Space/Enter, click or tap right when it is inside the green zone to thread a bead cleanly onto the elastic cord. A miss frays your pattern accuracy — let it run out and the bracelet is ruined. Thread 5 beads to tighten the pattern and speed up the next round!
