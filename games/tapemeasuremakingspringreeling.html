📏 Tape Measure Making Spring Reeling
Press and hold to wind the recoil spring. The tension needle climbs while you hold — release the instant it's inside the green calibration zone to lock in a perfect reel.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) on the reel to wind the spring — the tension needle rises the longer you hold. Release right when the needle is inside the green zone to lock a perfect coil and advance. Release too early or too late, or let the spring overwind past the top, and you damage Spring Integrity. Let it empty and the reel snaps.
