🔑 Key Holder Making Hook Welding
Press and hold the torch to heat the hook joint. Release the instant the heat needle sits inside the bright weld zone for a clean fusion. Too cold leaves a weak joint, too hot burns through the metal.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) on the torch to heat up the weld joint — the heat needle climbs while you hold and cools while you don't. Release exactly when the needle is inside the bright weld zone to fuse a clean hook. Releasing outside the zone, or letting the needle overheat past the top, damages Joint Integrity; let it run out and the hook snaps off.
