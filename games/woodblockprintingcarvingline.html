Follow the ink line with your knife — drag, use arrow keys, or A/D.
How to play: A wavy ink guideline is printed on the woodblock. Guide the carving knife along the line by dragging it, using the ◀ ▶ buttons, the Left/Right arrow keys, or A/D — the knife always creeps forward on its own. Staying tight to the line builds your blade control meter; drifting off the line's tolerance band drains it and costs points. Carve the full length of the line before your blade control runs out to complete the key block and move to the next, trickier line.
