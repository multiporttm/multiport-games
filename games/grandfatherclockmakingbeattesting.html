Grandfather Clock Making Beat Testing

Watch the pendulum swing. Tap, click, or press Space (or Left/Right arrows) the instant the bob reaches the far left or far right of its swing to mark the tick or tock in beat. Early or late taps throw the escapement out of beat.
Beat testing is the clockmaker's final listening test before a grandfather clock leaves the bench: a true escapement ticks and tocks in perfectly even time, and the only way to confirm it is to listen closely and tap along with every swing. Watch the pendulum bob sweep from side to side and tap, click, or press Space (Left and Right arrows work too) the instant it reaches the far left or far right of its arc — that is the moment a real escapement would release its tick or tock. Tap right on the beat for a Perfect mark, a little early or late for a Good one, and badly mismatched or missed taps are judged out of beat and cost you one of your three lives. Chain perfect taps together to build your combo and the pendulum's tempo gradually quickens to test your ear further, so listen closely and keep your taps steady and true.
