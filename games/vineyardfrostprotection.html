Vineyard Frost Protection
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1/6
How to play: A hard frost is settling over the vineyard. Each block has a temperature gauge that drifts down toward freezing. Click/tap a block to light its smudge pot (warms it steadily but burns out over time) — click again on a lit block to spin up its wind machine instead (warms faster but drains quickly). Keep every block above the blue freeze line until dawn breaks. Letting a block freeze solid costs a life. Use 1-9 or Tab/Space to select and toggle blocks by keyboard. Each new round adds more blocks and colder, faster drops. 3 lives — survive all 6 rounds to win!
