How to play: A glowing raku pot rotates slowly on the turntable while a sweep marker glides back and forth across a dial. Tap the Dip Feather button, click anywhere on the canvas, or press Space / Enter the instant the marker is inside the bright sweet-spot band to sear a crisp carbon trail onto the glaze. A dead-center hit burns a long, dark trail for big points; an edge hit burns a short, pale one; missing the band entirely wastes the feather with no mark. You have a limited number of feathers per pot — reach the target trail coverage before they run out to complete the piece, and the sweet-spot band narrows and speeds up on every pot after that.
