Get ready to sprint leg 1!
Alternate Left / Right arrow keys (or tap the ◀ ▶ buttons) as fast as you can to sprint your leg of the relay. When you reach the handoff zone, a marker sweeps across a bar — press Space (or the PASS button) the instant it's inside the green zone to hand the baton cleanly to your AI teammate. A late, early, or missed pass costs your team time. Your teammates run the rest of the leg automatically — race again and again to beat your best total team time against rival AI teams!
