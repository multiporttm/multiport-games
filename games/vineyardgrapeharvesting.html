Vineyard Grape Harvesting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Row 1/6
How to play: Move your picker with the Arrow Keys/A D, or drag/tap on mobile, then press Space, click, or tap a cluster to snip it. Snip deep-purple ripe clusters into your basket before they shrivel and rot. Leave pale-green unripe clusters alone — snipping them costs a life. Watch for wasps buzzing the row; touching one also costs a life. Each new row ripens faster and grows denser vines. 3 lives — clear all 6 rows to win!
