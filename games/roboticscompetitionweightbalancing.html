Robotics Competition Weight Balancing
Round 1Lives: ❤️❤️❤️Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag the component weights (battery, motors, arm) left and right along the chassis — with mouse, touch, or by selecting one with Tab and nudging it with Arrow Left / Arrow Right. Watch the balance needle below and get it into the green safe zone, then press Enter or LOCK IN BALANCE. Miss the zone and the robot tips over. Layouts get trickier each round.
