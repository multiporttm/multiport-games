Linocut Design Transferring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Block 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead carries the carbon design around the linoleum block — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your stylus tip inside the orange tolerance tube that follows it. Drift too far and your transfer meter drains; empty it and the line smudges, costing a life. Trace cleanly the whole way to finish the block and move on. The bead speeds up and the tube narrows every block — lose all 3 lives and the design is ruined!
