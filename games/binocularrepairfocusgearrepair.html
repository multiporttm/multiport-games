How to play: A brass focusing pinion spins against a fixed meshing window on the diopter rack. Watch the pointer sweep around the gear and click, tap, or press Space the instant a tooth passes through the lit window to seat it cleanly — this builds your seating streak and nudges the gear train true. Pressing early or late lets the tooth strike the rack edge, chipping it, snapping your streak, and draining your remaining turns; the wheel also speeds up as more teeth are seated, so later teeth demand sharper timing. Seat every tooth around the wheel before your turns run out to true the focus gear and move on to the next, faster wheel.
