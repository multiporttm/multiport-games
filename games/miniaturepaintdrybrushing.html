Miniature Paint Drybrushing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Miniature 1
Sweep speed — stay in the golden safe zone
How to play: Hold the brush down (mouse/finger down, or hold Space) and lightly sweep it over the miniature's raised texture areas (highlighted in gold) with the mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys. The gauge above shows your sweep speed — keep the needle inside the golden safe zone for a light, controlled pass. Sweep too slowly and you load on too much paint, flattening the texture and costing a life; sweep too fast and nothing catches, leaving dull, uncovered texture. Lightly dust the whole textured area before the paint tacks up to advance — lose all 3 lives and the texture is ruined. Each round brings a more textured miniature and a narrower safe zone.
