Cheese Affinage Cellar
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
A new cheese wheel waits above the cellar — its color shows which zone it needs. Press 1/2/3 (or tap a zone) to place it in Cave A, B, or C before its timer runs out. Wheels ripen only in their correct zone; click a glowing (fully ripe) wheel — or press Enter — to collect it for points. Wrong-zone wheels and overripe wheels you don't collect in time spoil and cost a life. Zones fill up and wheels arrive faster every level.
