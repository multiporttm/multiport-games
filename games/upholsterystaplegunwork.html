Upholstery Staple Gun Work
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Frame 1
How to play: Follow the glowing target around the frame edge — it marks the next staple point in the correct working sequence. A needle sweeps back and forth across the tension gauge below it; press Space, click, or tap STAPLE the instant the needle is in the green zone for a perfect, taut staple. Firing while it's in the yellow is an okay but slightly loose staple; firing in the red — or missing the window — puckers the fabric and costs a life. Finish every point on the frame to trim the excess and move to the next, tougher frame. Lose all 3 lives and the fabric is ruined!
