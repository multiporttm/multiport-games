Ballet Costume Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Costume 1/3
How to play: Pin points glow one at a time on the costume, each shrinking inside a closing ring. Click or tap the pin point before the ring collapses — or move the cursor with the Arrow Keys and press Space to pin it. Pin it early for a better fit score; let the ring close or click the wrong spot and the pin pricks the dancer, costing a life. Finish every pin on a costume to move on to a more elaborate one with more pins and faster timing. Lose all 3 lives and the costume doesn't fit!
