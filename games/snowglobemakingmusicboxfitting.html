Drag the music box mechanism onto the marked slot shown in the diagram. Use Arrow Keys to nudge it and Q/E (or the rotate buttons) to rotate it until the spindle lines up exactly with the target dot. Press Space or click Drop to lock it in — a perfect, gap-free fit advances you to a trickier shape!
