Puppet Costume Design
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Brief 1
How to play: Read the character brief at the top, then click or tap the matching fabric piece, color swatch, and accessory from the tray below, and click the puppet's head, torso, or legs slot to dress it. Use Arrow Keys + Enter if you prefer the keyboard. Confirm the outfit once all three slots look right — get it wrong and you lose a life. Briefs get pickier and the clock shrinks every round — dress enough puppets correctly to win!
