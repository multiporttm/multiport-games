Batik Wax Removal
Round: 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Move the heated iron (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys) over every pale waxed patch to melt and lift the wax, revealing the dye beneath. Hold it steady on a patch to clear it, but don't linger too long once it's clear — the fabric will scorch! Clear every patch before the timer runs out to advance to a round with more patches.
