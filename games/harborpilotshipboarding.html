Harbor Pilot Ship Boarding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sea State 1
How to play: Both boats bob on the swell. Use ↑ / ↓ (or drag) to raise or lower your pilot boat until it lines up with the ship's boarding platform, then press Space / tap / Board! at the right moment to grab the ladder. Once aboard, press Space / tap / Climb! in rhythm as the timing marker sweeps through the green zone to climb rung by rung. Mistime it and you fall — 3 falls ends your shift. Sea state gets rougher with every ship.
