Click/tap a frame, then hold and drag it out slowly (not too fast, or you'll agitate the bees!). Once pulled, inspect the comb: press Left Arrow / H to flag a problem (disease, mites or low stores) or Right Arrow / K if it's healthy. You can also click the on-screen buttons. Clear every hive before you run out of lives!
