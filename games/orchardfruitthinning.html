Orchard Fruit Thinning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Row 1
How to play: Click or tap a fruit (or move the cursor with Arrow keys and pluck with Space) to pull it off the branch. Thin every cluster down to exactly one fruit before the timer runs out. Strip a whole cluster bare and you lose a life. Clear a row to move on to a more crowded one. Press Space or tap to begin.
