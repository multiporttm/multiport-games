How to play: Entries for the soap carving competition wait under covered pedestals before they can be set on the display table. Click or tap two pedestals — or move the highlight with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space — to lift their covers and see the carved bars underneath. If both bars show the same design, that pair is pinned to the table and scores points. If they differ, both covers drop again and a move is spent from your limited display time. Pair up every entry on the table before your moves run out to finish the display and move on to a bigger table with more entries.
