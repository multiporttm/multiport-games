Medal Engraving Polishing
Round 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Move the polishing wheel (mouse, finger, or arrow keys) over every dull patch on the medal to buff it to a shine before time runs out. Don't linger too long in one spot — the metal will overheat and scratch, costing you the round. Clear every medal each round for a bigger, more intricate medal next!
