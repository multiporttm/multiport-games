Leather Tanning Fleshing
Hide: 1 Time: 0s Fleshed: 0% Tears: 0 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag the fleshing knife across the hide (or use Arrow keys to move the blade and hold Space to scrape) to strip away every patch of flesh and fat before the timer runs out. Clear at least 90% to pass inspection — but scraping the same spot too many times tears the hide and costs you points, so keep the blade moving!
