Astronaut waiting to exit. Close inner hatch, open vent valve to depressurize, then open outer hatch.
Use the buttons or keys I / O / V / P to toggle the Inner Hatch, Outer Hatch, Vent valve and Pressurize valve. To send an astronaut out: close the inner hatch, vent the airlock down to zero pressure, then open the outer hatch. To bring one back in: close the outer hatch, pressurize back to full, then open the inner hatch. Opening a hatch at the wrong pressure costs a life — cycle as many astronauts as you can before the shift timer runs out or you run out of lives.
