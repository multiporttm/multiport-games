Taxidermy Mount Posing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Pose 1/5
How to play: A dashed ghost shows the target pose. Drag any joint handle (mouse/finger) to rotate that limb, or press a number key to select a joint and rotate it with the Arrow Keys. Each joint turns green when it is close to the target. Hold every joint in the green zone at once to fill the pose meter and lock the mount in place before time runs out. Later poses add more joints, tighter tolerance, and less time — lose all 3 lives and the mount collapses!
