Rare Book Gilding Technique
Cover 1 Phase: Gesso Score: 0 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Click or tab through each node to lay gesso along the tooled border.
Three phases per cover: click/tap each glowing node along the tooled border in order to lay gesso (keyboard: Tab/Arrow keys to move focus, Space to apply). Once gesso is complete, press Lay Leaf to apply gold leaf. Then burnish each section by pressing Space or clicking the gauge exactly when the needle sweeps through the green zone — hit it cleanly or the leaf flakes and costs a life. Borders get longer and the burnishing window narrower each round.
