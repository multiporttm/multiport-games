Round 1
 
Press and hold Space, Up Arrow, or press-and-hold on the glove with your mouse/finger to smooth the lining and raise the coverage needle. Release to let it settle. Bring the needle into the green safe zone — not a gap below it, not a wrinkle above it — then press Enter or click Lock Lining to fit it. The safe zone narrows on more intricate gloves each round. Three bad fits or timeouts and it's game over!
