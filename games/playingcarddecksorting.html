—
A shuffled pile lands on your station. Pick the cards back into brand-new-deck order — each suit in order from Ace up to King — by clicking, tapping, or moving focus with the Arrow Keys and confirming with Enter or Space. The Next card needed is always shown. Picking the wrong card costs a life; running out the quality-check timer costs one too. The pile gets bigger and the mix of suits trickier each level.
