Your falcon flies in loops around the field, drifting toward whatever distraction (crow, drifting leaf, rival hawk) is nearest. Press Space or Click/Tap to whistle — each whistle pulls the falcon's heading toward your glove in the corner. Keep whistling at good moments to steer it all the way onto the glove's landing circle before the interest meter empties. Distractions multiply and the glove zone shrinks every round.
