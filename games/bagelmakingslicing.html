Bagel Making Slicing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bagel 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead travels the guide line through the center of the bagel — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your knife tip inside the golden tolerance tube around it. Wander too far and your grip meter drains; empty it and the slice is ruined, costing a life. Stay close the whole way across to finish the slice and move to the next bagel. The line curves more and the tube narrows every bagel — lose all 3 lives and the rack is ruined!
