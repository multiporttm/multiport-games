Click/tap a signal (or press 1, 2, 3) to cycle it through Stop (red) → Caution (yellow) → Clear (green). Only one train may occupy the junction at a time — set signals to Clear only when the junction is empty, or two trains will collide! Let as many trains through safely as you can.
