Move with arrows or drag, hold SCRAPE or Space on a rough patch, and let go once it's pale.
How to play: The hide is divided into rough patches. Move the crescent knife with the arrow keys, a mouse drag, or a touch drag, then hold Space, click, or the SCRAPE button to smooth whichever patch the knife sits over. Watch the patch lighten as it smooths and let go as soon as it turns pale; keep scraping after that and the knife cuts clean through, opening a hole that counts against you. Smooth enough of the sheet before time runs out to finish it and move on to a bigger, thinner sheet.
