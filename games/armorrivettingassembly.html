Rivetting: Click/tap a rivet in the tray (or press its number key), then click/tap the matching punched hole on the plates (size must match) to set it. A wrong-size rivet costs a life. Flex test: once every joint is riveted, the joint swings back and forth — press Space or tap the plate the moment the needle is inside the green safe zone, {n} times, to pass.
