How to play: Each recipe card shows the exact drop ratio of food coloring needed for the target candy color. Click the colored drop buttons to add drops to the hot syrup kettle, matching the ratio exactly — too many or too few drops of any color will spoil the batch. When your batch color looks right, click Pour Batch to check it. Nail trickier target colors each round to keep your candy streak going!
