Alternate LEFT and RIGHT in a steady rhythm to press cider!
Press the LEFT and RIGHT handles one after another in a steady, evenly-paced rhythm (use the A/D or Left/Right arrow keys, or tap the buttons) to operate the apple press. Keeping each stroke inside the green rhythm zone fills the pressing meter efficiently — button-mashing or uneven timing wastes effort. Fill the meter fully to score a batch of cider; each new batch shrinks the ideal rhythm window, so stay smooth and consistent!
