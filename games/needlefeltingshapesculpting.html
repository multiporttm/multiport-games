Needle Felting Shape Sculpting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Shape 1
A puffy wool blob is divided into wedges, each puffed out past the dashed target silhouette. Use ← / → or tap a wedge to select it, then press Space or 🪡 Jab to compress it inward. Stop each wedge exactly inside its target band — jab a wedge too many times and you felt it too thin, snapping a fiber and costing a life. Bring every wedge into its band to finish the sculpture!
