Yurt Making Smoke Hole Fitting

Balance atop the roof ring and ease the smoke flap cord taut only when the needle rests in the green arc.

How to play: A needle sweeps around the crown dial. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter when it sits inside the green arc to fit the smoke-hole flap correctly. A bad fit drains your focus meter — run out and the flap jams, ending the round. Five good fits advance you a round, where the needle spins faster and the target arc shrinks. Fit as many as you can for the highest score!
