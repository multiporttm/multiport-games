THICKNESS
Click Flood Layer (or press Space/tap the rink) to spray a thin coat of water over the bare concrete. Each layer needs to freeze before you can add the next — watch the countdown ring. The depth gauge shows total ice thickness building toward the yellow target line: stop a layer's freeze early (click again mid-freeze) and it stays slushy and weak; keep flooding after the target is reached and the rink cracks from the weight. Land the freeze right on the target line to finish a perfect rink — more layers and a tighter target window each round!
