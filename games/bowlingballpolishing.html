Bowling Ball Polishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Coverstock 1
How to play: Hold down Space, click, or touch to buff the spinning ball and raise the shine meter, watching for the glowing target band. Release right while the meter is inside the band to polish it perfectly. Let go too early and it's still dull; hold too long and it's over-polished — both cost a life. The band shrinks, the buffer speeds up, and it starts drifting on tougher coverstocks.
