Click or tap the ceiling pulleys in the glowing order to thread the chain (arrow keys plus Space also work), then press and hold Wind Crank to hoist the weight — release before the tension gauge maxes out or the chain snaps.
Every grandfather clock runs on falling weights, and rigging them correctly is the final test before the movement can run. A weight for the time, strike, or chime train hangs below a row of ceiling pulleys, and a sequence of pulleys glows briefly to show the order the drive chain must be threaded through. Click or tap the pulleys in that order, or move a cursor with the arrow keys and confirm with Space, to thread the chain correctly, then press and hold the Wind Crank button to hoist the weight toward the clock's seat board. Watch the tension gauge as you wind — release the crank in the safe green zone to seat the weight perfectly, but hold too long and the overstrained chain snaps, costing you chain supply and the attempt. Later weights add more pulleys to the threading sequence and tighten the safe winding zone, so a careful memory for the thread order and a gentle hand on the crank will see every weight safely hoisted before your chain supply is exhausted.
