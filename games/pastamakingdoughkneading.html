Click/tap or press Space to push the kneading hand, release to let it ease back. Keep the elasticity needle inside the glowing green safe zone as long as you can each batch — push too much and you over-knead (tough dough), let it fall too far and you under-knead (crumbly dough). Survive the timer to advance to a bigger batch!
