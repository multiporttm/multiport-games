Pick a bobbin color, then weave it into the matching squares
ReferenceYour weaving
Choose a weft bobbin color from the palette, then click/tap (or move the cursor with Arrow Keys and place with Space/Enter) each square of your loom on the right to pass that bobbin through the correct spot, matching the reference tapestry on the left. Placing the wrong color frays a thread and costs a life. Finish the design to move to a bigger, more detailed tapestry!
