Press and drag along the glowing line
Press/click and hold on the start of the glowing guide line, then drag the hot tip along the path. Keep your speed inside the green zone on the heat gauge — too slow scorches the wood too dark (over-burn), too fast leaves it too pale (under-burn). Trace the whole design accurately and evenly to score well. Each round adds a more intricate design.
