Incense Making Essential Oil Dosing
Batch: 1 Bowls Left: 6 Score: 0 Best: 0
Hold SPACE (or press and hold the dropper) to squeeze the bulb and fill the dose gauge. Release when the fill level is inside the gold target band to dose the bowl perfectly.
Essential oil must be dosed precisely into each bowl of incense paste — too little and the scent is weak, too much and it overpowers the wood. Hold SPACE or press and hold the dropper bulb (on the canvas or the Hold to Squeeze button) to let the dose gauge rise; release the moment it sits inside the gold target band to perfect the dose. A perfect center release scores the most; releasing outside the band ruins the bowl. Dose enough bowls correctly before you run out to move on to a trickier batch with a narrower target!
