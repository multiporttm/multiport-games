💿 Turntable Making Platter Making
A raw platter is spinning off-balance on the calibration rig. Tap the rim exactly when the gate lines up with the pulsing target to clamp a counterweight and smooth the wobble.
Watch the glowing target arc on the spinning platter's rim. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter exactly when the white gate marker sweeps into the target to clamp a counterweight for a clean hit. Good hits steady the platter and speed up the spin; missed taps increase wobble and drain the Platter Balance meter — let it empty and the platter shatters.
