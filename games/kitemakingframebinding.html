Press and drag in a circular motion around the center spool (mouse or touch) to wind the binding thread around the kite's crossed spars. Watch your winding speed on the Binding Strength meter: an even, steady pace keeps it healthy, winding too fast snaps the thread and drains it fast, and stopping lets it sag. Finish the wind count before the thread snaps to bind the joint and move to the next one.
