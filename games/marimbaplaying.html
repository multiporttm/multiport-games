Marimba Playing

Hit the bars in rhythm as notes reach the line.

Notes fall toward the golden hit-line above the wooden bars. Press keys 1-5 (or tap the matching bar) exactly as a note crosses the line. Pink/cyan notes spanning two bars are chord hits — hit both bars at the same moment. Timing accuracy and streaks raise your score — tempo and chords increase each round.
