Beach Lifeguard Tower Watch
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Many swimmers splash and bob normally, but one is in real trouble — drifting quietly out past the others instead of bobbing in place. Use Left/Right Arrow to move the scanning cursor between swimmers (or just tap/click a swimmer directly), then press Space or tap again to sound the whistle on whoever is selected. Pick right before the timer runs out! Each round adds more swimmers and less time.
