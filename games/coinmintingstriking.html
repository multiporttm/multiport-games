Coin Minting Striking
Batch 1/6Score: 0Hi: 0
 
How to play: Hold Space, click, or press and hold to build up press force on the vertical gauge. Release while the needle is inside the glowing safe band to strike a perfect coin. Release too early (under-strike) and the detail is weak; hold too long past the band (over-strike) and you crack the die. Strike a full batch of coins to advance — the safe band narrows and the force builds faster on later batches.
