Cheese Vat Stirring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Vat 1
How to play: Move your mouse/finger in a steady circle around the vat (or hold Left/Right Arrow to turn) to drag the paddle through the curds. Keep your stirring speed in the green zone shown on the speed dial — stir too slowly and curds clump, stir too fast and the whey overheats. Clumping and heat meters above the vat fill up when you drift out of range; max out either one and you lose a life. Bigger vats and longer sessions follow each round — lose all 3 lives and the batch is spoiled!
