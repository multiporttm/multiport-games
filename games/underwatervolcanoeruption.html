Underwater Volcano Eruption
Distance: 0m Best: 0m Hull: 3
How to play: Use Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag/tap, to steer your submersible away from the erupting volcano at the left. Dodge rising superheated plumes, flying rock debris, and the lava flow spreading across the bottom. Each hit damages your hull — three hits and it's over. Survive and keep moving right toward safe open water for the highest distance!
