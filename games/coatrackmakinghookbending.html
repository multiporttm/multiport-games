Drag the lever around the pivot to rotate the rod into the dashed target angle, then confirm the bend. Precise bends keep the jig in good shape; sloppy ones wear it down.
Drag (mouse or finger) anywhere on the canvas to rotate the solid rod lever around its pivot — or use the Left/Right arrow keys. Accurate bends raise your score and speed things up; missing the angle by too much damages the bending jig's Condition meter. Let it break down and the line stops.
