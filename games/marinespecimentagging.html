Marine Specimen Tagging
Score: 0 Round: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Each specimen swims across the tank with a glowing green tag spot and a red sensitive spot marked on its body. Click/tap and drag the tag from the tray onto the green spot to tag it safely, or press Arrow Keys to move the cursor and Enter/Space to place the tag. Hitting the red spot injures the specimen and costs a life; letting one swim off-screen untagged also costs a life. Tag the whole catch each round to advance — specimens get faster and spots get smaller as you go. Three lives.
