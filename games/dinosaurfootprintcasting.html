Dinosaur Footprint Casting
Casts: 1
Lives: ❤❤❤
Hi: 0
How to play: A needle sweeps back and forth across the meter for each step of casting a fossil footprint — Mix Plaster, Pour, then Lift. Press Space, Click, or Tap to lock the needle while it's inside the green zone. The closer to center, the better the cast. Three solid locks finish a cast; miss the zone badly too many times and you lose a life. Casts get trickier (faster needle, tighter zone) as your score grows.
