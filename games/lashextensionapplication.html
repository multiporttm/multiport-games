How to play: A glowing bead carries a fresh-dipped lash from the adhesive dish toward the next natural lash — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your applicator tip on the bead the whole way. Finish all twenty lashes on both eyes before you run out of lives!
