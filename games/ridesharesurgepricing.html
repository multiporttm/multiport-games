How to play: Each zone shows rider demand (orange) vs driver supply (blue). Select a zone (1-4 keys, or ◀▶) and raise/lower its surge multiplier (Up/Down arrows, or +/− buttons) to pull the two bars together. Raising price attracts more drivers but cools demand; lowering it does the opposite. Let a zone's gap stay critical too long and you lose a life — survive and score points as zones balance, with demand swinging faster each level.
