SPEED
Drive with Left/Right Arrows or A/D (or drag left/right on mobile). Brake with Down Arrow/S or tap the brake side of the screen. As you approach a bus stop, ease your speed into the yellow zone on the gauge and come to a full stop right at the sign — then hold still while the stop-arm swings out and students cross. Drive off too soon or slam on the brakes too hard and it's unsafe! Complete every stop on the route before time runs out to advance to a busier route.
