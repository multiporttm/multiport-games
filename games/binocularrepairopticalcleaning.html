Drag across the hazy smudge to wipe it clean — steer around the grit flecks.
How to play: A smudge-covered lens fills the canvas, with a few sharp grit flecks hidden in the haze. Click and drag, touch and drag, or hold Space while steering with the arrow keys to drag the cloth across the smudge and wipe it away cell by cell, filling your cleaning progress and building a wipe streak. Dragging over a grit fleck scratches the glass instead, costing polish strength and breaking your streak, so plan a path around the flecks. Clear every cell of smudge before your polish strength runs out to finish the lens and move on to a dirtier one.
