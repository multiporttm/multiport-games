Bladesmithing Grinding
Blade: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Score: 0 Hi: 0
How to play: Use Up/Down arrows, the on-screen buttons, or drag up/down on the belt, to steer the grind angle marker into the green safe zone on the angle gauge and hold it there as the blade sweeps across the belt grinder. Drifting outside the zone grinds too deep and heats up the steel — if the heat meter fills completely, the temper is ruined. Finish the whole pass with heat under control to move on to a longer, trickier blade.
