Your caravan gathers at the edge of the dunes, ready to depart.
How to play: Guide your caravan of camels across the desert to the city. Each day choose the Risky Shortcut (covers more ground but higher chance of sandstorms and bandits), the Safe Trail (slower but steadier), or Rest & Ration at your current spot to recover water and food. Keep every camel's water and food above zero — a thirsty or starving camel loses health and can die, taking its carried goods with it. Reach the city with as many goods intact as possible for your final score.
