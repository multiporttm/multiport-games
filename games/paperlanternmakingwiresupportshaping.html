Hold the pliers and drag in/out to match the glowing target radius around the hoop.
How to play: A bare wire hoop rotates slowly on the bench while a faint target curve shows the bulge each segment needs to hold the lantern's shape. Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) then drag up and down, or use the Up/Down arrow keys, to pull the pliers in and out and bend the current segment to match the target radius. Bending close to the target fills your temper gauge and strengthens the frame, bending past it kinks the wire and wastes temper fast, and drifting far from target leaves the segment slack. Shape every segment before the wire's temper runs out to complete a sturdy frame and move on to a bigger hoop with more segments.
