How to play: A faint ghost outline on the rail shows where the new cushion belongs. Match its angle and position by dragging the sliders, clicking + / −, or pressing Tab then Left/Right arrows to adjust the selected dial. When it looks lined up, press Glue & Test (or Enter) to fire a test ball — a true rebound bounces straight back, a bad glue job sends it off at the wrong angle and costs a life. Straight rails give way to angled corner cushions, with tighter tolerances and less time on every new section.
