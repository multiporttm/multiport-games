Spinnaker Hoisting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Mark 1
How to play: After rounding each mark, pull the halyard with Space, Up Arrow, or a Tap/Click in a steady rhythm. Pull again too fast and the spinnaker wraps the headstay; wait too long between pulls and it collapses. Keep a smooth, even beat to hoist it clean. Each mark the rhythm window gets tighter — lose all 3 lives and the race is over!
