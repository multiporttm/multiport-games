Blade Sharpening Whetstone
Score: 0 Grit: Coarse Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Angle: Press Up/Down Arrow to tilt the blade, or drag up/down on the stone, until the angle needle sits inside the glowing target band. Stroke: press Space or release your drag/tap to draw the blade across the stone — strokes inside the band sharpen the edge, strokes outside introduce a flaw. Three lives, and the target band drifts after every stroke and narrows through coarser to finer grits and higher levels.
