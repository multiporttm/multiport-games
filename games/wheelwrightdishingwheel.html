Wheelwright Dishing the Wheel
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Wheel 1
The flat wheel rests dish-side up on the bending blocks, mallet in hand. A marker sweeps back and forth along the gauge above the wheel, showing where your next strike will land. Press Space, tap, or click 🔨 Strike the instant the marker is inside the glowing target zone to drive that spoke into a shallow, even cone. Strike too early or too late and the spoke bends too much or too little, racking the wheel out of true — three bad strikes and the frame is ruined! Dish every spoke on a wheel to move on to a wheel with a narrower, faster-moving sweet spot.
