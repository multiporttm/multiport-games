Nail Art Stenciling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Nail 1/10
How to play: Watch the reference stencil flash its color order, then reproduce it by clicking/tapping the paint swatches (or pressing number keys 1–6) in that exact order before the drying timer runs out. A wrong color or running out of time costs a life. Each nail adds another color to the sequence and gives you less drying time — lose all 3 lives and the set is ruined!
