Epoxy Resin Curing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Shelf 1
Each resin piece on the shelf has a cure gauge and a target temperature that shifts as it cures. Use Up/Down Arrows (or the Piece buttons) to select a piece, and Left/Right Arrows (or Cooler/Warmer) to slide it between the five temperature stations. Keep every piece near its glowing ideal station so its stress stays low — if stress maxes out, it cracks! Fully cure every piece before the humidity timer runs out. More pieces join the shelf each round.
