Blanks: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Click or tap the gray waste wood to swing the mallet and chisel it away from the horse outline.
How to play: A square basswood blank fills the canvas with a faint outline of a leaping carousel horse traced across it. Click, tap, or press Space while aiming with the arrow keys to swing the mallet at the gray waste wood just outside the outline, chopping it away in chips. Hitting clean waste wood fills your rough-out progress and builds a strike streak; hitting too close to the outline gouges the horse and costs arm strength, and hitting wood that is already cleared wastes a swing and dulls your streak. Clear every waste section before your arm strength runs out to finish roughing out the horse and move on to a trickier blank.
