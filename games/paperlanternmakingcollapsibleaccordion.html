Paper Lantern Making Collapsible Accordion

Press and hold, then release right as the gauge lands in the glowing target band.
How to play: A paper lantern cylinder sits marked with faint crease guides. Press and hold (mouse, finger, Space, or Up arrow) to push the current pleat inward along the gauge, and release right when the needle sits inside the glowing target band so the pleat sets cleanly. Releasing too early or too late leaves the pleat loose and costs paper strength, while holding past the band tears the thin paper outright. Fold every pleat around the lantern before your paper strength runs out to finish a fully collapsible lantern and move on to a taller shade with more pleats.
