Chess Set Felt Lining
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Piece: 1/5 Coverage: 0% Time: 20 ♥♥♥
Move the cursor with Arrow Keys and press Space to glue felt, or just click/drag or tap/drag across the piece's base. Cover every highlighted cell with felt before the glue sets — but stay inside the outline: gluing a cell outside the piece wastes felt and costs a life. Clear the base completely to move to the next piece; the shapes get bigger and the clock gets shorter as you go.
