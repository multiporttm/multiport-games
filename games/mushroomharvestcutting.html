Mushroom Harvest Cutting
Score: 0 Cluster: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
The knife edge glides up and down the glowing mushroom's stem. Press Space (or tap/click) the instant it's inside the narrow green cut zone right at the base to harvest it cleanly. Cut too high and you leave stem behind; cut too low and you gouge the mycelium bed — either way costs a life. Clear the whole cluster before the clock runs out; the clusters get denser and the cut zone gets tighter each round!
