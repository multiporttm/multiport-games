Skateboard Deck Sanding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Deck 1/8
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to sweep the sanding block over every rough brown patch on the deck blank before the timer runs out. Each patch needs a moment of sanding to turn smooth — but don't linger in one spot too long or you'll gouge the wood and lose a life. Clear every patch to finish the deck. Decks get bigger and rougher each round — lose all 3 lives and sanding stops for good!
