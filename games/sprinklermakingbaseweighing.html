Hold (mouse, finger, or Space) to pour ballast into the sprinkler base. Release when the weight needle lands inside the green target zone — too little or too much and the scale tips!
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space/Enter) to pour weighted ballast into the sprinkler base, filling the weight needle up the scale. Release right when the needle sits inside the green target zone to lock in a perfectly balanced base. Releasing too early (underweight) or too late (overweight and tipping) costs Scale Stability — let it run out and the line stops. Each round the target zone gets narrower and the fill pours faster.
