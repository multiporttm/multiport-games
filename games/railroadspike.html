Railroad Spike
Spike: 1/8Bends: 0Train: 60sHi: 0 ties
How to play: A hammer swings back and forth above the spike. Press Space / click / tap exactly when the hammer's target ring lines up with the sweet spot to strike the spike squarely. Perfect hits drive it in fast; sloppy timing drives it in slowly; missing the window badly bends the spike (costly — it needs extra hits to fix). Drive all 8 spikes in a section before the supply train arrives to advance to the next, faster section.
