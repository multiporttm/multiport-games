Upholstery Fabric Measuring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bolt 1
How to play: Each pattern piece in the tray must be laid on the fabric bolt below. Click a piece to select it, then click/tap the fabric to drop it there (or use Arrow Keys to move the ghost outline, R to rotate, Enter/Space to place). Keep the piece upright so its grain runs with the fabric's vertical grain lines for full value, and try to land its top edge on a pattern-repeat stripe for a bonus. Overlapping another piece or hanging off the bolt wastes a measuring attempt and costs a life. Clear every piece to roll out a new, tighter bolt — leftover empty fabric counts against your score. Lose all 3 lives and the bolt is ruined!
