Quilting Binding Folding
Score: 0 Round: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Press Space or Tap right when the swinging needle sits inside the green safe zone on the fold-width gauge to fold and press the next bit of binding evenly. Miss the zone and the binding twists — costing a life. Fold all the way around the edge before the edge runs out, then do it again on a longer edge, with a faster swing and a tighter zone.
