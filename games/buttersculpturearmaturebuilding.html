Butter Sculpture Armature Building
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Frame 1
A wire-and-wood armature sits in the vise, waiting to hold the butter firm under the hot show lights. Press Space, tap, or click 🔧 Bend to start bending the joint, and hold (press and hold, or tap and hold) to keep bending it toward the target angle — release right as the gauge needle reaches the locking band, no sooner, no later. Bend too little and the joint stays loose and wobbly; bend too far and the wire snaps clean — three snapped joints and the frame is scrapped! Finish every joint in a frame to move to a trickier frame with a narrower locking band.
