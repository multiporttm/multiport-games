Coat Evenness
Drag the varnish brush back and forth over the frame and lift it the instant the stroke marker glides through the glowing green zone, laying down one smooth, even coat. A clean stroke raises evenness and your score; lifting too early or late leaves drips or bare patches and drains your evenness meter. Land 5 clean strokes to finish a coat and speed up the next one. Tap/click the brush, or press Space/Enter, at the right instant. Empty your evenness meter and the finish is ruined — game over.
