A blank die face is clamped under the engraving burin, and the needle on the pressure gauge keeps swinging between too light and too heavy as the tool rides the grain of the material. Use the Left/Right arrow keys or tap ◁ Ease Up / Press Down ▷ to nudge the burin's pressure, watching the needle sweep across the gauge, and press Space, tap, or click ✍️ Strike the instant the needle swings into the glowing target band to cut a crisp, perfectly even pip into the face. Strike too light or too heavy and the pip comes out shallow or blown out, costing you a life — three ruined pips and the die is scrapped! Finish every pip on a face to move on to a trickier face with a narrower target band.
