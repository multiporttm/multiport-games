Man Overboard Rescue
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Rescue 1
How to play: When someone falls overboard, press Space (or GRAB) to call the alert and mark the position. Steer with Left/Right Arrows (or the side buttons) through the glowing checkpoints in order — that's the turning sequence that loops you back onto the person. Once you reach them, press Space/GRAB again while close and slow to retrieve them before the current carries them off. The sequence gets longer and the current stronger with every rescue — lose all 3 lives and the rescue fails!
