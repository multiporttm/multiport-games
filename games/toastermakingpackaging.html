📦 Toaster Making Packaging
Boxed toasters roll down the line in three colours. Tap the matching shipping crate (or press 1 / 2 / 3) the instant a toaster crosses the drop line.
Toasters roll along the conveyor belt, each tagged with a coloured label. When one crosses the glowing drop line, tap (or click) the crate below that matches its colour — or press 1, 2 or 3 for the left, middle and right crates. Correct sorts raise your score and speed up the belt; wrong crates or toasters that roll off the end drain your Shipping Accuracy meter — let it empty and the line is shut down.
