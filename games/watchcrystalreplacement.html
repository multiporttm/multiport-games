Watch Crystal Replacement
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
A glowing press point appears around the crystal's rim — click/tap it (or press Space while it's highlighted) exactly when the pressure gauge needle is in the green zone to seat it evenly. Pressing too early, too late, or missing a point cracks the crystal and costs a life. Seat every point around the rim to finish the case. You have 3 lives — rims get more points and the green zone narrows each case.
