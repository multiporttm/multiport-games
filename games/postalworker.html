Click a package, then click its matching mailbox

Click a package on the belt, then click the matching colored mailbox (shown by the letter code) to sort it before it reaches the end. You can also press 1-4 on the keyboard to sort the selected package. The belt speeds up over time — 8 misses ends your shift!
