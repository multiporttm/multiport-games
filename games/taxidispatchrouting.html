Taxi Dispatch Routing
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Click/tap a flashing ride call to select it, then click/tap a free (blue) cab to dispatch it — the closer the cab, the more points you earn. Or press a number key (1-9) to select a call and a letter key (A-F) to dispatch that cab. Calls that time out cost a life. Three lives — calls arrive faster and your fleet grows every level.
