🪵 Drum Making Shell Sanding

Time each sanding pass to the green zone. 5 passes per round — keep your smoothness meter full!

Drag the sandpaper block across the shell at exactly the right moment: tap, click, or press Space/Enter when the marker sweeps into the green sweet zone of the sanding gauge. A clean pass smooths the grain; a mistimed pass leaves rough patches and drains your Smoothness meter. Finish enough passes to clear the round and the grit gets finer (and faster) each time.
