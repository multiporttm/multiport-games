Gold Panning
Gold: 0Time: 60Hi: 0
How to play: Click/tap and drag in a circular motion to swirl the pan. Swirling washes lightweight gravel and sand over the rim while heavier gold flecks (yellow) sink and stay put. Swirl too hard or tilt too far and gold spills out too! Once the gravel clears, collected gold is banked and a fresh pan load arrives. Bank as much gold as possible before time runs out.
