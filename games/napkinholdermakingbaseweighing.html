⚖️ Napkin Holder Making Base Weighing
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
Press and hold (mouse or finger) to pour molten metal onto the scale pan; release to stop. Stop as close as you can to the glowing target weight to cast a perfectly balanced napkin holder base. Overpour too far past the target too many times and the mold overflows — game over. Each round narrows the safe margin and asks for a different target weight.
