Incoming requests (book them before the timer runs out!)
How to play: Each request needs a block of consecutive free time slots in one room. Click (or tap) a request, then click the slot where its block should start — any room with enough open space works, but a slot that is already booked will reject the request. Keyboard: Arrow keys move the grid cursor, Tab cycles the selected request, Enter books it at the cursor. Book correctly for points; a request that times out or an overlap costs a life. You have 3 lives — survive all 6 queues as exam season brings bigger, faster-arriving requests!
