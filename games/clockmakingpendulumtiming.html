Clockmaking Pendulum Timing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Clock 1/6
How to play: The metronome dot on the left flashes the target tempo. Drag the bob on the pendulum rod (mouse or touch), or use ↑ / ↓ Arrow Keys or the Raise / Lower buttons, to change the pendulum's length until its swing speed matches — watch the tempo needle settle into the glowing safe zone. Air drag and workshop drafts keep shifting the timing, so you must keep correcting to hold the match until the progress bar fills. Drift too far out for too long and you lose a life. Every new clock shrinks the safe zone and strengthens the drift!
