Turning Precision
Mount a post on the lathe and press the chisel home right as the marker sweeps through the glowing green zone to carve a clean, even taper. A clean pass builds your turning precision and your score; a bad cut gouges the wood and drains your precision meter. Land 5 clean passes to finish a round and move the lathe faster for the next one. Tap/click the lever, or press Space/Enter, at the right instant. Empty your precision meter and the post splits — game over.
