Harbor Pilot Tugboat Docking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: Guide the big cargo ship into the glowing berth gap in the pier. Use ↑ / ↓ (or drag on the water) to steer the tugboats and line up with the gap, and ← / → to control forward thrust. Arrive inside the gap slow to dock gently — hit the pier walls, another ship, or arrive too fast and you lose a life. 3 crashes ends your shift. Berths get narrower and the safe docking speed gets stricter with every ship you bring in.
