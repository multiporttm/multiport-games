Your Diorama

Pick an element from the tray, then click a matching spot in your diorama.
How to play: Compare the Reference Scene to your empty Diorama grid. Click an element in the supply tray, then click the cell in your grid at the matching position — get it right and it's placed; get it wrong and the piece bounces back to the tray. Recreate the whole scene before the timer runs out to move to the next diorama. Running out of time costs a life; run out of lives and it's game over. Each new diorama has a bigger grid and more elements.
