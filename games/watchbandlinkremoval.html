Watch Band Link Removal
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Band 1/6
How to play: Use the Left/Right Arrow keys or the Select Left/Select Right buttons to pick which end link of the band to work on — only end links are accessible. Watch the pin pusher gauge: a marker slides back and forth. Press Space, click Push Pin, or tap the canvas the instant the marker is inside the glowing tolerance zone to pop the pin out cleanly and remove that link. Miss and you bend the pin, costing a life. Shrink the band to the target length shown to finish — six bands await, each with more links and a tighter pin tolerance.
