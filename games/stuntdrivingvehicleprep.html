Stunt Driving Vehicle Prep
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Prep 1/10
Click the real safety steps in order — avoid the decoys!
How to play: Every round scrambles a checklist of prep steps on screen, each tagged with a number key. Click the steps (or press their number key) in the correct real-world order — Roll Cage Bolts, Harness, Window Net, Kill Switch, Fire Suppression, Helmet, Radio Check, Fuel Cell Seal — before the timer runs out. Some tiles are decoy steps that do not belong on a real checklist; clicking one or letting the timer hit zero costs a life. More steps, more decoys, and less time appear every round. Survive all 3 lives through 10 rounds to clear the whole prep crew!
