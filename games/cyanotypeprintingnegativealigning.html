Cyanotype Printing Negative Aligning
Printed: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Drag or nudge the negative onto the registration marks and square up its angle before exposure finishes.
How to play: A sheet of cyanotype-coated paper sits under glass with a transparency negative resting loosely on top of it, and the UV exposure lamp is already counting down. Drag the negative with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with the arrow keys, A/D (and up/down or W/S to move vertically), and rotate it a notch at a time with Q/E or the rotate buttons, or the on-screen move and rotate buttons. Line the negative's corner marks up with the glowing registration guides and square its rotation to the target angle shown at the top before the exposure timer reaches zero. The closer you land on position and angle, the sharper and more valuable the finished print; drift too far off-register and the exposure finishes with a ruined, blurry print instead. Align as many negatives as you can before you run out of sensitized paper sheets to post a high score.
