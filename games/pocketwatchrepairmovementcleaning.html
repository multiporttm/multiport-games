Pocket Watch Repair: Movement Cleaning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Movement 1/8
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to sweep the brush over every grimy green part on the movement plate. Each pass of the brush cleans a part a little; clean them all to push the gauge into the glowing safe zone and finish the movement. Once a part is already clean, brushing over it again "over-scrubs" it — do that too much and you will crack a delicate jewel, costing a life! Each new movement packs in more parts, tighter together. Lose all 3 lives and the repair job is ruined.
