Billiards Slate Levelling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Table 1
How to play: This table's floor is uneven, so every slate section sits at a different height. Add or remove shims under each section — with the + / − buttons, by tapping a section, or by selecting one with the Left/Right arrows and pressing Up/Down — to cancel out the bumps. Watch the spirit level's bubble: it drifts toward whichever section sits lowest. Get the bubble inside the green center zone, then press Enter or Lock In Level before the clock runs out. A bad lock-in costs a life, and each new table adds sections, rougher floor dips, and a tighter center zone.
