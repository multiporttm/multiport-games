Puppetry Joint Assembly
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1 — Joint 1/3
How to play: The top diagram shows the order and rotation each pin/socket joint piece must be fitted in. Click (or Tab / Arrow Keys + Enter) the matching numbered socket on the workbench below in that exact order, then click the rotate arrows on the highlighted piece until its orientation matches the diagram before locking it in with Space or a second click. Lock all joints correctly, then watch the range-of-motion test — if a joint was wrong it will pop loose! More joints and trickier rotations appear every round. Lose all 3 lives and the puppet falls apart.
