The iron bar on the anvil glows through colors as it heats. Swing the hammer only while it's glowing bright cherry-red — too early or too late and the metal is ruined.
Watch the glowing bar on the anvil cycle from dull red through bright cherry-orange and back to dark. Swing the hammer (click, tap, or Space/Enter) only while it's glowing bright orange-white — that's forging temperature. Hammering cold or overheated metal cracks it and drains Forge Heat; hit the bar hot and you'll shape it cleanly, scoring more for faster reactions. Empty heat ends the game.
