Ballet Barre Installation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Wall 1/3
How to play: First, press Space, click, or tap to stop the rising gauge inside the glowing barre-height zone. Then bolt the barre to the wall: mounting points glow one at a time with a closing ring — click, tap, or move the cursor with the Arrow Keys and press Space to bolt each one before its ring collapses. Miss the height or a mounting point and you lose a life. Finish a wall section to move on to a trickier layout with more mounting points and tighter timing. Lose all 3 lives and the barre comes loose!
