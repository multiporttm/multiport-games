🎀 Kite Making Tail Attaching
Ribbon bows fall down three lanes. Tap the lane (or press 1/2/3, or Left/Down/Right) exactly as a bow crosses the tie-line to attach it to the tail.
Ribbon bows drop down three lanes toward the tie-line near the bottom. Tap the matching lane on the screen, or press 1/2/3 or the Left/Down/Right arrow keys, the instant a bow crosses the tie-line to attach it to the kite's tail. Clean catches build your combo and score; letting a bow pass uncaught or tapping with nothing there rattles the Tail Balance meter — let it empty and the tail rips off.
