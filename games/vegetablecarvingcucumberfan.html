Vegetable Carving Cucumber Fan
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Fan 1/8
How to play: A glowing bead travels down each guide line marked on the cucumber half, one slit at a time — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your knife tip inside the green safe zone around it. Straying too far drains your precision meter; empty it and the slit cuts all the way through, costing a life. Finish every slit in the row to automatically fan the cucumber open and move to the next round. Every round adds more, closer-together slits — lose all 3 lives and the cucumber falls apart!
