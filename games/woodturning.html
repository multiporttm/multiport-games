Hold and drag along the block to carve
Hold the mouse button (or touch and drag, or hold Space + arrow keys) and move left/right along the spinning block to press the chisel in and carve wood away. Match the golden target profile shown as a guide line — carving past it ruins the piece! Release when you're happy, then finish the piece. More intricate profiles appear each round.
