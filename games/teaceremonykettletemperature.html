Tea Ceremony Kettle Temperature
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Space, Up Arrow, or press and hold on the kettle to fan the charcoal and heat the water — release to let it cool. Settle the needle inside the glowing target zone for the tea being prepared and hold it there to lock it in. Overshoot into the red and you will scald the leaves; drift too cold and the tea will not steep. You have 3 kettles (lives)!
