Batch 1: Washing
Rub and rinse the rice until the water runs clear
How to play: Click/tap or hold Space to rub-rinse the rice bowl until the water runs clear (watch the clarity bar — stop at the right moment, not too early or too late). Then watch the steam gauge rise and press Steam Off (or Space) the instant the needle is inside the green target zone to get a perfectly textured batch. You have 3 lives — a bad rinse or a missed steaming window costs one. Each batch gets trickier. Survive as many batches as you can for the highest score!
