Click (or use Arrow Keys + Enter) to select an ESC wire on the left, then click the matching labeled port on the flight controller to solder it. Correct connections glow green; a mismatched connection sparks and costs a life. Wire every ESC to finish the harness — more motors and trickier scrambled ports appear each round!
