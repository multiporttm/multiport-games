Jigsaw Puzzle Image Printing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Proof 1/10
How to play: Drag an ink slider (or press 1-4 to select a channel and Up/Down Arrows to adjust it) until the press swatch on the right matches the proof swatch on the left. Press Space or tap RUN BATCH to lock it in before the timer runs out. A close enough match prints a clean batch; a bad match or an empty clock ruins it and costs a life. Trickier proof colors — and a fourth Black ink channel — appear as you go. Lose all 3 lives and the press shuts down!
