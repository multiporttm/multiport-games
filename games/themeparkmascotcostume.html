How to play: Use the Arrow Keys or WASD (or drag/tap on the canvas) to waddle your mascot around the meet-and-greet area, dodging carts and puddles. When a guest with a camera appears, walk into them to pose for a photo and score points — press Space or tap them to wave first for bonus points. Every move builds heat inside the costume and drains stamina; stand still near the misting fan (the blue zone) to cool down and catch your breath. Let heat max out or stamina hit zero and you'll need to step out, costing a life. Guest traffic gets busier and obstacles multiply each shift. Lose all 3 lives and your shift ends!
