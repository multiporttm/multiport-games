Weathervane Making Silhouette Cutting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Sheet 1
A sheet of copper has a rooster silhouette chalked onto it, and a glowing cursor creeps along the outline while a pressure needle sweeps back and forth across a narrow gauge beside it. Press Space, tap, or click ✂️ Snip the instant the needle sits inside the glowing target band to make a clean cut along the line. Snip too early or too late and the shears skid off the line, costing you a life — three ragged cuts and the sheet is ruined! Finish every notch on a sheet to move on to a trickier silhouette with a narrower target band.
