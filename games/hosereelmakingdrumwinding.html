🌀 Hose Reel Making Drum Winding
Hold down (mouse, finger, or Space) to spin the drum faster. Keep the speed needle inside the green safe zone to wind the hose smoothly. Too slow and the hose tangles; too fast for too long and it snaps!
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space/Enter) to spin the winding drum — the longer you hold, the faster it spins. Release to let it slow down. Keep the speed needle inside the moving green safe zone to steadily wind hose length onto the reel. Drifting too slow for too long lets the hose tangle; staying too fast for too long snaps it. Fill the Wound Length meter each round to advance — the safe zone gets narrower and faster each round.
