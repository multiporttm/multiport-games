Leash Walking Practice
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: The dog pulls on the leash, filling the tension bar. Press Space or tap Correction to give a gentle leash correction and bring the tension back down into the calm green zone. Watch for distractions (squirrels, other dogs) that spike the pull — react quickly! Let tension hit maximum 3 times and the walk ends. Walk far enough while staying mostly calm to complete the route; distractions grow more frequent and the pull gets stronger every block.
