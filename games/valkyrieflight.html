Steer your valkyrie with Arrow Keys / WASD, or press-and-drag on the sky. Dodge the dark storm clouds and enemy ravens — hitting them costs health. Click or tap (a quick tap without dragging) to swing your blade and strike any glowing spirit or raven within reach for points. Fly as far as you can before your health runs out!
