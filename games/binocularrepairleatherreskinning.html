Binocular Repair Leather Reskinning
Bodies: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Click a bare panel to glue it, then click each glowing stitch point around its edge in order.
How to play: A binocular body has lost its old cracked hide, leaving bare panels to reskin. Click or tap a bare panel to press a fresh leather patch into place and spread glue, then click or tap each glowing stitch point around its edge in order, or use the arrow keys plus Space to select and stitch, to sew it down before the glue skins over. Work too slowly on a glued panel and the glue skins before you finish stitching, leaving a bubble that costs progress; stitching points out of order leaves a loose edge. Reskin every panel on the body before your glue pot runs dry to finish the body and move to a trickier one.
