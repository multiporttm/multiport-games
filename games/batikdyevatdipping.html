Batik Dye Vat Dipping
Layer: 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Space / Click / Tap — release inside the target band
Hold the Dip button (mouse, finger, or Space) to lower the wax-resisted fabric into the dye vat. Release when the rising timing needle sits inside the glowing target band to achieve the correct shade. Release too early and the dye is too light; hold too long and it over-dyes. Nail the timing across each dye-layer round — the target band narrows and the pour gets trickier every level.
