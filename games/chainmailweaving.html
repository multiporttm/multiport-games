Reference Weave (European 4-in-1)

Your Sheet — link the glowing ring

Click the glowing new ring, then click each ring it must pass through, in any order.
How to play: The reference panel shows the European 4-in-1 weave — every new ring links through 4 existing rings above it. On your sheet, the ring that needs linking pulses gold. Click it, then click each of the existing rings it should pass through (shown as targets) — order between the targets doesn't matter, but every target must be clicked before moving to the next new ring. Wrong or skipped links weaken the sheet's integrity. Once every ring is linked, click Stress-Test Sheet to see if it holds. Weave sheets correctly to score; each round adds more rings and a larger pattern.
