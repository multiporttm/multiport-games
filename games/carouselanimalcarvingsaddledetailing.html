How to play: A carved carousel saddle is studded with round engraving points. Watch the pattern as it lights up point by point to show the carving order, then click, tap, or use the number keys 1-9 (matching the glowing stud numbers) to carve the studs back in that exact order. Carving the full sequence correctly extends your streak, earns points, and reveals a longer pattern next time; tapping a stud out of order chips the design and costs a tool. Run out of tools before finishing a saddle's pattern and the piece is ruined, so watch closely as the scrollwork grows longer and trickier with every saddle.
