Quill Pen Making Barb Stripping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Feather 1
A raw goose feather is clamped to the bench and its lower barbs have to come off in one clean pull before the shaft can be cut into a pen, but pull the blade too fast and it gouges the shaft, too slow and the barbs just snap back instead of stripping free. Watch the speed needle sweep back and forth across the dial, then use the Up/Down arrow keys or tap ⚡ Faster / 🐌 Slower to nudge it toward the glowing target band, and press Space, click, or tap ✋ Strip the instant the needle sits inside that band to pull a perfect clean length of bare shaft. Strip at the wrong speed and the feather is ruined — three ruined feathers and the batch is lost! Clear every length on a feather to move on to a tougher one with a narrower target band.
