Vineyard Trellis Repair
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1/6
How to play: A windstorm snapped the trellis wires! Drag each loose wire coil (or tap a coil, then tap its target anchor) to the glowing anchor point that matches it. Wires must be restrung in tension order — lowest wire first, then the next one up — shown by the numbered anchors. Restringing out of order costs a life. Finish all wires on all posts before the windstorm timer runs out. 3 lives — clear all 6 levels to win!
