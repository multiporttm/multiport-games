Rum Smuggling
Tap crates to hide rum barrels inside them. Click "Seal Hold" when ready.
Barrels hidden: 0 / 10  |  Round: 1  |  High score: 0
How to play: Your cargo hold is a grid of crates. Click/tap crates to hide rum barrels inside them (🛢️) — hide the required number, but leave enough plain crates untouched to look innocent. Click Seal Hold when your barrels are placed. Customs inspectors then get a limited number of inspection points to search slots. If they find fewer than half your barrels, you smuggle successfully and score points — find too many and you're caught! Click New Round any time to reset.
