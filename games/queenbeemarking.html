Queen Bee Marking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Frame 1/5
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your marking pen hovering right over the queen bee — the larger gold bee weaving across the frame. Stay close and your steady-hand meter fills; drift away and it drains. Bump into a worker bee (small dark bee) and you'll spook the frame, costing a life. Fill the meter to finish her mark and move to the next, more crowded frame. Three lives — don't harm the queen!
