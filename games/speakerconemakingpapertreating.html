A fresh paper cone blank arrives on the arm. Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) to dip it into the chemical treating bath; a soak bar fills while it's submerged. Release while the bar is inside the glowing green window to pull out a perfectly treated cone. Release too early and the paper stays untreated; hold too long past the window and the cone over-soaks and falls apart, costing a life.
