Click a plot to water it — watch the thirst bar!
You have a limited water supply each round. Click a crop plot to give it one dose of water — the bar under each plot shows how thirsty it still is (some crops need several doses, some need just one). Unwatered crops wilt and die over the round. When you're ready, click Harvest Round to collect your surviving crops and move on — later rounds bring tighter water budgets and thirstier crops.
