Soap Carving Rough-Out Cutting
Blocks Cut: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Tap CUT, click, or press Space when the blade meter is in the blue clean-cut band.
How to play: A soap block is marked with the rough silhouette of the finished carving, and the excess corner chunks must be sheared off one at a time. A blade meter sweeps back and forth across a dial; press Space, click, or tap the CUT button exactly when the meter sits inside the blue clean-cut band to remove the marked corner in one clean stroke. A good cut builds your streak and bonus score; cutting too early or too late tears the soap and cracks your bar-integrity meter. Clear every marked corner before the bar cracks to finish the rough-out and move on to a bigger block with trickier, faster corners.
