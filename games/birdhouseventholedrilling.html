Birdhouse Vent Hole Drilling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1/8
How to play: A side panel shows faint marked X positions for vent holes with a dashed centerline for spacing. Aim your drill with the mouse, touch or Arrow Keys, and press Space / click / tap to drill. Land inside the yellow ring for a hit — the closer to dead center, the more points. Drill outside the ring and the wood splinters, costing a life. Finish every hole on the panel to move to the next, trickier birdhouse with more holes and tighter rings!
