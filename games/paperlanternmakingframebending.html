Drag across the canvas, or press the ← and → arrow keys, to swing the bamboo rib until it lines up with the dashed template angle. Release the drag, or press Space/Enter, to lock the bend in place. A close match bends the rib cleanly and raises your score; a bad match cracks the bamboo and drains your Frame Quality meter — let it empty and the lantern frame is ruined.
