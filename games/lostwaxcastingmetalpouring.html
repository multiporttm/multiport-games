Lost-Wax Casting Metal Pouring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Mold 1/8
How to play: Use the Up/Down Arrow Keys (or drag/tap up and down over the crucible) to tilt it and control the pour angle, and hold Space, mouse, or finger to pour molten bronze into the mold. Keep both the angle needle and the pour-speed gauge inside their green safe zones — pour too fast or at a bad angle and turbulence creates porosity in the casting, draining your quality meter. Fill the mold while staying safe to move to the next, trickier mold — lose all 3 lives and the casting is scrapped!
