For each animal shown, click/tap (or press 1-4) the enrichment activity that matches its natural behavior — puzzle feeders for foraging, scent trails for tracking, climbing structures for climbers, water play for swimmers. You can never assign the same activity to an animal twice in a row. Wrong matches or repeats cost a life — three lives, and the schedule gets busier and the clock shorter each level.
