Buckle Attaching
Score: 0 Belt: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Each buckle shows marked rivet holes. Press P or tap Punch Hole on the highlighted mark to punch it, then press Space or tap Hammer Tap exactly the number of times shown to set the rivet. Too few taps leaves it loose, too many dents the leather — either way you lose a life. Finish all holes on the strap before time runs out.
