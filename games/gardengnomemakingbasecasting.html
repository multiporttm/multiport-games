🧙 Garden Gnome Making Base Casting
Hold (mouse, finger, or Space) to pour liquid clay into the base mold. Release exactly when the level reaches the glowing fill line — too little leaves a weak base, too much overflows the mold.
Press and hold (click, tap, or Space/Enter) to pour clay into the mold — the level rises while you hold. Let go right as the rising level crosses the glowing green fill band to cast a perfect base. Releasing too early or too late weakens the base and drains your Mold Integrity meter; letting the mold overflow does the same. Empty the meter and the casting is scrapped.
