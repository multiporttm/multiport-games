Bowling Gutter Guard Install
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Lane 1/5
How to play: Each lane's gutters need bumper-rail sections installed in order, from the pin deck down to the approach, alternating left then right. Use the Arrow Keys to move the selection cursor between the empty numbered slots (or just click/tap a slot), then press Enter/Space or click it to pick the next section — picking the wrong number costs a life. Once the right slot is picked, a snap needle slides across a bar: press Space, Enter, or tap SNAP IN when the needle is inside the green zone to lock the section in; miss it and try again, but it costs a life. Finish all five lanes — each longer with a tighter snap zone — before your 3 lives run out!
