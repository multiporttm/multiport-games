Click the grid point that matches the current bearing and distance.
How to play: The bearing sheet lists each leg of the course as a compass bearing (0°=North, 90°=East, 180°=South, 270°=West) and a distance in grid squares, starting from the flag. Click the grid intersection that lies at the current leg's bearing and distance from your current position — a faint ring shows the correct distance and click-tolerance to help you sight the right point. Click the wrong point and you'll miss the leg, costing points, but you can try again. Complete every leg to reach the hidden destination marker and finish the course. Complete courses correctly to score; each round adds more legs and trickier bearing combinations.
