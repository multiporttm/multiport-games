Cheese Curd Cutting
Score: 0 Vat: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Press Space or Tap the instant the sweeping knife lines up with the glowing guide line to make a clean cut. Cut every guide line in the vat, then press/tap again when DRAIN NOW flashes to drain the whey. Miss a cut or the drain window and you lose a life — the cube size (and your precision window) gets tighter with every vat.
