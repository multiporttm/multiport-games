Submarine Rescue Operation
Rescued: 0 Best: 0 Mission: 1
How to play: Use Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag/tap, to pilot your DSRV rescue vehicle down to the disabled submarine. Line up your hatch (the ring) with the sub's escape hatch and hold steady inside the green docking zone to begin mating — stay aligned until the docking meter fills to complete the rescue. The crew's air gauge is ticking down the whole time, and current drift makes holding position tricky, so dock fast!
