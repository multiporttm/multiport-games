The finished bow hangs on the tiller scale, string hooked to the gauge, and every customer's order calls for a precise draw weight before the bow can ship. Hold Space, hold the mouse down, or press and hold 💪 Hold to Pull to draw the string back and watch the weight needle climb — release the instant the needle sits inside the glowing target band to log a perfect test pull. Release too early, too late, or hold on too long and overdraw the bow past full draw, and the limb cracks, costing a life! Log enough clean pulls to certify that bow and move to the next order with a narrower target band and a faster-climbing needle.
