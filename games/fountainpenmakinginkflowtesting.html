Fountain Pen Making Ink Flow Testing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Nib 1/10 Hold: 0%
How to play: The gold needle on the gauge shows the nib's current ink wetness, and it drifts and wobbles on its own. Drag the dial (or use Left/Right Arrow Keys, or A/D) to open or close the tine spacing and steer the needle into the green target zone. Keep it inside the zone to fill the hold meter — fill it fully to pass the nib. Swing too far past the zone into flood or starve territory and you will lose a life. Target zones get narrower and the needle gets twitchier with every nib!
