Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Line 1
How to play: Fruit rolls down the belt labeled with its grade. Press 1, 2, or 3 (or tap/click a bin) to send the highlighted piece of fruit into the matching bin — Premium, Standard, or Juice/Reject — before it rolls off the end. Use Left/Right Arrow to change which piece is highlighted. A wrong bin or a missed piece costs a life. The belt speeds up and grading gets trickier each line.
