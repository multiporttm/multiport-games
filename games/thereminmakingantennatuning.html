📡 Tune The Antenna
Drag up and down to move the tuning dial. Keep it inside the drifting target band to hold the lock.
Press and drag (mouse or finger) anywhere on the gauge to slide the tuning dial up and down the frequency scale. The target band drifts on its own — keep your dial inside it to earn score every instant and refill your Lock meter; drift outside the band and Lock drains fast. Let Lock hit zero and the antenna de-tunes. Each round the band narrows and drifts quicker, so stay locked on to beat your best score.
