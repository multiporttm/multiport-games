Opera House Set Changeover
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Scene 1
How to play: Each numbered crate, flat, and prop is a set piece for the next scene. Click or tap them in order — 1, then 2, then 3 — or press the matching number key, before the countdown bar empties and the curtain reopens. Clicking out of order costs time and points. Clear the scene change in time to move to the next, trickier changeover. Three botched changeovers and the show stops.
