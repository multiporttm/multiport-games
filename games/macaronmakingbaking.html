How to play: The dashed line on the chart is the target temperature curve for this bake; the solid pink line is your real oven temperature. Press Up/Down arrows, or click/tap the + / − buttons, to adjust the oven. Keep your line hugging the target band as it rises, holds, and drops. Drift too far above and the shells crack; drift too far below and they stay hollow. The curve gets steeper and twistier every bake!
