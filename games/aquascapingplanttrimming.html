Move the scissors with your mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys. Hold the mouse button, touch, or Space to engage the blade, then sweep down slowly into the glowing target band on each stem. Stop inside the glow to trim it safely — cut too low and you will damage the plant. More plants appear each round.
