Greenhouse Watering
Level 1 Lives: ❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0
How to play: Each plant has a soil-moisture bar and a target zone (yellow band). Click/tap a plant to give it a splash of water, or use Arrow Keys + Space/Enter on keyboard. Get every plant's moisture inside its target zone before the sunlight timer runs out — too little and it wilts, too much and the roots drown. Finish a round to advance; run out of lives and the greenhouse closes for the day.
