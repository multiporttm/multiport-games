Click or tap the glowing marked joint on the diagram to select it, then press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) to heat the solder. Release while the gauge needle is inside the safe band — too short and the joint stays weak, too long and you melt through the thin wire. Solder every joint on the base to finish; more joints appear each round!
