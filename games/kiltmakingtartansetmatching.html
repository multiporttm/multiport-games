Click or tap two swatch tiles to flip them over — if the hand-drawn tartan setts match, the weaver keeps that pair pinned to the board. Use arrow keys to move the cursor and Enter/Space to flip a tile. Match every pair on the loom before your turns run dry to move to a bigger loom with more setts. Fewer wasted turns means a higher score!
