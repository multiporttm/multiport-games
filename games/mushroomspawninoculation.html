Two markers sweep back and forth: the spacing marker across the bag and the depth needle down the plug. Press Space (or tap/click the bag) the instant both are inside their green target zones at the same time to inoculate cleanly. Getting only one lined up — or neither — contaminates the bag and costs a life. Clear every bag before the sterile window runs out; the zones get tighter and the markers move faster with each new substrate type!
