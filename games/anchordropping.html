Anchor Dropping
Location 1 | Secured: 0 | Hi: 0
Watch the depth needle sweep up and down the gauge in the choppy water. Press Space, click or tap to drop the anchor the instant the needle is inside the green seafloor zone. Too early (needle above the zone) and the anchor won't reach bottom — the ship drifts. Too late (needle below the zone) and the chain runs out. Three misses ends the run — secure as many locations as you can!
