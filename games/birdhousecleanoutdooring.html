Birdhouse Cleanout Dooring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Door 1/8
How to play: Slide the cleanout door into its cutout using Arrow Keys or by dragging it with mouse/touch. Watch the two side gauges — horizontal and vertical fit — and keep both needles inside their glowing green safe bands at the same time to sit the door flush. Press Space / tap the latch button to secure it once aligned; locking it while off-band jams the door and costs a life. Fit every door to advance — later rounds add rotation to the fit and shrink the safe bands!
