Mechanical Calculator Register Reset
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Teardown 1
How to play: Each digit register's needle sweeps back and forth. Working left to right, press Pull Zero Crank (or Space/Enter, or tap the canvas) the instant the active register's needle sits inside the glowing safe zone. Hit it clean and the register snaps to zero; pull early or late and the carriage jams, costing a life. Clear every register in order before the clock runs out. Each new teardown adds more registers and speeds up the sweep.
