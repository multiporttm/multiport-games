Automaton Linkage Assembly
Level 1Score: 0Lives: 3Hi: 0
Connect the pivots in order, 1 → N
Click, tap, or press the number keys to connect the numbered pivot points in ascending order, assembling the linkage before the cam timer runs out. You can also use arrow keys to move the focus highlight between pivots and Enter/Space to connect the focused one. Finish all pivots to send the rod through its motion. Three mistakes or timeouts end the run — later rounds add more pivots and less time.
