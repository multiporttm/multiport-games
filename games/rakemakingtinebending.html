🧹 Rake Making Tine Bending
Drag the bending lever around the dial to match the glowing target angle, then lock it in. Land close bends to keep the rake head true.
Drag anywhere on the dial (mouse or touch) to swing the bending lever, or use Left/Right arrow keys for fine control. Line the lever up with the glowing target wedge, then tap "Bend Tine" (or press Space/Enter) to lock the bend. Close bends score more and keep the Temper meter full; bending too far off the target cools the metal and drains Temper. Let Temper hit zero and the tine snaps.
