How to play: The box needs its 54 cards (Spades, Hearts, Clubs, Diamonds — Ace to King — then both Jokers) packed in order, one at a time. Click/tap the card shown as "Needed" from the tray below, or press the matching number key (1-6), before the belt timer runs out. A wrong pick or a timeout costs a life; every few correct picks the belt speeds up and the tray gets more decoys!
