🛼 Rollerblade
Jump gaps and rails, land grinds for bonus points, and dodge pedestrians!
Press Up / Space / Tap to jump over gaps and rails. While airborne over a rail, press Down (or tap again) to grind it for bonus points. Avoid pedestrians and obstacles — hitting one ends the run. Score is based on tricks landed and distance traveled.
