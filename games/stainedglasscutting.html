Stained Glass Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Pane: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Press mouse/finger down on the glowing dot and drag the cutter steadily along the pattern line — stray too far off the line and you lose your progress on that cut. Reach the end to ready a clean score line, then click Snap! (or press Space) the instant the bar is in the green zone to break the glass cleanly. Three missed cuts or bad snaps and it is game over. Panes get curvier and the snap timing window gets tighter as you go.
