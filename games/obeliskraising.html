Obelisk Raising
Obelisks raised: 0 | Hi: 0Crew stamina: 100
Hold Space or Up Arrow (or tap Pull Ropes) to haul the obelisk upright — it drifts off-balance as it rises. Use Left/Right Arrows or A/D (or the side buttons) to counter-pull the crew on the leaning side, and press S or tap Sandbag to drop a counterweight and steady it. Let the tilt go too far and it topples!
