Airbrush Art Stencil Cutting
Stencil: 1 Accuracy: 100% Score: 0 Best: 0
Press and hold the mouse or your finger on the glowing blade and drag it slowly around the dotted stencil line (arrow keys also nudge the blade). Stay inside the pink safe-zone band the whole way around — straying off the line scuffs your accuracy, and running the blade off the edge of the paper ruins the cut. Make it all the way around before the paper curls up (the timer) to finish the stencil and bank your score, which is higher the cleaner your cut was!
