Quality control for incense means confirming every stick smells exactly as designed. A test stick smolders and puffs out a sequence of colored scent notes one at a time — memorize the order, then click the matching note jars on the bench below in the same order to certify the aroma. Get the whole sequence right to advance to a longer, trickier one; a wrong jar costs a life. Run out of lives and the test batch is sent back to the workshop!
