Silkscreen Stencil Burning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: A UV lamp exposes your photo-emulsion screen through a stencil. Watch the gauge needle sweep across the bar and press Space or Tap (or the Pull Screen button) the instant it is inside the glowing green target band to burn a clean image. Release too early and the image washes out underexposed; too late and the stencil burns through overexposed. Stencils get more intricate and the target band narrows every round — lose 3 screens and the run ends.
