Mechanism Precision
Click, tap, or press Space right as the file gauge sweeps into the green tolerance zone to fit the part.
How to play: A tolerance gauge sweeps back and forth as you file the nut, sear, and lever. Click, tap the gauge, or press Space/Enter the instant the marker is inside the green tolerance zone to fit the part perfectly. A clean fit scores points and advances your fit counter; a miss files the part out of tolerance and drains your Mechanism Precision meter. Fit enough parts cleanly to finish the trigger set and move on to a tighter, trickier build. If Mechanism Precision runs out, the trigger set is ruined and the run ends — try to beat your best round and score.
