Cigar Wrapper Leaf
Cigar: 1 | Wrap: 1/3 | Hi: 0
Press and hold Space, Mouse, or Touch to stretch the wrapper leaf's tension, then release when the needle sits in the glowing target band to lay a clean spiral wrap. Release too early and it stays loose with gaps; hold too long and the delicate leaf tears. Each cigar needs several wraps, and the band gets tighter every round!
