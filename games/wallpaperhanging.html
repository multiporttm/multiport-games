Wall 1
Tap a strip, then tap the slot to hang it aligned with its pattern
Select a wallpaper strip from the tray, then tap the slot on the wall where its pattern lines up with the strip beside it — matching strips glow green, mismatched ones glow red and cost points. Once every strip is hung, trim the excess by tapping the highlighted zones at the ceiling and floor. Paper full walls correctly to score — more strips and busier patterns each round.
