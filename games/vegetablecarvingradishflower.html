Vegetable Carving Radish Flower
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Flower 1/6
How to play: A glowing bead sweeps out from the radish's center along one petal line after another — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your knife tip inside the tolerance ring around the bead. Drift too far and your cut control drains; empty it and the petal detaches too early, costing a life. Stay close through every petal to finish the flower and move to the next radish. Every round adds more petals and tightens the tolerance — lose all 3 lives and the radish crumbles!
