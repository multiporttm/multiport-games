How to play: A rusty escapement mechanism sits on your bench with a rack of teeth swinging back and forth past a spring-loaded dog. Watch the marker move and press Release Dog (or the spacebar, Enter, or on-screen button) the instant the pointer sits inside the glowing target zone over a tooth. A clean release inside the bright center nudges the tuning meter up a lot; a release near the edge of the zone still counts but earns less. Release too early or too late and the dog slips or binds, costing you a strike. The target zone narrows and the rack speeds up as your clean-step streak grows — rack up enough clean releases before three bad strikes jam the mechanism for good, and chase a new high score.
