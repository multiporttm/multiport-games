Paddleboard Balance Training
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Stretch 1/5
How to play: Hold Left/A or Right/D (or touch the left/right half of the board) to shift your weight and counter the rocking from boat wakes and swell. Keep the yellow balance marker inside the green safe zone — if it slides all the way to either edge, you fall in and lose a life. Paddle far enough to clear each stretch of water; the wakes get bigger and more frequent on every stretch. Lose all 3 lives and the session is over!
