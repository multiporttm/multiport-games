Wheelwright Rim Bending
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Lath 1
A steamed hickory lath is pulled hissing-hot from the steam box and clamped against the circular bending form. Press Space, tap, or click 🔥 Bend to start pulling the lath around the form, and hold (press and hold, or tap and hold) to keep bending it further around the curve — the target band drifts as the wood cools, so release right as the curve reaches it, no sooner, no later. Release too soon and the lath springs back short of true; hold too long and it scorches and cracks — three ruined laths and the session is wasted! Bend every lath in a batch to move to a trickier batch with a faster-drifting target.
