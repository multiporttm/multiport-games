Chocolate Mold Filling
Sheet: 1 | Lives: 3 | Hi: 0
Click/tap a cavity (or press Space for the next one) to start pouring, release / press Space again to stop right at the fill line. Then press T / tap again to tap out air bubbles, and drag sideways or press Left/Right to scrape off excess before it sets. Work through the whole sheet before it cools too fast!
