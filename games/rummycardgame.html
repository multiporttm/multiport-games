Draw a card first, then click a card in your hand and discard it to end your turn.
How to play: Each turn, draw a card from the Stock pile or the Discard pile, then click a card in your hand to select it and click Discard Selected to end your turn. Try to arrange your 10-card hand into sets (3+ cards of the same rank) and runs (3+ consecutive cards of the same suit) — cards forming a valid meld glow green. Once your entire hand melds into sets/runs (with at most one leftover card to discard), click Go Out to win the hand! The AI opponent plays too — if it goes out first, the hand ends and you score based on your unmelded card value.
