How to play: A glowing gold bead travels a decorative engraving pattern across the pen cap — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your graver tip inside the narrow tolerance line around it. Wander off the line and your steadiness meter drains; empty it and the engraving is ruined, costing a life. Stay precise the whole way to finish the cap and move on. Patterns get more intricate and the line narrows every cap — lose all 3 lives and the set is scrapped!
