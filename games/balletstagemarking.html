Ballet Stage Marking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Formation 1/4
How to play: Study the glowing choreography diagram showing where each dancer stands, then it fades to a blank stage. Click, tap, or move the crosshair with the Arrow Keys and press Space to lay a tape mark at each matching spot, one dancer at a time. Land inside the shrinking tolerance ring for a better score — miss too far away and the mark is wrong, costing a life. Clear a formation to see a bigger, trickier one with more dancers and a tighter tolerance. Lose all 3 lives and the marking is scrapped!
