Lost-Wax Casting Wax Modeling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Model 1/8
How to play: A glowing bead of molten wax travels the target contour of the sculpture — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your heated carving tool tracking inside the orange tolerance band around it. Stray too far and you either melt away too much wax or gouge the model, draining your wax-integrity meter; empty it and the model is ruined, costing a life. Stay close the whole way to finish the model and move to the next casting. The bead speeds up and the band narrows with every model — lose all 3 lives and the commission is lost!
