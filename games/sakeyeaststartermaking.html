How to play: Each day the shubo starter needs rice, koji, and water added in specific amounts, shown as green target zones on the three bars. Click/tap +Rice, +Koji, and +Water (or press R, K, W) to fill each bar, then click Confirm Day (or press Space) once all three levels sit inside their green zones. Get all three right to grow a healthy culture; miss badly and you lose a life. The targets get narrower and trickier with each new recipe — keep the starter alive across as many days as you can!
