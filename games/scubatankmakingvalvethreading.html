🤿 Scuba Tank Making Valve Threading
Hold to spin the valve wheel up to speed, then keep the needle inside the green thread-pitch zone long enough to seat a clean pass.
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space/Enter) to spin the valve wheel faster; let go to let it coast down. Keep the speed needle inside the moving green thread-pitch zone to fill the seating ring and complete a pass — spin too fast or drift out of the zone and the Alignment meter drains. Each completed pass speeds up the round and narrows the zone. Let Alignment hit empty and the valve cross-threads, ending the run.
