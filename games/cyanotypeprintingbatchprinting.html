Rinsed: 0 Score: 0 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
Click a sheet or arrow-select it, then rinse it the instant its meter enters the green zone.
How to play: A whole rack of cyanotype sheets sits out in the sun together, each one's exposure meter rising toward burnt at its own pace, and fresh sheets keep landing on the rack as the batch continues. Click or tap directly on a sheet to select it, or use the left/right arrow keys, A/D, or the on-screen select buttons to move the selection highlight between sheets, then press Space, click the Rinse Selected button, or tap the selected sheet again to carry it to the rinse bucket. Rinsing a sheet while its meter sits inside the green ready band earns a full-value score bonus; rinsing it outside that band, or letting its meter run all the way to the burnt red zone, ruins the sheet and costs a life. The batch gets busier as your streak grows, with more sheets landing on the rack at once, so keep scanning the whole rack and rinse every sheet you can at the right moment. Lose three sheets and the batch ends; rinse as many correctly timed prints as you can to post a high score.
