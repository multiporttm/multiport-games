Snow Sculpting Detailing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Detail 1/6
How to play: A glowing point travels along a fine snowflake-pattern guide line — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your detailing tip inside the narrow tolerance line around it. Wander off the line and your precision meter drains; empty it and the fine detail is ruined, costing a life. Trace smoothly to the end of the pattern to finish the detail. Every round the pattern gets more intricate, the line moves faster, and the tolerance narrows — lose all 3 lives and the carving is spoiled!
