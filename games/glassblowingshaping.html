Glassblowing Shaping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/8
How to play: Hold Space or press-and-hold to blow air and inflate the bubble. Use Arrow Keys or drag your mouse/finger left-right to press the wet wooden tool against the bubble and shape it, matching the dashed target outline (vase, bowl, or ornament). Lock in the shape with Enter or by releasing when the match meter is high. Match too poorly and the piece cracks, costing a life — targets get more intricate and tolerances tighter each round. Lose all 3 lives and the session ends!
