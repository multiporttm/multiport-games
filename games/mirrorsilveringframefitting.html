Mirror Silvering Frame Fitting
Batch: 1 Time: 0s Fitted: 0/0 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag each silvered pane on the workbench (bottom) up to the frame opening that matches its size and shape. Drop it near the right opening to snap and lock it with clips. Use Tab or click to select a pane and Arrow keys plus Enter to place it with the keyboard. Fit every pane before time runs out — wrong-sized drops bounce back and cost a moment!
