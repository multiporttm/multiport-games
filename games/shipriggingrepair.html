Ship Rigging Repair
Repaired: 0/0 | Hull: 100 | Hi: 0
How to play: Move along the rigging with Left/Right Arrow keys or by tapping the left/right side of the canvas, or drag. When you're lined up under a torn sail or snapped rope (glowing), press Space or tap TIE ROPE to repair it. The ship rocks violently and lightning flashes — watch the rock meter: tying during a big rock (shown in red) makes you slip and lose a sail! Repair everything before the storm damage sinks the ship.
