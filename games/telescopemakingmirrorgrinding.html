Grit Stage: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Score: 0 Hi: 0
How to play: Use Up/Down arrows, the on-screen buttons, or drag up/down on the mirror, to steer the curvature needle into the green safe zone on the gauge and hold it there as the abrasive tool sweeps a figure-eight across the glass blank. Drifting outside the zone grinds unevenly — if the unevenness meter fills completely, the curve is ruined. Finish the whole pass with the surface even to move on to a finer grit stage.
