Watch the marker sweep across the spinning blank — cut when it hits the glowing zone.
How to play: A rough wooden blank spins on the lathe while a cutting marker sweeps left and right across it. Press the CUT button, tap the canvas, or hit the spacebar the moment the marker overlaps the glowing cut zone to shape that section of the pawn cleanly. Land several cuts in a row to raise your batch quality streak and score bonus; miss the zone and you chip the blank, losing one from your limited supply. Finish shaping a pawn to send it to the batch and move to a faster, trickier blank. Run out of blanks and the batch ends.
