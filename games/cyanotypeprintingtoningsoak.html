Cyanotype Printing Toning Soak
Toned: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Balance the tea bath to steer the print's tone into the target band, then pull it at the right moment.
How to play: A washed cyanotype print sits soaking in a toning tray, and a jug of hot steeped tea and a jug of cool wash water stand ready beside it. Add hot tea with the Add Hot Tea button, the up arrow, or W to push the bath strength higher, or add cool water with the Add Cool Water button, the down arrow, or S to bring it back down; the print's own toned color drifts toward whatever the bath strength is currently pushing it toward, shown by the moving swatch against the warm plum-brown target band. Watch both the bath strength meter and the tea supply bar, which drains as you pour, then press Space, click, or tap the Pull Print button the instant the print's color sits inside the target band. Pull too early and the print stays cool and blue; wait too long or let the bath run too hot and the print over-tones into muddy brown; either way the print is ruined. Tone as many prints as you can before your tea supply runs dry to post a high score.
