How to play: Each pool has three phases. Skim: drag the net (click/touch-drag) across the water to catch every leaf before time or misses pile up. Vacuum: drag the vacuum head over the pool floor in overlapping passes — the heatmap shows what's covered — until the whole floor is clean. Chemistry: adjust the pH, Chlorine and Alkalinity sliders to land inside the target range, then hit Test Water. Finish all three phases before the timer runs out to move to a bigger, dirtier pool!
