Telescope Making Coating Application
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
Move the applicator with Left/Right Arrow Keys (or the Move buttons) and hold Space / HOLD TO SPRAY to deposit aluminum coating. Keep every section's coverage bar inside the green safe band — lingering too long over one spot clumps it (overcoated), while skipping a section leaves a thin spot (undercoated). Finish the sweep cycle with the whole mirror in the safe band. Mirrors get bigger and the band narrows every round.
