How to play: Each recipe card shows a target priming-sugar dose in grams. Use +1g, +5g and -1g (or Arrow Keys for ±1g, Shift+Up for +5g) to scoop sugar onto the scale, then click Bottle It! (or press Enter) once your scoop matches the target within the safe tolerance shown. Too little sugar and the cider goes flat; too much and the bottle becomes a bomb — both cost a life! Targets get trickier and tolerance tightens each round.
