Board Game Card Sleeve Insertion
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Deck: 0/10 Combo: 0 ♥♥♥
Cards slide down the line toward the open sleeve. Press Space or click/tap the sleeve the instant the card lines up with the glowing slot to insert it clean. Insert too early, too late, or let a card fall off the end and you bend a corner, costing a life. Clear the whole deck to advance — the line gets faster and the slot gets tighter with every deck.
