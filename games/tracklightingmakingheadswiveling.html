🔧 Track Lighting Making Head Swiveling
Drag the lamp head to swivel it. Line it up inside the glowing aim zone, then tap Lock before your steadiness runs out.
Drag left and right on the track head (mouse or touch) to swivel it, or use the Left/Right arrow keys. Line the head up inside the glowing aim zone, then press the Lock button (or Space/Enter) to fasten the joint. A clean lock scores points; locking outside the zone, or running out of steadiness, costs you. The aim zone shrinks and the steadiness meter drains faster each round.
