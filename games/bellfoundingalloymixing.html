Bell Founding Alloy Mixing
Batch: 1 Heat: 0% Score: 0 Best: 0
Shovel scoops of copper (press C or tap the copper pile) and tin (press T or tap the tin pile) into the crucible to match the target ratio shown on the gauge. Each scoop raises the furnace heat a little — if it maxes out before you pour, the melt scorches. When the needle sits in the gold target band, press Enter/Space or hit Pour Melt to cast that batch and move on to a tighter target.
