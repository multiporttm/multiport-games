A morsel of food swings back and forth over the chick's beak. Press Space (or Click/Tap the canvas) exactly when the morsel sits inside the glowing sweet-spot window to feed it and raise the bond gauge. Feeding outside the window overfeeds the chick and knocks the bond back down. Fill the bond gauge to the target line before the timer runs out to imprint the falcon and advance — the window narrows and the swing speeds up every round.
