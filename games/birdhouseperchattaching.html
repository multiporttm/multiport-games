Birdhouse Perch Attaching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Perch: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Drag the perch (or move it with arrow keys) onto the marked spot below the entry hole, then press Space / Enter or tap to glue it in place. While the glue sets, keep the balance needle centered with Left/Right arrows (or the Balance buttons) — if it drifts off too far for too long, the perch slides off and you'll need to redo it!
