How to play: A blocky wood blank shows a glowing facet with a small arrow telling you which way to swing the blade. Click or tap the glowing facet to chip it off, or use the arrow keys to steer the blade cursor onto it and press Space to strike. Chipping the correct facet in the shown direction reveals more of the knight head and fills your control meter; hitting the wrong facet or swinging the wrong way nicks the grain and drains control fast. Clear every facet before control runs out to finish the knight head and move on to a pose with more facets.
