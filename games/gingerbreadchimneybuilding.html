A brick swings left and right above your chimney. Press Space, click, or tap to drop it — line it up over the brick below and match the next color in the pattern strip above the canvas. A bad drop raises the wobble gauge; max it out and the chimney topples, costing a life. Use Left/Right Arrow to nudge the brick while it swings. Reach the target height to move to a taller, trickier chimney!
