How to play: The rig order at the top lists the numbered attachment points in the order you must rig them — click each point (or press its number key) in that exact order. Rig one out of order and the stunt is blown, costing a life. Finish the order correctly and a tension needle appears — press Space or tap to lock the cable tension the instant the needle sits inside the glowing green window. More points and a tighter tension window appear every rig. Survive all 3 lives through 10 rigs!
