Leather Tanning Hide Soaking
Batch: 1 Pits full: 0/3 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag a waiting hide into an open lime pit (or press 1/2/3 to select a hide and Space to drop it in the first open pit). Each pit has a moisture dial on its side that rises as the hide soaks — click the pit (or press A/S/D for pits 1/2/3) to pull the hide out right when the dial sits in the green "ready" band. Pull too early and the hide stays stiff; wait too long and it rots in the lime. Tan every hide in the drum to finish the batch!
