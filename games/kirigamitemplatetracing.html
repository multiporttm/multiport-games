Kirigami Template Tracing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Template 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead creeps along the template's design line — drag your pencil (mouse / finger, or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep its tip riding right along that line as it moves. Drift too far and your steady-hand meter drains; empty it and the tracing goes wrong, costing a life. Stay close the whole way to finish the template and move on to the next design. Every round the pattern gets more intricate and the tolerance line narrows — lose all 3 lives and the sheet is ruined!
