🛴 Segway Ride
Lean forward to speed up, steer around obstacles, and don't let the wobble meter max out!
Press Up/Down (or drag up/down) to lean forward/back and control speed. Press Left/Right (or drag left/right) to steer. Going fast, hitting obstacles, or hugging the path edges raises your wobble meter — let it max out and you'll tip over. Score is based on distance traveled and course sections completed.
