Adjust the languid height and lip width until pitch and tone both sit inside the target bands.
How to play: A fresh metal pipe sits on the voicing bench, fed by wind from a hand bellows. The upper lip of the pipe mouth can be widened or narrowed (making the pitch sharper or flatter), and the languid underneath can be raised or lowered (making the tone chiffier or rounder). Use the arrow keys (left/right for the lip, up/down for the languid), A/D and W/S, the on-screen buttons, or touch the buttons, to nudge both toward the target pitch and tone bands shown on the two meters. Hold an adjustment too long past the target and you overshoot, costing accuracy; get both meters inside their green target bands before the bellows runs out of wind to voice the pipe cleanly and move to the next one in the rank. Voice as many pipes as you can before the wind runs out to post a high score.
