Luthier Purfling Inlay
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Body 1/8
INLAY ORDER
How to play: The small diagram on the right numbers every purfling strip in the order they must be inlaid around the violin body's edge. Click or tap each groove node on the big outline in that exact order — the next correct strip presses in cleanly. Press the wrong one and it pops out of alignment, costing a life. Finish every strip before the time bar runs out to complete the body and move on — each new body has more strips in a more intricate edge shape. Lose all 3 lives and the inlay job is ruined!
