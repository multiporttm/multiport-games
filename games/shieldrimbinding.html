🛡️ Shield Rim Binding
Pass: 1 Score: 0 Best: 0
Click, tap, or press Space/Enter the instant the rivet tool sweeping around the rim passes through the glowing green arc to drive a clean rivet. A clean rivet boosts your score and evenness meter; a mistimed rivet buckles the rim and damages evenness. Drive 5 clean rivets to finish a pass and start a faster, tighter one. If evenness runs out, the rim is ruined and the run ends!
