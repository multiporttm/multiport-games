Labyrinth Minotaur
Mazes Escaped: 0 | Lives: 3 | Best: 0
Use Arrow Keys or WASD (or the on-screen D-pad / swipe on the maze) to find the glowing exit while a lurking Minotaur hunts you through the dark. Getting caught costs a life and sends you back to the entrance. Collect torches 🔥 to light up more of the maze!
