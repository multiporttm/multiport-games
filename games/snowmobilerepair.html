How to play: Click each part of the snowmobile (Spark Plug, Engine Belt, Track) to inspect it. Damaged parts turn orange. Pick the matching tool from the Tool Kit — watch out for decoy parts that don't fit — then click the damaged part to install it. Repairs must be done in order: Spark Plug, then Belt, then Track. Finish before the Cold meter fills up or you'll succumb to frostbite!
