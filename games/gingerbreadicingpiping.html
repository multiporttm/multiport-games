Gingerbread Icing Piping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Design 1/10
How to play: A glowing decoration — a snowflake, swirl, or row of dots — appears on the cookie. Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) along the glowing guide to pipe icing over every bit of it before your icing bag runs dry. Staying on the glow fills the decoration and barely drains the bag; drifting off burns through icing fast. Finish the whole design before the bag empties to move to the next, more intricate decoration — run out of icing 3 times and the cookie batch is ruined!
