🔮 Marble Making Glass Swirling
Drag your finger or mouse to keep the swirl rod glued to the glowing bead as it spirals through the molten glass.
A glowing bead spirals through the molten glass blob. Press and drag (mouse or touch) to move the swirl rod and keep it as close as possible to the bead at all times - staying close paints bright color swirls and builds score, while drifting away cools the glass and drains the Glass Heat meter. Let the glass cool completely and the marble is ruined.
