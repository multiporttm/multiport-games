🪢 Saddle Making Stirrup Attaching
Watch the stirrup leather's rivet holes — one lights up at a time. Tap it fast before the buckle tongue swings past, or the strap tears.
A rivet hole on the stirrup leather lights up at random. Tap (or click, or press Space/Enter) that exact glowing hole before the timer bar empties to rivet it tight. Tapping the wrong hole or running out of time tears the strap and drains Strap Integrity; empty it and the stirrup falls off. Each successful rivet speeds things up and adds more holes.
