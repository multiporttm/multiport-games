Find the buried spring before the water skins run dry.
How to play: Click or tap a tile to dig it (or use the arrow keys to move the cursor and Space to dig). Each dug tile reveals a number showing how close the buried spring is — lower numbers mean warmer, wetter sand. Reach the hidden water tile before your dig party's water supply runs out. Some tiles collapse when dug, costing extra water and revealing nothing — dig carefully!
