Testing a finished sundial for accuracy means comparing the shadow it casts against a known true clock time across a full day of trials, since even a beautifully carved dial is worthless if its hour lines lie. Each round posts the true time on a reference clock while the sundial's own shadow needle sits off true by some random amount, and a calibration dial lets the tester nudge the gnomon's effective angle to correct it. Drag the calibration dial with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with the Left and Right arrow keys, until the shadow needle lines up as closely as you can manage with the true time mark, then press Lock Reading or tap Space to record that trial. A precise calibration passes the trial cleanly and earns a generous bonus, while a sloppy calibration only partially corrects the reading and burns through one of your limited test attempts. The true time shifts to trickier positions and the shadow drifts faster with each trial, so a careful, patient tester will complete all nine trials and certify the dial before running out of attempts.
