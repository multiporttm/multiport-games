Woodworking Joint Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Joint 1/8
How to play: A bright marked cut line runs across each joint piece — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your saw blade tracking inside the tan tolerance strip around it. Wander off the line and your precision meter drains; empty it and the cut is ruined, costing a life. Stay on the line the whole way to finish the cut cleanly and test-fit the joint. The line gets trickier and the strip narrower every joint — lose all 3 lives and the project is scrapped!
