Hockey Arena Scoreboard Operator
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Period 1
How to play: Watch the ice feed above the scoreboard. The instant an event fires — a goal, a shot on goal, or a penalty — press the matching button, tap it, or press keys 1-6 before the whistle timer runs out: 1 Home Goal, 2 Away Goal, 3 Home Shot, 4 Away Shot, 5 Home Penalty, 6 Away Penalty. Press the wrong control or let the timer expire and you lose a life. Events come faster and the timer shrinks as the period goes on — lose all 3 lives and the broadcast cuts out.
