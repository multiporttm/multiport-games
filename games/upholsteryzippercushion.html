Use Up/Down Arrow, drag, or touch to move the needle so it stays inside the stitch lane as the zipper tape scrolls by. Stray too far from the teeth and the fabric starts to pucker — let the pucker meter run out and you lose a life. Finish each cushion's seam to move to the next, faster one.
