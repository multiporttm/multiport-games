🎨 Chopstick Making Engraving
Pattern: 1 Score: 0 Best: 0
The engraving stylus sweeps around the dial tracing the rim of a chopstick. Click, tap, or press Space right as the tip crosses the glowing green arc to carve a clean groove of the decorative pattern. Miss the arc and your steady-hand meter takes a hit — empty it and the session ends. Every 5 clean grooves the stylus spins faster and the sweet arc narrows!
