Watch the zones light up in pour order, then click (or tap, or use number keys 1-9) the same zones back in the exact same order. The sequence grows by one zone each round.
Every encaustic cement tile pattern is built one pigment layer at a time: a stencil is set down over one zone, colored cement is poured and leveled, the stencil lifts, and the next stencil goes down over the next zone — always in a strict order so wet colors never bleed into each other. In this game a tile's zones flash one by one in their correct pour order; memorize that sequence, then click or tap the zones back in the exact same order (the number keys 1 through 9 also work, matched to each zone's position). Get the whole sequence right and the round advances with one more zone added to memorize, while a wrong zone in the sequence bleeds two colors together and costs you a batch of cement — lose all three batches and the run ends. Score climbs with every correct zone and every completed round, so keep a cool head as the pour order grows longer and the zones multiply.
