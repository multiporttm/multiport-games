Get ready...
Each note shows the 6-hole fingering pattern it needs — filled circles mean covered. Click/tap the holes (or press 1-6) to match the pattern, and toggle the register key (R or the red button) when a note needs the upper octave. Match the exact pattern and register right as the note crosses the hit line for the best score. Tempo and pattern complexity increase every level!
