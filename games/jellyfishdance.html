Jellyfish Dance
Score: 0Height: 0mHi: 0
Click or Tap (or press Space) in rhythm with the glowing beat ring to pulse your jellyfish upward and rise gracefully. Collect glowing bioluminescent particles for points. Pulsing off-beat drains extra energy and can drift you into hazards — nets and other jellyfish. Rise as high as you can before your energy runs out!
