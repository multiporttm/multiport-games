Weather Radar Interpretation
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
Read the radar return on screen, then click (or press 1-4) the correct warning: a faint, low-intensity blob is All Clear, a bright orange/red blob with no spin is a Severe Thunderstorm, a red blob with a curling hook echo is a Tornado Warning, and a large, slow, saturated-green/yellow blob is a Flash Flood. Classify correctly before the timer bar runs out — three misses or timeouts ends the game, and cells arrive faster every level.
