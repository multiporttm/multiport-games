Cross-Stitch Fabric Cutting
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press and drag your scissors (mouse or touch, or Arrow Keys) along the glowing guide line to cut the aida cloth into the template shape. Stray too far off the line and the edge frays — fray too much and the fabric is ruined! Finish the whole outline to complete the shape. Shapes get more intricate each round.
