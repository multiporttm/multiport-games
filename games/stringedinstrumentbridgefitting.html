Stringed Instrument Bridge Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Body 1/5
How to play: Carving: drag your mouse or finger (or hold the Arrow Keys) to run the knife across the bridge foot, shaving it down flush with the glowing target curve of the instrument body. Carve too deep past a column and you gouge it — too many gouges ruins the bridge. Fitting: once flush, hold the bridge's height (drag or Up/Down) steady inside the target zone for proper string action until it locks in. Run out of time or lives and the bridge is scrapped!
