POSITION
Press FIRE when the marker is in the green zone

SATELLITE LOST

It drifted off course.
How to play: A marker sweeps back and forth across the readout. Press Space, click, or tap FIRE / RELEASE the instant it's inside the green target zone. Each satellite needs three locks in a row — Position, Velocity, and Angle — to settle into a stable orbit. Miss a lock and you lose a life; the sweep gets faster and the target zone shrinks as you deploy more satellites.
