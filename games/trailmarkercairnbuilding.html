Trail Marker Cairn Building
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Pick a rock size, then click/tap the cairn to place it. Build the stack large-to-small and keep each rock centered to stay stable. Reach the target height before the wind gust timer runs out to finish the cairn and mark the route. Arrow Keys nudge rock placement left/right, Space drops it. Topple the cairn 3 times and the storm ends your run.
