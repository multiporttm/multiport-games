Grooming Salon Scheduling
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
How to play: A new pet appointment appears at the top, showing its service (bath, full groom, or nail trim) and how many slots it needs. Press 1-4 (or tap a row) to pick a groomer, use ←/→ (or tap a slot) to choose the starting slot, then press Enter/Space (or tap Book It) to confirm. Bookings must fit without overlapping, and must finish before closing time. Miss the timer or double-book a groomer and you lose a life — three strikes and the shop closes early!
