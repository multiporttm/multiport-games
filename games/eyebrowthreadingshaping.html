Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: Move the thread cursor over a brow with Arrow Keys / WASD or by dragging on the canvas, and press Space / Pluck (or tap directly on a hair) to remove it. Only pluck the red stray hairs — leave the brown keeper hairs alone or you'll lose a life and ruin the symmetry. Clear both brows before the appointment clock runs out. More stray hairs appear and the clock gets tighter every level — survive with 3 lives!
