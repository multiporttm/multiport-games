Tap only the patches that glow too bright (too thin) or too dim (too thick).
How to play: The sheet is held against a lamp and split into a grid of patches, each glowing by how much light passes through its skin. Most patches are an even, mid-toned glow — leave those alone. Tap or click only the patches that glow too bright (too thin, will tear) or too dark (too thick, won't fold) to mark them for the shaving knife. Marking a good patch costs lamp oil, and so does running out of time. Find every flaw in a sheet before the lamp burns out to move on to a bigger sheet with subtler, harder-to-spot flaws.
