Encaustic Tile Making Surface Polishing
Tile: 1 Pads Left: 5 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag/tap over patches to grind them, or move the pad with arrow keys and hold Space. Watch the heat meter on each patch — a patch that overheats scorches and is lost. Finish the tile before your grinding pads run out.
After an encaustic cement tile cures, its surface is still rough and dull until a wet grinder works it down in progressive grits to reveal a bright, even shine. In this game each tile is divided into a grid of patches, each shown with its own dullness level and a small heat meter. Hold the grinding pad over a patch — by dragging with the mouse, touching it, or moving the arrow-key cursor onto it and holding Space — to steadily polish it, but friction heat builds the longer you stay in one spot; lift off and move to another patch before the meter maxes out, or that patch scorches its pigment and is lost for the round. Spread your grinding time evenly across the whole tile, bringing every patch to a bright shine before your limited supply of grinding pads wears out from overuse. Later tiles bring bigger grids and faster heat buildup, so keep the pad moving and finish each tile as efficiently as you can.
