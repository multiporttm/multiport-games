How to play: A customer steps up to the kiosk with a craving in a speech bubble. Click the vendor stall that matches it (or press 1-4) before their patience runs out. Each stall's own line drains over time as it serves people — send too many to one stall and its line overflows, angering everyone waiting there. Send customers to the correct, less-crowded stall to keep the whole court flowing!
