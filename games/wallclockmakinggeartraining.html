⚙️ Gear Training
A loose drive gear spins around the clock movement. Tap, click, or press Space the instant its teeth line up with the green mesh zone to seat it cleanly. Miss three times and the movement is ruined!
Watch the small drive gear orbit the big clock-movement gear. Tap the screen (or click, or press Space/Enter) the moment the drive gear's teeth enter the glowing green mesh zone to lock it into place. A clean mesh scores points and speeds up the next gear; a bad mesh costs you one of your three gears. Lose all three and training is over - try to set a new high score!
