Click any tile (or press 1-9) to begin the countdown
How to play: Launch Control is polling every station. The NEXT prompt names the station that must call go/no-go right now — click its tile, or press the matching number key 1-9 shown on the tile, before the amber clock bar runs out. Call the wrong station and you lose precious time; let the clock hit zero and you lose a life. Tiles reshuffle position after every correct call. Each hold adds more stations and a faster-draining clock — clear all 6 holds to light the engines, but lose all 3 lives and the countdown is scrubbed!
