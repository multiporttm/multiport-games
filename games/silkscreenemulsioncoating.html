Press Start Pass (or Space) to begin sweeping the scoop coater up the screen in one smooth, automatic pass. Steer its angle with the Left/Right arrow keys or by dragging left/right on the canvas, keeping the needle inside the green safe zone on the side gauge the whole way up. Drifting out of the zone pools or skips emulsion and fills the defect meter — keep it below the limit to finish the pass, across larger screens and narrower safe zones each round!
