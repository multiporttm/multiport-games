Basket Weaving Coil Binding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ●●● Round 1
Bind a spiraling coil basket by stitching a thin reed over a thick coil core in a steady rhythm. Press Space, click, or tap to lock a stitch while the moving marker sits inside the glowing target band on the gauge. Lock it too soon and stitches bunch up; too late and gaps open. Keep an even rhythm across larger coils each round!
