Tiffany Lamp Making Glow Testing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Lamp 1
A finished lamp sits on the test bench, wired to a dimmer whose needle sweeps back and forth across the brightness dial, hunting for the warm glowing zone that shows the bulb and glass are working together perfectly. Press Space, tap, or click 💡 Flip Switch the instant the needle sweeps inside the glowing zone to pass the lamp with a rich, even glow through the glass. Flip too early and the lamp stays dim and flat, too late and the bulb flares and flickers — either way the lamp fails inspection and you lose a life! Pass every lamp in the batch to move on to a trickier batch with a narrower, more precise glow zone.
