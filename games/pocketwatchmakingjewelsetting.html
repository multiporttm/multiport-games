💎 Pocket Watch Making Jewel Setting
Score: 0 ❤❤❤ Combo x0 Best: 0
Drag (mouse or finger) left and right, or use the Arrow Keys, to slide the setting tray under the watch movement. The socket at the top glows a jewel color - catch only falling jewels of that color in the tray to seat them cleanly, and dodge the rest. Catching the wrong jewel or missing the right one costs a life and resets your combo; the jewels fall faster the longer you survive.
