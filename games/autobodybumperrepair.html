Auto Body Bumper Repair
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bumper 1/8
How to play: A glowing weld bead travels along the crack — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep the welding rod tip inside the orange heat zone around it. Drift away and your weld temperature drops; let it fall to zero and the crack reopens, costing a life. Stay locked on the whole way across to finish the weld and move to the next bumper. The bead speeds up and the heat zone narrows with every bumper — lose all 3 lives and the repair is scrapped!
