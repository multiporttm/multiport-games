Each dog shows the position number it must take on the gang-line (1 = lead dog, closest to the sled driver's call). Drag (or use Arrow keys + Enter) each dog from the kennel onto its matching slot in the correct order. After it clips in, drag the tension slider that appears into the green zone to cinch the line snug before time runs out. Mis-ordered clips or bad tension cost a life — team up all the dogs to mush out before the timer hits zero!
