Butter Sculpture Block Chilling
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Block 1
A fresh 90-pound block of butter just came out of the walk-in warm and mushy, and it needs to sit in the cold room until it reaches the sweet spot where a carving knife glides clean. Press Space, tap, or click 🧊 Chill to start the compressor and hold (press and hold, or tap and hold) to keep dropping the temperature — release right as the gauge settles in the green carving window, no sooner, no later. Release too early and the block stays warm and droopy; hold too long and it freezes rock-hard and cracks when carved — three ruined blocks and the session is wasted! Chill every block in a batch to move to a trickier batch with a narrower window.
