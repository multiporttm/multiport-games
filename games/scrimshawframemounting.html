Scrimshaw Frame Mounting
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag each numbered pin onto its matching marked slot on the frame diagram. On keyboard, Tab/Arrow Keys cycle the active pin, Arrow Keys move the chosen target slot, and Space/Enter drops the pin there. Mount every pin correctly to secure the piece without cracking it. Mistakes crack the material — three cracks and the piece is ruined.
