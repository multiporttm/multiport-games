How to play: Each day, add green (food scraps) and brown (dry leaves/cardboard) material to your compost bin — aim for roughly 1 part green to 3 parts brown. Watch the temperature gauge: too cold and decomposition stalls, too hot and the pile can "burn out" and kill the good microbes. Turn the pile every couple of days to add oxygen and cool it down if it's overheating. Survive all 10 days with your compost healthy to win!
