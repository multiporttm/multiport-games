Hold the lever to swing the carriage, then release it inside the margin window.
How to play: A stuck carriage sits on your bench waiting for its return spring to be re-tensioned. Press and hold the return lever (spacebar, the on-screen button, or a press-and-hold touch/click) to swing the carriage leftward across the page toward the margin stop; let go of the lever and the carriage keeps gliding on its own momentum. Release the lever while the carriage is still inside the glowing margin window so the detent catches cleanly — let go too soon and the carriage stalls short of the window, let go too late (or never) and it slams past into the bell, overshooting. Each clean catch repairs a line and tightens next line's margin window a little, so keep your releases precise and keep the streak going before you run out of spring tension.
