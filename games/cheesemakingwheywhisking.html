Cheesemaking Whey Whisking
Session: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Space, hold the mouse button, or hold your finger on the vat to whisk — release to slow down. Keep the gauge needle inside the green safe zone: too slow and curds clump, too fast and they break apart. The whey thickens and the safe zone narrows as each whisking session goes on!
