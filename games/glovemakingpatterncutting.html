How to play: A glowing bead creeps along the outline of each leather glove pattern piece — drag your mouse or finger, or steer with the Arrow Keys, to keep your knife tip inside the tan tolerance tube around it. Wander too far and your cutting steadiness drains; empty it and the leather is ruined, costing a life. Stay close the whole way to finish a clean cut and move on to the next piece. Every round brings a more intricate pattern piece with a narrower tube — lose all 3 lives and the hide is wasted!
