Raw mineral pigment needs patient grinding before it becomes usable ink. Drag the stone muller (or hold arrow keys to orbit it) around the slab in smooth circles — grind too slowly and the pigment stays coarse, too fast and it splatters out of the zone. Keep your orbit speed inside the glowing white band on the speed meter to steadily raise the fineness percentage, then finish the batch before your stamina runs out for the best score.
