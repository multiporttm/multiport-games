Jukebox Restoration Coin Mechanism Fixing
Unit: 1 Phase: Clean Score: 0 Best: 0
Two-stage repair: first scrub the rusty gears by clicking/tapping or pressing Space repeatedly until each gear turns freely. Then watch the coin slide sweep back and forth and press Space (or click the mechanism) the instant the slide's trigger gate lines up with the glowing acceptance zone to release the coin. Nail the timing for a bonus; miss the zone too many times and the mechanism jams for good on that unit.
