🪄 Paddleboard Making Fiberglass Coating
Drag the squeegee back and forth across the glassed board to spread the resin evenly before it cures. Thin patches stay dry, thick patches pool and sag — keep the whole board in the green.
Drag (or use Arrow Left/Right) the squeegee left and right across the board to spread resin evenly over every column before the cure timer runs out. Each pass thickens the resin under the squeegee; too thin leaves dry patches, too thick makes it sag and drip — keep every column in the green band to finish the coat.
