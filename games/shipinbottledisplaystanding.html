How to play: Use Left/Right arrows (or the on-screen buttons, or drag) to tilt the display cradle and keep the bottle rolling near the center. Wind gusts shove it toward the edges — hold it on the cradle for the full bar to shelve the bottle safely. Let it roll off either edge and it falls. Each new stand is narrower and windier.
