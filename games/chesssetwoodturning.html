Chess Set Wood Turning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/6
How to play: A cutter sweeps back and forth across the spinning blank. Hold Space, click, or touch-and-hold to push the cutter inward and carve the wood down; release to pull it back out. Keep the carved radius tracking the glowing target profile as the cutter sweeps along the piece from base to top. Straying too far from the target drains your finish meter — empty it and you gouge the blank, costing a life. Finish all six pieces (pawn, rook, knight, bishop, queen, king) to complete the set; the profiles get fussier and the tolerance narrows each time. Lose all 3 lives and the blank is scrapped!
