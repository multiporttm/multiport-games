Score: 0 Hi: 0 Bike: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Drag or click each numbered header pipe onto the matching engine port, then click the muffler and heat shields into place in order. Once everything is mounted, bolts appear one at a time — press Space, click, or tap right when the sliding marker is inside the green zone to torque each bolt to spec. Miss the zone or mount a pipe on the wrong port and you lose a life!
