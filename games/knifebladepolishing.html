Knife Blade Polishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Lives: ❤️❤️❤️
Drag your pointer (or use Arrow Keys) to move the blade left/right across the belt sander and polish every section evenly. Watch the heat gauge — holding one spot too long overheats the steel and ruins the temper. Fully polish each grit stage to advance through increasingly tricky blade shapes before you overheat three times!
