Judging starts soon, and every finished butter sculpture needs the exact pedestal whose chill setting matches its tag — ❄️ deep-freeze, 🧊 chilled, or 💧 cool-only — or it slumps under the show lights. Use ← → (or tap a pedestal) to choose a stand, then press Enter, Space, or tap it again to set the sculpture down before the melt bar runs out. Guess wrong, or dawdle until it melts, and you lose a sculpture. Place enough correctly to clear the round and the judges add another pedestal to track, tightening the clock for the next round. Lose three sculptures and the exhibit closes early.
