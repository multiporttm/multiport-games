Vineyard Canopy Hedging
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Row 1/6
How to play: Your tractor drives automatically down the row. Use Up/Down Arrow or W/S (or drag up/down on mobile) to raise or lower the cutter bar so it stays inside the yellow target band. Canopy trimmed inside the band scores points; drifting above the band leaves leaves uncut, and dropping below it cuts into the fruit zone and costs a life. Rows get longer and the target band narrower each round. 3 lives — finish all 6 rows to win!
