Blend cyan, magenta and yellow pigment to match the target swatch, then lock in your mix.
How to play: A target color swatch sits beside your mixing palette. Raise or lower the cyan, magenta and yellow pigment sliders using the on-screen buttons, your finger, or the 1/Q, 2/W and 3/E keys, watching the mixed well update live as you blend toward the target shade shown beside it. When the mix looks close, lock it in with the confirm button, space bar, or by tapping the mixed well; a near-exact match earns a big score bonus, while a rough match still scores something smaller, and the shared timer bar at the top keeps draining the whole time you spend mixing. Match swatch after swatch, each with a trickier target color and a shorter timer, to blend your way to the highest score you can before the palette timer runs dry.
