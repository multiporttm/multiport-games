Beekeeping Swarm Capture
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: A runaway swarm of bees is loose in the meadow! Move your smoker with the arrow keys or WASD, or by dragging/tapping on the field (touch), and release a puff of smoke with Space or a tap near the swarm — bees drift away from smoke, so herd them gently toward the wooden capture box. Get the whole swarm inside before too many escape off-screen. Each level brings a bigger, faster, jumpier swarm.
