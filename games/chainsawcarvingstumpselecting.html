Chainsaw Carving Stump Selecting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Stump 1
A fresh tree stump is rolled up for inspection. Check its width, look for dark rot blotches and jagged crack lines across the face. Press → / D or tap ✅ Select if it is wide, solid and crack-free — good enough to carve a sculpture from. Press ← / A or tap ❌ Reject if it is too small, cracked or rotten. Decide before the clock bar runs out! Three wrong calls and your carving lot runs out of usable wood.
