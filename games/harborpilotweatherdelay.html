How to play: Each ship movement comes with a wind, visibility, and wave report. Decide GO (safe to proceed), DELAY (wait it out, conditions are marginal), or NO-GO (too dangerous, cancel) by clicking a button, tapping, or pressing G / D / N before the clock runs out. A wrong call or a timeout costs a life. Correct calls get trickier and the clock gets shorter every shift.
