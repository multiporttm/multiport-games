Organ Windchest Building
Level: 1 Lives: ♥♥♥ Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Select a channel, then place parts in order: Valve, Pallet, Seal
Select a channel by clicking/tapping it or moving with the arrow keys, then place its parts in the correct order — Valve, then Pallet, then Seal — by clicking the tray buttons or pressing 1, 2, 3. Get every channel sealed in order before the wind pressure timer runs out, or a wrong-order part will leak air into the next channel. Windchests grow bigger and trickier each level!
