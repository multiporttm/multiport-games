Notes scroll toward the yellow hit-line to the beat, but this is reggae — the orange notes are the off-beats, and those are the only ones you should hit! Press the SKANK button (or spacebar / tap) exactly as an orange note crosses the line. The dull green notes are the main beat — hitting those, or hitting nothing on an orange note, breaks your combo. Tempo and rhythm complexity increase each round.
