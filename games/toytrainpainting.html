Follow the reference strip: pick the matching color swatch (click/tap or keys 1-5), then click/tap the train section it belongs on (body, wheels, trim, numbering — or keys Q W E R). Wait for each coat to dry (the bar fills) before painting the next section, or you'll smudge it!
