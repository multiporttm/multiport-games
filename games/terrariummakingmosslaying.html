🌿 Terrarium Making Moss Laying
Score: 0 Round: 1 Best: 0
Watch the trowel gauge sweep side to side above the soil bed. Press Space, click, or tap the moment the marker sits in the bright green zone to tuck down an even, springy tuft of moss. A sloppy press leaves a bald patch and chips away your coverage meter — empty it and the bed is ruined. Five clean tufts in a row and the sweep quickens for the next patch.
