Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) to charge the swing-power meter, which oscillates back and forth. Release exactly when the needle is inside the green lock zone to snap-fold the leg cleanly. Releasing too early or too late bends the hinge and drains Hinge Integrity — let it empty and the leg mechanism breaks.
