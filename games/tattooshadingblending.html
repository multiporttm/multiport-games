Tattoo Shading Blending
Score: 0 Design: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Drag across the design (mouse or touch) to lay soft ink passes, or use Arrow keys to move the shading cursor and Space to pass over a cell. Build each cell up to match the faint target density — dark on the left blending smoothly to light on the right. Overworking a cell past the target causes a blowout and costs a life. Press Enter (or the button) to step back and check the shading before the timer runs out!
