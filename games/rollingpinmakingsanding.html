🪵 Rolling Pin Making Sanding
Tap left and right in a steady alternating rhythm, in time with the pulsing ring, to drag the sandpaper block smoothly back and forth over the pin.
A pulsing ring tells you the beat. Tap the LEFT half of the frame when the ring is on the left and the RIGHT half when it's on the right (or use the Left/Right arrow keys) to push the sanding block back and forth in rhythm. Hitting the beat builds Surface Smoothness; tapping the wrong side or missing the beat gouges and burns the wood, draining it. Empty smoothness ends the run.
