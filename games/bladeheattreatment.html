Blade Heat Treatment
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●● Phase: Heat
Heating: hold Up Arrow or press/hold the forge (click/tap) to heat the blade; release when the temperature needle sits in the non-magnetic critical zone and press Space (or tap Quench) to quench. Quench too early, too late, or in the wrong medium and you lose a life. Tempering: each cycle, press Space (or tap Temper) when the oven needle is in the green zone, repeating for every required cycle. Three lives — the critical-temperature zone narrows and more tempering cycles are needed each level.
