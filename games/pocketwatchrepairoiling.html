Pocket Watch Repair Oiling
Round: 1
Pivot: 0/0
Time: 30s
Hi Round: 0
Move the applicator (mouse, arrow keys, or touch drag) over a glowing pivot, then hold the mouse/touch down or press Space to feed oil. Watch the drop gauge — release once it sits in the glowing safe zone. Overfill and dust sticks to the pivot (fail); underfill and it stays dry. Oil every pivot before time runs out to advance; more pivots and a tighter gauge await each round.
