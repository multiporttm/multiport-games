Kayak Cockpit Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Cockpit 1/7
How to play: Drag the orange cockpit rim (or nudge it with the Arrow Keys) onto the dashed template outline on the deck, and rotate it with the Rotate buttons or Q / E keys until the bolt-hole marks line up. Press Set Rim (or Enter) to lock it in — if the gap around the seal and the bolt alignment are both within tolerance, the cockpit fits! A bad attempt costs a life and shows you the gap so you can correct it. Cutout shapes get trickier and tolerances tighten every round — lose all 3 lives and the deck is scrapped!
