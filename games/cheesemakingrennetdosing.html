Cheesemaking Rennet Dosing
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Read each vat's recipe chart for its target number of drops. Click the dropper (or press Space) to add one drop of rennet to the selected vat, use Left/Right Arrow or tap a vat to select it, then click Stir (or press Enter) once the dose is exact. Finish every vat before the timer runs out — over- or under-dosing spoils the batch!
