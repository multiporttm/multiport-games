Step 1: hold and drag the knife left-right across the frame to shave off the wax caps evenly (don't dig in too deep!). Step 2: once uncapped, use Left/Right Arrow or A/D to control the extractor's spin speed and keep the needle in the green zone until the honey spins out. Too slow wastes time, too fast cracks the comb!
