Needle Felting Wool Tabbing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Tuft 1
A barbed felting needle bobs up and down over a loose wool tuft. Press Space, tap, or click 🪡 Tab Needle the instant the tip enters the bright felting band so it catches fibers and packs them tight. Tab too early or late and you miss the fibers — and tabbing too fast in a row overworks the needle until it snaps. Pack every tuft to move on — three snapped needles and the project is ruined!
