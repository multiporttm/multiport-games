How to play: Adjust Drill Speed (Up/Down arrows) and Drill Pressure (Left/Right arrows), or use the on-screen buttons. Each ice layer has a different safe zone (shown as white marks on the gauges) — drilling too fast or too hard for the current layer builds up Core Stress. Keep stress low while reaching 100m depth to extract an unbroken core with readable climate-history layers. Let stress max out and the core snaps!
