Traffic Light Timing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Survived: 0s
How to play: Only one road can have the green light at a time. Click a road (or press its number key, 1-6) to send the green light there — cars on that approach pass through and score points, while every other road keeps queuing up. Switching costs a brief all-red safety gap, so time it well. New roads join the intersection the longer you survive. If any road's queue overflows, it's gridlock — game over!
