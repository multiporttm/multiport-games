Drag each crosshair toward the center ring with its screws, then hold both steady together.
How to play: Two crosshairs, one per barrel, drift around the collimator screen. Click and drag a crosshair directly, or select it with SWAP BARREL and nudge it with the arrow keys or the on-screen screw buttons, to walk it toward the center ring. Each barrel's own mechanical bias keeps tugging its crosshair back off true, so small frequent corrections beat one big shove. Both crosshairs must sit inside the center ring at the same time, held steady for a moment, to collimate the pair; overcorrecting sends a crosshair flying past center and costs turn budget, so work both barrels evenly before the turn budget bar runs out.
