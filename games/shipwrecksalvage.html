Shipwreck Salvage
Salvage: $0Best: $0
Steer your diver with WASD / Arrow Keys or by dragging on the canvas (touch or mouse). Swim through the wreckage to collect glowing treasure — bigger gems are worth more. Avoid drifting debris (it costs you oxygen on contact) and murky low-visibility fog patches that hide nearby dangers. Your oxygen drains over time; surface value comes from every treasure grabbed before you run out. Reach 0 oxygen and the dive ends.
