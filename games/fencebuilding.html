Fence Building

Work the marked spot left-to-right
Each dashed box is a fence-post spot. Click the active (yellow) spot to dig and set the post — a balance bar pops up, moving side to side; click Level It right when the green marker sits in the center zone to set it plumb. Then click again to attach the rail, and once more to attach the picket. Complete every post in order — a mistimed level or an out-of-order click costs points. Finish the whole line to move on to a longer, trickier fence!
