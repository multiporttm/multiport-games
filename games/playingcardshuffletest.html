Playing Card Shuffle Test
❤❤❤
Lv 1
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
New decks roll down the conveyor through the riffle-shuffle tester. Smooth decks ripple cleanly — let those pass. Defective decks jam, stick, or spray cards unevenly — Click or Tap them (or use Arrow Keys to select and Space to reject) before they reach the end. Rejecting a good deck or missing a bad one costs a life. The tells get subtler and the line speeds up with trickier finishes each level.
