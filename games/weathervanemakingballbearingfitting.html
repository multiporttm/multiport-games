Weathervane Making Ball Bearing Fitting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Batch 1
A rotor housing needs its bearings fitted, and a cradle sits on a track beneath three sockets of different sizes while bearings drop one at a time from above. Use ← →, tap the ◀ Slide / Slide ▶ buttons, or drag on the track to line the cradle up under the socket that matches the falling bearing's size before it lands. Fit it right and the rotor spins smoother; fit the wrong size and the bearing jams, costing you a life — three jams and the rotor seizes! Clear enough bearings to move to a faster batch with trickier sizes.
