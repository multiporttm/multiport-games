Clockmaking Hand Alignment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Clock 1
Match the reference time shown at the top by rotating each hand onto the center post: drag around the dial, or select a hand with Tab and rotate with the arrow keys. Lock a hand in with a click/tap/Enter once it is lined up — but hands must never touch or cross each other or they'll snag and jam the movement. More hands and tighter angle tolerances appear on later clocks.
