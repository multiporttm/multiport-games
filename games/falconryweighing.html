Falconry Weighing
Score: 0 Round: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Each falcon arrives at a random weight. Use −/+ (or Left/Right Arrow for ±2g, Shift+Left/Right for ±10g) to feed or withhold food until the needle sits inside the green target band, then press Fly Falcon (or Enter/Space) to send it hunting. Settle in too few adjustments for bonus points — the target band narrows and the starting weight gets wilder every round.
