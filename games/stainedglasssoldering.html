Stained Glass Soldering
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Panel: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Press mouse/finger down on the glowing dot and drag the soldering iron along the copper seam. Move too fast and you leave a gap; linger too long in one spot and solder pools into a blob (watch the blob meter below the panel — it empties while you move steadily and fills while you dawdle). Reach the end with a clean bead to score. Three gaps/blobs or strayed seams and it is game over. Panels get bigger and the safe speed window gets tighter as you go.
