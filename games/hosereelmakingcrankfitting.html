⚙️ Hose Reel Making Crank Fitting
Use the rotate buttons (or arrow keys) to turn the crank gear and line its notch up with the spinning shaft marker. Tap FIT (or Space) while they're aligned to lock it in before the timer runs out!
Tap the ↺ / ↻ buttons (or Left/Right arrow keys) to rotate the crank gear's notch step by step. The shaft marker spins on its own — watch it and time your rotation so the two notches line up. The instant they're aligned, tap FIT (or press Space/Enter) to lock the crank onto the shaft before the Shaft Timer runs out. Each successful fit speeds up the shaft and shrinks your fitting window for the next round.
