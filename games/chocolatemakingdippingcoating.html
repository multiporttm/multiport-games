Chocolate Making Dipping Coating
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1/8
How to play: Press and hold Space / Arrow Down (or press and hold anywhere on the canvas) to lower the chocolate center into the pool of tempered chocolate. Watch the timing gauge fill as it dips — release right when the needle is inside the glowing target band to build a smooth, even shell for that piece. Three good dips finish the round. Release too early and a thin spot shows through; hold too long and chocolate drips and pools — either way the piece is ruined and you lose a life. Each round narrows and shifts the target band!
