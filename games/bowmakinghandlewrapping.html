Bow Making Handle Wrapping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Grip 1
A length of waxed leather cord is being wound around the bow's bare grip, and each loop needs to be cinched down at just the right tension — too loose and the wrap will slip, too tight and the cord will snap. Use the Left/Right arrow keys, drag, or tap ◀ Ease / Tighten ▶ to swing the tension needle back and forth, and press Space, tap, or click 🎀 Cinch the instant the needle sits in the glowing target band to lock that loop snugly in place. Cinch it wrong and the loop unravels — three unraveled loops ruins the wrap! Finish every loop in a grip to move on to a longer, trickier handle.
