Chocolate Making Tempering
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1/8
How to play: Hold Arrow Up (or tap/hold the top half of the pan) to heat the chocolate, and Arrow Down (or tap/hold the bottom half) to cool it over the ice bath. Guide the thermometer needle through each stage of the tempering curve — melt, cool and temper — keeping it inside the glowing safe zone shown on the gauge and chart until the progress ring fills. Let the chocolate scorch above the red danger line and you will ruin the batch and lose a life. Complete all three stages to finish a batch and move to the next, trickier curve!
