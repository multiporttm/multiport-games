Jukebox Restoration Selector Button Wiring
Panel: 1 Time: 0 Score: 0 Best: 0
Click or tap a numbered selector button on the left, then click or tap the matching terminal on the right with the same number to wire it up. On keyboard, press a number key to select that button, then press it again (or press Enter) to confirm the highlighted terminal match. Wire every button correctly before the panel clock runs out — a wrong terminal costs time!
