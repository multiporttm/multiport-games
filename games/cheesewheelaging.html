Cheese Wheel Aging
Score: 0 Day: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Each shelf slot holds an aging wheel. When a wheel flashes "NEEDS FLIP", press its number key 1-4 or tap it to flip & brush it before time runs out, or its rind spoils. When a wheel's ripeness bar glows inside the gold window, press/tap it again to pull it for sale — the bigger the bar's reward zone you hit, the more it's worth. Let a wheel over-ripen past the window and it spoils too. Lose a life per spoiled wheel — more shelves and faster flip demands open up as the days go by.
