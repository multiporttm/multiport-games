☀️ Solar Light Making Panel Wiring
Tap two terminals of the same color to connect a wire pair. Clear every pair on the panel before the charge timer runs out.
Tap or click two terminals of the same color (or use Tab/arrow keys + Space/Enter) to wire them together; a correct pair lights up and disappears, a mismatched pair sparks and costs Battery Charge. Clear every terminal on the panel before the round timer bar runs dry to advance; let Battery Charge empty and the panel shorts out.
