Papier-Mâché Armature Building
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Frame 1/8
Click pieces in blueprint order, or use ← → and Space/Enter
How to play: The top strip is the blueprint — it shows the exact order of wire and cardboard pieces needed to build the frame. Click the matching piece in the tray below (or use Arrow Keys to highlight one and Space/Enter to tape it on). Pick pieces in the right order and the frame grows sturdy and solid. Pick the wrong one and a joint comes loose — you lose a life and must redo the frame. Frames get more intricate every round — lose all 3 lives and the armature collapses!
