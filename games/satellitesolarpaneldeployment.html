Satellite Solar Panel Deployment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lives: 3 | Array: 1
How to play: A deployment indicator sweeps back and forth across each folded panel hinge. Press Space, click, or tap DEPLOY exactly when the indicator is inside the green lock zone to unfold and lock that panel. Panels must be deployed left to right, in order — missing the zone or deploying out of order costs a life. Lock every panel to complete the array and move to a trickier multi-panel design!
