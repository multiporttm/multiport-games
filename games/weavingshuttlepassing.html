Weaving Shuttle Passing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Speed 1
How to play: The shuttle glides back and forth across the warp. Press Space or click/tap the instant it crosses the glowing open-shed window to weave a clean row. Too early, too late, or missing the window entirely costs a life. Weave enough rows and the shuttle speeds up with a narrower window.
