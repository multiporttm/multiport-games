Puppetry Voice Throwing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: A teal target band scrolls across the stage showing the pitch and volume your thrown voice needs to reach for the distant puppet to "speak." Press and hold Space, the HOLD TO THROW VOICE button, or your finger to raise your yellow voice line, and release to let it fall. Keep the line inside the moving teal band as much as possible — your breath meter rises while you're inside it and falls while you drift outside. Empty the meter and you lose a life; survive the waveform to advance. Every round brings a trickier, faster waveform and a narrower target band. Lose all 3 lives and your voice throw fails!
