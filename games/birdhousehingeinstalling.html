Birdhouse Hinge Installing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Door 1/8
How to play: A cleanout door shows the hinge diagram with marked screw positions. Click (or Tab/Arrow Keys + Enter) the next glowing marked hole to position the driver there. Then hold Space / click-and-hold / press-and-hold to drive the screw and release to stop — keep the depth needle inside the green safe band and release while it's inside to set the screw. Drive it too deep into the red zone and the wood splits, costing a life. Set every screw to finish the hinge — more screws and a narrower safe band appear each round!
