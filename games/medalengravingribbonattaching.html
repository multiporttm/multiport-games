Medal Engraving Ribbon Attaching
Round 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag the ribbon loop onto the marked spot shown on the medal diagram, then hold Space (or press and hold on touch) to crimp the fitting — release when the gauge needle is inside the green safe band. Crimp too weak and it falls off; too hard and you crush the medal's edge. Use arrow keys to nudge the ribbon for precise placement.
