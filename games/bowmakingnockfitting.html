A water-buffalo horn nock needs to be carved and seated onto the bow's tip, and that takes getting two things right at once: the carving angle of the string groove and how deep the nock seats onto the tip. Use the A/D keys or tap ◀ Angle / Angle ▶ to adjust the groove angle, use the W/S keys or tap ▼ Depth / Depth ▲ to adjust the seating depth, and watch both bars glow green when they sit inside their target zones together. Press Space, tap, or click 🔩 Fit Nock once both are glowing to lock the nock in place — fit it with either bar off target and the horn cracks! Fit every nock on a tip to move on to a trickier, narrower-tolerance tip.
