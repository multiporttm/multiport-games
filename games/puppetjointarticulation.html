Select a joint with Up/Down Arrow (or tap it) and use Left/Right Arrow (or drag) to tune its pin tension into the green zone — too loose and the limb flops, too stiff and it locks. Press Space/Enter to test the puppet once every joint is set. You have 3 lives — more joints are added each round!
