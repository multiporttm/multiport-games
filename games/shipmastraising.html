Ship Mast Raising
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Press Space / Up Arrow or tap Haul Tackle to pull the mast upright in controlled increments. Use Left/Right Arrow or the Guy Rope buttons to counter any lean and keep the mast within the safe zone while it rises — let it swing too far and it comes crashing down.
