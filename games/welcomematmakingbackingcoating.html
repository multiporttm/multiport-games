🧽 Welcome Mat Making Backing Coating
Press and drag the roller left to right across the mat to spread liquid rubber backing. Roll at a steady, moderate pace — too fast leaves it thin, too slow pools it thick. Cover the whole mat before time runs out!
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or hold Space while using Arrow keys) and drag the roller steadily across the mat from left to right to spread an even rubber backing coat. Rolling too fast leaves thin, cracked patches; rolling too slow pools thick, heavy spots — both hurt your coat score. Cover the whole mat within the time limit each round; a poor batch costs a point of Batch Quality, and losing it all ends your shift.
