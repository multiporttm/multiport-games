Special Effects Makeup Prosthetics
Level 1 Place the piece Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤
How to play: Drag the silicone prosthetic piece (mouse, touch, or arrow keys) onto the dashed target zone on the actor's skin. Once it snaps into place, hold the mouse/finger down (or hold Space) and sweep around the seam to feather the edge until the blend meter fills to 100% before the timer runs out. Miss the deadline and you lose a life — later levels bring trickier, longer seams with less tolerance.
