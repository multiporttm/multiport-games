Body Painting Glitter Applying
Lives: 3 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Move to each glowing wet patch and shake glitter onto it before it dries out.
How to play: A stylized mannequin figure is covered in fresh wet paint patches for a festival look, and glowing patches pop up one at a time waiting for glitter. Move your glitter shaker over a patch with the mouse or your finger, or steer it with the arrow keys or WASD, then press Space or the Shake button while standing on the patch to dust it. Shake while the patch still glows bright for a big sparkle bonus, shake again as it starts to fade for a smaller reward, but if it dries out completely before you reach it, it is lost and costs you a life. Patches appear faster and fade sooner the longer your streak runs, so stay quick and precise — lose all three lives and the round ends, so chase the highest sparkle score you can before then.
