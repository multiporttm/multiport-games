Submarine Periscope Scouting
Distance: 0m | Lives: 3 | Hi: 0m
How to play: Your submarine cruises forward through 3 lanes. Use Left/Right Arrow keys or tap the left/right side of the screen to change lanes. Hold Up Arrow, Space, or the periscope button to raise the periscope — while raised you can see reefs, storms and enemy ships ahead so you can dodge them, but if an enemy ship gets too close while you're spotted you'll be detected and take damage! Release to submerge safely, but you'll be navigating blind from memory. Survive as long as you can.
