Kilt Making Fringe Trimming
Row: 1 Strand: 1/8 Score: 0 Best: 0
A shears blade swings up and down over each hanging wool fringe strand. Press Snip (or Space / tap the canvas) the instant the blade crosses the gold target line to trim the strand to the correct length. Cut too high and the fringe stays ragged; cut too low and you lose the kilt's edge. Land close snips on all 8 strands in a row to finish it and move to a faster, trickier row.
