Rotate cameo:
Drag the carved shell cameo (mouse, finger, or Arrow keys) into the bezel of the mount and use the rotate slider to true up its angle until the center ring glows green. Then click Press Nearest Prong (or Space) on each of the four prongs in turn — pressing one too early while misaligned can crack the shell! Lock all four prongs down to finish the setting. Each new mount is trickier to center.
