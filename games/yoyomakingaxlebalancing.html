🪀 Yo-Yo Making Axle Balancing
The axle is spinning off-center. Tap the lighter side to nudge weight back to center before it wobbles out of control.
Watch the axle wobble left and right as it spins. Tap the ARROW on the side the axle has tipped TOWARD (or press the Left/Right arrow keys) to nudge a counterweight and pull it back to center. Keep the Axle Balance meter above empty - if the axle tips too far for too long it snaps and the game ends. Every few successful corrections, the spin gets faster.
