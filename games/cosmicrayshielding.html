Radiation climbs in every crew compartment during the solar storm. Click/tap the compartment currently showing the highest reading to redirect your limited shielding charge there — it works at full strength only on the current highest reading, and at half strength otherwise. Recharge takes a moment between uses. Keep every compartment below 100% until the storm timer runs out to survive; each storm survived adds more compartments and faster radiation buildup.
