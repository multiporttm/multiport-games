Herb Garden Drying
Score: 0 | Hi: 01:30
Take each bundle down in its green zone — not too early, not too late!
How to play: Fresh herb bundles hang to dry, each slowly drying up on its own gauge. Click (or tap) a bundle — or press its number key — to take it down. Taking it down while the fill is inside the green zone is a perfect dry for points; too early (still damp) molds and too late (overdried) crumbles — both cost points. New bundles keep appearing, so watch them all and harvest each one at just the right moment before time runs out!
