Watch Balance Wheel Regulation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag the regulator lever left (slower) or right (faster) — or use the Left/Right Arrow keys — to bring the balance wheel's rate to the target beats-per-hour shown, then press Space (or tap Lock In) while it's within tolerance to lock the regulation. You have 3 lives; locking in outside tolerance costs one. Tolerances get tighter each watch.
