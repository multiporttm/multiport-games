How to play: Each antique piece shows its old finish type — click (or press 1-5) the chemical stripper that actually dissolves it. Then watch the neutralizer needle sweep the gauge and click Neutralize (or press Space) while it sits in the green zone. Finally brush on exactly the number of coats shown using Apply Coat (or Space), then click Seal (or Enter) — too few or too many coats ruins the finish. Wrong choices cost a life; more finishes and tighter timing arrive each round!
