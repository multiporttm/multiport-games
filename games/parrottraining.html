Click "Start Training" to teach your parrot its first trick!
How to play: Each round, a sequence of trick icons lights up — watch closely. Then click the icons back in the same order (number keys 1-9 also work) to teach your parrot the pattern. After you succeed, the parrot demonstrates the trick back to you — but it sometimes makes a mistake! If it does, click Show Again to re-demonstrate the pattern and correct it. Each round the sequence gets one step longer. Score is the number of tricks your parrot has fully learned.
