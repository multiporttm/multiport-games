How to play: Click a symbol in the palette to select it, then click a slot on the black flag to place it (click a filled slot to remove it). Arrange symbols to exactly match the captain's requested count of each symbol — no more, no less — then click Submit Design. Arrow keys move the slot cursor; Enter/Space places or clears it. Three rejected designs and you're relieved of flag duty!
