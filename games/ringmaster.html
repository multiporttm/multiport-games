You're the Ringmaster! Tap an act card, then tap an empty time slot to book it into your show's running order — tap a filled slot to send that act back to the pool. Two acts that need the same prop (🎪 ring, 🪢 rope, ⭕ hoop) can't run back-to-back — they need setup time, and neighboring same-prop acts get flagged and penalized. Crowds love a show that builds — arrange rising excitement toward a big finale for a pacing bonus. Fill every slot you can, then click Curtain Up! to score your show. Each round adds more acts and slots.
