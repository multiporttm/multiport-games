How to play: A glowing raku pot is cooling in the open air. Press and hold the Cool button (or Down Arrow / Space / tap-and-hold the canvas) to accelerate the cooling rate, and release to let it coast and slow down. The gold band in the meter is the safe thermal-shock zone: keep the cooling rate inside that band and crackle lines spider across the glaze, earning you crackle score. Stray below the band and nothing cracks; stray above it for too long and the pot shatters, ending the piece. Grow enough crackle coverage before your temperature runs out to complete the piece and move to a trickier glaze.
