S'mores Making
Perfect: 0 / 0Best: 0
Hold the Roast button (or Space) to roast your marshmallow over the fire. Release when the white marker sits inside the golden zone — too early is raw, too late is burnt. Then click the pieces in order: graham cracker, roasted marshmallow (already placed), chocolate, graham cracker — before the cooling bar runs out. Make as many perfect s'mores as you can; it gets trickier each round!
