Cheese Brine Soaking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Cheese wheels drop into salt brine tanks and each has its own ideal soak window, shown as a green band on its timer ring. Click/tap a tank (or press its number key) to pull its wheel the moment the ring's pointer enters the green band — too early (under-salted) or too late (over-salted) costs a life. More tanks open and the windows get tighter as rounds advance. Lose all 3 lives and brining is over!
