Beekeeping Smoker Lighting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: Press and hold Space, click, or touch-and-hold to pump the bellows of your smoker. Each pump pushes the smoke gauge up; it drifts down (faster in wind) when you let go. Keep the needle inside the green safe band for the full hold timer to calm the hive and clear the level. Too little smoke and the bees get defensive; too much and you smother the fire — both drain a life. Wind gusts and longer hold times make each level tougher!
