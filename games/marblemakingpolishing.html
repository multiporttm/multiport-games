⚪ Marble Making Polishing
A dull patch spins around the marble's rim. Tap it exactly where it sits on the edge to buff it bright.
A dull gray patch orbits around the rim of the spinning marble. Click or tap directly on the patch while it is on the rim to buff it to a shine. Tapping the wrong spot, or letting a patch spin past unpolished, drains the Marble Shine meter - empty it and the marble is scrapped. Each clean buff speeds up the spin for the next patch.
