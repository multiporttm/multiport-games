📦 Piggy Bank Making Packaging
Score: 0 Round: 1 Best: 0
Watch the tape-dispenser gauge sweep side to side. Press Space, click, or tap the moment the marker is in the bright green zone to wrap the tissue and seal the box tightly around the finished piggy bank. A careless seal chips away your packing-care meter — lose it all and a box ships open and damaged. Five tight seals in a row and the conveyor speeds up, so the pace quickens.
