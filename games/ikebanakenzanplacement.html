How to play: The kenzan (spiky flower frog) grid shows a glowing pin — that's where the next stem must be spiked. Click/tap that exact pin (or move the highlighted cursor with arrow keys and press Space) before the timer runs out. Hitting the wrong pin or a pin already full counts as a miss. Each successful spike speeds up the next round. Three misses ends the session.
