Drag the dial on the chart, or use the buttons / Left-Right arrow keys, to set your heading.
How to play: Each leg shows the waypoint's bearing/distance and the current's speed/direction. Rotate the compass dial — drag it, use Left/Right Arrows, or the ± buttons — to set the heading to steer that compensates for the current's drift, then press Set Course & Run (or Enter) to simulate the passage. Watch the GPS fixes track along your course; land within the tolerance circle around the waypoint to complete the leg. The tolerance narrows and the current gets trickier each leg — three missed plots and the passage ends!
