Wheelwright Iron Hooping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Tire 1
A red-hot iron tire has just been dropped over the wooden wheel, and it is shrinking fast as it cools, cinching the whole wheel tight. Press Space, tap, or click ⚙️ Hammer the instant the shrinking ring crosses each glowing anchor point to drive it down flush — too early and the iron is still too loose to seat, too late and it has already seized cold and immovable. Miss three blows and the tire cools crooked, ruining the wheel! Seat every anchor point around a tire to move to the next tire, which shrinks and cools faster.
