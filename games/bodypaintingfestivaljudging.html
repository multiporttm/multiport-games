Scan the design with the highlight box. Flag a flaw if you spot one, or call it clear if the design looks flawless.
How to play: A contestant walks onto your judging stage wearing a painted design that might be flawless, or might be hiding one small smudge, bare gap, or mismatched patch. Move the highlight box across the design with the mouse or your finger, or steer it with the arrow keys or WASD, scanning every part before the walk-on window closes. If the highlight box passes directly over a flaw you will see it called out; press the flag button or space bar the moment you spot one. If you scan the whole design and find nothing wrong, press the all-clear button or enter key instead. Correctly flagging a real flaw or correctly clearing a flawless design earns points and builds your judging streak, while missing a flaw or wrongly flagging a clean design breaks the streak and costs accuracy. Judge contestant after contestant, each with a shorter stage-time bar and a sneakier flaw placement, to reach the highest judging score you can.
