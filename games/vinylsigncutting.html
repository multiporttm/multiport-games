How to play: Cutting: drag your mouse/finger (or Arrow Keys) to track the glowing bead along the design outline at a steady pace — too slow and the pressure builds up and tears through the backing; too fast and the blade does not finish the cut before time runs out. Weeding: once cut, click/tap (or move with Arrow Keys and press Space/Enter) every dim excess square to peel it away before time runs out — touch a glowing letter square and you tear the letter, costing a life. Six increasingly intricate designs await — lose all 3 lives and the sign is scrapped!
