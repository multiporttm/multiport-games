Music Box Spring Winding
Lives: 3Level 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Wind the spring to the target zone, then play the song
Left/Right or A/D wind/release the spring · Space/Tap start playback or restart
Wind a music box's mainspring to just the right tension before it plays a full song. Use Left/Right or A/D to wind up or release tension, and Space or Tap to start the song once you're in the target zone. Wind too little and the song stalls partway; wind too much and the spring snaps. Songs get longer and need more winding as you level up.
