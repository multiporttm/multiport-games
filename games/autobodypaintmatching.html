Auto Body Paint Matching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Job 1
How to play: The left swatch is the factory panel color; the right swatch is your mix. Select a pigment with Left/Right Arrow and adjust it with Up/Down Arrow, or drag each slider directly with mouse/finger. When you think it's close, tap the SPRAY TEST button (or press Enter) to compare under the shop light — match within tolerance to finish the job. Each job gives you limited spray attempts; run out without a match and you lose a life. More pigments, tighter tolerance and fewer attempts arrive every job — lose all 3 lives and the mix is scrapped!
