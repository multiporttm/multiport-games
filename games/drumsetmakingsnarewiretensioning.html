🥁 Snare Wire Tensioning
Drag the slider up or down to match each wire's target tension mark, then let go to clamp it. Clamp close to the mark for bonus points before the timer bar runs out.
Drag (mouse, touch, or Up/Down arrows) the tensioner slider to line it up with the target mark on each snare wire, then release to clamp it in place. The closer to the mark, the more points you earn. A Clamp Timer counts down for each wire - clamp too far off the mark or let the timer run out too many times and the snare bed is scrapped.
