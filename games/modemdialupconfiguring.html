Modem Dial-Up Configuring
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrows (or tap a setting) to pick Baud Rate, Init String, or COM Port, then Up/Down Arrows (or the +/− buttons) to change its value. Press Space / tap Dial to attempt the connection — each setting turns green if correct, or shows ▲/▼ to nudge it. Run out of attempts and you lose a life. 3 lives, limited attempts per level.
