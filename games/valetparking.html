Valet Parking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | ❤❤❤
 
Use Arrow Keys / WASD (or the pad) to drive. Push an arriving numbered car into any empty grey spot to park it — don't ram parked cars. Once parked, drive your empty valet cursor to the requested ticket number and press Space / ✋ to fetch it back to the exit before time's up.
