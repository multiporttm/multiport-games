Ribbon Integrity
Weave the maypole ribbon in time with the beat! Watch the notes fall toward the target ring — when one arrives, press Up Arrow (or tap OVER) for an over-weave or Down Arrow (or tap UNDER) for an under-weave, matching the symbol shown. A wrong direction or a missed beat tangles the ribbon and drains its integrity.
