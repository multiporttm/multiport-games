Typewriter Restoration Decal Restoring
Decals Set: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Slide the gold decal onto its faded outline, then hold Burnish to press it down.
How to play: A lifted gold decal floats over the typewriter's black body panel, and a faded outline shows exactly where it belongs. Move it with the arrow keys, A/W/S/D, the on-screen drag, or by dragging/tapping on the canvas until it sits over the outline. Once it is reasonably well aligned, hold the Burnish button (or press and hold Space) to smooth it down — the burnish meter fills while you hold, but holding while badly misaligned creases the decal and some progress bleeds back out. Burnish it fully while centered to set it crisply for top points, or finish slightly off-center for a smaller reward. Letting the varnish timer run out before the current decal is set loses that decal; set every decal on the panel before time runs out to finish the restoration and move to a harder panel with more decals.
