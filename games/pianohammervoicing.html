Piano Hammer Voicing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Move the voicing needle over the hammer felt with the mouse/touch (or Left/Right Arrow keys), choose a depth with the Up/Down Arrow keys or the slider, and Click/tap (or press Space) to needle that spot. Shoulder jabs brighten, crown-center jabs soften — bring the tone bar to match the target zone before you run out of needle jabs. You have 3 lives; a hammer ruined by over-needling costs one. Targets get narrower each hammer.
