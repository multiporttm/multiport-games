⚖️ Weighing Scale Making Spring Calibrating
A test weight bounces the spring's needle up and down the gauge. Press the instant the needle crosses the reference line to lock in a calibrated reading.
Watch the bouncing spring needle and press (click, tap, or Space/Enter) exactly when it crosses the green reference line to calibrate the spring. Good calibrations raise your score and speed up the bounce; missed ones overstretch the coil and drain your Spring Tension meter — let it empty and the spring snaps.
