Orchard Ladder Safety
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Tree 1
How to play: Use Left/Right Arrow (or tap the left/right sides) to set the ladder's foot position against the tree, aiming for the safe-angle zone, then press Space or tap the top to plant it. Once planted, climb with Up Arrow or tap, and correct your balance with Left/Right as wind and reaching for fruit rock the ladder. Let it tip past the edge and you lose a life. Clear enough fruit to move on to a taller tree.
