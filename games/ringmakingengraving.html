🎨 Ring Making Engraving

Steady the graver over the band. Press when the stroke marker sweeps into the green zone to cut a perfect line.

A graver marker sweeps across the engraving gauge. Tap, click, or press Space/Enter the moment it enters the green zone to cut a clean line of the inscription. A shaky miss slips the tool and drains your steady-hand focus - empty it and the engraving is ruined. Five clean strokes in a row speed up the next round.
