Leather Carving Background Stamping
Round: 1Score: 0Hi: 0Lives: ❤❤❤
Stamp every glowing cell around the design — avoid the carved area!
How to play: The diagram shows a carved design (dark crosshatched cells, protected) surrounded by background cells that still need texture. Click or tap each glowing background cell to stamp it with the mallet, or move the highlighted cursor with the Arrow Keys and press Space/Enter to stamp. Cover every marked cell before the timer runs out to finish the round — striking the protected carved area costs you a life. Grids grow larger and the timer gets tighter each round. Lose all 3 lives and the hide is spoiled!
