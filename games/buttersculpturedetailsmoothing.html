❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Area 1
The rough-carved folds of drapery on the butter cow still show every tool mark, and a warm palette knife needs to trace the groove edge to edge without wandering off the surface. Use ↑ / ↓ or W / S, or drag with mouse/touch, to keep the glowing knife tip riding the wavy guide line as it scrolls past — staying on the line fills the polish meter, while drifting off it fills the mistake meter. Fill the polish meter before the mistake meter maxes out to finish the area and move to a trickier fold with a twistier groove and less room for error. Max out the mistake meter three times and the sculpture is left rough for judging.
