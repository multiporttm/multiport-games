Barbershop Straight Razor Shave
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round: 1 ❤❤❤
Drag the razor (mouse / touch) across each lathered patch with the grain, following the arrow shown on it. On keyboard, use Tab/Arrow keys to focus a patch and press A (down-left), S (straight down) or D (down-right) to match its grain arrow. Shave every patch before time runs out — shaving against the grain or missing causes a nick. Three nicks and it's game over!
