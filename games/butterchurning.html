Butter Churning
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Press Space repeatedly (or tap the Crank button) at a steady rhythm to turn the churn through whipped cream, the breaking point, and into butter grains. Press Enter (or tap Stop Churning) the moment the gauge is in the golden butter zone. Stop too early and it isn't butter yet; keep cranking past the zone and it over-churns. The zone shrinks and cream warms faster every level.
