Get ready...
Hold Space (or the Blow button) to keep the breath-pressure meter inside the golden sweet zone — that's what gives the reed its clean tone. When a note reaches the hit line, press 1, 2 or 3 (or tap the matching hole button) for its fingering. Good timing plus good pressure scores the most. Tempo and note density increase every level!
