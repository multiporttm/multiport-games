🐦 Birdbath Making Basin Molding
Drag your trowel back and forth over each lump in the wet basin rim. Glide at a calm, even speed — the gauge below shows your pace — to smooth lumps down. Dragging too fast gouges the concrete.
Hold down (mouse or finger) anywhere on the basin rim and drag side to side over the lumps. Keep your drag speed inside the glowing band on the pace gauge to smooth each lump flat — too slow does nothing, too fast gouges the concrete and drains your Basin Finish meter. Smooth every lump to advance to a lumpier basin; empty the meter and the basin cracks.
