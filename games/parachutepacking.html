Parachute Packing
Certified: 0 | Hi: 0
Strikes: 0/3
Pack the parachute in the correct order
How to play: Every pack follows the same certified sequence: straighten the suspension lines, fold canopy panels 1 through 4 in order, S-fold the lines, then stow the lines and close the deployment bag. The tiles are shuffled on the table each round — click (or tap) them in that exact order before the timer runs out. Any step clicked out of order is a packing failure (a strike). Three strikes ends the session — pack as many certified parachutes as you can before time runs out!
