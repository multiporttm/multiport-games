Grit:
Drag the mouse or your finger across the slab (or use Arrow keys to move the pad and hold Space to grind) to polish every patch evenly. Pick Coarse grit to knock down high spots fast, or switch to Fine grit for the glossy final pass — grinding any patch too long burns it black and ruins your score. Press Seal Slab once the slab is evenly glossy with no burns, before the sealant timer runs out. Even, unburned slabs score the most, and each new slab is bigger.
