Mechanical Calculator Case Polishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Case 1
How to play: Tarnish spots bloom across the case — click or tap one with your cloth while it still gleams to buff it clean. On keyboard, use the arrow keys to move the cloth reticle and Space/Enter to buff whatever spot it's over. Let a spot sit too long and it sets into a permanent stain, costing a life. Clear every spot on the case before the clock runs out — each new case brings more tarnish and a faster fade.
