Asteroid Mining
Ore: 0 Shields: 100 Time: 60 Best: 0
Use Arrow Keys/WASD or touch-drag to fly your mining ship. Press Space or tap/click to fire your mining laser. Blue-glowing asteroids are rich in ore — mine them! Avoid plain gray rocks and red enemy drones, which drain your shields. Collect as much ore as you can before time runs out or your shields hit zero.
