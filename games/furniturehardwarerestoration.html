How to play: Each brass piece names its tarnish level — click (or press 1-4) the correct cleaning method below. Then rub it clean by moving over it (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys) while holding click / tap / Space — stop once the shine meter sits inside the golden patina zone; keep rubbing past it and you'll polish the patina away! Finally click (or press 1-3) the correct empty mounting spot to reattach the piece. Wrong methods, over-polishing, or the wrong spot all cost a life.
