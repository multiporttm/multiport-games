🧵 Welcome Mat Making Fiber Tufting
Yarn tufts drop down four lanes toward the tufting gun. Tap the lane (or press 1-4) exactly as the tuft crosses the punch line to lock it into the backing cloth.
Four colored yarn tufts fall down their lanes toward the punch line. Tap the matching lane (click/tap that column, or press keys 1, 2, 3, 4) the instant a tuft reaches the line to punch it cleanly into the mat. A clean punch scores points and speeds up the gun; a missed or early/late punch frays the weave and drains your Weave Integrity meter — let it empty and the mat is ruined.
