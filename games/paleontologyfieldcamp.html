Assign your team, then click End Day.
How to play: You have 6 workers and 10 days. Each day, assign workers to three sites: Survey (reveals better dig spots), Excavate (digs up raw fossils, better on surveyed ground), and Prep (converts raw fossils into counted, scored specimens before they decay). Click the +/- buttons or press 1/2/3 to focus a site and Up/Down Arrow to assign, then click End Day. Manage your limited camp supplies and maximize total prepped fossils by the end of the season.
