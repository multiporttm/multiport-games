How to play: A glowing golden bead carries the drizzle pattern across each chocolate piece — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your piping tip inside the glowing guide path that follows it. Wander off and your chocolate meter drains; empty it and the drizzle sets wrong, costing a life. Trace cleanly the whole way to finish the piece and move on. The bead speeds up and the path narrows every piece — lose all 3 lives and the tray is ruined!
