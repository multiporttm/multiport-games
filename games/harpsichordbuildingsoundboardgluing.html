Harpsichord Building Soundboard Gluing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Rib 1
Hot hide glue cools on the brush as its tackiness level drifts back and forth across the gauge. A gold band marks the window where the glue is still warm and tacky enough to bond but not so wet it slides. Press Space, tap, or click 🪵 Press Caul the instant the gauge crosses into the gold band to clamp a rib down with a perfect bond. Pressing too early or too late leaves a weak glue joint — three failed joints and the soundboard is scrapped! Bond every rib on a board to move on to a bigger board with more ribs.
