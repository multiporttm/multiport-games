Catapult Launch
Level 1 | Score: 0 | Tries: 3 | Hi: 0
Drag back from the catapult (or touch-drag) to set power and angle, then release to launch. Arrow keys also adjust angle (Left/Right) and power (Up/Down); press Space or Enter to fire. Clear the castle wall and land on the target — watch the wind arrow! You have 3 tries per level.
