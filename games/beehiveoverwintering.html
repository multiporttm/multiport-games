Beehive Overwintering
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Winter 1/5
How to play: Click a hive (or move with Left/Right Arrow) to select it, then press 1/2/3 or tap a button below to check its food, add insulation, or reduce its entrance. Every task you finish on a hive before the cold front arrives (shown by the countdown) keeps it safe — leave any task undone when the front hits and that hive takes a hit. Each winter brings more hives and a faster, colder front. Lose all 3 lives and the apiary doesn't make it through winter!
