Ice Cream Cone Waffling
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Watch the waffle cook on the iron and press Space or Tap the instant it's in the golden zone to pull it off. Then quickly alternate Left/Right Arrow (or tap the left/right sides) to roll it into a cone before it cools and hardens flat. Rounds get faster — you have 3 lives.
