How to play: Use tweezers to place tiny plants into the soil at precise spots. A marker sweeps across the bar below the jar — tap/click the game area, or press Space or Enter, exactly when it is over the green zone to set a plant down perfectly. Miss and your steady-hand meter drops from the slip; empty it and the arrangement is ruined. Place five plants cleanly to move to a harder round with a faster, narrower zone.
