Squid Grip
How to play: The giant squid's tentacles are numbered 1-5. Watch the flash sequence shown at the top, then repeat it by pressing keys 1-5 or clicking/tapping the matching tentacles in the same order to flash your lights and startle the squid. Mistakes and running out of time damage your hull. Clear sequences to loosen the squid's grip and escape before your hull is destroyed!
