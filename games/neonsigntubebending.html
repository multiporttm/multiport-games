Neon Sign Tube Bending
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Letter 1/6
Heat
Glass
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to hold the torch over the glowing bead as it travels the letter template. Staying close to the bead heats the glass and bends it along the line — but linger too long and it will overheat and collapse. Pull away and the glass cools; if your workable-glass time runs out before the bend is finished, it hardens wrong. Keep the heat in the sweet spot all the way through to finish the letter — lose all 3 lives and the sign is scrapped!
