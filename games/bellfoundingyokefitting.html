Bell Founding Yoke Fitting
Yoke: 1 Time: 0s Score: 0 Best: 0
Use Left/Right arrow keys (or drag left/right on the canvas) to rotate the yoke until its bolt holes line up with the bell crown's fixed staples. Press Enter/Space (or tap the lit hole) to lock a bolt in once it is aligned, then watch the torque needle sweep and press Enter/Space again when it is in the green zone to drive it home. Fit every bolt before the clock runs out to hang a solid bell!
