Canyon Rappelling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Wall: 1 Time: 60
Hold (mouse/finger/Space/Down) to feed rope and descend faster, release to brake. Keep your speed in the green zone of the danger meter — going too fast risks rope burn and falling! Steer left/right with Arrow Keys / A-D or by dragging to dodge rock outcrops, and pass through golden foothold rings to rest. Reach the bottom safely before time runs out — canyon walls get taller and trickier each round.
