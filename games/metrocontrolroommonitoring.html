Trains run along four lines on the board. When an alert icon appears on a train — Delay (clock) needs a Hold, or Signal Fault (triangle) needs a Reroute — click/tap the train (or press its line number 1-4, then H to hold or R to reroute) with the matching action before it collides with the train ahead. Applying the wrong action, or reacting too late, costs a life. Three lives — more trains and faster alerts arrive every shift.
