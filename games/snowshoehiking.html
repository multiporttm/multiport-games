Snowshoe Hiking
Distance: 0 / 1000mStamina: 100%Hi: 0
How to play: Alternate Left Arrow / Right Arrow (or tap the Left Foot / Right Foot buttons) to lift-and-plant your snowshoes and walk forward. Pressing the same foot twice in a row makes you stumble and costs stamina. Deep drifts (dark patches) drain extra stamina — try to step around them. Reach the finish flag before your stamina runs out!
