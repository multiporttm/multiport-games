Leather Burnishing
Score: 0 Round: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Press and hold (mouse/touch or Space) and glide the slicker left/right (drag, or Arrow Keys) at a steady speed across the strap edge to build up gloss. Too fast scorches the leather, too slow does nothing. Finish straps before you run out of lives — it gets trickier each round.
