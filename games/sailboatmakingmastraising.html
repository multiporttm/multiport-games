⛵ Sailboat Making Mast Raising
Hold the left or right stay line to counter the wind and keep the mast plumb as it rises. Let it tip too far and it comes crashing down.
Press and hold the Port or Starboard stay button (or the Left/Right arrow keys) to pull the mast back toward center whenever a gust tilts it. The mast rises automatically while it stays near plumb; let the tilt run too far past either edge and the mast topples. Reach 100% height to win the round and face a stronger gust.
