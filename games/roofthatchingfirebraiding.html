Follow the crossing pattern: press Left, Mid, or Right to braid before the torch catches up.
How to play: Three straw straps lie across the fireproofing bench with a creeping torch sliding in from the left. A pattern of highlighted straps shows above the bench — read it and press the matching key (A/Left for the left strap, S/Down for the middle, D/Right for the right strap), tap the matching strap on the canvas, or use the on-screen Left/Mid/Right buttons, in the order shown. Each correct crossing locks in a segment of the braid and pushes the torch back a little; a wrong or missed crossing lets the torch creep forward instead. Complete enough correct crossings to finish the fire-resistant band before the torch reaches the end of the straw.
