🛡️ Shield Boss Smithing
Heat: 1 Score: 0 Best: 0
Click, tap, or press Space/Enter the instant the sliding hammer marker crosses the glowing green zone on the gauge to land a clean strike on the shield boss. A clean hit boosts your score and metal quality; a mismatched strike damages the quality meter. Land 5 clean strikes to advance to a hotter, faster heat. If the quality meter empties, the boss is ruined and the run ends!
