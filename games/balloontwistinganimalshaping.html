Balloon Twisting Animal Shaping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Animal 1/8
How to play: A diagram on the left shows the target balloon animal, split into numbered segments and twist folds. Click/tap the highlighted segment on your balloon (right side) in the order shown, or press number keys 1-9 to pick a segment. Then twist it by holding (mouse/finger down, or Space) until the tension needle sits in the green safe zone, and release at the right moment to lock the twist — too little and it slips loose, too much and it pops! Finish every segment in order without a pop to complete the animal and advance to a more complex design.
