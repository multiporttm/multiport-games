Drag your finger or mouse around the tuning knob to rotate the needle toward the glowing target frequency on the dial. Watch the signal meter climb as you get closer, then tap the Lock button (or press Space/Enter) while the needle sits in the green zone to tune in the station cleanly. Locking in with heavy static drains your Signal Health meter; let it empty and the radio goes dead.
