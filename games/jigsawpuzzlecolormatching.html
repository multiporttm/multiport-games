Jigsaw Puzzle Color Matching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1/10
How to play: A reference ink swatch glows at the top. Click or tap the one cartridge swatch below that is an exact match, or move the selection with Arrow Keys and confirm with Space or Enter. Picking a mismatched shade or letting the timer run out costs a life. Survive 10 rounds to finish the shift — every round adds more cartridges and the shades get trickier to tell apart. Lose all 3 lives and the press gets loaded with the wrong ink!
