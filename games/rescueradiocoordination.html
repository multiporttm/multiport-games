Respond to the most urgent message first!
Radio messages stack up, each tagged CRITICAL, HIGH, or NORMAL. Always respond to the single most urgent message first — click it or press its number key. Responding to the wrong one, or letting a top-priority message's timer bar run out, costs a life. Keep the operation organized!
