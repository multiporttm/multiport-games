Barber Pole Sign Painting
Level: 1 Time: 30 Lives: 3 Score: 0 Best: 0
Keys: 1/2/3 pick color · ↑/↓ move ring · Space/Enter paint · Click/tap a ring to paint it directly
Pick a paint color (red, white, or blue) then click or tap the pole's rings to repaint them. Match the barber pole's classic spiral: colors must repeat in the same cyclic order, top to bottom, in stripes of exactly the required even width. Click Check Pattern when the whole pole is painted. Get it right before time runs out — three mistakes and the shop stays closed!
