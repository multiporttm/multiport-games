Cigar Bunching
Bunch: 1 | Hi: 0
Use Arrow Keys, Mouse, or Drag to slide the bunching tray and catch falling filler leaf chunks — dodge the woody stems. Watch the weight gauge rise, then press Space, Click, or Tap to tie off the bunch while the needle sits in the glowing target band. Too light falls apart, too heavy won't draw. Bands get tighter each round!
