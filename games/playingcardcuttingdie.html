Playing Card Cutting Die
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sheet 1/6
How to play: Drag with your mouse/finger, or nudge with the Arrow Keys, to slide the dashed printed sheet onto the solid white die outline. Rotate it with Q / E (or the on-screen buttons) until the corners line up. Press Space or tap the canvas to trigger the cut once it is aligned — cutting while off target ruins the sheet and costs a life. Every sheet adds more card outlines to match and shrinks the tolerance!
