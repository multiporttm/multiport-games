Jigsaw Puzzle Mounting Board
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sheet 1/6
How to play: A glue roller sweeps across the printed puzzle sheet from left to right. Hold Space, click, or touch-and-hold to press the roller down; release to lift it. Keep the press pressure inside the glowing target band the whole way across to bond the sheet evenly onto the cardboard backing. Press too lightly and air bubbles form; press too hard and the board warps — both drain your lamination meter. Empty the meter and the sheet is ruined, costing a life. Finish all 6 sheets to complete the batch; sheets get larger and the target band narrows with every one. Lose all 3 lives and the run is scrapped!
