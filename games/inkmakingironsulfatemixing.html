Traditional iron gall ink needs its ingredients in careful balance. Each round shows a target recipe — percentages of gall extract, iron sulfate, gum arabic and water. Drag the four sliders (or select one with ←/→ and adjust it with ↑/↓) until your beaker's mix is as close to the target as possible, then press Stir Into Batch before the brewing timer runs out. The closer your match, the more points and the finer the ink — but push the timer too far and the batch spoils!
