🎹 Wire The Buttons
Tap a button terminal, then tap the wire of the same color to connect it. Wire every button before the Signal meter empties.
Tap a colored terminal on the left (a keytar button) then tap the matching colored wire-end on the right to connect them — order of wires is scrambled each round. A correct match wires the button and tops up your Signal meter; a wrong match sparks, costs Signal, and clears your pick. Wire every pair before Signal drains to zero. Each cleared round adds more buttons and drains Signal faster, so work fast and precisely to set a new high score.
