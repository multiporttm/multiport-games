Billiards Cue Balancing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Cue 1
How to play: Each cue on the bench shows a target weight and target balance point. Thread weight bolts into the five slots inside the cue's butt end — use the + / − buttons, tap a slot, or select a slot with the Left/Right arrows and adjust with Up/Down — to steer the seesaw gauge's needle into the green target zone for both weight and balance, then press Enter or Lock In Balance before the clock runs out. Guess wrong or run out of time and you lose a life. Every new cue tightens the tolerance and lengthens the bolt combination you need.
