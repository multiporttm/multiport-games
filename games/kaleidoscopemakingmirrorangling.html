Kaleidoscope Making Mirror Angling
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Mirror 1
A fresh brass kaleidoscope tube sits on the workbench with three glass mirrors propped loose inside it, and each one has to be angled just right or the reflected image will come out broken and lopsided. Use the Left/Right arrow keys, drag, or tap ◀ Tilt / Tilt ▶ to sweep the mirror's angle back and forth, and press Space, tap, or click 🔒 Lock Angle the instant the angle needle swings into the glowing target wedge to seat that mirror perfectly. Lock it too early or too late and the glass sits crooked, throwing off the whole reflection — three crooked mirrors and the tube is ruined! Seat every mirror in a tube to move on to a trickier tube with a narrower target wedge.
