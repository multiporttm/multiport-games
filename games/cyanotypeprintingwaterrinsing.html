Cyanotype Printing Water Rinsing
Prints Rinsed: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Sweep the nozzle across every stained cell until it runs clear blue, and catch falling droplets for bonus time.
How to play: A just-exposed print lies under running water, its grid of cells still stained yellow-green with unreacted iron solution. Aim the spray nozzle by dragging with the mouse or your finger, or move it with the arrow keys or WASD, sweeping the stream over every cell so it washes down into the clear, rinsed blue range shown in the middle of the color scale. Keep moving: hold the stream on one cell too long and you scrub straight through the image into a bleached-out hole, which counts against you just like a cell left unrinsed. Watch for bonus water droplets falling from the top of the frame — guide the nozzle under one to catch it for a splash of extra time on the water timer. Rinse every cell into the clear range before the water runs dry to finish the sheet cleanly, then move on to a bigger print with less time to spare, building the highest score you can.
