Violin Neck Gluing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Neck 1/8
How to play: Drag the neck (or steer with the Arrow Keys) left/right to set the angle and up/down to set the projection height. Get the crosshair inside the glowing green target zone and hold it there — the clamp will slowly tighten, filling the meter. The target creeps slightly as the glue grabs, so keep correcting. Drift out of the zone too long and the joint shifts, costing a life. The target drifts faster and the zone shrinks with every neck — glue all 8 necks to finish the batch!
