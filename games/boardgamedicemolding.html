Watch the moving fill marker and press Space, click or tap the big button (or the mold) when it lines up with the glowing zone to inject the resin and later to stop the cure timer in its target window. Then repeat the color sequence shown with the pip buttons (or keys 1-4) to polish the dice. Three bad moves costs a life — run out and it's game over. Each round brings more dice, a tighter window and longer polish sequences.
