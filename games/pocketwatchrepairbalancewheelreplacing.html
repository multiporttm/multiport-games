Pocket Watch Repair Balance Wheel Replacing
Round: 1
Stage: Remove
Time: 30s
Hi Round: 0
Step 1: Click/tap or press Space on the damaged wheel to remove it. Step 2: Drag the replacement wheel (mouse or touch) onto the crosshair marking the staff position. Step 3: Rotate it with the ⟲ ⟳ buttons or Left/Right arrow keys until the hairspring tail lines up with the dashed target angle. Press Fit Wheel (or Enter) to lock it in — any position or angle misalignment fails the attempt. Movements get trickier and tolerances tighten each round.
