Chess Piece Carving Set Matching
Sets: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Click or tap two tiles to flip them. Match the piece types before time runs out.
How to play: Carved chess pieces sit face-down on the workbench in a grid. Click or tap a tile, then another, or move the cursor with the arrow keys and press Space, to flip two tiles at a time. If their piece types match they stay face-up and fill your focus meter; if they do not match they flip back face-down and a few seconds burn off the workshop timer. Clear every tile on the board before time runs out to box up the finished set and move on to a bigger board with more piece types to track.
