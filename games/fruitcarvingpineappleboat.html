Fruit Carving Pineapple Boat
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Boat 1/8
Phase: Cut Outline
How to play: Each pineapple boat has two phases. Cut phase: a glowing bead travels the boat-shaped outline — drag your mouse or finger (or use the Arrow Keys) to keep your knife inside the yellow safe zone around it, or your precision meter drains. Scoop phase: once the outline is cut, sweep your knife back and forth inside the marked cavity to clear out the fruit chunks before the timer runs out — but stay inside the dashed cavity bounds or you will cut through the shell. Finish both phases to move to a trickier boat design — lose all 3 lives and the carving set is ruined!
