Weaving Loop Tensioning
Lives: 3 Level: 1 Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Keep every thread marker inside the green band
Every warp thread's tension drifts on its own — too loose or too tight and it snaps. Click/tap a thread to select it (or use Left/Right Arrows), then drag up/down or press Up/Down Arrows to nudge its marker back into the green band. Hold ALL threads steady in-band to fill the loom progress bar and finish the level. A snapped thread costs a life. Across looms with more threads and faster drift!
