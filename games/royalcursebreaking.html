How to play: Nine ritual symbols glow one after another — memorize the order, then click or tap them back in the exact same sequence. Each correct round adds one more symbol to the chant. Complete all three rites (growing sequences) to break the curse before the curse meter at the top fills up. A wrong click adds a big jolt to the curse meter — three rites of increasing length must be cleared to win!
