Storm Chaser Tracking
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
Steer your chase truck with the Left/Right Arrow keys (or A/D), or drag/tap left and right of the truck on touch screens. Dodge the dark red hail core, flying debris, and lightning strikes while grabbing yellow camera spots for bonus points. Hitting a hazard costs a life — lose all three and the chase ends. The road speeds up and the core widens every level.
