Hold "Pour" to fill this layer to the target line
Press and hold Hold to Pour (or Space) to fill the current resin layer toward the dashed target line shown on the gauge — release close to the line, not over or under. While the layer is wet you can click a charm icon then click inside the mold to drop it in before curing. Click Cure Layer when ready to lock it in and move to the next layer; layers cure automatically if you wait too long. Match the reference cross-section on the left across all layers for the best score. More layers are added each design.
