Fruit Carving Watermelon Basket
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Basket 1/6
How to play: A glowing bead zigzags along the handle and rim of a watermelon basket design — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your knife tip inside the tolerance band around the bead. Wander off the line and your cut accuracy drains; empty it and the rind tears, costing a life. Stay on the line the whole way to finish the basket and move to the next watermelon. Every round adds more zigzag teeth and narrows the tolerance — lose all 3 lives and the basket collapses!
