Binocular Repair Case Brassing
Cases: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Click and hold a fitting to buff it to a warm shine — don't buff through to bare metal.
How to play: A leather case is studded with tarnished brass fittings. Click and hold, touch and hold, or move the arrow-key cursor over a fitting and hold Space to buff it; its ring fills from dull tarnish toward a warm golden shine. Let go before it fills and the fitting slowly re-tarnishes, so you will need to return to it. Keep buffing after it reaches full shine and it wears through to bare metal, ruining that fitting and costing case strength. Bring every fitting on the case up to a warm shine at the same time before your case strength runs out to finish the case and move on to a more tarnished one.
