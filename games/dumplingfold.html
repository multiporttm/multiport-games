Drag from each glowing dot along the wrapper's edge to the center dot, keeping your finger or cursor inside the highlighted guide band, to fold a perfect pleat. Straying outside the band lowers your accuracy for that pleat. Complete all pleats to seal the dumpling — later dumplings need more pleats folded within a tighter band. Works with mouse or touch.
