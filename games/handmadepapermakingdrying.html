Click/tap a sheet: mist a dry-and-hot sheet, or flip a sheet whose underside is scorching. Arrow keys select, Space acts.
Freshly couched paper sheets sit on the drying rack near the kiln. Each sheet has a moisture bar and a heat bar. Watch them closely: a sheet that gets too dry while still too hot will scorch, and a sheet left face-down too long while damp will curl or mold. Click a sheet to mist it (cools and dampens) when its heat is climbing, or flip it when its underside glows red, to keep every sheet evenly cured. Let three sheets ruin and the rack is lost — clear enough sheets to fully dry and move to the next, more crowded rack!
