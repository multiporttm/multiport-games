Glove Making Stitching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Seam 1/8
How to play: A glowing needle travels along the seam of two glove pieces — drag your mouse or finger, or steer with the Arrow Keys, to keep your needle tip inside the pink tolerance tube around it. Small stitch ticks light up gold the moment the needle passes them while you are close to the line, and dim when you wander — land stitches evenly to fill the thread tension meter. Drift too far for too long and the tension meter drains, pulling the seam crooked and costing a life. Finish the whole seam to move on — every round brings a longer seam with a narrower tube, so land all 3 lives' worth of mistakes and the glove falls apart!
