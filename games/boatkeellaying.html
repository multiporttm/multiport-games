Use Left/Right Arrow (or the nudge buttons) to steady the drifting level bubble, then press Space or tap Lock Section while the bubble sits inside the green zone to scarf the next keel section on dead straight. Locking off-level costs a life — the drift speeds up and the zone shrinks every round.
