Pick a shingle, then click a green slot to place it
Click a shingle from the queue, then click a green-highlighted slot on the roof to place it. Shingle bottom row to top — each row must overlap the seams of the row below for full weatherproof coverage, so a slot only lights up green once the row beneath it is covered. Any slot left uncovered when you click Finish Roof is a leaky gap (shown in red) and costs points. You have a limited shingle budget each round, so place carefully. Cover the whole roof under budget to advance to a bigger, steeper roof!
