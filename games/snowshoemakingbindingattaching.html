BINDING BENCH

Toe cord · instep strap · heel strap. Press Start, then time your cinch to the green zone.

The leather binding strap threads through the webbing toward the toe cord. When the sweeping needle crosses into the green zone, press Space, click, or tap to cinch the buckle. A clean cinch locks the strap down tight; a miss leaves it loose and drains your Binding Hold meter. Secure 5 straps to finish a binding and move to a trickier harness — lose all your Binding Hold and the boot pulls free!
