Drag each jewel from the tray to the socket that matches its color before the glue sets.
How to play: Empty sockets on the carousel horse's bridle each glow with a dab of colored glue, and a tray of loose jewels below holds one jewel of each matching color. Drag a jewel with the mouse or your finger, or select a jewel with the number keys and press Space to drop it into the socket you are aiming at, onto the socket that shares its color. A correct match seats the jewel and extends your streak; a wrong-colored match bounces the jewel back and costs you time. Every socket's glue dab slowly sets, so work quickly — fill every socket before glue sets empty on too many of them to finish the horse and move on to a trickier horse with more sockets and faster glue.
