Airbrush Art Mask Taping
Panel: 1 Tape left: 5 Score: 0 Best: 0
Click and drag (or touch and drag) across each glowing yellow no-paint zone to lay a strip of tape over it — press Space to snap the current strip taut and cut it. Use Arrow keys to nudge your start point for tricky corners. Every zone must be fully covered before you spray, but tape is limited — overlapping too much wastes a strip! Cover every zone to pass inspection and move to the next panel.
