Gemstone Facet Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrow or drag left/right to rotate the dop holding the gem to match the target angle shown on the diagram, then press Space / tap the lap wheel to grind that facet. Match closely for a clean facet — grind at the wrong angle and you will scratch or over-grind the stone. Cut styles get more complex each round.
