Encaustic Tile Making Hydraulic Pressing
Tile: 1 Molds Left: 10 Score: 0 Best: 0
Tap Pump (or Space, or tap the canvas) to push the ram and raise pressure. Hold the needle inside the green safe band until the hold meter fills — but don't let it spike into the red zone.
After pigment sets in its brass-divided compartments, the whole mold goes under a hydraulic press that compacts the tile into a dense, durable slab. Tap Pump (press and release Space, click the Pump button, or tap the canvas) to kick the ram and nudge the pressure gauge needle upward — pressure bleeds off on its own between pumps, so you have to keep tapping in rhythm to hold the needle inside the glowing green safe band. Stay inside the band and the hold meter fills toward a completed press; let the needle drift below the band and the tile stays crumbly, or pump too hard and send it past the safe band into the red zone and the tile cracks, ending the round early. Later tiles need a longer hold and a narrower band, so find your rhythm and keep the pressure steady before you run out of molds.
