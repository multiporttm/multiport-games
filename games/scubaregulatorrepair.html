Inspect: click/tap or press the matching number key on every worn O-ring or diaphragm (cracked, discolored) to flag it, then press Confirm. Reassemble: memorize the flashed order, then click/tap or press the matching letter key on the parts in that exact order. Test: watch the sweeping needle and press Confirm or tap the gauge the instant it's inside the green zone. Get it all right to send the regulator back into service!
