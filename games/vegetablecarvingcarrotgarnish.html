Vegetable Carving Carrot Garnish
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Garnish 1/6
How to play: A glowing bead runs along a row of V-shaped notch marks down the carrot — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your knife tip inside the tolerance band around the bead. Drift too far and your carving evenness drains; empty it and the notch cuts too deep or crooked, costing a life. Stay on the pattern the whole length to finish the garnish and move to the next carrot. Every round adds more notches and narrows the tolerance — lose all 3 lives and the carrot snaps!
