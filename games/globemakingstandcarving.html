A wooden meridian stand spins on the lathe while the depth needle sweeps back and forth across the dial. A gold wedge marks the exact depth this decorative ring needs to be cut. Press Space, tap, or click 🪚 Carve Cut the instant the needle sweeps into the gold wedge to bite the chisel in cleanly. Cut too deep and the wood splits; cut too shallow and the ring is left unfinished — three ruined cuts and the stand blank is scrapped! Finish every ring on a stand to mount a tighter, faster lathe spin.
