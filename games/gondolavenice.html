Gondola Venice
Trips: 0 Time: 60 Best: 0
Alternate J / L keys (or the two paddle buttons) to row forward — paddling on one side also nudges you the other way, so alternate evenly to go straight. Use Up/Down Arrows or drag on the canal to shift lanes, avoiding other boats and the canal walls. Reach the glowing dock to pick up or drop off a passenger. Complete as many trips as you can before time runs out!
