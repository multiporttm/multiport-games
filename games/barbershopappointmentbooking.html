Barbershop Appointment Booking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Client 1/18 Needs: Haircut (1 slot)
How to play: Each client needs a run of open slots in one chair's column — a haircut needs 1 slot, a shave needs 2, and a full service needs 3. Use the Arrow Keys to move the cursor and Space/Enter to book, or just click/tap the starting slot. Book before the clock bar empties! A double-booking or a missed clock costs a life — the clock gets shorter as the day fills up. Lose all 3 lives and the shop closes early.
