Horse Wrangling
Corralled: 0/5Time: 75sHi: 0
How to play: Ride your horse with Arrow Keys / WASD (or drag on mobile). Chase down a loose wild horse, get close, then press Space / tap to throw your lasso. Once roped, guide it steadily toward the corral in the corner — moving too erratically or getting too far from it lets it break free again. Corral every wild horse before time runs out!
