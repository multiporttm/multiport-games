Select a cell and brush carefully — don't rush the ash!
How to play: Click/tap an ash cell, or move the cursor with Arrow Keys and press Space, to brush it away. Digging slowly (waiting between digs) is gentle and safe. Digging fast, back-to-back, is aggressive — it clears cells quicker but cracks the stability of neighboring cells. Any cell (ash or fossil) that loses all stability collapses and is lost. Reveal as much of the hidden fossil as you can before the whole layer caves in!
