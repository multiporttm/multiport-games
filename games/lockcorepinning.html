Lock Core Pinning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Core 1
How to play: The bitting chart at the top shows the key pin length needed for every chamber. Click a chamber on the lock core (or use Left/Right Arrow to move between chambers) then press a number key 1-9 (or tap the keypad) to drop in that key pin — the driver pin above it fills in automatically. Press Enter or Insert Key once every chamber is set. Get it right before the timer runs out to pin the core; get it wrong or run out of time and you lose a life. Bitting codes get longer and the clock gets shorter as you go.
