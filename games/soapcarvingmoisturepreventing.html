Soap Carving Moisture Preventing
Wave: 1 Sealed: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Move the brush with the arrows and press SEAL, or just tap a bar, before it cracks.
How to play: Freshly carved soap bars are set out on a curing rack, and open air slowly dries and cracks their fine detail. Move the sealing brush along the rack with the arrow keys or the ◀ ▶ buttons, then press Space or SEAL to coat the bar under the brush — or simply click or tap a bar directly. Seal a bar before its moisture gauge fills all the way to keep its carving safe and score points; a bar that cracks drains your wax supply. Seal enough bars in the wave before your wax supply runs dry to finish the rack and move on to a busier, faster-drying rack.
