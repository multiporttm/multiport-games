Upholstery Pattern Layout
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1
How to play: Click/tap the numbered pins on the draped frame in order, from 1 up — that's the order the fabric was marked. Clicking the wrong pin tears the drape and costs a life. Once every pin is marked, the panel outline is transferred to pattern paper and a seam-allowance line appears; use + / − Allowance (or the Up/Down Arrow keys) to match the target allowance shown, then press Cut (or Space) to cut the piece. The closer to the target, the higher your score — cutting way off costs a life too. Every new panel has more pins and a tighter allowance tolerance. Lose all 3 lives and the pattern is scrapped!
