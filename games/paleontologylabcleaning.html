How to play: The marker vibrates back and forth along the gauge. Press Space, click, or tap the instant it's inside the green safe zone to gently remove matrix with the air scribe. Pressing while it's in the red zone chips the fossil beneath and costs a health point. Clean 5 safe strikes per patch and 4 patches per specimen before you run out of health!
