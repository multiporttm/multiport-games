How to play: Each kit has two steps. First, watch the brief flash for a hairline crack, then click (or press the matching number key) the cracked cymbal before the clock runs out. Second, click the replacement cymbal whose weight marker lands inside the glowing target tone-balance zone. Guess wrong on either step and you lose a life — more cymbals, fainter cracks and tighter zones arrive each kit!
