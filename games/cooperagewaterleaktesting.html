Cooperage Water Leak Testing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: The barrel is filled with water and leaks randomly spring at stave seams, shown as blue streams. Click or tap a leak to hammer its hoop tight and seal it — each hit also nudges the nearest hoop ring. Every leak left open drains the barrel faster; let the water level hit empty and you lose a life. Clear the round's quota of leaks before the barrel runs dry to advance. Leaks spawn faster and the barrel drains quicker every round. You can also press number keys 1-9 to seal the leak flashing that number.
