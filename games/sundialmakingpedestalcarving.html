Sundial Making Pedestal Carving
Ring: 1/9 Stone Left: 14 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag the chisel rest (or use Left/Right arrows) so the chisel tip lines up exactly on the chalk depth mark, then press Carve Ring or hit Space. Match the mark closely to carve a crisp ring — miss badly and the stone block is wasted.
Carving the pedestal that will hold a sundial aloft means turning a rough block of stone on the lathe into a column of crisp decorative rings, each cut to a precise depth. Each round, the spinning pedestal shows a chalk mark on its depth scale showing exactly how far the chisel must travel, while the chisel rest itself starts off a random distance away. Drag the chisel rest with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with the Left and Right arrow keys, until the chisel tip sits as close to the chalk mark as you can manage, then press Carve Ring or tap Space to press the chisel into the turning stone. A precise cut carves a crisp, even ring into the pedestal and earns a generous bonus, while a sloppy cut leaves a rough gouge that only partly shapes the ring and wastes some of your limited stone. The pedestal spins faster and the chisel rest drifts more as the column grows taller, so a careful, steady hand will see the full pedestal through to its final polished form before the stone block runs out.
