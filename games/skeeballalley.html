Skee-Ball Alley
Score: 0 | Balls left: 10 | Best: 0
Arrow keys / drag to aim • Hold Space or press & hold the lane to charge • Release to roll
Aim left/right with the Arrow keys (or drag left/right on the lane), then press and hold Space or press-and-hold on the lane to charge your throw power, and release to roll the ball up the ramp. Land it in a scoring ring at the top — small rings are worth more points but are harder to hit. You get 10 balls per game. Get the highest score you can!
