Kirigami Layering
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: The diagram on the left shows the correct stacking order of cut-paper layers, bottom to top. Drag each layer from the tray onto the stack in that exact order, then nudge it (mouse/finger drag, or Arrow Keys + Enter to lock) until its little registration marks snap onto the marks of the layer beneath. Lock in the wrong layer, or lock one in misaligned, and you lose a life. Finish the stack correctly to advance — every round adds another layer and shrinks the snap tolerance!
