A marker sweeps across the alignment gauge as you clamp the metal axle between the two yoyo halves. Press SPACE, click, or tap when it lands in the green zone to lock the axle dead-center. Miss and the axle alignment drops - let it empty and the round ends. String together dead-center clamps to boost your score as the pace quickens!
