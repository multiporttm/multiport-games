How to play: Learn your first dance one move at a time. A move icon slides toward the pink line — press its key or tap its button right as it crosses (← Step Left, → Step Right, ↑ Forward, ↓ Back, S Twirl, D Dip) for a Perfect or Good hit. Land 3 good hits in a row to master a move — it joins the routine, and you practice the whole sequence from the top. No fail state, just keep practicing until the full routine runs smoothly!
