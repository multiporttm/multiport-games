🐦 Sparrow's call
Watch the pattern, then repeat it!
Watch the bird call pattern light up across the four chirp icons. Then repeat it back in the same order — either tap the matching colored buttons or press Q / W / E / R on your keyboard — before the timer runs out. Get it right to advance to a new bird species with a longer, trickier pattern. A wrong button or running out of time ends your run — but you can always click Watch Call Again to review the pattern before answering.
