Silkscreen Frame Tensioning
Frame: 1 Lives: ♥♥♥ Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press and hold the glowing bolt to tighten it
Hold Space or press/hold the glowing bolt — release inside the green band
Each round shows a frame with bolts around its border. A glowing bolt is the next one in sequence — press and hold it (mouse/tap on the bolt, or hold Space) to raise its tension gauge, then release while the needle is inside the green safe band. Pressing the wrong bolt out of order costs a life, and letting a bolt's tension hit the top tears the mesh and also costs a life. Tension every bolt in sequence to finish the frame — larger frames with more bolts and narrower safe bands follow each round!
