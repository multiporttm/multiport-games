Museum Painting Restoration
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Painting 1/5
How to play: Each gray patch is flaking paint. Drag the R, G, and B sliders (or press 1/2/3 to select a channel and use the Arrow Keys) until the swatch matches the patch's surrounding color, then click or tap the patch to dab it in. A close match restores it; a bad match wastes the dab and costs a life. Clear every patch before the timer runs out. Later paintings have trickier colors and less time!
