🎾 Tennis Racket Making Frame Laminating
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
Three glue clamps run independent sweeping gauges down the laminated wood layers. Tap, click, or press 1 / 2 / 3 on the keyboard the instant each clamp's needle enters its green zone to press that layer evenly. Hit all three clamps in the same round to keep the layers flat and build your Even Press meter; missing a clamp, or pressing it badly out of time with the others, warps the frame and drains the Warp meter — let it max out and the frame is scrapped.
