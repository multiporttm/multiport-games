🪕 Banjo Making String Winding
Score: 0 Round: 1 Best: 0
Watch the winding gauge sweep around. Press Space, click, or tap the moment the marker is in the bright green zone to lay down a tight, even coil. Uneven winds unravel your tension meter — let it run out and the string slips free. Five even coils in a row speeds up the peg.
