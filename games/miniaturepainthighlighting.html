Miniature Paint Highlighting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Miniature 1/10
How to play: A glowing bead travels along the raised edge of the miniature's armor and folds — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your fine brush tip inside the thin tolerance line around it. Drift too far and your steady-hand meter drains; empty it and the highlight paint dries wrong, costing a life. Stay precise the whole way to finish the edge highlight and move on to the next miniature. The bead speeds up and the line narrows every miniature — lose all 3 lives and the paint job is ruined!
