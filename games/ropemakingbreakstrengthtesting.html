Pump the crank to raise the load, then lock in your rating before the hidden breaking point.
How to play: A sample of rope is clamped into the breaking rig, and your job is to pull it as close to its true breaking point as you dare without snapping it. Hold or repeatedly press the spacebar, or hold the "Pump Crank" button with mouse or touch, to wind the winch and raise the load shown on the gauge; let go, or hold "Ease Off" (the Backspace key also works), to bleed off load and slow the climb. The rope creaks and frays visibly as the load nears its hidden snap point, which is different for every sample, so watch those cues closely. When you feel you are close to the limit, press "Lock In Rating" (or Enter) to record the current load as your tested rating — the closer that locked rating is to the true breaking point, the higher your score for that sample, but lock in too low and the rating is marked weak. If you keep pumping past the snap point before locking in, the sample parts explosively and the rating is lost entirely, costing you a strike. Test sample after sample, each with a new hidden breaking point — three snapped samples ends your testing run.
