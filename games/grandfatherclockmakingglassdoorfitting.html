Grandfather Clock Making Glass Door Fitting
Door: 1 Panes Left: 5 Score: 0 Best: 0
Watch the marker slide along the measuring bar. Tap, click, or press Space the instant it crosses the green target zone to lock in the width, then the height, then the centering. Missing the zone wastes glass.
A beveled glass door only closes true when its pane is trimmed to the frame's exact opening, and that takes three careful measuring passes: the width of the cut, the height of the cut, and how well the finished pane centers inside the frame's rails. For each pass a marker slides back and forth along a measuring bar beneath the door frame, and your job is to lock the cut — by tapping, clicking, or pressing Space (or Enter on keyboard) — in the narrow instant the marker crosses the glowing green target zone that represents a true fit for that pass. Lock all three passes inside their zones and the glass door is fitted clean, but a pass locked outside its zone, or missed while the marker sweeps past, costs you a pane from your limited glass supply. The marker sweeps faster and the target zones narrow as you fit more doors, so watch closely and time your lock with a steady eye.
