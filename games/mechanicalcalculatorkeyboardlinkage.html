Mechanical Calculator Keyboard Linkage
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Teardown 1
How to play: Click (or tap) a loose key rod, then click the register slot tagged with that same number to connect it. On keyboard, use Left/Right to move the cursor, Enter/Space to pick up a rod and again to place it, and Escape to drop it. Connect every key to its matching register before time runs out — a wrong connection springs the rod loose and costs a life. Each new teardown adds more keys to relink.
