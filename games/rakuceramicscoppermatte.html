Set the three sliders to match the target swatch, then Fire the tile to see your result.
How to play: Each round shows a target matte-copper swatch next to your blank test tile. Drag the Copper Load, Reduction Smoke, and Cooling Speed sliders — or focus one with Tab and nudge it with the arrow keys, or press 1/2/3 to select a slider and Up/Down to adjust it — to aim for settings you think will reproduce that swatch, then press Fire Tile (or Enter). Your tile's fired color and sheen depend on all three settings together, so compare the result against the target and adjust before firing again. A close match passes the tile for points; a dead-on match earns a big bonus. Pass enough tiles before you run out of firings to complete the test series, and each new series narrows the margin for error.
