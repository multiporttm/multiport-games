Good ink must flow smoothly from a pen, not too thin or too thick. Each test sets a target fall-time window for a bead dropped through a vial of your ink. Add thinning water with ↓ or the − Thinner button to speed the bead up, or thickening gum with ↑ or the + Thicker button to slow it down, then press Drop Bead or SPACE to release it and watch it sink. Land its fall time inside the gold target window on the gauge for a perfect score, and move on to a tighter, trickier window!
