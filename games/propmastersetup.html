Curtain Up!

Watch the prop table while the lights are up — each numbered slot shows its prop. When the lights go down, place every prop back on its correct numbered slot from memory before the curtain timer runs out. Click/tap a tray prop then its slot, or use number keys 1-9 to pick a prop and letter keys A-I to place it.
