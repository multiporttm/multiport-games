Surfboard Shaping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Board 1/4
How to play: The dashed line is the target rocker/thickness profile; the tan foam is your blank. Move the planer with Left/Right Arrow (or drag your mouse/finger) and hold Space or hold the mouse/finger down to shave foam at the tool's position. Get every section within the pale tolerance band around the target to send the board to glassing. Shave below the band anywhere and that board is ruined — lose a life and start the blank over. Each board shape has a tighter tolerance and a shorter clock!
