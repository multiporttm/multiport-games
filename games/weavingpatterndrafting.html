How to play: Click/tap a column header to cycle that warp thread's heddle, or a row header to cycle that row's treadle — or use Arrow keys to move the highlight, Space to cycle it, and digits to set it directly. Match your drawdown on the right to the target pattern on the left, then click/press Enter on Check to weave it. A wrong check costs a life; a correct one unlocks a bigger, trickier target.
