Fountain Pen Making Nib Grinding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Nib 1/8
How to play: A target angle marker sweeps back and forth across the gauge below the grinding wheel — drag your mouse or finger left/right (or use the Arrow Keys) to keep your nib's angle indicator inside the glowing gold safe zone. Stray outside it and the wheel grinds the nib wrong, draining its integrity; empty it and the nib is ruined, costing a life. Hold steady inside the zone until the pass finishes to shape the nib correctly and move to the next one. The safe zone narrows and the sweep gets trickier with every nib — lose all 3 lives and the batch is ruined!
