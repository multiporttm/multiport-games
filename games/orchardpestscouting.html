Orchard Pest Scouting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0Lives: ❤❤❤Orchard: 1
Scan the rows of fruit trees for pest damage — holes, discoloration, or webbing. Click/Tap the infested trees to tag them before scouting time runs out. Use Arrow Keys + Enter/Space to select and tag a tree with the keyboard. Wrongly tagging a healthy tree, or missing an infested one when time runs out, costs a life. Larger orchards with subtler signs await at higher levels!
