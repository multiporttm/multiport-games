Soap Carving Design Sketching
Designs: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Press and drag along the glowing line to sketch the pattern onto the bar.
How to play: A faint glowing guide path is laid over a fresh bar of soap. Press and hold (mouse, finger, or drag) and move your pencil along that path to sketch the carving design onto the surface. Staying close to the line fills your precision score and keeps your steady-hand meter topped up; wandering far from the line drains the meter fast. Reach the end of the path before the meter runs dry to complete the design and move on to a trickier pattern with sharper curves. Lift the pencil anytime with the button, mouse-up, or touch-end.
