Press and drag the cloth evenly across the piece to build shine without scorching it.
How to play: Press and hold (mouse, finger, or arrow keys plus space) and move your cloth over the carved chess piece to buff its surface. Spreading your strokes evenly across every patch raises the overall shine meter; buffing the same small patch over and over makes that patch overheat and scorch, draining shine instead. Keep your heat meter under control while filling every patch to fully polish the piece before your cloth wears thin. Finish before it wears out to complete the piece and move to a bigger one with more patches to cover.
