Guide the applicator along the glowing crack to seal it before it reaches the edge.
How to play: A hairline crack creeps across the case's crinkle-paint shell, slowly growing toward the case edge. Move the epoxy applicator with the arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen arrow buttons, or by dragging/tapping on the canvas, keeping it as close to the glowing crack line as you can. While the applicator sits right on the crack, filler lays down and the seal meter rises; drift away from the line and no filler is applied while the crack keeps spreading. Seal the crack fully before its leading tip reaches the edge of the case to repair it and move to the next, harder case with a faster-spreading, twistier crack — let a crack reach the edge and the case is ruined.
