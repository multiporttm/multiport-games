Train Coupling Operation
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Goal: Hold Right Arrow / Throttle to speed up the rolling railcar, hold Left Arrow / Brake to slow it down. Watch the speed meter's green safe-coupling zone — let the car drift to a stop and it won't lock on either. Reach the standing car while your speed is inside the green zone to couple safely and score; hit it too fast (red zone) and you lose a life from the collision. The safe zone narrows and the yard gets longer every level. Three lives.
