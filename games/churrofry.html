Drag across the piping canvas to draw a wavy churro matching the dashed length guide, then click Drop in Oil. Watch the frying gauge move from pale to golden to burnt, and click Remove from Oil! while the marker is in the golden zone. Finally, click Shake in Sugar! when the moving marker is inside the green zone to coat it. Make as many perfect churros as you can — shapes and timing get trickier each round!
