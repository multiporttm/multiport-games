Hold mouse/finger down (or hold Space) and move slowly with Arrow Keys/WASD or your pointer to brush matrix away from the fossil. Move slowly near the bone — brushing too fast over it chips the fossil and costs a life. Clear every tile of matrix to reveal the specimen and advance — it gets faster every round.
