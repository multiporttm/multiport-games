🦺 Life Jacket Making Buckle Attaching
Two buckle prongs bob up and down independently, left and right. Tap the LEFT side when the left marker is in its green zone, and the RIGHT side when the right marker is in its zone — lock both within a short window of each other to snap the buckle shut.
Two buckle halves move up and down on their own tracks, left and right. Tap or click the left half of the frame (or press A) exactly when the left marker is inside its green zone, and tap the right half (or press D) when the right marker is in its zone. Both sides must be locked within a short window of each other to snap the buckle. A side locked outside its zone, or locked too long before its partner, botches the snap and drains your Strap Quality.
