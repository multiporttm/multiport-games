Downhill Skiing
Course 1Time: 0.0sBest: —
Use Arrow Keys / WASD or drag to steer your skier left and right. Weave through the slalom gates (pairs of flags) as you race downhill — missing a gate adds a time penalty! Dodge trees and rocks or you'll crash and lose time. Reach the finish line as fast as you can. Each new course gets more technical.
