Concrete Casting Sealing
Round: 1 Lives: 3 Score: 0 Best: 0
Dry patches appear on the slab and shrink as they cure. Aim the sprayer with your mouse, finger, or arrow keys, and click/tap or press Space to hit the nearest patch in range — each hit coats it a bit more. Fully coat a patch before it cures away to score points; let three patches cure dry and the round ends. Later rounds spawn patches faster and shrink them quicker.
