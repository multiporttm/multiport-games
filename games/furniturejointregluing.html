Furniture Joint Regluing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Joint 1/10
How to play: First scrub — move your tool (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys) over the dried glue patch and hold click / tap / Space to clean it fully before time runs out. Then click Apply Glue (or press Space) once the joint is clean. Finally rotate the joint with Left / Right Arrow (or drag) to match the target angle shown by the dashed guide, then press Clamp (or Enter) while lined up. Miss and a leg stays wobbly, costing a life — the clean target and clamp tolerance get stricter every joint!
