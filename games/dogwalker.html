Dog Walker
Treats: 0 | Lives: 3 | Score: 0 | Best: 0
Use Arrow Keys/WASD or touch-drag to move the walker down the park path. Collect treats for points and dodge puddles and other dogs. Squirrels will make a dog pull on its leash — if the leashes cross too much they'll tangle and cost you a life. Lose all 3 lives and the walk is over!
