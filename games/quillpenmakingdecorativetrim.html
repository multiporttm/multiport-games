Quill Pen Making Decorative Trim
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Quill 1
A finished sample quill sits on display with a row of tiny gems and ribbon charms glued down the shaft in a particular order, and a customer wants their own blank quill trimmed to match it exactly. Study the glowing charms as they light up in sequence along the sample, then tap the matching charm buttons below in the same order — using number keys 1-6, mouse clicks, or taps — to glue them onto the blank quill. Use 👀 Show Again or press R if you need another look before you start tapping. Get the order wrong and the glue sets crooked, ruining the trim — three ruined quills and the commission is lost! Match every pattern correctly to move on to a longer, trickier design.
