Candle Wax Pouring
Score: 0 Level 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
Hold H (or the 🔥 Heat button) to warm the wax into the green temperature zone. Hold Space (or 🫗 Pour) to pour it into the jar. Press Enter (or ✅ Done) once the fill line sits inside the target band to seal the candle. Pour outside the safe temperature and the surface cracks — overflow the jar and it's ruined. 3 lives, levels get tighter and faster.
