First apply polish: drag across the shoe to cover it evenly (watch the coverage heatmap — don't leave bare patches). Then buff: rapidly drag back-and-forth over the polished shoe to bring up a shine. Buff too little and it stays dull; buff too much in one spot and you'll wear straight through the polish. Finish each shoe within the time limit — later batches give you less time.
