Clockmaking Case Polishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Case 1 — 60s
Hold and drag the polishing wheel (mouse, touch, or arrow keys + Space) over every dull, scratched patch on the watch case to buff it to a shine before the timer runs out. Watch the heat meter — lingering too long in one spot overheats and warps the case. Each new case is a more intricate shape with more patches.
