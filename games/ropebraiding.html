How to play: A reference diagram at the top shows the repeating crossing pattern for this braid (e.g. Left-over-Center, Right-over-Center). Click the strand buttons below the braid in that exact order to cross them correctly. Each correct crossing extends your braid; a wrong crossing unravels a chunk of progress and costs a mistake. Run out of mistakes and the braid fails. Finish the braid's full length to score — more strands and longer, trickier patterns appear each round.
