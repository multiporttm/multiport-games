Tap the PUMP button, press Space, or tap the canvas repeatedly to drive the screw press down — the pressure gauge rises with every pump and slowly drifts back down on its own. Keep the needle inside the green safe zone to squeeze water from the stack and fill the progress bar. Pump too fast and the needle enters the red zone, bursting a sheet; stop pumping too long and the press loses pressure. Finish pressing before your three strikes run out!
