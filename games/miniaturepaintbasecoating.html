Sweep speed — stay in the green safe zone
How to play: Hold the spray trigger (mouse/finger down, or hold Space) and sweep the primer sprayer across the miniature with the mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys. The gauge above shows your current sweep speed — keep the needle inside the green safe zone to lay down even primer. Sweep too slowly over a crevice (joints, folds) and paint pools there, costing a life; sweep too fast anywhere and you leave thin, missed patches. Fully and evenly coat the miniature before the primer tacks up to advance — lose all 3 lives and the model is ruined. Each round brings a more intricate miniature and a narrower safe zone.
