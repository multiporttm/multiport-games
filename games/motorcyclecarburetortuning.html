Motorcycle Carburetor Tuning
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Turn the idle mixture screw with Up/Down (or the on-screen +/- buttons) and the idle speed screw with Left/Right. Watch the tachometer and the spark plug color — too lean (white/pale) causes a stall, too rich (black/sooty) causes a backfire. Hold both needles steady inside the green target zone for the hold timer to dial in the idle and pass the test ride. Three lives — the target window tightens and the engine drifts faster every level.
