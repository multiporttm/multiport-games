Mushroom Grow Kit Monitoring
Score: 0 Cycle: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Select a kit with Left/Right Arrows (or tap a kit), then press M to mist it or F to toggle its fan — or tap the Mist / Fan buttons on a kit directly. Misting raises humidity but fans must run to exchange fresh air; keep each kit's humidity and air both in the green range as it grows from pin to harvest. Neglected kits lose health and cost a life if they dry out or suffocate.
