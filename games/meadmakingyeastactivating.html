Hold Space, Up Arrow, or Tap/Hold the jar to warm the water; release to let it cool. Keep the thermometer inside the glowing activation band long enough to fully wake the yeast. Overheating kills it instantly — stay too cold too long and the activation window closes. Target bands get trickier each round!
