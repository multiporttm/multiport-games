Hockey Zamboni Parking Align
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Run 1
How to play: Use Arrow Keys / WASD (or drag with mouse/finger toward where you want the back of the Zamboni to go) to reverse the machine through the tunnel without clipping the walls, then straighten up and ease it fully into the glowing parking slot. Clip a wall and you lose a life; park crooked or outside the slot and you don't advance. Each run the tunnel gets tighter and the clock gets shorter — lose all 3 lives and intermission resurfacing is delayed!
