How to play: The diagram in the corner shows the numbered order to connect this round's overhead pulley points. Click or tap the points in that exact order (or use Tab to cycle and Enter to confirm) to rig the rope before the curtain-up timer at the top drains away. A correct connection draws a taut line; a wrong point costs a life and resets the current rig. Finish rigging every point before time runs out to raise the curtain — more pulley points and a tighter timer await each round. Lose all 3 lives and the show is cancelled!
