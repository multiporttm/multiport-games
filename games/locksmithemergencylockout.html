Locksmith Emergency Lockout
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Call 1
How to play: Read each emergency call, then pick the matching tool — Slim Jim, Pick Gun, Bypass Tool or Drill — with buttons, keys 1-4, or tap. Pick wrong and you lose a life. Pick right and a mini-task starts: for the Slim Jim and Bypass Tool, click/tap or press Space the instant the marker is inside the green zone. For the Pick Gun and Drill, press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space) to fill the meter and release inside the green band. Finish before the call's clock runs out — miss it and you lose a life. Clear a full shift of calls to win; lose all 3 lives and the shift ends.
