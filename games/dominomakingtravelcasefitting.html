Domino Making Travel Case Fitting
Case: 1 Tray Left: 20 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag a tile from the tray up into a case slot of the exact same length (shown in matching width). Tap a tile then tap a slot on touch screens, or use arrow keys plus Enter to select and place.
Every travel case has a felt-lined tray of slots cut to specific lengths, and a tray of freshly finished domino tiles needs to be packed into it before shipping. Drag a tile from the bottom tray up into any case slot whose width matches the tile's length exactly — on touch screens, tap the tile and then tap the slot instead. A tile that does not match a slot's length will not fit and bounces back, wasting a turn from your tray. Fill every slot in the case to seal it and move on to a trickier case with more slot sizes to juggle, all before your tile tray runs dry!
