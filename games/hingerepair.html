Press Start to begin your repair shift!
How to play: Each glowing hinge shows a problem icon: 🔩 loose screw, ⚙️ worn pin, or 🔧 bent leaf. Pick the matching tool from the toolbar below, then click the glowing hinge to fix it. Using the wrong tool costs points! Fix every hinge before the timer runs out to advance — each round adds more hinges and less time. Works with mouse, touch, or number keys 1-3 to pick a tool.
