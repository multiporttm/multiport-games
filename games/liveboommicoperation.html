Live Boom Mic Operation
Score: 0 Scene: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Controls: Move the mouse / drag your finger (or use Left/Right Arrow and Up/Down Arrow) to swing the boom mic over the actor who is currently speaking (highlighted, with a sound-wave icon). Keep the mic above the red frame line — dropping below it dips into shot. The closer the mic is to the active speaker (while staying out of frame), the more points you earn each second. Speakers switch without warning — react fast! Three lives — switches speed up and the frame line creeps higher every scene.
