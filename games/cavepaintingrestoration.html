Cave Painting Restoration
Stamina: 60 Restored: 0% Best: 0%
Pick the right tool for each grime type, then clean the painting.
How to play: Each patch of grime needs a specific tool: dusty grey needs the Brush, white mineral crust needs the Pick, and green moss needs the Sponge. Press 1/2/3 or click a tool button to select it, then click or drag across the grime to clean it — using the correct tool several times clears a cell, while the wrong tool damages the pigment underneath. You have limited stamina (one action per click/drag step). Restore as much of the painting as you can, undamaged, before your stamina runs out!
