Stage: Inflate
Stage 1 — Inflate: press and hold Space, click, or touch-and-hold the button to fill the balloon; release while the gauge needle is inside the green target zone. Stage 2 — Attach: click (or press R/P) the Radiosonde and Parachute buttons in the order shown. Stage 3 — Launch Window: press Launch (or Space) while the moving marker is inside the green launch window. Mistakes cost a life — lose all three and the mission ends. Target zones shrink every level.
