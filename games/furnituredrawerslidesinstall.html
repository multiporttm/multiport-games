Furniture Drawer Slides Install
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Drawer 1/8
How to play: First, keep your level marker riding the glowing guide line as it slides the drawer in and out of the cabinet — drag with mouse or finger, or steer with the Arrow Keys, staying square the whole way to keep the level meter up. Drift too far and it drains; empty it and the slide binds, costing a life. Once mounted, screw points flash one by one along the slide — press Space / Click / Tap the instant each one lights up to drive it home. Miss the timing and the drawer sticks. The guide line speeds up and the screw windows shrink with every drawer — finish all 8 for a perfectly smooth cabinet!
