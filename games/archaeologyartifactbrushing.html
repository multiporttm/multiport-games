Brush speed
How to play: Click/tap and drag — or use the Arrow keys — to sweep your brush slowly across the dirt and reveal the artifact underneath, cell by cell. Watch the speed bar: sweep too fast and the brush slips, scratching the artifact and costing a life. Clear enough of the dirt to move on. Each new artifact is more fragile and more detailed, demanding slower, steadier strokes.
