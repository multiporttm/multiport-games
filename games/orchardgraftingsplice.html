Orchard Grafting Splice
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Graft 1
How to play: Rotate the knife with Arrow Left/Right (or drag) to line up the dashed target angle, then press Space (or tap) to cut. Cut both the rootstock and the scion, then watch the binding strip and press Space (or tap) when the marker is inside the green zone to wrap the union tightly. A bad angle or a mistimed bind costs a life. Grafts cycle through whip, cleft, and bud types with tighter tolerances each round.
