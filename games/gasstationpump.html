How to play: Cars pull up wanting a specific fuel grade and amount. Click a pump bay to select it (or press 1/2/3), pick the matching grade button (or press G to cycle), then click Start Pump (or Space) to begin filling. Click it again to stop — try to land as close as possible to the requested amount without going over! Wrong grade or big overfills anger the customer and cost you a life.
