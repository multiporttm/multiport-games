How to play: Pollen pellets drop from the trap at the hive entrance. Each pellet's color matches a flower jar at the bottom — use Arrow Keys to move the hand lens (or drag/tap on touch) under the falling pellet and sort it into the matching jar before it hits the ground. Correct sorts log data for the study and raise your score; wrong jars or missed pellets cost a life. Pellets fall faster and jar colors multiply each level.
