Dye Wetness
Press and hold on the glowing bead, then trace your brush smoothly along the guide path as it travels the design before the dye dries. Stray too far from the path and you lose accuracy; let the Dye Wetness bar run out and the detail line is ruined. Complete the path to finish the motif. Paths get longer and twistier each round.
