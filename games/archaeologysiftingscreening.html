Archaeology Sifting Screening
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Click/tap Shake Screen (or press Space) to tumble a fresh load of soil onto the mesh — small shiny artifact fragments appear mixed in with plain rocks and dirt clumps. Click or tap the artifacts before they fall through the screen and are lost; clicking a rock or clump by mistake costs a life. Collect enough artifacts to pass each round — later rounds use finer screens with faster, more convincing lookalike debris.
