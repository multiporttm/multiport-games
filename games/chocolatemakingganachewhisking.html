Chocolate Making Ganache Whisking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1/8
How to play: Hold Space / Arrow Up (or press and hold anywhere on the bowl) to whisk faster, and release to let the whisk slow down. Keep the whisk-speed needle inside the glowing safe zone on the gauge until the emulsion progress ring fills to fold the cream smoothly into the chocolate. Whisk too fast for too long and the ganache will split — ruining the batch and costing a life. Finish each batch to move on to a larger, trickier one!
