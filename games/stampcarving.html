Stamp Carving
Round 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Carve away everything outside the golden design!
How to play: A raised golden design sits inside a block of rubber. Drag your mouse/finger across the canvas (or use Arrow Keys to move the cursor and hold Space) to carve away the rubber outside the design. Carving into the design itself ruins the stamp! Click Finish Stamp when you've cleared enough rubber, or the timer will finish it for you. More intricate designs appear each round.
