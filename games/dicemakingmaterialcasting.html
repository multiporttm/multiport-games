Liquid casting resin is being poured into a dice mold a cupful at a time, and the stream never holds perfectly steady — it surges and eases as the pour continues. Use the Left/Right arrow keys or tap 🐢 Slow Flow / Fast Flow 🐇 to nudge the pour rate, watching the level rise and fall inside the mold, and press Space, tap, or click 🫗 Seal Mold the instant the level sits inside the glowing target band to seal that mold with a perfect, bubble-free cast. Seal it too shallow or let it overflow and the cast is ruined, costing you a life — three ruined casts and the pour session is over! Fill every mold in a batch to move on to a bigger batch with a narrower target band.
