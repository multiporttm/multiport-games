Pocket Watch Repair: Jewel Setting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Plate 1/8
How to play: Drag the glowing jewel with your mouse or finger (or nudge it with the Arrow Keys) over its matching pivot hole. When it is lined up, press Space or tap the jewel to seat it. Line it up inside the bright tolerance ring first — pressing while off-center cracks the jewel and costs a life! Set every jewel on the plate to move on; each new plate adds more jewels and shrinks the tolerance ring.
