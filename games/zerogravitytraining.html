Goal: you float freely in zero-g — you can only change direction while touching a wall. Touch a wall, then press an Arrow Key / WASD (or tap a D-pad button, or tap anywhere on the cabin to aim) to push off toward the glowing target. Aim well — poor pushes and passive wall bumps add spin, and too much spin means you tumble out of control and lose a life. Reach the target slowly and under control to clear each run.
