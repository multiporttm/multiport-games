Miniature Paint Varnishing
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Space or press and hold / click to spray the can while sweeping it with Left/Right Arrows or by dragging. Keep the spray needle inside the safe band on the gauge — too slow pools varnish in the crevices, too fast leaves glossy unvarnished gaps. Fully and evenly coat the miniature to advance; later minis have more crevices to manage.
