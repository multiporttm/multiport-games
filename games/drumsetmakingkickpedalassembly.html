🥁 Kick Pedal Assembly
Hold down to wind the spring, release to ease off. Keep the tension needle inside the green calibration zone to lock each pedal in place. Too loose or too tight and the spring fails.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space) to wind the kick pedal's tension spring - tension rises while held and relaxes when released. Keep the needle inside the green zone as it drifts and narrows each round to lock in a clean calibration. Drifting too far outside the zone drains Spring Integrity; let it hit zero and the spring snaps.
