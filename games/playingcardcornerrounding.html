Playing Card Corner Rounding
❤❤❤
Lv 1
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Watch the punch needle swing back and forth above the stack of cut cards. Press Space, Click, or Tap the instant the needle crosses the glowing green zone to round the corner cleanly. Land in the bright center for a Perfect, the wider edge for a Good, or miss the zone and crack the corner. Three cracked corners ends the run — the zone narrows and the needle speeds up as the card stock gets thicker each level.
