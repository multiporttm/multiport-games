How to play: A grimy vintage keycap sits on the bench, its glass top covered in a patchwork of dust and dried ink. Move the cleaning brush left and right with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging on the canvas, and hold the Scrub button (or hold Space, or press and hold the on-screen button) to clean the grime patch directly under the brush. Each keycap has several grime patches scattered across it — clear them all before the solvent patch dries out and leaves streaks. Scrub too long in one exact spot once it is already clean and you risk clouding the glass, costing you points. Clean enough keycaps before you run out of solvent uses to post a high score.
