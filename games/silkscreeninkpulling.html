Silkscreen Ink Pulling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: Press Space or Tap Start Pull to begin dragging the squeegee across the screen in one steady pass. While it moves, hold ↑ / Press Harder or ↓ / Lighten Up (or drag up/down on the screen) to keep the pressure needle inside the glowing safe zone — it drifts on its own, so keep adjusting. Ink only sets cleanly on fabric where pressure stays in the zone; drift out too long and that patch streaks. Reach the far edge with enough clean coverage to pass. Prints get bigger and the safe zone narrows every round — streak three prints and the run ends.
