Model Train Turnout Switching
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
Each incoming train carries a letter for its platform — A, B or C. Throw Turnout 1 and Turnout 2 (press 1/2, or tap the buttons) so every train rolls onto the matching siding before it reaches the junction. Route a train wrong, or let two trains collide, and you lose a life. Busier layouts with more trains arrive every level.
