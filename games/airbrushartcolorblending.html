Click (or tap) the Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, White, or Black paint bottles — or press C/M/Y/W/K — to add one drop of that color into the mixing cup. Watch the cup's color update live and compare it to the target swatch shown above. When your mix is a close enough match, press Confirm (or Enter) to lock it in and score points — the closer the match, the bigger the bonus. Add too many drops and the cup overflows, ending the round, so blend carefully!
