Glassblowing Furnace Gathering
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Furnace 1/8
How to play: Hold Space, Up Arrow, or press-and-hold on the canvas to dip the blowpipe into the furnace and rotate it, gathering molten glass — the meter rises. Release in the gold target zone to gather a perfect amount. Release too early (too little glass) or too late (too much glass, it droops and falls) and you lose a life. The furnace gets hotter and the target zone narrows each round — lose all 3 lives and the gather is ruined!
