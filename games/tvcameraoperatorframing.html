Controls: Arrow keys (or drag on the canvas) to pan/tilt the camera, and W/S (or the on-screen +/- buttons / pinch) to zoom in and out. The director calls a shot cue at the top (e.g. "Medium shot, headroom tight") — move your framing box to match the anchor's position and size under that composition rule within the time shown. Hold the correct frame steady until the meter fills to lock the shot. Three lives — cues arrive faster and tolerances tighten every round.
