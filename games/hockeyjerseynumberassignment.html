Hockey Jersey Number Assignment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Roster 1
Click a player, then click a number to assign it.
How to play: Click or tap a player on the roster to select them (or press 1-9 / 0 for the first ten players), then click or tap an open number, or move the highlighted cell with the Arrow Keys and press Enter/Space to assign it. Numbers already retired by the team or taken by a teammate can't be used. Match a player's listed preference for a bonus. Fill the whole roster before the clock runs out to open the next, tougher roster — run out of time three times and it's game over.
