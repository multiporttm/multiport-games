Ironing

Smooth every wrinkle before time runs out.

First set the heat dial (drag it, or use Left/Right arrows) to match the fabric shown — too cold leaves wrinkles, too hot scorches the fabric. Then drag the iron in smooth, overlapping strokes across the wrinkled cloth to press every wrinkle smooth. Finish items quickly and cleanly for a higher score — more items and trickier fabrics appear each round.
