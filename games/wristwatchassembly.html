Movement Plate

Drag or tap the highlighted part below, then drop it on its glowing slot.
How to play: The blueprint lists the correct build order for a watch, from the mainspring barrel up through the gear train, escapement, dial, hands, case, and strap. One slot on the movement plate glows gold — drag the matching part from the tray below onto that slot (or tap the part then tap the slot). Placing a part out of order, or on the wrong slot, costs points and skips it back to the tray. Finish every slot in order to complete the watch. Assemble watches correctly to score; each round adds more components to build.
