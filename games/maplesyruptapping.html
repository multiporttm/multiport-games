Maple Syrup Tapping
Time: 60Sap: 0Best: 0
Tap a tree to start tapping it, then collect each bucket in the green sweet zone!
Click a tree to start tapping it — sap fills its bucket over time. Click the bucket again to collect once the fill meter is inside the green sweet zone. Collect too early and you waste sap; wait too long and the bucket overflows and spills. More trees start tapping as time goes on — keep watch on all of them and collect as much sap as you can before time runs out!
