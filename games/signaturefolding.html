Signature Folding
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Each sheet has pages printed out of order, some upside-down. Click (or use arrow keys + Enter) an upside-down page to flip it right-side up, then click the pages in ascending page-number order to fold a correct signature. A wrong click or an upside-down page sent to the bindery costs a life. Wrong, or over-cautious — but don't check too closely; just unflip and order them! More pages and more flips appear every level.
