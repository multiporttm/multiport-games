⚖️ Weighing Scale Making Dial Painting
Hold the brush dot on the thin ring and drag it all the way around to paint the dial's tick marks. Drift off the ring and your hand slips, spattering paint.
Press and drag (mouse or touch) the brush dot and sweep it around the circular guide ring, staying as close to the line as you can, to paint the dial face. Straying far off the ring drains your Paint Steadiness meter; complete a lap before it runs out to finish a pass and start a tighter one.
