String Condition
Click, tap, or press Space right as the wax block sweeps into the green zone to lay an even coat.
How to play: A block of wax sweeps back and forth along the crossbow string below. Click, tap the gauge, or press Space/Enter the instant the block is inside the green sweet zone to lay down an even coat of wax on that section. A clean stroke scores points and advances your stroke counter; a miss leaves the string dry or over-waxed and drains your String Condition meter. Finish enough clean strokes to fully wax the string and move on to a longer, trickier string. If String Condition runs out, the string frays and the run ends — try to beat your best round and score.
