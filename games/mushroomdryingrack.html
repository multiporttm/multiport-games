Mushroom Drying Rack
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
A new mushroom slice drops in on the left: thin slices dry fast, thick slices dry slow. Press 1/2/3 or tap a tier to place it on the matching rack tier (top = thin, middle = medium, bottom = thick). Then watch each tier's drying bar — press Space or tap the tier again to pull it when the bar is in the green "perfect crispness" zone. Pull too early (soggy) or too late (burnt) and you lose a life. The pace quickens every level — you have 3 lives.
