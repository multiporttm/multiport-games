Epoxy Resin Bubble Popping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round 1
Move the torch with your mouse, finger, or the Arrow Keys, and hold Space or click/tap to breathe its flame onto the resin. Hover the flame over a rising bubble for a moment to pop it before it fully sets. Don't hold the flame in one spot too long — the heat bar will fill and scorch the resin, costing a life. Pop every bubble before the timer runs out to seal the pour!
