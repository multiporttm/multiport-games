Sake Bottling Pasteurizing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0♥♥♥
How to play: Hold Space, click, or touch-and-hold the bottle to fire the heater and raise the sake's temperature — release to let it cool. Get the temperature needle into the gold target zone and keep it there for the whole hold timer to pasteurize the batch perfectly. Overheat past the red danger line and you cook off the flavor; let it drop out of zone too long and the timer resets. You have 3 lives — each ruined batch costs one. Target zones get narrower and hold times get longer with every batch!
