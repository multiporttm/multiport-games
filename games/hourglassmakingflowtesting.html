Calibration Accuracy
Watch the sand stream through the test bulb and seal the valve the instant the timing needle sweeps into the glowing green window — that's exactly one calibrated minute of flow. A perfect seal raises your accuracy and score; sealing too early or late spills sand and drains your accuracy meter. Nail 5 perfect seals to finish a round and speed up the next one. Tap/click the valve, or press Space/Enter, at the right instant. Empty your accuracy meter and the test batch is ruined — game over.
