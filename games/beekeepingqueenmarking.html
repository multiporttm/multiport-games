Beekeeping Queen Marking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: A swarm of worker bees crawls across the frame, with one unmarked queen hiding among them (bigger body, longer abdomen, no stripes-only pattern). Click/tap and hold on her to press the paint marker — fill the dab meter all the way before she wanders off or you release too soon. Hold steady with Space on keyboard once she is centered under the cursor. Mark the wrong bee and you startle the hive — lose a life! Each round brings a bigger, faster swarm.
