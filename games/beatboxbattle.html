Beatbox Battle
Memorize the beatbox pattern, then beat it back in order!
The game plays a beatbox pattern of Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, and Vocal hits. Watch and listen, then reproduce it in the same order by pressing D / F / J / K or tapping the matching pads — each hit must land before the timer runs out. Get it right and one more hit is added to the pattern next round. A wrong pad or a timeout ends the run. Works with keyboard or touch.
