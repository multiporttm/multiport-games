Drag the sliders (or press 1/2/3 to select one, then ←/→ to adjust) so the three bars in the bowl match the target recipe lines as closely as possible.
A good ink needs the right ratio of pigment, water and gum arabic binder or it will crack, smear or never dry. Drag the three sliders — or press 1, 2 or 3 to select Pigment, Water or Gum Arabic, then use the arrow keys to fine tune it — until the colored bars in the mixing bowl line up with the thin target marker lines. Bind the batch once you are close for the highest score, before the gum sets and locks your ratio in for good.
