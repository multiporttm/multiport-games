Limb Balance
Click, tap, or press Space right as the needle sweeps into the green zone to lock in even tension.
How to play: The tension needle sweeps back and forth around the dial while you draw the limb. Click, tap the dial, or press Space/Enter the instant the needle is inside the green sweet zone to lock in a perfectly even pull. A clean pull scores points and advances your pull counter; a miss over- or under-tensions the limb and drains your Limb Balance meter. Finish enough clean pulls to tension the crossbow and move on to a stiffer, trickier limb. If Limb Balance runs out, the limb snaps and the run ends — try to beat your best round and score.
