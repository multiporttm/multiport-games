How to play: Use the −/+ buttons (or Arrow Left/Right for temperature, Arrow Up/Down for humidity) to set the incubator's shared temperature and humidity, then click Advance Week (or press Enter) to simulate a week. Each egg develops when the incubator is within ITS ideal range, and regresses otherwise — watch each egg's progress bar and its target range shown underneath. The environment drifts a little each week, so keep checking in. After 10 weeks, eggs that reached full development hatch successfully. Maximize how many of your clutch hatch!
