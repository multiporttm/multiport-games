Hold Go (or Up Arrow / W) to move the cable car up the line, release to pause. Wind gusts push you sideways — counter-steer with Left/Right (or A/D) to keep it steady and stop precisely at each station platform. Dodge birds and icy patches on the cable — hitting one costs a life! Wind grows stronger with every ascent.
