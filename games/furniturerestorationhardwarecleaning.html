A tarnished brass pull, hinge, or lock plate sits under a clock that is quietly ticking down while oxidation slowly creeps back across the metal. Press Space, tap, or click 🧽 Scrub to work polish into the tarnish — each scrub chips away at the grime bar, but scrub too hard once it is nearly clean and you will scratch through to bare brass. Finish cleaning before the timer runs out and keep the scratch damage low to earn a shining bonus; run out of time or scratch the patina too badly three times and the hardware set is ruined! Clear every piece in a set to move on to a grimier set with a faster-creeping tarnish.
