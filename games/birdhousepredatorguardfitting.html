Birdhouse Predator Guard Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Guard 1/8
How to play: Drag the metal predator guard (mouse/touch) or nudge it with Arrow Keys until its outline lines up with the faint target diagram around the entry hole. When it's inside the tolerance ring, the numbered mounting holes light up — click (or press the matching number key) the screws in the exact order shown, 1, 2, 3..., to lock it in place. Fitting it off-position or driving screws out of order lets the plate slip, costing a life. Mount every guard to advance — shapes get trickier and the fit window tightens each round!
