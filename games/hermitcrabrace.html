Hermit Crab Race
Heat: 1Position: 1stShells: 0 | Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrow Keys / A/D, or drag on touch, to scuttle your hermit crab sideways along the beach. Race the other crabs to the finish line on the right — grab bigger 🐚 shells along the way (walk into them or tap them) for a temporary speed boost, but watch out for diving 🐦 seagulls that will knock you back. Score is based on your finishing position and shells collected, across multiple heats.
