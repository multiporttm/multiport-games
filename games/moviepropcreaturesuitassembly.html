Movie Prop Creature Suit Assembly
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Suit 1/8
How to play: Segments for the creature suit are scattered around the performer, each glowing with a number. Click or tap the segment for the next joint in the chain — shown highlighted on the performer and listed at the top — to snap it into place (use Tab/Shift+Tab to cycle the highlighted target and Enter to attach the currently selected segment, if you prefer the keyboard). Attach a segment out of order and a life is lost. Finish the whole suit before the cure timer empties. Suits get more segments and the timer gets tighter every round!
