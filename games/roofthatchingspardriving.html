Roof Thatching Spar Driving
Rows Driven: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Tap DRIVE, click, or press Space when the marker is in the green zone to seat each spar.
How to play: A row of bent hazel spars waits along the ridge, each one started but not yet seated. A drive meter sweeps back and forth above the active spar. Press Space, click, or tap the DRIVE button exactly when the marker sits inside the green zone to swing the mallet square and sink that spar flush into the ridge. A good strike advances you to the next spar and builds your streak; a mistimed strike bends the spar sideways and drains your grip meter. Drive every spar in the row before grip runs out to finish the ridge and move on to a longer row with a faster-moving meter.
