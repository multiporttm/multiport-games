Soap Carving Polishing Buffing
Pieces: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Drag, or move with arrows and hold Space, over each dull patch to buff it to a shine.
How to play: Every dull patch on the finished carving needs an even shine. Click and drag (or touch and drag) the buffing cloth over a patch, or move the cursor with the arrow keys and hold Space to buff in place, to raise that patch's shine meter into its glowing target band. Keep buffing after it gleams and the patch starts to wear thin and dull again, so move on once it is bright. Buffing drains your arm-stamina meter, which only refills while you are not buffing — tap REST ARM, or just let go, to recover. Bring every patch into its shine band before stamina runs out to finish the piece and move on to a bigger carving with more patches.
