Drag the felt pad to center it and drag its edge to resize it, then press Stamp.
How to play: A base outline shows where the felt pad needs to go. Drag the pad's center (mouse, finger, or arrow keys) to move it, and drag its edge handle (or use W/S or the +/- keys) to resize it, until it sits just inside the base outline with an even margin all the way around. Press Stamp, Enter, or the button to glue it down before the bead dries. A well-centered, well-sized pad fills your glue budget and precision score; a pad that is too big, too small, or off-center wastes glue fast. Land enough bases before the glue runs out to finish the set and move on to oddly shaped bases.
