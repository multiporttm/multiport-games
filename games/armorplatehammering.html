Armor Plate Hammering
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
The hammer sweeps around the glowing ring on the plate. Press Space or click/tap the plate the instant the sweep crosses an unworked gray wedge to plant a clean planishing blow on it. Miss a wedge or strike one twice and you leave a hammer mark. Too many marks on one ring costs a life. Work each ring smooth, inside out, to move to the next — rings get wider and the hammer sweeps faster with every level.
