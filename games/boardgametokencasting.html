Board Game Token Casting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Mold 1/6
How to play: Watch the temperature gauge climb as the crucible heats — press Space, click, or tap the crucible the instant the needle sits inside the green pour zone to pour into the current mold cavity. Each cavity then needs a few moments to cool: wait until its ring turns solid green before pouring the next cavity or demolding, but do not wait too long or the metal cools wrong. Fill every cavity cleanly and demold the finished tokens to clear the mold and move to the next, trickier multi-cavity mold. A bad pour (too hot, too cold, or rushed) ruins a cavity and costs a life. Lose all 3 lives and the batch is scrapped!
