Caramel Pulling Stretching
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Alternate Left/Right Arrow keys, or tap the left and right side of the caramel in turn, to pull and fold it — this lightens its color and keeps it warm. Press Space or tap CUT when the color gauge sits in the glowing ready zone. Cut too early, too late, or let it cool and harden, and you lose a life. Three lives — the ready zone narrows and the caramel cools faster with every level.
