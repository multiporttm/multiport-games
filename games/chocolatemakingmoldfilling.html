Chocolate Making Mold Filling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Tray 1/10
How to play: Hold Space (or press and hold on the canvas) to pour chocolate into the glowing cavity. Watch the gauge on the left — release the pour while the needle sits inside the green safe band to fill the cavity cleanly. Release too early and an air pocket ruins it; keep pouring past the band and it overflows — either way costs a life. Fill every cavity in the tray to move on to the next, more intricate mold!
