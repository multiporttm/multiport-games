How to play: Pick a tool — 🦴 Skeleton, 📋 Placard, or 🖼 Backdrop (keys 1-3, 4 or X erases) — then click/tap a floor tile to place it (click again with the same tool to remove it). Arrow keys move the highlighted tile and Space/Enter places the selected tool there too. Match the brief: skeletons need a nearby placard and a clear sight-line toward the entrance (bottom row), backdrops belong along the back wall, and every row needs at least one open walking path.
