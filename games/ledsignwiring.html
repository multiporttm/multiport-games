LED Sign Wiring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Layout 1/8
How to play: Each LED module shows how many wires it needs. Click or tap one module, then an adjacent module (or move the highlight with Arrow Keys and press Enter/Space) to connect or disconnect a wire between them — a wire will not attach if it would overload either module's terminal count. Give every module exactly its required wires and connect them all back to the PSU to light the whole sign before time runs out. Eight layouts bring more modules and a tighter clock!
