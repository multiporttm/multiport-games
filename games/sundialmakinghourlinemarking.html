Sundial Making Hour Line Marking
Hour Line: 1/10 Brass Polish: 14 Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag the scribe arm (or use Left/Right arrows) so it lines up exactly on the faint shadow line, then press Etch Line or hit Space. A close match brightens the dial — miss badly and the brass is scarred, costing precious polish.
Marking the hour lines is the most delicate stage of building a sundial, where a careless scribe can ruin a dial plate that has already taken hours of polishing. Each round, the workbench shows a blank brass dial with a faint shadow line sweeping toward the angle where the next hour line belongs, while a scribe arm pivoted at the center starts off pointed somewhere else entirely. Drag the scribe arm with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with the Left and Right arrow keys, until its edge sits as close to the shadow line as you can manage, then press Etch Line or tap Space to cut the mark into the brass. A precise etch brightens the dial with a crisp new hour line and earns a generous bonus, while a sloppy etch gouges the brass off the mark and only partly completes that hour, wasting some of your limited polish. The shadow sweeps faster and the angles grow tighter as more hour lines are etched, so a steady hand and a careful eye will see the full twelve-line dial through to completion before your polish runs dry.
