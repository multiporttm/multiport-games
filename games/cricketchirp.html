Cricket Chirp
Listen to the cricket...
Streak: 0
Best: 0
Watch and listen to the cricket's chirp pattern (small dot = short chirp, wide bar = long chirp). Then hold down the CALL button (or hold Space) for a short or long press to match each chirp in order, releasing between each one. Match the whole rhythm to attract the cricket — get it wrong and the attempt resets. The pattern gets longer and faster each round!
