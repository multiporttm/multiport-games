Surfing Wave
Score: 0 | Best: 0
Press Up/Down Arrow (or drag up/down) to move your surfer and stay in the wave's glowing sweet spot — staying in it builds your speed and score. When the wave crests and TRICK! flashes, press Space (or tap) at the right moment for bonus points. Drift out of the sweet spot too long and you'll wipe out, ending your run.
