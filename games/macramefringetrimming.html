Macrame Fringe Trimming
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤
Move the scissors over a strand with your mouse/finger (or Arrow Keys) until they line up with the dashed guide line, then press Space or Tap to snip. Cutting right on the line scores big; drift too far above or below and the fringe comes out uneven. Later rounds add more strands and slanted, trickier guide lines!
