Pewter Sprue Removal
Casting: 1 Sprues left: 4 Score: 0% Best: 0%
Move the cutter crosshair (mouse, touch, or arrow keys) to the small clip mark where each sprue meets the casting, then click/tap or press Space to snip it. Cut right at the mark for a clean snip, too far out leaves a stub, and cutting into the piece nicks the casting. Clear every sprue to finish — later castings bring more sprues and a tighter clean-cut window!
