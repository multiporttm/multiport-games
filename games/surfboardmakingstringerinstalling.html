🪵 Surfboard Making Stringer Installing
Nudge the wooden stringer so it lines up dead-center between the two foam halves, then hold it steady while the clamps bite and the glue sets. Drift away and the glue grabs it crooked.
Drag the wooden stringer left/right (or use Arrow Left/Right) to line it up inside the centered target slot, then hold it steady there to fill the Clamp Hold meter before the glue sets. Finishing a stringer speeds up the next one; if the glue sets while you are off-target, that stringer is ruined.
