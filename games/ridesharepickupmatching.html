Rideshare Pickup Matching
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Click/tap a driver (numbered car) then a passenger (lettered pin) to pair them — or press a number key for the driver and a letter key for the passenger. Pair every driver, then press Space/Enter or tap MATCH FLEET to lock it in. The closer your total pickup distance is to optimal, the more you score — a bad match costs a life. Three lives — more riders appear and the margin for error shrinks every level.
