STRETCHING RACK

Deer hide soaked soft, scraped clean of hair. Press Start, then time your stretch to the green zone.

A soaked rawhide strip is racked on the stretching frame. When the sweeping needle crosses into the green zone, press Space, click, or tap to pull even tension along the strip. A clean pull leaves it smooth and even; a miss leaves a thin spot and drains your Rawhide Evenness meter. Even out 5 strips to finish a rack and move to tougher hide — lose all your Rawhide Evenness and the strip tears!
