Butter Sculpture Animal Modeling
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Part 1
A fifty-pound block of chilled butter is mounted on the armature, ready to become a prize dairy cow. Press Space, tap, or click 🐄 Press to start pressing butter outward from the core, and hold (press and hold, or tap and hold) to keep building the part up toward its true size — release right as the swell reaches the shaping band, no sooner, no later. Press too little and the part stays flat and shapeless; press too much and the soft butter slumps off the armature — three slumps and the block is ruined! Finish every part (head, body, legs, tail) to move to a trickier block with a narrower shaping band.
