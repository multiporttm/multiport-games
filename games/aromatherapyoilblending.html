How to play: Each client ticket shows a desired aromatherapy effect (Relaxing, Energizing, Focus, etc.). Click the essential oil bottles (or press 1-6) to add drops to your carrier-oil beaker, aiming for the correct blend ratio for that effect. Press Enter or click Pour & Submit when ready, and Backspace to empty the beaker and start over. You have 3 lives — a wrong or sloppy blend costs one. Rounds get trickier with more subtle, overlapping requests as you go.
