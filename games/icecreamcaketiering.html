Ice Cream Cake Tiering
Score: 0 Tier: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
A new layer of cake, ice cream, or crunch slides back and forth above your tower. Press Space or Tap to drop it — line it up as closely as you can with the tier below to keep the cake level. Miss too far and you lose a life; the tower gets taller and faster every tier.
