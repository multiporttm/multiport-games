A bead of warm sandarac varnish slides back and forth across the heated plate as you tilt it. A gold band marks the exact moment the bead is centered for an even, bubble-free coat. Press Space, tap, or click ✨ Sweep the instant the bead crosses into the gold band to lay down a perfect seal. Sweeping too early or too late leaves a drip or a bubble — ruin three coats and the plate is scrapped! Seal every plate in a batch to move on to a hotter, faster batch.
