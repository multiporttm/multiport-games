Pick the right tool
How to play: Each watch runs through five steps. First pick the matching tool for the case back shown — the Case Knife (key 1) for snap-fit backs or the Case Wrench (key 2) for screw-down backs; the wrong tool costs a life. Then hold Space/Do It (or click/hold the canvas) to pry or turn the case open, releasing when the gauge needle is in the green unlock zone. Drag the old battery (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys) out along the lane while staying clear of the red movement zone. Press F/Flip Battery if needed so the + mark matches the slot's polarity mark, then press Space/Do It to drop it in. Finally hold Space/Do It again to reseal the case in the lock zone. Later watches add the trickier screw-down back and a narrower safe lane.
