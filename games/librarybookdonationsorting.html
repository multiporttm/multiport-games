How to play: Donated books pile up with a condition and relevance tag. Click (or tap) a book to select it, then click Keep, Sell, or Recycle (or press K, S, R) — damaged books are always Recycle, good books that are still wanted are Keep, and good books that are outdated or duplicates are Sell. Sort correctly for points; a wrong call or an overflowing bin costs a life. You have 3 lives — survive all 6 batches as donations arrive bigger and faster!
