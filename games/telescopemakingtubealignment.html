How to play: The diagram shows the marked target positions for the primary mirror's tilt (left slider) and the secondary mirror's offset (bottom slider). Drag each slider (or use Up/Down Arrows for the primary, Left/Right Arrows for the secondary) until both markers sit inside their glowing tolerance bands, then click Lock Collimation (or press Space). Lock in with either mount outside its band and it comes out misaligned. Every tube design narrows the tolerance!
