How to play: Phase 1 — use Up/Down (or the arrows) to steer the folded ship through the winding bottle neck without touching the glass. Phase 2 — hold Space (or the center button) to charge the pull, then release while the bar is inside the glowing zone to raise the masts and finish the bottle. Each bottle after the first has a narrower neck and a tighter mast zone.
