Heat Dial
Drag the Heat Dial to bring the crucible's temperature into the glowing green window on the gauge. Click Pour to start a timing meter — click again while the pointer is inside the green pour zone. Then click Cool to let it set, and Release Piece to finish. Windows get trickier each round!
