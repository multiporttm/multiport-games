Fade Haircut Clipper Work
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round: 1 ❤❤❤
Drag up/down over a column (mouse or touch) to set the clipper guard length there, blending smoothly from short at the bottom to long on top. On keyboard use Left/Right to pick a column and Up/Down to change its length. The faint outline is the target fade — click Check in Mirror (or press Enter) when it's blended to lock it in before time runs out!
