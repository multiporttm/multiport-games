⏰ Alarm Clock Making Bell Mounting
Hold to charge the mounting press and release when the force needle sits in the green zone to bolt the twin bell on cleanly.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) to charge the mounting press — the force needle will swing up and down. Release at the moment it sits inside the green zone to mount the bell cleanly. A good mount scores points and speeds up the needle; a bad mount cracks the casing and drains your Casing Integrity meter — let it empty and the clock is scrapped.
