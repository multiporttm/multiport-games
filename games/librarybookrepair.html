Each new book has more damage and less margin for error.
How to play: Each book arrives with several damaged spots — torn pages, a loose spine, a bent corner — marked on the cover. Select the matching tool (Tape for tears, Glue for the spine, Press for bent corners) from the tray below, then click the damaged spot to fix it with that tool. Using the wrong tool on a spot costs one of your 3 lives. Finish every spot to complete the book and move to a more damaged one. Keyboard: 1/2/3 selects Tape/Glue/Press, Tab then Enter targets a damage spot.
