Coin Minting Edge Milling
Run 1/6Score: 0Hi: 0
 
How to play: Hold Right Arrow / Up Arrow (or press and hold / drag right) to speed up the coin feed against the milling wheel, and let go or press Left Arrow / Down Arrow to slow down. Keep the speed needle inside the green safe band the whole time the coin is milling to cut even reeded ridges. Drifting out of the band for too long ruins the edge. Mill every coin in the run to advance — the safe band narrows on later runs.
