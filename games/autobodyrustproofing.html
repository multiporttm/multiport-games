Auto Body Rustproofing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Section 1
How to play: Click, tap, or use the Arrow Keys + Enter/Space to spray rust inhibitor coating into every glowing access hole on the undercarriage — every panel seam, wheel well, and hollow frame section. Spray them all before the coating cures (watch the timer bar)! Miss the deadline and you lose a life and the panel has to be redone. Each round adds more access holes and less time — lose all 3 lives and the car rusts out.
