Press and hold (mouse or touch) to lower the shears onto the chalked cutting line, then drag along the dashed curve from the start marker to the end marker, staying as close to the line as you can. Release early and you will need to start the cut again. Use arrow keys plus Enter to nudge a keyboard cursor and cut in small steps if you prefer. A clean, close cut scores the most and unlocks a trickier apron pattern.
