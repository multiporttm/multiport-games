Woodwind Reed Crafting
Level: 1 Lives: ♥♥♥ Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Select a spot on the reed, then shave it to the target band
Click, tap, or use Left/Right arrows to select a spot along the cane reed's profile. Press Shave Selected (or Space / Down Arrow) to shave a little cane from that spot. Each spot turns green once its thickness falls inside the dashed target band — but shave past the bottom of the band and that spot is ruined, costing a life! Shave every spot into the band to finish the reed, across trickier target profiles as you level up.
