Locomotive Boiler Pressure
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Goal: Keep the boiler needle inside the green safe zone for the whole route. Hold Up Arrow / F or press Shovel Coal to add heat (raises pressure, needed to climb grades); hold Down Arrow / D or press Inject Water to cool the boiler (lowers pressure). Pressure drifts up on grades and down on flats automatically. Let the needle sit in the red zone too long — low (stalled) or high (safety valve blow) — and you lose a life. Grades get steeper and the safe zone narrows every level. Three lives.
