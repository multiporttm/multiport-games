The top trace is the clean reference signal; the bottom trace is the live faulty circuit. Compare them and pick the matching fault below with mouse/touch, or press 1, 2, or 3 (use Left/Right Arrow + Enter too). Faults: a clipped signal has its peaks flattened, the wrong frequency trace is faster or slower than reference, and a noisy trace is jittery. A wrong diagnosis costs a life, and the clock gets shorter each round. Three lives.
