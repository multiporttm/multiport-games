Puppetry String Threading
Score: 0 | Hi: 0❤️❤️❤️Round 1
Click or tap the glowing peg on the control bar for the current numbered string, then drag down to the matching numbered joint on the puppet — steer your drag around strings already tied so your route does not cross them. On keyboard, use Tab/Arrow keys to select the peg then its joint and press Enter to thread a straight line. More strings and trickier routing arrive each round.
