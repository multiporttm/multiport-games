Cheese Rind Development
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Wheel 1
How to play: Each wheel is aiming for a target rind — Natural, Washed, or Waxed — shown at the top. Every simulated week (or press Space to advance), pick the matching action: Brush for a natural rind, Wash for a washed rind, Wax for a waxed rind, or Let it rest if nothing is due that week — use the number keys 1–4 or click/tap the buttons. The right action on the right week lowers mold risk; the wrong action (or letting mold run too high) costs a life. Finish all the weeks for a wheel to move on — rind styles and mold speed get trickier each round!
