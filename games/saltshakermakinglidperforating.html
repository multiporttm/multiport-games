Holes light up on the lid grid one at a time, each ringed by a shrinking circle. Tap (mouse or finger) the hole while the ring is inside the thin green target ring to drill a clean perforation. Tap too early or too late and the hole tears, draining your Lid Integrity meter. Rounds add more holes per lid and shrink the rings faster — keep your streak alive to raise your score.
