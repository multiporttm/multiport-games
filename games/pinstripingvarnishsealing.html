Pinstriping Varnish Sealing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1
How to play: Drag your mouse/finger across the flame design to brush on varnish, or move the highlighted cell with Arrow Keys and hold Space to brush it. Watch each patch's color — too light means a dry spot, too dark/shiny means pooled varnish. Get enough of the design into the green gauge band and press Enter or the Seal It button before time runs out to move on to a bigger panel.
