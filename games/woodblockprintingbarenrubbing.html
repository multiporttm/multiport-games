How to play: A sheet of paper rests face-down on the inked woodblock. Drag the baren with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with the arrow keys / on-screen arrows, in smooth overlapping spirals to burnish every part of the sheet. Lingering too long in one spot tears the thin paper, while rushing in straight lines leaves pale missed patches. Watch the Ink Coverage meter climb, then click "Lift the Print" once coverage is high enough to score a clean impression before the paper gives out.
