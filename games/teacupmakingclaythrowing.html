🍵 Teacup Making: Clay Throwing
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
How to play: A lump of clay spins on the wheel while a bright pointer sweeps around the centering gauge. Click / tap the wheel or the Start button, or press Space / Enter, exactly as the pointer passes through the glowing green zone to make a clean centering-and-pulling pass. Miss and the clay wobbles off-center, damaging your shape quality meter; empty it and the cup collapses for good. Land 5 clean passes to finish a round and pull up the walls — the wheel spins faster and the safe zone narrows every round after.
