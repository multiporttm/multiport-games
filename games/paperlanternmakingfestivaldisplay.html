Pick the lantern color shown on the glowing hook, in order, left to right.
How to play: A festival rope has empty hooks, and faint colored silhouettes above each hook show exactly which lantern color belongs there. Tap the matching colored lantern button below, or press keys 1-4, to hang the correct color on the next empty hook, working left to right. A correct match lights the hook and keeps your rope tension steady; a wrong color wastes rope tension fast. Fill every hook correctly before your rope tension runs out to finish the display and move on to a longer rope with a trickier color pattern.
