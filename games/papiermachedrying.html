Each piece has a spinning dial. Tap (or select with Tab/Arrow Keys and press Space/Enter) a piece the instant its marker enters the glowing green turn zone to rotate it evenly. Missing the zone — or rotating outside it — warps the piece. Keep every rack safe until the humidity timer ends!
