Each jar in the cooler is steeping a cold-brew batch with its own grind size and water ratio. Watch the countdown bar: click/tap the jar (or press its number key 1-4) the instant the bar turns green to pull a perfect batch. Too early or too late ruins it. New batches arrive faster each round — you have 3 lives.
