Ferris Wheel Gondola Balancing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Rotation 1
How to play: Guests are waiting in the queue on the right. Click/tap a gondola on the wheel (or use the Left/Right Arrow Keys to pick a gondola and Space/Enter to load it) to seat the next guest in line. Watch the balance meter — keep the wheel's left-side and right-side weight close together. Load every gondola before the rotation timer runs out to send the wheel spinning and score big; let the imbalance go too far or run out of time with empty gondolas and you lose a life. Queues get longer and gondolas get tighter on weight limits as rotations advance. Lose all 3 lives and the shift is over!
