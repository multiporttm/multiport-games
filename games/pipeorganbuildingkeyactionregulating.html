Select a key, then shorten or lengthen its tracker rod until the gauge sits in the green band.
How to play: A row of tracker-action keys sits on the workbench, each one linked to its pallet by a tracker rod with an adjustable wire nut. Select a key with the Left/Right arrows, A/D, the on-screen Key buttons, or by tapping a key directly, then shorten or lengthen the selected key's rod with the Up/Down arrows, W/S, or the on-screen Rod buttons, watching its depth gauge move. Get every key's gauge inside the green target band before the timer runs out to regulate the whole console. Overshoot a rod far past its target and the key binds, costing you time; regulate all the keys cleanly to finish the console and post a high score.
