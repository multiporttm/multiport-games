Elevator Operator
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lives: 3
How to play: Hold Up Arrow/Down Arrow (or the on-screen buttons) to move the elevator. It has momentum — release the button a little early so it glides to a stop exactly at a floor line. Land within the green zone to open the doors: waiting passengers board, and any riders heading to that floor get off. Deliver everyone before their patience (shown as a shrinking bar) runs out — you only have 3 lives!
