How to play: A new hex cell glows on the comb — use Arrow Keys or Tap/Click a neighboring empty cell to guide the bees there before the timer ring closes. Keep the pattern even: cells placed next to 2+ finished neighbors score big and keep the comb efficient, isolated cells risk becoming wasteful "burr comb." Clear each frame to advance; miss three cells and the hive loses the round.
