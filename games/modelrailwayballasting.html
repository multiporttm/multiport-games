Model Railway Ballasting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Section 1 Coverage: 0%
How to play: Drag the ballast hopper left and right along the track (mouse, finger, or the Left/Right arrow keys). Hold the mouse button / finger down (or press and hold Space) to pour a steady trickle of gravel beneath the hopper. Keep every section of the gauge's shaded safe band filled to a good depth — but don't pour so much that gravel piles up onto the shiny rail heads, or it counts as a derail risk. Finish each pass within the time limit with high, even coverage to advance to a longer, trickier stretch of track!
