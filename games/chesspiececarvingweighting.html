Chess Piece Carving Weighting
Weighed: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Stack weights on the right pan to match the carved piece on the left, then lock it in.
How to play: Each carved chess piece sits on the left pan of a brass balance scale with a hidden target weight. Tap the weight buttons (or press number keys 1,2,5,0 and minus) to add or remove small discs from the right pan until the needle sits as close to level as possible, then press LOCK IN to check your balance before the clock runs out. A near-perfect balance scores big and keeps your streak alive; a bad guess wastes precious seconds. CLEAR PAN empties the right side if you want to start over.
