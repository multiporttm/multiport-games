Bell Founding Tuning Shaving
Bell: 1 Passes left: 0 Score: 0 Best: 0
Each shave pass thins the rim and raises the bell's pitch, shown by the needle climbing the tuning dial toward the red target zone. Hold Space or press and hold Shave Pass briefly for a light pass, or longer for a deeper one — release to stop shaving. Land the needle inside the target zone and lock it in with Enter; overshoot past the zone and the rim cracks, ruining the bell. You have a limited number of passes per bell, so shave with care.
