Traditional charcoal incense base is built up in even layers inside a wooden mold. A scoop of charcoal powder slides back and forth above the mold — press SPACE, tap the canvas, or press the Drop Layer button when the scoop sits over the gold alignment mark to lay down a flat, even layer. A centered drop stacks cleanly and scores big; an off-center drop tilts the stack, and three wobbly layers in a row collapses the whole mold. Stack as many clean layers as you can before you run out of scoops!
