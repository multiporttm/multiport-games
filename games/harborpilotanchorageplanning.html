Harbor Pilot Anchorage Planning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Anchorage 1
 
How to play: Move the reticle with your mouse, finger, or the Arrow Keys and press Click/Tap/Space to drop anchor for the current ship inside the dashed anchorage zone. Each ship needs enough swing room — its circle must not touch the zone edge or any other ship's circle. A bad drop costs a life. Anchor every ship in a round to move to a busier anchorage with more ships and less room.
