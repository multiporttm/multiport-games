Seam Alignment
A sewing needle sweeps across the gauge beside the zipper track. Tap the canvas, press the button, or hit Space/Enter the instant it crosses the green zone to lock in a clean, even stitch. Miss and the seam puckers, costing alignment — empty it and the zipper jams for good. Lock 5 stitches to finish a run and speed up the machine.
