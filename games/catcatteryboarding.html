Cat Cattery Boarding
Score: 0 | Lives: 3 | Level: 1 | Hi: 0
New cats need their own empty suite — click/tap an empty suite (or press its number key) to check an arriving cat in. Select an occupied suite and press L (litter), F (food), or E (enrichment) — or tap its icon — to service it before the bar empties. Let a bar hit zero and the cat leaves unhappy!
