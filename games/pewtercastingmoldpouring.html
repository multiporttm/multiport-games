Pewter Mold Pouring
Mold: 1 Fill: 0% Score: 0 Best: 0
Tilt angle:
Use the slider (or Left/Right arrow keys) to tilt the crucible of molten pewter over the mold. Hold Hold to Pour (or Space / press and hold the canvas) to stream metal into the cavity. Fill the mold up to the marked line without overflowing the edges or leaving bubbles below the line — the stream drifts with your tilt angle, so steady hands win. Each new mold is a trickier shape!
