Rescue Dog Scent Tracking
❤❤❤ Score: 0 Hi: 0 Level: 1
Your search dog follows a missing person's scent. When the trail splits, watch each lane's alert bar — the lane with the strongest alert is the real trail. Press the matching number key (1, 2, 3…) or tap that lane before time runs out. Pick wrong or wait too long and the trail goes cold!
