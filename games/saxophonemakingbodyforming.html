🎷 Saxophone Making Body Forming

Roll and form the raw brass sheet into a true conical saxophone body, one pass at a time.

Press Space, tap the gauge, or hit the FORM button the instant the marker sweeps through the green zone to roll a clean, even section of brass. A clean pass raises score and shape-evenness focus; a mistimed press leaves a dent and drains your focus meter. Land five clean passes to begin a harder, faster round. Run out of focus and the body is ruined - how high can you build your score?
