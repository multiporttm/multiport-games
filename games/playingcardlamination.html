Playing Card Lamination
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sheet 1 — Stock 1/6
How to play: Two gauges drift over time: feed speed (top bar) and roller pressure (bottom bar). Use Up/Down Arrow or drag vertically to adjust speed, and Left/Right Arrow or drag horizontally to adjust pressure — or just drag anywhere on the canvas to steer both at once. Keep both needles inside their green safe zones while the progress bar fills to seal the sheet. Drifting outside a zone fills the bubble meter; let it overflow and you lose a life. Heavier card stock drifts faster and the safe zones narrow every sheet!
