Cheesemaking Brine Soaking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Several cheese wheels are soaking in brine tanks at once, each with its own target time shown on its tank's schedule dial. Click or tap a tank (or press the number keys 1-9) to pull its wheel out. Pull it while the dial's needle is inside the glowing green zone for a perfect brine, early for an under-salted wheel, or late for an over-salted ruined wheel. More tanks start soaking and the perfect zones get tighter every round — keep an eye on all of them!
