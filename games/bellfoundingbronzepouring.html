Bell Founding Bronze Pouring
Lives: 3 Round: 1 Score: 0 Best: 0
Hold Space, the mouse/touch button, or tap and hold anywhere on the canvas to tilt the crucible and pour a stream of molten bronze. Release to stop. Fill each mold up to its glowing fill line as it glides past — overflow scalds the floor and spoils the casting, while a mold left short rings hollow. Clear molds to speed up the conveyor and earn bigger scores, but you only get three ruined pours before the furnace shuts down.
