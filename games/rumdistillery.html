Batch 1 of 3 — watch the still and collect at the right moment!
How to play: A marker sweeps back and forth across the still's temperature gauge. Press Space or click Collect while it's inside the green "hearts" band for a pure batch — too early (left of the band) runs weak, too late (right of the band) runs impure. Collect three batches, then drag the blend sliders (or use arrow keys on a focused slider) to mix them until the result lands inside the target proof range, and click Bottle This Blend to finish the round.
