Rare Book Spine Titling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Title 1/8
How to play: A moving pressure needle sweeps back and forth across a gauge for each letter of the title. Click, tap, or press Space/Enter when the needle is inside the gold "clean zone" to stamp that letter onto the spine in gold leaf. Stamp too early or too late (needle in the red) and the letter smudges or ghosts, costing a life. Each new title has more letters and a narrower, faster-moving clean zone — lose all 3 lives and the spine is ruined!
