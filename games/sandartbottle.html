Click a sand color swatch, then click-and-drag downward inside your bottle to pour that color — the longer and steadier your drag, the more sand falls into that layer. Watch the pour amount: too short under-fills a stripe, dragging too far overfills it and throws off the pattern. Build up layers to match the striped reference on the left, then click Finish Bottle to see your score. You have a limited number of pours each design, and later designs use more, thinner stripes.
