A patch of veneer has chipped away from the tabletop, leaving a bare hole of a particular shape with the wood grain running at a particular angle. Study the dashed hole, then press 1–6, tap, or click the one donor patch below that matches both its outline and its grain direction exactly before the timer runs out. A correct match glues in seamlessly; a wrong shape or crooked grain leaves a visible seam and costs you a life — three bad patches and the tabletop is ruined!
