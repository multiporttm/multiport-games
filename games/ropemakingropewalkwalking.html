Rope Making Ropewalk Walking
Rope Laid: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0 Lives: 3
Switch tracks to dodge posts and grab fiber bundles as you walk the rope out.
How to play: You walk backward down a long ropewalk shed, three worn tracks running side by side beneath your feet, while a spinning jack behind you twists out the rope you are laying. Switch between the top, middle and bottom track with the up and down arrow keys, W and S, the on-screen buttons, or a swipe or drag, dodging the wooden support posts, low beams and stray rope coils that appear ahead in each track. Clear track is safe to walk; clipping an obstacle costs you one of your three lives and knocks your footing for a moment, while scooping up a golden fiber bundle along the way adds bonus points on top of the distance you cover. The jack spins faster and obstacles come thicker the further you walk, so the shed only gets more hazardous as your rope grows longer. Lose all three lives and the walk ends, scored on how much rope you managed to lay and how many bundles you gathered along the way.
