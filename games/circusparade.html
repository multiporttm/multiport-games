Circus Parade
Score: 0 Formation: 100% Hi: 0
How to play: Use Arrow Keys/WASD, or drag on touch, to steer the parade lead down the street. Your performers follow behind with a short delay — weave too sharply and they'll fall out of formation. Dodge street obstacles (cones, puddles, lampposts). Press Space or tap the crowd to wave and pump up crowd excitement for bonus points. Survive the whole parade route with high formation and excitement for the best score!
