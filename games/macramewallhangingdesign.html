How to play: Study the target wall hanging at the top, then recreate it in your frame below using the palette of knot sections, bead accents, and fringe lengths. Click/tap a palette item to select it (or press its number key), then click/tap a frame slot to place it — use Arrow Keys + Enter to select a slot and Backspace to clear one. Press Submit Design (or Enter with a filled frame) to check your work. Match enough slots to pass — briefs get longer and pickier each round, and 3 wrong submissions ends the game!
