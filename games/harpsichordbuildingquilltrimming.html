Harpsichord Building Quill Trimming
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Quill 1
A paring knife sweeps back and forth across the raw crow quill clamped in the vise. A gold band marks the exact width where the tongue will flex with just the right spring. Press Space, tap, or click 🔪 Trim the instant the blade crosses into the gold band to pare a perfect plectrum tongue. Trimming too wide or too narrow splits the brittle feather — three split quills and the batch is scrapped! Finish every quill in a batch to move on to a finer, narrower batch.
