Rocket Staging Separation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Stage 1/6
How to play: Watch the needle sweep back and forth across the dial as the rocket climbs. Press Space, click, or tap the moment the needle is inside the glowing green window to separate the stage and ignite the next one. Fire too early or too late and you waste a life. The needle speeds up and the window shrinks with every staging event — complete all 6 to reach orbit, but lose all 3 lives and the mission is lost!
