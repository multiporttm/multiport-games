How to play: Move with WASD or Arrow keys (or drag anywhere on mobile). Click/tap a spot to throw a snowball toward it. Duck behind the snow forts (gray mounds) to block incoming snowballs — they can't hit you while you're behind one. Score points for every opponent you hit; lose health when you get hit. Survive and rack up hits before your health reaches zero — more opponents join as your score climbs!
