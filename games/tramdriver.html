Hold Go (or Up Arrow / W) to accelerate and Brake (or Down Arrow / S) to slow down. Stop inside the marked station zone at every stop, and watch for pedestrians and cars crossing the tracks — hitting one costs a life! At junctions (fork markers), click/tap left or right side of the track, or press A/D, to choose your route. Streets get busier every round.
