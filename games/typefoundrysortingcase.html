Typefoundry Sorting Case
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round 1 Pieces left: 0
How to play: A mixed pile of metal type sorts sits in the top tray. A row of case compartments, each labelled with a letter, waits below. Click or tap a piece in the tray, then click or tap the compartment with its matching letter to file it away. Sort every piece before the clock runs out to clear the round and earn a time bonus — misfile a piece and you lose a little time. Each round adds more letters and a bigger pile.
