Sweep the ladle along the casting table and hold Pour to lay down an even sheet of molten pipe metal.
How to play: A crucible of molten tin-lead alloy sits above a long woolen-cloth casting table. Move the ladle left and right with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging on the canvas, and hold the Pour button (or hold Space, or press and hold the on-screen button) to release metal onto the table under the ladle. Keep an eye on the temperature gauge: pouring while the metal runs too hot makes the sheet ripple and thin out, while pouring too slowly lets it cool and solidify into lumpy blobs with holes. Sweep steadily across the full length of the table to build a smooth, evenly thick sheet before the crucible empties. Casting too thin or leaving gaps ruins the sheet and costs you points. Finish enough flawless sheets before you run out of alloy to post a high score.
