Typefoundry Alloy Mixing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1/8
How to play: The recipe card shows the target percentage of Lead, Tin, and Antimony for this batch. Hold the + button (or Q/W/E keys) under each metal's ladle to pour it into the pot — release (or tap - / A/S/D) to stop or back off. Each metal's gauge must land in its green tolerance band at once before you click/tap Cast Batch (or press Enter). Cast with any metal outside its band and the batch is ruined, costing a life. Targets get trickier and tolerances tighter every round.
