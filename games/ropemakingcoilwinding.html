Guide the winding hand around the spool, laying each loop snug against the last.
How to play: Rope feeds steadily off the ropewalk toward a winding spool, and your job is to guide the winding hand so each loop lays down snugly beside the loop before it, building a neat, even hank. Move the hand with the arrow keys or WASD, or drag it with your mouse or finger, tracing a loop around the spool at a steady pace — the rope behind the hand only winds in while you keep moving it along the spool's rim. Lay a loop at the right spacing, next to the previous one, and it locks in cleanly for points; lay one too fast, too slow, or crossing an old loop and it piles up into a lumpy tangle that wastes rope and slows your score. Watch the slack meter at the top: it fills as rope feeds off the walk faster than you wind, and if it ever tops out the slack hits the floor and the hank is ruined. Finish a hank by filling the spool with clean loops before the slack runs out, then start the next hank — each one feeding a little faster than the last.
