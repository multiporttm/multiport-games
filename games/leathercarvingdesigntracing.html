Leather Carving Design Tracing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Design 1/8
How to play: A glowing bead travels along the guide path cut into the damp leather — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your stylus tip inside the tan tolerance band around it. Wander too far and your tracing meter drains; empty it and the design is ruined, costing a life. Stay close the whole way to finish tracing the pattern and move to the next hide. The path grows more intricate and the band narrows every design — lose all 3 lives and the hide is spoiled!
