🕯️ Candle Making Wax Pouring
Hold the pour button to fill the mold with melted wax. Release right at the fill line for a perfect candle. Pour too little or overflow the mold and you waste wax.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) to pour hot wax into the mold. Watch the rising wax level and release right inside the gold target band to cast a perfect candle. Releasing too early leaves it underfilled; holding too long makes it overflow — both drain your Batch Wax meter. Fill enough candles before the batch runs dry!
