Wax Heat Control
A heated trowel sweeps back and forth over the reed plate seam, melting beeswax as it goes. An amber zone marks the moment the wax is perfectly melted and workable. Press Space, tap the pot, or click the big button the instant the trowel is in the amber zone to press a clean, even seal. Seal too early or too late and the wax drips or scorches, draining Wax Heat Control — let it run empty and the batch is ruined! Seal enough seams to melt a hotter, trickier pot of wax.
