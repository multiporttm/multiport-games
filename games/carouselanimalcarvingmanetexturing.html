Hold the rasp and drag along the glowing curl to texture each lock of mane.
How to play: A carousel horse neck shows a faint glowing curl tracing where the current lock of mane should flow from its root to its tip. Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) then drag with the mouse, finger, or arrow keys to pull the rasp along the curl, following it closely from root to tip. Staying tight on the curl fills your texture progress and builds a streak that recovers grip strength; drifting off the curl makes the rasp skip across the grain and drains grip fast. Finish every lock before your grip strength runs out to texture the whole mane and move on to a wilder, curlier mane.
