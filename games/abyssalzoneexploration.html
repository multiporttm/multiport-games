Abyssal Zone Exploration
Found: 0 / 0 Best: 0 Depth: 0m
How to play: Use Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag/tap on the water to pilot your submersible. Your floodlight only reaches a short distance, so explore carefully to reveal points of interest scattered across the abyssal zone. Every move drains your battery — watch the gauge and always leave enough charge to glide back to the glowing home base before you're stranded in the dark. Collect as many points of interest as you can each dive!
