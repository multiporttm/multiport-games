Submarine Depth Gauge Reading
Score: 0 Lives: ❤️❤️❤️ Round: 1 Best: 0
Watch the depth and pressure gauges. Hold Up Arrow / W (blow ballast, rise) or Down Arrow / S (flood ballast, dive) — or use the on-screen buttons — to keep the needle inside the green target band without crossing crush depth. Hold the band through each round's timer to advance; rougher seas and tighter bands come with every round. Three hull strikes and the dive is over.
