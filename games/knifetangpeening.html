Watch the power meter rise and fall. Press Space or click/tap when the needle is inside the safe tap zone to deliver a light, well-placed hammer blow that spreads the tang evenly over the pommel. Tap too hard (outside the zone) and you risk cracking the handle — flatten the tang fully across increasingly fragile handle materials before you crack it three times!
