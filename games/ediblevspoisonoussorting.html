Edible vs Poisonous Sorting
Score: 0 Round: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Mushrooms ride the conveyor belt toward your basket. Check the cap: smooth, evenly colored caps are edible; spotted, bright, or ragged caps are poisonous. Press Left Arrow or E (or tap "Edible") to sort it edible, or Right Arrow or P (or tap "Poisonous") to sort it poisonous, before it reaches the end of the belt. Speed increases and trickier lookalikes appear each round — you have 3 lives.
