How to play: Roll the brayer (ink roller) across the carved woodblock by dragging it, or steering it with the arrow keys or WASD. Every pass leaves a thin coat of ink; keep moving to spread coverage evenly, since dwelling too long in one spot over-inks it into a soggy, score-costing blot. Reach full even coverage of the block before your ink supply runs out to pull a clean print — the smoother your coat, the bigger your score.
