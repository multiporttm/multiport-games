Select a stop knob from the tray and carry it to its matching labeled slot on the jamb.
How to play: A tray of engraved ivory stop knobs sits beside the console jamb, each one labeled with a rank name, while a row of empty drawknob slots waits above, each with its own engraved label. Move the selector with the arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen arrows, or by tapping, onto a knob in the tray and press Grab (Enter, Space, or the on-screen button) to pick it up. With a knob held, move the selector onto the slot whose engraved label matches it and press Grab again to seat it with a solid click. Fill every slot correctly before the round timer runs out. Seating a knob in the wrong slot pops it right back out and costs you time, so read each label carefully. Fit every knob on the jamb to post a high score.
