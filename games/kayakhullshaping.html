Kayak Hull Shaping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Hull 1/8
How to play: A glowing line sweeps across the hull mold showing the target profile — drag your mouse or finger up/down (or use the Up/Down Arrow Keys) to keep your sanding block riding exactly on that line as it travels left to right. Stray too high (a high spot) or too low (cut too deep) and your surface-quality meter drains; empty it and the hull is ruined, costing a life. Finish the full sweep to move to the next hull. Every round the hull profile gets more intricate and the tolerance tightens — lose all 3 lives and the job is scrapped!
