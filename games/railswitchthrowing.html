Rail Switch Throwing
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Goal: Each incoming train shows a colored signal for its destination track (top = red, middle = yellow, bottom = cyan). Click/tap the lever for that track, or press 1/2/3 (or ↑/→/↓), to throw the switch before the train reaches the junction line. Right switch = safely through; wrong switch, or no switch in time, costs a life. Trains come faster and closer together every level. Three lives.
