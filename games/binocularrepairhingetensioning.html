Binocular Repair Hinge Tensioning
Hinges: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Turn the tension screw until the feel-gauge needle rests in the green band, then hold it through the test swing.
How to play: A center-hinge tension screw controls how stiffly the binocular barrels swing. Press and hold, or tap, TIGHTEN or LOOSEN (or hold the Right/Left arrow keys) to turn the screw; the feel-gauge needle swings left or right as tension changes. Stop turning once the needle settles inside the green sweet-spot band — too loose and the hinge will not hold its spacing, too tight and you strip the threads, ending the attempt. Once the needle is in the band, hold your position through a full test swing of the barrels to lock the tension in and finish the hinge, then move on to a hinge that drifts differently before your driver grip strength runs out.
