Each passenger shows a ticket. Check the type and details, then click Valid (accept) or Invalid (reject) before the door-closing bar runs out. Watch for expired dates, wrong routes, and day passes used after their window. Right calls score points and speed up the line; wrong calls cost points and time. Tickets get trickier the further you get!
