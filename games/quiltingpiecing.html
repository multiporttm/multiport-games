Study the quilt block diagram on the left — each patch has a color, a triangle orientation, and a number showing sewing order. Click a tray patch to select it, click it again to rotate it 90°, then click the next numbered slot on the sewing board to place it. Placing a patch backwards or out of sequence costs a life. Complete the block before you run out of lives to move on to a more intricate block!
