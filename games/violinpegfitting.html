Violin Peg Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Peg 1/8
How to play: Drag left/right (or use the Left/Right Arrow keys) to move the reamer and match the drifting taper target. Hold inside the gold zone to fill the meter. Once the hole is reamed, fit the peg: keep the turning pressure inside the zone — too far left and it slips loose, too far right and it sticks — to seat it fully. Miss too many times on one peg and you lose a life. Taper targets drift faster and the zone narrows with every peg — seat all 8 pegs to win!
