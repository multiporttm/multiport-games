Opera House Rigging Haul
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Scene 1
How to play: Hold Up Arrow / W or the Haul Up button to pull the backdrop up on the rope; hold Down Arrow / S or Run Out to let it drop. Guide the backdrop into the glowing target band and keep it there to fill the hold meter before the cue timer runs out. Scenery gets heavier (faster, harder to control) and target bands get narrower with every scene. Three failed cues end the show.
