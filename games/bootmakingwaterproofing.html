Seal Coverage
An applicator sweeps around the dial below the boot. Tap the canvas, press the button, or hit Space/Enter the instant it crosses the green zone to lay down a clean, even coat of sealant. Miss and the coat pools or thins, costing coverage — empty it and the boot leaks for good. Lay 5 coats to finish a panel and speed up the applicator.
