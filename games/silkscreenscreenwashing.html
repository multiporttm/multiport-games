Silkscreen Screen Washing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1 Time: 20
How to play: Move the spray nozzle with your mouse, finger, the on-screen arrows, or the Arrow Keys — it sprays continuously wherever it goes. Sweep it across every mesh cell to wash out the ink and stencil residue (red fades to clean). Any cell left untouched keeps drying and will clog permanently (shown in black) if you take too long over it. Clear the whole screen with few or no clogs before the timer runs out. Screens get bigger and dry faster every round — ruin 3 screens and the run ends.
