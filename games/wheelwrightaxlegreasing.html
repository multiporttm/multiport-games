Wheelwright Axle Greasing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Axle 1
The wooden axle is up on the stand, its journals bare and dry. A grease gun follows the cursor shown over the shaft — use the ◀ ▶ arrow keys, drag, or the buttons to slide it over the next bare journal, then press Space, hold the mouse, or hold 🛢️ Pack to squeeze in grease until the gauge fills to the marked band, no more. Pack a journal too thin and it will run dry on the road; pack it too thick and it squeezes out and fouls the wheel — three fouled journals and the axle is pulled for rework! Pack every journal on an axle to move on to a longer axle with tighter bands.
