How to play: Click or tap empty chart cells to lay waypoints connecting your ship from the start port to the destination, one segment at a time. Each segment must stay clear of reefs, shallow water and rival territory. Arrow keys move the cursor and Space/Enter places a waypoint there; Backspace undoes the last one. Reach the destination within your distance budget and click Submit Route for the captain's approval. Three bad submissions and the voyage is scrubbed!
