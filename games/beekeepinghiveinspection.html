Beekeeping Hive Inspection
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Click/tap the frame (or press Space) to pull it out of the hive and look closely. Spot signs of trouble — mottled brood (disease), tiny reddish mites on larvae, or a peanut-shaped queen cell — then choose Keep for a healthy frame, Treat for mites or disease, or Remove for a queen cell frame (prevents swarming). You can also press K/T/R. Wrong calls cost a life. Each round adds more frames and sneakier, subtler signs!
