Drag the gimbal so its pins align with the marked holes, then rotate it level
Drag the camera gimbal assembly (or use Arrow Keys) onto the drone's frame so its mounting pins line up with the marked holes in the diagram. Use the Rotate buttons (or Q/E) to level it within the tolerance band shown — any wobble (misalignment) fails the fit. Click Fasten Gimbal to lock it in. Gimbal shapes and hole patterns get trickier each round.
