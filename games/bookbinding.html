Bookbinding
Click the pages in order 1, 2, 3... to bind the book!
Books bound: 0 | Lives: 3
Click the scattered numbered page cards in ascending order (1, 2, 3...) to bind them into a book before the timer runs out. Click a card out of order and you lose a life; complete a book and the next one has more pages and less time. Reach 0 lives and the game ends.
