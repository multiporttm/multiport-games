Library Bookshelving
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ♥♥♥ Level 1
Click a book, then click the shelf slot where it belongs.
Return cart (shelve before it overflows!)
Call numbers get trickier and the cart fills faster as you level up.
How to play: Books pile up on the return cart, each with a call number. Click (or tap) a book to select it, then click the empty shelf slot that keeps the whole shelf in ascending call-number order, or drag the book straight onto its slot. Shelve correctly for points; a wrong slot or an overflowing cart costs a life. You have 3 lives — survive as many levels as you can as the cart fills faster and the call numbers get trickier. Keyboard: Tab/Arrow keys to pick a book, Enter to drop it on the highlighted slot.
