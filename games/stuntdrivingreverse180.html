Stunt Driving Reverse 180
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Run 1/10
Steer: —
How to play: A speed needle sweeps the dial. An arrow tells you which way to steer. Press Left Arrow/Right Arrow (or tap the left/right half of the canvas) matching the arrow at the exact moment the needle sits inside the glowing green speed window to pull off a clean bootlegger reverse 180. Steer the wrong way, or steer outside the window, and the car spins out — costing a life. The window narrows and the needle sweeps faster every run. Survive all 3 lives through 10 runs!
