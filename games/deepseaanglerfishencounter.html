Deep Sea Anglerfish Encounter
Agitation: 0% Progress: 0% Best: 0
Use Arrow Keys / WASD or drag to steer your submersible leftward past the anglerfish toward safety. Staying near its glowing lure or bumping it raises its Agitation — if it maxes out, it charges and lunges at you. Dodge sudden lunges and keep moving to reach the far side!
