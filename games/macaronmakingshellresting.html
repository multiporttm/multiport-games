Macaron Making: Shell Resting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 🤍🤍🤍 Round 1 Trays left: 0
How to play: Each tray shows a skin-formation gauge (how dry the piped shells are) and a shrinking humidity timer ring. Click/tap a tray, or press its number key, to move it to the oven. Send it while the gauge is inside the green target window for a perfect bake. Send it too early (still wet) or too late (over-dried) and it's ruined — and if the timer runs out before you send it, the humidity ruins it too. More trays arrive and the windows get tighter every round!
