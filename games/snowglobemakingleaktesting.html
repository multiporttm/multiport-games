Snow Globe Making: Leak Testing
Round 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Click/drag the globe (or use Left/Right Arrow Keys) to tilt it and watch closely for tiny bubble trails or drips escaping the seal — that means a leak. Press P or click PASS if it looks clean, or F / click FAIL if you spot a leak, before the timer runs out. Leaks get subtler each round!
