ReadyDistance: 0m / 500mBest: 0m
How to play: Your ore cart rolls automatically along the rails. Use Up/Down Arrow (or the Lane buttons / swipe) to switch between the 3 tracks and dodge collapsed tunnels. Press Space or the PUSH button when the white marker is inside the green zone of the gauge to boost your momentum — momentum drains over time and crashing into a collapsed tunnel drains it fast. Reach the surface elevator before momentum hits zero!
