Each broken slot in the gear train shows how many teeth the missing gear needs to mesh with its neighbors. Click (or use Left/Right Arrow + Enter) to pick the replacement gear with the matching tooth count from the bin below, then click the broken slot to fit it. Fit every gear correctly to make the train turn end to end. Wrong gears cost a life — you have 3 lives. Trains get longer each round.
