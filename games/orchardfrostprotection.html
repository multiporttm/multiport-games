Orchard Frost Protection
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Night 1
How to play: Press W or tap the left fan icon to toggle the wind machines, and S or tap the right sprinkler icon to toggle irrigation sprinklers. Watch the blossom temperature — when it nears freezing, turn on the right protection to hold it up. Running equipment burns fuel or water, shown in the bars below, so switch off when you don't need it. Let blossoms freeze too many times and you lose a life. Survive the night using as little fuel/water as possible for a higher score.
