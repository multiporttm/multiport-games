Handle Smoothness
Drag the sanding block back and forth over the raw wooden fork handle and press the instant the pass marker glides through the glowing zone, laying down one clean, even pass. A clean pass smooths the handle and raises your score; pressing too early or late leaves rough patches and drains your smoothness meter. Land 5 clean passes to finish a grit stage and move to a finer, faster one. Tap/click the block, or press Space/Enter, at the right instant. Empty your smoothness meter and the handle is ruined — game over.
