Each facet needs a target angle shown above the dial. Drag the angle dial (or use ← / → arrow keys) until the readout matches, then click 💎 Cut Facet or press Space to lock it in. Land within the tolerance to cut a true facet — miss too far and the facet is cut wrong, scarring the gem. Angle every facet on the gem correctly — three bad cuts and the stone is lost!
