🔥 Teacup Making: Kiln Firing
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
How to play: The kiln roars as its temperature needle sweeps around the firing dial. Click / tap the kiln or the Start button, or press Space / Enter, exactly as the needle passes through the glowing green zone to vent or stoke the heat for a perfect firing stage. Miss the window and scorching or underfiring damages your glaze quality meter; empty it and the teacup cracks in the kiln for good. Land 5 clean stages to finish a round — the needle spins faster and the safe zone narrows every round after.
