Furniture Caning Weaving
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Pass 1/12
How to play: A sequence of weaving passes flashes across the chair frame — vertical (↑), horizontal (←), and two diagonals (↗ and ↘). Watch it carefully, then repeat the exact sequence using the Arrow Keys or the matching on-screen buttons to thread the cane strands through the holes. Each pass you weave correctly adds a new strand to the octagon pattern; a wrong strand costs a life. The sequence grows longer and faster every round — survive all 12 rounds to bind off the edge and finish the seat!
