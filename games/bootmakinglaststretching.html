Leather Torn!

A tension gauge needle sweeps across the dial as you pull leather over the wooden last. Click, tap, or press Space/Enter right as the needle crosses the glowing green zone to lock the pincers for an even stretch. Even stretches score points and keep the upper's tension healthy; uneven pulls strain the leather. Finish each pass to move faster to the next, tighter pass. Let the tension meter run out and the last session ends.
