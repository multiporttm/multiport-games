Each batch calls for a target mix of lavender, vanilla and rose oil drops. Tap the oil buttons to add drops to the pot, then lock it in once the ratio matches the recipe card.
Each round shows a recipe card with a target ratio of lavender, vanilla and rose oil drops. Tap the three oil buttons to add drops to the pot, watching the mix bar update live, then tap Lock In once your ratio is close to the target. A good match raises your score; a bad match wastes fragrance and drains your Fragrance Stock meter. You can also add drops with keys 1, 2, 3 and lock in with Enter.
