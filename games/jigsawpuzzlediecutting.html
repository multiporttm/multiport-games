Jigsaw Puzzle Die Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Pattern 1/10
How to play: Drag the printed puzzle sheet with your mouse or finger (or nudge it with the Arrow Keys) until it lines up under the die press's cutting grid — the alignment meter turns green when you're close. Press Space or tap STAMP to punch the sheet. Stamp while misaligned and the pieces come out with cut-off edges, costing a life. Each new pattern packs in more pieces and demands tighter alignment — run out of lives and the batch is scrapped!
