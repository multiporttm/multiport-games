Auto Body Frame Alignment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Each bar is a laser measurement point on the frame; the glowing band is the factory spec it must land in. Click/tap a point (or use Left/Right Arrow to select) then drag up/down (or hold Up/Down Arrow) to pull it toward spec — pulling one point also tugs the points beside it, since the frame is all one piece. Get every point inside its band at the same time before the clock runs out to pass the round. More points and tighter bands arrive each round — run out of time 3 times and the frame is scrapped!
