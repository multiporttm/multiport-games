Weaving Beater Adjustment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Beat 1
How to play: Hold down Space, click, or touch to swing and charge the beater's pressure, watching the power meter and the swinging marker. Release right as the marker crosses the glowing beat-up window with the pressure meter in the highlighted target band to pack an even row. Bad beats cost a life; the window and pressure band shrink every level.
