Zones of the wet sheet light up one at a time with a shrinking ring around each — click/tap the zone (or press the matching 1-9 number key) right as the ring reaches the target size to press it down evenly onto the felt. Press too early or too late and that zone creases or stays damp. Couch every zone well to peel a clean, even sheet free; sheets get more zones as you progress!
