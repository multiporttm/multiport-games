📦 Blender Making Packaging
Finished blenders drop down three conveyor lanes. Slide the packing crate left and right to catch each one in its matching colored box before it hits the floor.
Move the packing crate between the three lanes — drag/tap left or right of the crate, or use the Left/Right arrow keys — to catch each falling blender in the lane it drops in. Catching one scores points and speeds up the line; letting one hit the floor dents your Line Stability meter. Empty Line Stability and the packing line shuts down.
