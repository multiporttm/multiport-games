Cheesemaking Aging Room Rotation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
How to play: Rows of cheese wheels age on cellar shelves. A glowing cue briefly highlights one wheel at a time — click or tap that exact wheel (or press its number key) while it's glowing to rotate or flip it for even rind development. React in time for a good turn; miss the cue, turn the wrong wheel, or let a cue expire and mold spots start creeping in. Lose three wheels to mold and the aging room is ruined. More wheels, more shelves, and faster cues arrive every round!
