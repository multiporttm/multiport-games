Metal Quenching Tempering
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Quench: each metal needs a specific liquid. Click/tap Water, Oil or Brine (or press 1/2/3) while the cooling temperature marker sits inside the glowing quench window. Temper: pick the correct oven heat — Low, Med or High (1/2/3 again) — then press Space or tap Pull when the rising duration gauge is inside the hardness window. Wrong choices or bad timing cost a life. Windows get tighter and the metal cools faster each level.
