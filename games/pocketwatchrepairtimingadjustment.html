Use Left/Right Arrow keys, the ◄ ► buttons, or drag on the canvas to nudge the regulator lever. The needle on the timing machine gauge tracks the beat rate — bring it into the glowing safe zone and hold it steady there to fill the hold meter and pass inspection. Push the lever too far and the escapement slips, failing the round. Starting rates drift further off and the safe zone narrows each round.
