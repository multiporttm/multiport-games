Kite Building Sail Attaching
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Sail 1 ♥♥♥
Press and hold on the glowing pin (or hold Space) to pull the sail taut — a needle climbs the tension gauge. Release (or let go of Space) while the needle is inside the green zone to pin that edge. Pin every point in order, left to right along the gauge, without tearing the fabric. Bigger sails with tighter tension zones appear each round!
