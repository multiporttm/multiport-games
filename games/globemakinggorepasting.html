A pasted gore swells and shrinks against the curved plaster sphere as the paste soaks in. A gold zone marks the exact amount of pressure needed to press out every bubble without tearing the damp paper. Press Space, tap, or click 🌐 Smooth the instant the gauge crosses into the gold zone to bond the gore flat. Smoothing too early leaves bubbles, smoothing too late lets it wrinkle — three bad gores and the sphere is ruined! Bond every gore on a sphere to begin a tighter, higher-gore globe.
