⚖️ Hand Balancing
The hour and minute hands are spinning out of true on the calibration spindle. Hold LEFT or RIGHT to counter the drift and keep the needle inside the green zone for the whole timer.
The clock hands drift off balance on their own. Press and hold the LEFT or RIGHT button (or hold the Left/Right arrow keys, or touch the left/right half of the beam) to nudge the needle back toward center. Keep the needle inside the green calibration zone for the full hold timer to pass the round - drifting too far off either edge costs you a spindle. Lose all three and calibration fails!
