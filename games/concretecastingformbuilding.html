Click or tap grid cells along the dashed mold outline to place plywood formwork panels — choose Small (1x2) or Large (2x1) panels, press Rotate to spin the panel, or use arrow keys to move the cursor and R to rotate and Enter/Space to place. Cover every outline cell with no gaps or overlaps before the pour clock drains, then press Lock Formwork to pour. Full coverage with no wasted panels scores the most, and each new mold brings a trickier shape.
