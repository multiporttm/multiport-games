Taxi Cab Wash Bay
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
How to play: Each cab must go through four stages in order: Soap, Brush, Rinse, Dry. A needle sweeps back and forth on the gauge — press Space, click, or tap GO while it's in the green zone to pass the stage. Miss the green zone and the cab's quality drops; miss too many times on one cab and you lose a life. Finish all four stages to send the cab out clean and score points. The needle moves faster and the green zone shrinks every level.
