Bioluminescent Plankton Trail
Score: 0 Pattern: 1 Hi: 0
How to play: Glide your glowing creature with Arrow Keys / WASD, or by clicking/dragging/touching the water. Your movement disturbs bioluminescent plankton, leaving a glowing trail. Visit the pulsing target markers in order to trace the requested light pattern before the glow timer runs out. Complete a pattern to advance to a harder one — run out of time and the trail fades for good.
