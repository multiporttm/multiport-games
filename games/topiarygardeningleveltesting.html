Topiary Level Testing

Drag or use the arrow keys to tilt the spirit level, and tap "Mark Level" when the bubble sits in the center zone.
Each hedge top needs checking with a spirit level before it is signed off. Drag left/right on the bar (or use the ←/→ arrow keys) to tilt the level until its bubble settles in the glowing center zone, then press Mark Level or tap Space to confirm. Each hedge gives you a shrinking time window — the tolerance zone narrows as you progress through the row. Three missed calls and the inspection ends.
