🪡 Doormat Making Edge Binding
Drag the needle along the winding edge of the doormat from the start pin to the finish pin, staying inside the stitch corridor to bind the edge tightly.
Press and drag the needle (mouse, touch, or Arrow keys) from the start pin to the finish pin, following the winding dashed edge line. Stay inside the colored stitch corridor — straying outside it frays the thread and drains your Thread Tension meter. Reach the finish pin to complete the binding and move to a tighter, trickier edge; let the tension meter empty and the binding tears.
