How to play: Click or tap any grid cell to fire a sonar ping (use Arrow Keys to move the cursor and Enter/Space to ping on keyboard). Each ping reports a return-signal strength — hot colors mean the wreck is close, cold colors mean it's far. Use the clues to narrow your search and ping the wreck's exact cell before your limited pings run out. Finding a wreck grants bonus pings and starts a new, trickier search.
