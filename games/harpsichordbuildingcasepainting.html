Harpsichord Building Case Painting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Panel 1
The brush glides back and forth above the chalked scroll pattern on the case panel as the loading gauge sweeps across the scale below. A gold band marks the exact amount of paint the bristles are carrying for a smooth, even stroke. Press Space, tap, or click 🎨 Paint the instant the gauge crosses into the gold band to lay down a clean decorative stroke. Painting outside the band drips pigment and ruins a stroke — three ruined strokes and the panel has to be sanded back to bare wood! Finish every stroke on a panel to move on to a panel with a finer, trickier pattern.
