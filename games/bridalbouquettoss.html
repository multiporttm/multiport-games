Bridal Bouquet Toss
Toss 1/5 | Hits: 0 | Hi: 0
How to play: You're the bride, back turned to the crowd! Press Space, click or tap once to lock your aim as the marker sweeps over the shifting crowd, then press again to lock your power as the meter fills, launching the bouquet. Land it in the hands of the gold-highlighted target guest — the crowd keeps shifting between tosses, so time each throw carefully across all 5 attempts!
