Cooperage Barrel Head Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1
Order: —
How to play: A round barrel head is split into numbered wedge pieces scattered around the rim. The diagram above the barrel shows the required fitting order. Click/tap the wedges in that exact order to drive them home; a wedge tapped out of order springs loose and costs a life. Fit all wedges before the "stave stress" meter maxes out to seal the head. Each round adds more wedges and shuffles their position, so watch the diagram carefully. Use number keys 1-9 as a keyboard alternative, matched to wedge positions clockwise from the top.
