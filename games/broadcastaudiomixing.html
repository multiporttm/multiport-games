Every mic's speaker volume drifts on its own. Use Left/Right (or tap a channel) to select a fader and Up/Down (or drag/scroll it) to ride the gain, keeping each channel's output needle in the green zone. Hold a channel in the red too long and you lose a life. More mics and faster swings arrive every level.
