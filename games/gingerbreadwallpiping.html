Gingerbread Wall Piping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Wall 1/8
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) along the glowing guide path edging the gingerbread wall to pipe a line of icing. Hold your icing tip inside the pink tolerance tube to keep tracing — stray off it and your pressure needle drifts toward the red zone. Keep the needle in the green safe zone: too little pressure and the line breaks, too much and it blobs. Either one costs a life! Finish the path before pressure fails to decorate the wall and move to the next, trickier trim — lose all 3 lives and the piping bag is ruined.
