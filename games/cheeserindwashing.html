Cheese Rind Washing
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Each wheel has a moisture bar that slowly dries out. Use Left/Right arrows (or tap a wheel) to select one, then press Space (or tap it again) to wash it with brine. Keep every wheel's moisture inside the glowing target band — too dry or over-washed and the wheel spoils, costing a life. More wheels and faster drying arrive every level.
