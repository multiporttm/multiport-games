Archaeology Excavation Gridding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Square 1
How to play: Each dig square hides an artifact at a random depth. Hold Space, your mouse, or your finger on the canvas to lower your trowel and dig deeper — release or tap Stop & Brush (Enter) the instant the warning line shows you're near the find. Dig too fast or go too deep past the artifact and you damage it, costing a life. Clear 3 squares to win — grid squares get deeper and trowel speed increases as you advance.
