Moon Landing
Fuel: 100 Alt: 0 Speed: 0.0 Level: 1 Best Fuel: 0
Use Arrow Keys / WASD (Up/W = thrust, Left/Right or A/D = rotate) or the on-screen buttons. Descend slowly and land level on the glowing green pad — too fast or too tilted and you'll crash! Fuel is limited, so thrust wisely. Score is your remaining fuel on a successful landing.
