Music Box Governor Regulation
Lives: 3Level 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Keep the needle inside the glowing target band
Left/Right or A/D open/close the flap · Drag the flap bar directly
Hold a music box's playback tempo steady by regulating the governor's spinning air-brake flap. Use Left/Right or A/D, or drag the flap bar, to open or close the flap. Spring tension keeps drifting the tempo, so keep adjusting to stay inside the glowing target band before your off-tempo meter maxes out. Target tempos get trickier and drift gets wilder as you level up.
