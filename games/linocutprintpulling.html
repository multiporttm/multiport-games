Linocut Print Pulling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Print 1/8
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to rub the baren over every patch of the sheet. Keep your stroke speed inside the green band on the pressure gauge — crawl too slowly and a patch stays pale and unprinted, rub too fast and the patch smudges black, costing a life. Clear every patch before the timer runs out to pull a clean print and move on. Each print sheet is bigger than the last — lose all 3 lives and the edition is spoiled!
