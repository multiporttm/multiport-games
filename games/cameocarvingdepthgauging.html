Cameo Carving Depth Gauging
Zone: 1/4 Round: 1 Score: 0 Best: 0
Carve depth gauge
Click a zone on the shell relief to select it, then hold Hold to Carve (or press and hold Space) to cut it deeper — watch the gauge bar climb toward the gold target line. Release and click Set Depth (or let go of Space) once you are close: carve too shallow and the figure looks flat, carve past the line and you gouge straight through the shell. Gauge all four zones to their exact depth to finish the relief.
