Bladesmithing Handle Fitting
Handle: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Score: 0 Hi: 0
Keyboard: Left/Right to select a pin hole, Enter to tighten it.
How to play: A new handle scale is fitted onto the tang with its pin holes numbered. Memorize the order while the numbers are shown, then tap each hole (or press Left/Right to move the cursor and Enter to tighten) in that exact order. Tightening a pin out of order, or missing a hole, is a misaligned pin and costs a life. Handle shapes get more intricate — more pins, tighter tolerance — every round.
