Chainsaw Carving Torch Finishing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Panel 1
A carved wood panel waits to be flame-finished. Press and hold Space, or press and hold the 🔥 Hold Torch button, to build up char — then release right as the char meter sits inside the bright target band for a rich, even scorch. Release too early and the panel stays pale; hold too long past the band and it burns black. Three ruined panels and the finishing pass is over!
