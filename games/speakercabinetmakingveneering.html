🪵 Speaker Cabinet Making Veneering
Score: 0 Strip 1 Best: 0
Press and drag the roller left to right (mouse or finger) across the glued veneer strip. Keep your dragging speed inside the glowing band on the speedometer - too slow and the glue dries before it bonds, too fast and the veneer bubbles. Reach the right edge with a good Bond meter to finish the strip and move to the next, tighter one.
