How to play: Drag up/down on the sky (or use the Up/Down Arrow Keys) to swing the sextant's horizon mark onto the drifting sun. When you think it's lined up, press Enter/Space or tap 🔭 Shoot to lock your angle reading before the sight timer runs out. Land within tolerance to fix your position — miss too far and you lose a life. Later sights turn overcast (the sun fades in and out) or hit polar-day glare (a low, barely-moving sun with razor-thin tolerance). Survive all five sights with 3 lives!
