Ice Cream Making Scooping
Scoops: 0 | Hi: 0
Press and hold Space, click, or hold the scoop button to dig into the tub — the size gauge needle rises as you dig. Release exactly when the needle is in the green safe zone for a perfectly round scoop. Scoop too little and it crumbles, too much and it is unservable. The ice cream gets firmer and the safe zone narrower each round!
