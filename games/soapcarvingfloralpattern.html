Tap CARVE, click, or press Space when the gouge is lined up with the glowing petal.
How to play: A fresh soap bar waits to have a flower carved into its face. A gouge sweeps steadily around the bar on a rotating guide. Press Space, click, or tap the CARVE button exactly as the gouge crosses the next glowing petal to cut a clean, even groove. A clean cut advances to the next petal around the flower and builds your streak; carving too early or too late tears the petal and chips your soap supply. Carve every petal around the whole flower before your soap supply runs out to finish the pattern and move on to a fuller, faster-spinning flower.
