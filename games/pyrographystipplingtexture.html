Pyrography Stippling Texture
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1
Click/tap inside each glowing zone to stipple dots of burned texture onto the wood. Use Tab to select a zone and Space/Enter to tap it with the keyboard. Build each zone's dot density up to its target darkness — a green ring means it's done. Overdo it and the zone scorches black. Finish every zone to clear the panel; more zones await each round.
