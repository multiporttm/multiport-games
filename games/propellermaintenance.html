How to play: Click/tap the red nicks on each blade to file them smooth. Drag the weight sliders (or focus one and use Arrow Keys) to balance the propeller — watch the vibration meter drop toward the tolerance band in the middle. When the meter sits inside the band, press Run Test to spin it up and certify the propeller. A failed test or an over-filed blade costs a life — lose all three and the shift ends.
