Sundial Making Motto Inscribing
Letter: 1/14 Tablet Left: 14 Score: 0 Best: 0
Watch the chisel swing across the strike bar, then click, tap, or press Space the instant it crosses the glowing zone to inscribe the current letter. Strike true to carve cleanly — mistime it badly and the stone tablet chips.
Inscribing the motto is the final flourish on a hand-carved sundial, where a short Latin phrase like TEMPUS FUGIT is cut letter by letter into a stone tablet set beneath the dial. Each round shows the next letter of the motto above a strike bar, with a chisel indicator swinging back and forth across it at a steady rhythm. Watch the swing carefully and click, tap the canvas, or press Space the instant the indicator passes through the glowing strike zone in the middle of the bar to drive the chisel home. A perfectly timed strike carves the letter crisp and deep and earns a generous bonus, a near-miss still carves a usable but shallow letter, and a badly mistimed strike chips the stone tablet and wastes some of your limited material. The swing speeds up and the strike zone narrows as more letters are completed, so a sharp eye and steady timing will see the full motto inscribed before the tablet runs out.
