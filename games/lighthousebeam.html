Lighthouse Beam
Ships Saved: 0
Best: 0
Time: 60s
Drag across the sky, or use Arrow Keys / A-D, to rotate your lighthouse beam. Each ship flashes a distress light as it nears the rocks — sweep your beam onto the ship while it flashes to guide it safely to harbor. Miss the flash and the ship wrecks. Guide as many ships as you can before the night ends!
