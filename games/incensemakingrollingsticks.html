A good incense stick needs an even, unbroken coat of fragrant paste rolled onto its bamboo core. Press and hold SPACE (or press and hold the canvas/touch) to apply rolling pressure as the core sweeps left to right across the paste strip; release to ease off. A pressure needle shows how hard you are pressing — keep it inside the green even-coat band for as much of the pass as you can. Too little pressure leaves bare, uncoated patches that ruin the stick; too much snaps the brittle bamboo core outright. Each full pass with a high enough even-coat percentage finishes a stick for points based on coverage quality. Finish enough sticks before your bundle of cores runs out to advance to a faster, trickier bundle!
