Roof Thatching Ridge Capping
Caps Pegged: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Walk to a bare patch of ridge and peg a cap-piece down between the wind gusts.
How to play: Walk your thatcher along the finished ridge with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by tapping a side of the canvas. Bare patches of ridge appear along the spine, each one shrinking and eventually blowing away if left too long. Stand on a bare patch and press Peg Cap (Space, the button, or a tap) to drive a cap-piece down — but watch the gust meter: pegging during a calm spell seats the cap firmly, while pegging right as a gust hits blows the cap-piece loose and costs you wind-resistance. Cap every patch along the ridge before the wind meter fills completely and strips the roof bare.
