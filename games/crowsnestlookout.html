Drag the view or use ← → to scan the horizon for threats.

How to play: The horizon is wider than your spyglass view. Drag the sky (or press ←/→) to pan and search for approaching ships, storms and reefs, shown growing larger as they close in. Once a threat is inside your view, press Space or tap 🔔 Ring Alarm to warn the crew about the most urgent one in sight. Spot threats early for more points — let one get too close unspotted and you lose a life!
