How to play: A new book arrives with its title, author, and a short blurb. Read the blurb and pick the correct Subject Heading, Call Number Class, and Format chip for it, then click Catalog It before the timer runs out. Correct, complete entries score points; a wrong field or a missed deadline costs one of your 3 lives. Books get more ambiguous and the timer gets shorter each round. Keyboard: 1-9 picks the highlighted field's option, Tab moves between fields, Enter submits.
