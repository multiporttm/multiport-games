Needle Felting Finishing Trim
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Piece 1
Tiny scissor blades sweep back and forth along the edge of the finished sculpture, hunting for a fuzzy stray wisp. Press Space, tap, or click ✂️ Snip Wisp the instant the blades reach the glowing wisp to trim it clean. Snip too early or late and you nick the smooth felted surface — and snipping too fast makes your hand shaky, risking a bigger gouge. Clear every wisp on a piece to move to the next — three nicks ruins the finished sculpture!
