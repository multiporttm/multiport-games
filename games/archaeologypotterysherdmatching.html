How to play: Drag the scattered pottery sherds on the left into their matching slots in the vessel outline on the right. Click a sherd (or press R while it's selected) to rotate it 90° — edges and painted decoration must line up correctly to lock a sherd in place; a wrong placement costs a life. Reconstruct the whole vessel before the timer runs out to advance — each new vessel is more shattered and larger than the last.
