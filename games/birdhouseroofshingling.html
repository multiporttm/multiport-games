Birdhouse Roof Shingling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round: 1 ♥♥♥ Time: 0
How to play: Move the cursor with the arrow keys (or on-screen pad) and press Space / Enter / PLACE to nail down a shingle — or just tap a cell. Shingles must be placed row by row, starting at the bottom, exactly as the little diagram shows. Placing out of order exposes bare wood and costs a life. Fill every row before the clock runs out to move to a bigger, steeper roof!
