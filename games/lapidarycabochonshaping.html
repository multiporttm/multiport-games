Lapidary Cabochon Shaping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Stone 1
The rough stone slides back and forth against the spinning grinding wheel. Press Space, tap the circle, or click ⚙️ Grind the instant it crosses the bright template band to shape a clean facet of the dome. Grind too early or too late and the cabochon chips. Shape every facet to finish the dome — three chips and the stone is ruined!
