Papier-Mâché Varnishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/8 Time: 32
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to sweep the varnish brush over the whole painted piece. Each spot builds up a glossy coat the longer the brush lingers — the glowing yellow band on the gauge is the safe thickness zone. Too little varnish leaves a bare spot; too much makes it pool. Get the whole piece evenly coated before the varnish sets. Pieces get larger every round — lose all 3 lives and the finish is ruined!
