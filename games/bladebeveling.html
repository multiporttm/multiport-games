Blade Beveling
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●● Heat: 0%
Mouse/Touch: drag up/down to adjust grinding angle, hold and move the blade left-right across the belt to grind. Keyboard: Up/Down Arrows to adjust angle, Left/Right Arrows to sweep the blade, hold Space to press the blade against the belt. Keep the angle inside the green zone while sweeping evenly across the whole edge on both sides. Let go or ease off if the heat meter gets too high, or the steel will overheat and you'll lose a life. Finish both sides fully and evenly to pass the level. Three lives — the angle window narrows and the belt speeds up each level.
