Test each tank's water: read the pH, Ammonia, Nitrite and Salinity gauges against the green safe zone for that species. Click/tap a treatment button (or press 1-6) to dose the matching correction, watching each gauge so you don't overshoot. When every gauge sits in its green zone, click/tap Confirm Safe (or press Space/Enter) before the clock runs out. Confirming early or letting the timer expire costs a life — three lives, and the safe zones get tighter and the clock shorter each level.
