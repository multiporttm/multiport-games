Rocket Recovery Barge Landing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Sea State 1 ♥♥♥
Hold Space/Up Arrow (or press and hold on the canvas) to fire the landing burn and slow your descent. Use Left/Right Arrows (or drag) to steer over the moving barge. Legs deploy automatically near the deck — touch down slow, upright, and centered to land safely. Rougher seas make later landings tougher!
