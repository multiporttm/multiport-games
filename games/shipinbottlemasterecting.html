Tap PULL (or press Space) in a steady rhythm to raise the mast.
How to play: A hinged mast lies flat on deck, tied to a raising-thread. Tap the PULL button (or press Space/Enter, or tap anywhere) in a steady rhythm to swing the mast upright — each tug adds swing speed, but gravity and drag slow it between tugs. Pull too fast and the mast overshoots past vertical and swings down the other side; stop pulling and it droops back down. Watch the angle gauge and click "Lock Mast Upright" while the mast sits inside the glowing vertical tolerance band to lock it in and score. Each new mast is taller and less forgiving.
