Car Detailing Wax Application
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Move the applicator with Arrow Keys or drag/tap it to spread an even coat of wax, then press Space or tap the button when enough is spread. Watch the haze gauge and press the button again right inside the highlighted window — too early or too late costs a life. Then move the towel over the panel to buff the haze off before time runs out.
