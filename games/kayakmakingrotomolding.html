🛶 Kayak Making Roto Molding
Keep the mold's spin speed inside the target band so molten plastic coats the kayak hull evenly. Too slow and it pools; too fast and it thins out.
Hold Slow or Fast (or the Down/Up arrow keys) to adjust the mold's RPM so the needle stays inside the moving green target band. Staying in-band fills the Coating gauge; drifting out of band drains Mold Integrity. Fill the coating gauge each heat cycle before Mold Integrity runs out.
