Sea Serpent Battle
Segments destroyed: 0/0 | Hull: 100 | Hi: 0
How to play: Steer your ship with Left/Right Arrow keys, A/D, or by dragging/tapping the left or right side of the canvas. Dodge the serpent's heads as they strike down — getting hit damages your hull. Each head briefly exposes a glowing weak-point segment: press Space or tap FIRE while it's exposed and aimed near your ship's column to destroy it. Destroy every segment before your hull reaches zero to win!
