Jewelry Prong Setting Stones
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Click/tap the highlighted prong (or press its number key) in the order shown, then hold Space / press-and-hold to push it down over the stone with the right pressure — a meter fills while you hold. Release in the green zone to seat it snugly; too little and it is loose, too much and the stone chips. Settings add more prongs each round.
