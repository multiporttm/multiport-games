A jack slides up and down its guide slot while its plectrum tongue sweeps toward the string. A gold band marks the exact voicing depth where the quill grips the string with just the right bite. Press Space, tap, or click 🪶 Voice the instant the tongue crosses into the gold band to set a perfectly voiced jack. Voicing too shallow or too deep snaps the delicate quill — three snapped quills and the register is ruined! Voice every jack in a register to move on to a tighter, trickier register.
