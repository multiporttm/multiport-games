How to play: A folded sheet of parchment feeds steadily under the shears from right to left, marked by a wandering fold line. Drag your mouse or finger up and down on the sheet (or use the arrow keys / arrow buttons) to keep the shears riding the line as it wanders. Drift off the line and the edge frays, draining your steadiness. Ride the line all the way to the end of the sheet to cut a clean leaf and move on to a longer leaf with a twistier line and a faster feed.
