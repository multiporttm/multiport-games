Bright copper is being misted with aging solution, and the patina gauge climbs steadily from raw copper orange toward deep verdigris green while a second needle sweeps alongside it to mark the perfect shade. Press Space, tap, or click 🧪 Stop Misting the instant the rising patina level lines up with the glowing target band to lock in a museum-quality finish.
