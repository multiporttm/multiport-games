Hold Up Arrow or the Ascend button to rise; hold Down Arrow or the Descend button to sink back down. Stop and hold steady inside each glowing decompression stop band until its timer fills, then keep ascending. Blow through a required stop without completing it, or run out of air, and it is a dive injury!
