Click/tap or press Space repeatedly to flick the pan, keep it steady to let the motion settle. Keep the toss needle inside the glowing green safe zone to raise sauce coverage evenly — toss too hard and pasta spills out of the pan, too gently and sauce pools unevenly. Reach full coverage before you run out of spills to advance to a bigger batch!
