Auto Body Spray Paint Booth
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1
How to play: Hold the spray trigger (mouse/finger down, or hold Space) and sweep the gun across the panel with the mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys at a steady, moderate speed. Sweeping at the right pace paints the cells underneath; sweep too fast and you leave thin spots, linger too long in one place and paint runs down the panel (losing a life). Cover the whole panel before the bake timer runs out to move to the next, tougher panel — lose all 3 lives and the job is ruined.
