📐 Planter Box Making Corner Jointing
Drag around the angle dial to rotate the miter board until it falls inside the glowing target zone, then let go to lock the joint.
Press and drag (mouse or touch) anywhere on the dial to swing the miter board's needle around the arc. Release while the needle sits inside the glowing target zone to lock a tight corner joint - releasing outside the zone splits the grain and drains your Joint Precision meter. Each locked joint speeds up the next and shrinks the target zone; let precision hit zero and the planter box falls apart.
