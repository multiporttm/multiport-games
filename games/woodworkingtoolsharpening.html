Mouse/touch: drag the blade side to side across the stone, keep your vertical position in the green angle band. Keyboard: ← → to stroke, ↑ ↓ to adjust angle.
Sharpen the blade on each stone, holding a steady bevel angle while stroking it back and forth through coarse, medium and fine grits, strop the edge keen, then test the cut on a scrap of wood.
