Aquascaping Water Change
Round 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold S or ↓ (or the Siphon button) to drain old tank water, hold R or ↑ (or Refill) to add fresh water. Watch the gauge: get the needle inside the glowing safe zone and press Space or Lock In to confirm the water change before the tank overflows or runs dry. Each round brings a bigger tank and a tighter band!
