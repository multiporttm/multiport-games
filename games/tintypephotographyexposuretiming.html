Tintype Photography Exposure Timing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Plate 1
The light meter needle swings onto a brightness reading, which sets exactly how long the plate needs under the lens. Press and hold Space, or press and hold ⏱️ Hold Open (mouse or finger), to pull the cap off the lens — a gold band on the gauge shows the correct exposure window. Release while the fill sits inside the gold band for a perfectly exposed plate. Release too soon and the image stays a ghostly underexposed shadow; hold too long and the plate burns out to white — three ruined plates and the light is gone! Expose every plate in a sitting to move to a trickier sitting with a narrower window.
