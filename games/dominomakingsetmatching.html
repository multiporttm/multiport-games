Click or tap two tiles to flip them. Matching pip pairs lock into the travel box; mismatches flip back and cost a flip. Use number keys 1-9,0 as a quick-jump helper is not needed — just click the tiles.
A fresh batch of hand-carved domino tiles has just come off the workbench, each one laid face down before it is boxed into its travel set. Click or tap any two tiles to flip them over and reveal their pip patterns. If the two tiles match, they lock open and slide into the finished travel box; if they do not match, they flip back face down and cost you one of your limited flips. Clear every tile from the board before your flips run out to complete the set and move on to a bigger board with more tile pairs to track. Keep an eye on which patterns you have already seen — a sharp memory means fewer wasted flips and a higher score!
