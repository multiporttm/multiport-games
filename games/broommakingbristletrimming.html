✂️ Broom Making Bristle Trimming
A target cutline wanders up and down the bristle fan. Drag or move the shears to hug the line as closely as you can — straying too far ruins the edge.
Drag with your finger or mouse (or use the Up/Down arrow keys) to move the shears up and down, tracking the wandering target cutline across the bristle fan. Staying close to the line fills your score and Edge Evenness meter; drifting far from it drains the meter — let it empty and the trim is ruined. Survive the full timer for a bonus.
