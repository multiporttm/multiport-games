Spacewalk Tether Management
Hull: 100% Slack: 0 Dist: 0/0 Hi: 0
Move with Arrow Keys / WASD (or the pad). Hold X / Pay Out to let out tether slack and Z / Reel In to take it up. Keep the tether's slack matched to your distance from the yellow anchor: too little slack snaps it dangerously taut, too much slack near orange station structures risks a snag. Reach the green hatch at the far right with your hull integrity above zero to complete the spacewalk.
