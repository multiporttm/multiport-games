Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Barrel 1/8
How to play: A glowing bead travels the barrel's target profile as the lathe spins — drag your mouse or finger up/down (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your cutting tool inside the pink tolerance tube around it. Drift too far and your cut quality meter drains; empty it and you cut too deep or leave a bump, costing a life. Stay close the whole pass to finish the barrel and move on. The bead speeds up and the tube narrows every barrel — lose all 3 lives and the blank is ruined!
