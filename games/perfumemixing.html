Add drops to match the target scent profile, then bottle your blend.
A target scent profile shows the proportion of each note (Floral, Citrus, Woody, Musk) as a bar chart with a white target marker. Click a bottle (or press number keys 1-4) to add a drop of that essence to your blend — your current mix updates live as colored bars. Try to line each bar up with its white marker, then click Bottle It Up to lock in your blend and score it. Each new blend has a trickier target ratio!
