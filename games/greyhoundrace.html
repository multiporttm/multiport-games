Alternate Z and X (or tap the two RUN paddles) rapidly to build your greyhound's speed chasing the mechanical lure. Use Up/Down Arrow (or the Lane buttons) to weave between lanes and dodge slower dogs ahead of you. Sprinting drains your stamina meter — ease off to recover it for a strong late-race burst. Race across multiple heats; your total finish time and placings are scored, with a running best-total-time record.
