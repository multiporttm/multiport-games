Carousel Animal Carving Mirror Inlaying
Filled: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Rotate the shard to match a glowing socket's angle, then click that socket to glue it in.
How to play: A carved wooden carousel animal has several diamond sockets cut at different angles, and a mirror-glass shard waits at the bottom at a random tilt. Use the ↺ ROTATE / ROTATE ↻ buttons, the Left/Right arrow keys, or swipe left/right on the canvas to turn the shard, then click or tap the socket you want to fill once the shard's angle matches it. Matching exactly seats the shard and refills your glue timer; gluing a mismatched angle cracks the glass and costs glue time. Fill every socket before the adhesive sets to finish the carousel animal's inlay and move on to trickier socket angles.
