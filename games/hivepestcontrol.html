Hive Pest Control
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Inspect each frame and press 1/2/3 or tap a button to identify the pest (Varroa Mite, Wax Moth Larvae, or Small Hive Beetle), then press 1/2/3 again to apply the matching treatment (Sugar Dusting, Screened Bottom Board, or Beetle Trap) before time runs out. You have 3 lives — the hive gets more infested each round.
