Check the plank's thickness and species, press Space or tap Start/Stop Steam to begin steaming, then press it again right when the gauge hits the correct time. Rush the hot plank to the hull and use Left/Right Arrow (or the bend buttons) to match the target curve before the cooling bar runs out. Misjudging either step costs a life — the window and the cooling clock get tighter every round.
