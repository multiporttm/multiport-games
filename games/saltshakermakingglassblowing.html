🫧 Salt Shaker Making Glass Blowing
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
Press and hold (mouse or finger) to blow air down the pipe and inflate the molten glass bulb; release to stop blowing. The glass cools and shrinks slowly on its own, and your target size glows on the gauge — release right when the bulb fills the target band to blow a perfect salt shaker body. Blow too big and the bulb bursts; let it cool too small too many times and the glass is scrapped.
