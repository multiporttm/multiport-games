Macaron Making Aging
Round: 1 Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Lives: 3
Each tray in the fridge has a maturation gauge filling toward its scheduled ready-time (shown as a target line). Click/tap a tray — or press its number key — the instant its gauge crosses the target line to pull it out perfectly ripe. Pull too early (under-aged) or too late (spoiled, gauge overflows and flashes red) and you lose a life. More trays join each round!
