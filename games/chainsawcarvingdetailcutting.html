↑/↓ or drag to steer the detail saw tip
A winding guide groove scrolls past the detail saw tip. Use ↑/↓ arrow keys, drag with your mouse, or touch and drag to steer the tip and keep it inside the narrow groove. Staying on the line builds score; clipping the groove walls burns up your saw's health. Let the health bar run out and you lose a life — three lost lives and the detail pass is ruined! Finish the groove to move to a tighter, faster pass.
