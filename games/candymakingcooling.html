Candy Making Cooling
Round 1Score: 0 | Hi: 0♥♥♥
How to play: Each tray of hot candy cools over time on the rack. Watch its progress bar: candy is still too hot early on, becomes ready in the green zone, then turns stale (orange) if left too long, and finally over-hardens and is ruined if not collected in time. Click or tap a tray in the green ready zone to move it to storage for full points — act fast, because more trays join the rack and the schedule speeds up every round!
