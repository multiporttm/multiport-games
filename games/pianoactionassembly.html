Piano Action Assembly
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Drag each action part from the tray up onto its matching rail slot — or press Left/Right Arrow to pick a tray part and Space/Enter to drop it on the highlighted slot, Tab to move the highlighted slot. Parts must go on in the correct build order (hammer, then jack, then damper, left to right down the rail). You have 3 lives; a wrong-order placement costs one. Later actions add more notes and switch from upright to grand layouts.
