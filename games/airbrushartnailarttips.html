Airbrush Art Nail Tips
Nail: 1/5 Patience: 100% Score: 0 Best: 0
Hold the mouse (or your finger) down over the highlighted nail tip and sweep left-to-right (or use Left/Right arrow keys) to build the ombre gradient from the base color into the tip color evenly across the nail. Keep your sweeps smooth and even — a steady unbroken gradient scores big; blotchy spots cost points. Finish all 5 nails before the client's patience runs out to earn a tip!
