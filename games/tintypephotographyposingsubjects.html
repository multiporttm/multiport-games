Watch the director's cues
Before the long exposure, the studio director calls out a sequence of pose adjustments — head, arm, stance, chin rest — lighting up each cue on the posing stand. Watch closely, then repeat the exact sequence using the buttons, arrow keys, or taps, before your sitter grows restless. Match it to hold the pose perfectly; three fidgety mistakes and the sitter gets up! Every sitting you hold adds one more cue to memorize.
