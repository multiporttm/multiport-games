Butter Sculpture Warping Prevention
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Day 1
The hot show-tent spotlights keep pushing the display case toward a warp, and the chiller unit keeps overshooting toward a crack. Watch the needle drift along the gauge and keep it inside the pale safe band for as long as you can. Press ← or tap 🧊 Ice to cool the case, and press → or tap 🌬️ Fan to warm it back up — random heat spikes from passing visitors and cold snaps from the chiller will shove the needle around, so react fast. Drift all the way to the hot end and the sculpture warps; drift all the way to the cold end and it cracks — three ruined sculptures and the fair exhibit closes! The longer you hold the band, the faster your score climbs, and each survived day raises the stakes.
