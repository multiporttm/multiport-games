A plain felted figure needs its fine details added. Move the needle with your mouse, finger, or arrow keys onto the glowing target ring, then press Space, tap, or click 🪡 Jab Detail to felt the detail in place. The closer to the ring's center, the more points — and stray too far outside the ring and your focus meter fills, costing a life when it overflows. Finish every detail on the figure before the focus timer runs dry!
