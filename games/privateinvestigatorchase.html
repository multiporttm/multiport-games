Private Investigator Chase
Collars: 0 | Lives: ❤❤❤ | Hi: 0
How to play: Move with Arrow Keys/WASD or drag your finger on the canvas to chase the fleeing suspect through the block. Run through the yellow shortcut alleys for a speed burst. Press Space or the Tackle button when you're right behind the suspect to bring them down before they reach the getaway car. Crates slow you unless you vault near them — just keep moving past their edge. Miss three suspects and the case goes cold!
