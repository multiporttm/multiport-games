Coin Minting Blank Punching
Sheet 1/6Score: 0Hi: 0
 
How to play: A needle sweeps around the alignment gauge. Press Space, click, or tap when the needle is inside the glowing target window to strike the press and punch a clean, aligned blank. Striking outside the window wastes metal with a misaligned blank. Punch every blank on the sheet to advance — the target window shrinks and the needle speeds up on later sheets.
