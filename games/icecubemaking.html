Hold each compartment to pour water to the yellow line!
Press and hold (or hold spacebar/arrow keys on selected cell) each compartment to pour water — release right at the yellow fill line. Overfill and it spills, ruining the cube. Once every compartment is filled correctly, wait for the freeze timer, then click/tap each frozen cube to twist and pop it out. Score a full tray for each perfectly completed round — more compartments and tighter lines each round!
