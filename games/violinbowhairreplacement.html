Violin Bow Hair Replacement
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bow 1/6
How to play: Drag up/down (or use the Up/Down Arrow keys) to line each strand of hair up with its slot and hold it there to feed it through the frog. Thread every strand in the bunch, then switch to tensioning: drag left/right (or Left/Right Arrow) and hold inside the zone — too loose and the hair sags, too tight and it snaps. Miss too many times and you lose a life. Each bow adds more strands and a narrower tension window — finish all 6 bows to complete the rehair!
