Tattoo Cover-Up Planning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Client 1/10
How to play: An old faded tattoo needs hiding. Pick the Motif the client requested, then a Coverage Size and Ink Darkness strong enough to fully conceal the old tattoo's size and darkness shown at the top (meeting or beating both bars). Use Arrow Keys to move, Enter/Space to choose, or click/tap options directly. When all three rows are picked, click Sketch the Plan to submit. Too weak a cover-up or the wrong motif costs a life — three losses and the sleeve is ruined. Old tattoos get bigger and darker every client!
