Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Floor 1 | ❤❤❤
Drive the luggage cart with Arrow Keys / WASD (or the on-screen pad). Roll over a colored bag in the lobby to pick it up, ride the elevator (yellow tile) to the right floor, and push into the matching colored door to deliver it before the guest's patience runs out. You can only carry one bag at a time.
