Lighthouse Keeper Lamp Maintenance
Level 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Climb to the lamp room and keep the beacon alive! Press 1 to trim the wick (flame), 2 to refill oil, 3 to polish the foggiest lens pane — or tap the buttons / tap a pane directly. Don't let the flame die, the oil run dry, or any pane fog over completely. Survive as long as you can — it gets harder every round!
