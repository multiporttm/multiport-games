Bow Making Stave Tillering
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Stave 1
A rough-cut bow stave is clamped to the tillering tree, and every section along its length has to bend by just the right amount or the finished bow will twist, stack weight unevenly, or snap on the first full draw. Use the Left/Right arrow keys, drag, or tap ◀ Draw / Draw ▶ to flex the stave under tension, watching the bend needle sweep across the gauge, and press Space, tap, or click 🪓 Shave the instant the needle swings into the glowing target band to shave that section to a perfect, even bend. Shave too early or too late and the section comes out uneven, costing you a life — three bad cuts and the stave is ruined! Even out every section on a stave to move on to a longer, trickier stave with a narrower target band.
