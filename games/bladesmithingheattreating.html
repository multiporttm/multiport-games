Bladesmithing Heat Treating
Round: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Score: 0 Hi: 0
How to play: Hold Heat (or Space) to bring the blade up to temperature — watch it glow from dull red toward orange and white on the gauge. Heat keeps drifting upward for a moment even after you let go, so release early! Hit Quench (or Enter) while the gauge needle sits inside the green hardening band. Quench too cold and the blade stays soft; too hot and it warps. The target band gets higher and narrower every round.
