🔮 Lava Lamp Making Wax Heating
Keep the heating coil inside the molten zone to melt the wax into a smooth, rising blob. Too cold and it hardens; too hot and it scorches.
Hold the + button (or Up Arrow) to heat the coil and the − button (or Down Arrow) to cool it. Keep the temperature needle inside the glowing molten zone — it drifts and shrinks each round — to grow the wax blob. Drifting out of the zone cracks the wax and drains your Wax Quality meter; fill the blob meter before quality runs out to advance.
