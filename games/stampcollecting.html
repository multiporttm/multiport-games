Each album page shows empty slots labeled by country and theme. Drag the matching stamp from the supply pile below into each slot before the timer runs out. On touch or without a mouse, tap a stamp then tap the slot. Filling a slot correctly scores points; a mismatched stamp costs points. Clear the page to move on — later pages add more slots and trickier decoys.
