Stage 1: Place support posts
Follow the blueprint in three stages: first click every marked spot to place support posts, evenly spaced. Once all posts are down, click the highlighted bars to lay joists across the posts. Finally click the highlighted squares to lay decking boards on top of the joists. Building out of order or skipping proper spacing weakens the structure — click Run Load Test any time to preview it: a badly built deck collapses! Get it all correct to finish the layout and move on to a larger, more complex deck.
