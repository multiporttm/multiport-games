Choose Heat or Pick (buttons, number keys 1/2, or H/D), then click/tap a cell — or move the cursor with Arrow Keys and press Space — to apply it. The cream-colored tusk cells must be melted with Heat first; digging into frost-covered tusk with the Pick cracks it! Clear every surrounding block of permafrost with the Pick to extract the whole tusk safely.
