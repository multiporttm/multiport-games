Luthier F-Hole Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ F-Hole 1/8
How to play: A glowing bead travels the f-hole's guide outline — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your blade tip tracing right along it. Slip too far off the line and your precision meter drains; empty it and the blade skids off the wood, costing a life. Trace closely the whole way around to finish the cut and move to the next f-hole. Every f-hole curls into a more intricate shape with a tighter line — lose all 3 lives and the carving session ends!
