Beach Cleanup
Score: 0 | Carrying: none | Time: 60s | Best: 0
Use Arrow Keys / WASD or touch-drag to walk the beach. Walk over a litter item to pick it up (one at a time), then walk it to the matching bin — Recyclable, Trash, or Hazardous — to score points. Beware: the tide keeps washing new litter ashore, and time is limited!
