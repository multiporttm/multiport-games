Drone Building Propeller Balancing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Prop 1 ♥♥♥
The spinning propeller is unbalanced — the wobble gauge shows which side is light. Click the lighter blade (or press 1/2/3 for each blade) to stick on a tiny weight; each click nudges the needle toward center. Press Enter or click LOCK IN once the needle sits in the green zone. Add too many weights and you'll overshoot into the red — ruining the prop! Props get trickier and the zone narrower each round.
