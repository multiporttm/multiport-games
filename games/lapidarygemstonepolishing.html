Lapidary Gemstone Polishing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Facet 1
The facet glides back and forth over the polishing lap. Press Space, tap, or click ✨ Sweep Lap the instant it crosses the bright polishing band to add shine. Sweep off the band and the facet scratches instead. Build full shine on enough facets to finish the stone — three scratched facets and the gem is ruined!
