Bow Making Glue Heat Lamination
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Stave 1
A fresh bamboo-and-hardwood lamination has been coated in hide glue and clamped onto the bow form, and now it needs steady heat to cure the bond without scorching the wood. Press and hold Space, mouse-down, or touch-and-hold the 🔥 Hold Heat Lamp button to push the temperature needle up into the glowing cure zone, then release before it slides into the red scorch zone at the top. Hold it in the cure zone long enough to fully bond that lamination, but let go too late and the glue joint scorches — three scorched joints ruins the stave! Bond every lamination in a stave to move on to a thicker, trickier bow blank.
