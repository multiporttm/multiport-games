Trophy Engraving Coating
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Trophy 1/6
Coverage: 0%
How to play: Move the spray gun across the trophy with your mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys, and hold the mouse button / finger down / Space to spray. Your sweep speed shows on the gauge above — stay inside the green safe zone to coat evenly. Sweep too slowly and lacquer pools (damaging that spot); too fast and you leave bare patches. Reach full, even coverage before time runs out to finish the trophy. Each round brings a bigger trophy and a narrower safe zone — lose all 3 lives and the finish is ruined!
