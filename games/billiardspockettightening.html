How to play: Dial each worn pocket's facing tension and screw torque into its hidden safe window by dragging the sliders, clicking the + / − buttons, or pressing Tab then Left/Right arrows to adjust the selected dial. When you think you're close, press Fire Test Shot (or Enter) to roll a ball at the pocket — too loose and it rattles back out, too tight and it bounces off the lip, just right and it drops clean. A bad test costs a life, and every new pocket shrinks the safe window and shortens your clock.
