Line the pointer up with an open target mark on the diagram (use Arrow Keys to rotate, or just tap/click near a mark) and press Space or tap to set the spoke. Hit every evenly-spaced mark to finish the base — a misaligned spoke costs a life. More spokes and tighter spacing appear each round!
