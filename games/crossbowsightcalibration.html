STEADINESS
A needle sweeps around the sight's calibration dial. Click, tap, or press Space/Enter to lock the adjustment ring the instant the needle crosses the green arc. Bad locks drain your steadiness meter — empty it and the sight goes out of true. Every 5 clean locks the needle spins faster and the arc narrows!
