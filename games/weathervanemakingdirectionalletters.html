Weathervane Making Directional Letters
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round 1
The forged iron letters N, E, S and W wait to be bolted onto the weathervane's crossbar, and the compass flashes them one at a time in the order they must be mounted. Watch the sequence light up, then repeat it using the ↑ ↓ ← → arrow keys (North/South/East/West), the N E S W buttons, or by tapping the matching point on the compass. Get it right and the sequence grows by one letter; mount the wrong letter and a bolt strips, costing you a life — three stripped bolts and the crossbar comes loose! Each round you clear adds another letter to remember.
