Watch the pestle marker swing across the gauge and press SPACE (or tap the mortar) the instant it is inside the gold crush zone. A clean hit crushes the nut into fine powder; a miss wastes it.
Gall nuts must be crushed at exactly the right moment to release their tannin for iron gall ink. A pestle marker sweeps back and forth across the mortar gauge — strike with SPACE or by tapping the Crush button (or the canvas) when the marker sits inside the gold zone. Perfect center hits yield the most powder and points; hits near the edge of the zone yield less, and missing the zone entirely wastes the nut. Crush enough nuts before your batch runs out to advance to a faster, trickier batch!
