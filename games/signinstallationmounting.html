Sign Installation Mounting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Sign 1/8
How to play: Drag the sign (or use Arrow Keys) onto the bracket marks, then click Confirm or press Enter once it is centered. Next, level it with Rotate buttons or Left/Right Arrows until the bubble sits in the green zone and confirm again. Finally tighten the numbered bolts in the exact order shown — click them or press the matching number key — before the sag meter empties. Mistakes or running out of time cost a life. Eight signs grow bigger, heavier, and less forgiving!
