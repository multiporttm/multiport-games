Step 1: click/tap the 4 side bars in the right order (top, bottom, then both sides) to nail the wooden frame together. Step 2: click and drag the wax foundation sheet into the frame and release near the center while it's stretched evenly (watch the tension gauge — not too loose, not torn taut). Step 3: press Space / tap WIRE at the right moment to crimp the wire taut without snapping it. Build enough frames before you run out of lives!
