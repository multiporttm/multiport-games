Jigsaw Puzzle Piece Counting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Box 1/10
How to play: The box needs an exact number of pieces, shown at the top. Click or tap pieces in the tray to count them in (click again to put one back), or move the yellow cursor with the Arrow Keys and press Enter/Space to toggle one. Press Space on empty focus or tap SEAL BOX once your count matches exactly. Sealing with the wrong count — or letting the timer run out — fails quality control and costs a life. Trays grow and counts climb every box!
