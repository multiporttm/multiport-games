🤿 Snorkel Making Valve Molding
Press and hold to inject hot rubber into the valve mold. Release the instant the pressure needle is inside the green band for a clean valve.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) to pump rubber pressure into the valve mold — the needle climbs steadily while you hold. Release exactly while the needle is inside the green band to pop out a clean one-way valve. Release too early and the valve is underfilled; hold too long and it blows out. Good releases raise your score and speed up the press; bad ones drain your Mold Quality meter — let it empty and the batch is scrapped.
