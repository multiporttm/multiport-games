Rotate the pliers to match the true-strike mark, then set the bend.
How to play: A bent typebar sits on a semicircular gauge, with a drifting true-strike mark showing where it needs to point. Rotate the adjustment pliers with the arrow keys, A/D, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging on the canvas, to line the pliers' needle up with the true-strike mark. Press Set Bend (or Space, or the on-screen button) to lock it in: landing inside the narrow green zone is a perfect realignment for full points, landing in the wider yellow zone is passable for partial points, and missing both zones snaps the typebar and costs a life. The true-strike mark drifts a little faster with every typebar you fix, so time your press carefully. Fix enough typebars before you run out of lives to post a high score.
