Sweep the beach to find a signal, then dig at the peak!
Move the detector with your mouse, finger drag, or Arrow keys across the sandy beach. The signal meter rises as you get closer to buried treasure. Click/tap (or press Space) to dig when the signal peaks — dig too early or too far away and you'll waste one of your limited digs. Find valuable coins and relics before time runs out. Each round hides trickier, more valuable loot!
