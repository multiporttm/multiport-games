Pet Daycare Nap Schedule
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Each dog's tiredness bar fills as the day goes on. Once it's past halfway, click/tap the dog (or press its number key) then click/tap an open nap cot (or press its letter key) to send it to rest. A dog left too long misses its rest and costs a life. Three lives — more dogs arrive and tiredness rises faster every level.
