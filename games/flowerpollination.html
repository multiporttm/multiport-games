Flower Pollination
Pairs: 0 | Hi: 0Time: 60Carrying: none
How to play: Move the bee with Arrow Keys/WASD, or tap/click anywhere in the garden to fly there. Land on a flower to collect its pollen, then fly to another flower of the same color to cross-pollinate the pair before the day ends. Pollinate as many matching pairs as you can before time runs out!
