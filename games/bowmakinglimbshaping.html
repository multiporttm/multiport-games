Bow Making Limb Shaping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Limb 1
The limb blank is locked in the shaving horse, and a faint dashed line on the wood shows the tapered profile it needs — thick near the handle, thin near the tip. Use the Left/Right arrow keys or tap ◀ Move / Move ▶ to slide the rasp along the limb, and press Down, Space, or tap 🪚 Rasp to shave one pass off the selected spot. Get every spot down within the dashed taper line before your strokes run out to finish the limb — but rasp a spot past the line and the wood snaps, costing a life! Finish with strokes to spare for a bonus, then move on to a longer limb with a tighter taper.
