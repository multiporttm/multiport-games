Watch the blade sweep back and forth over the marked blank. Press Cut (or Space/tap) the instant the blade lines up with the red cut mark to slice a clean piece. Cutting early or late wastes stock.
Fine domino tiles start life as blanks of bone veneer or ebony, each marked with the exact width the blade must land on. On the bandsaw table, a thin blade sweeps left and right across the blank while a bright cut mark shows where the slice should fall. Watch the blade's rhythm and press Cut (or Space, or tap the blade track) the instant it crosses the mark — a clean, centered cut scores big and saves stock, while an early or late cut shaves off a ragged, undersized sliver and burns through your limited supply of bone and ebony. The blade speeds up and the safe cutting window narrows as you work through tougher blanks, so stay sharp and keep your cuts precise before your stock runs out.
