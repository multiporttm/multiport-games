Ski Lift Operator Control
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Shift 1/6
How to play: Skiers board the chairlift at the loading gate. When a skier fumbles (a red warning flashes), press Down Arrow / S or tap SLOW to slow the lift so they can get seated safely. Once they are aboard, press Up Arrow / W or tap FAST to bring the lift back to full speed and keep the line moving. React too slowly to a fumble and the skier falls, costing a life. Survive all shifts with 3 lives to win!
