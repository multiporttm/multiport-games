How to play: Hold down (Space, click, or touch) to pour syrup into the highlighted mold; release to stop pouring. Keep the fill-level gauge inside the glowing safe band and then stop — overflow past the top and the syrup spills over, pour too little and an air pocket ruins the piece. Fill every cavity in the tray to clear the round. Trays get more cavities and trickier shapes as you go!
