Milk Pasteurization
Score: 0 Batch: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Hold Space (or the Heat button) to warm the milk into the glowing pasteurization band and keep it there until the hold meter fills. Let it drift too high and it scorches — lose a life. Once held, press C (or Cool) and keep it pressed to chill the batch below the safe line before time runs out. The target band narrows and heat rises faster with every batch.
