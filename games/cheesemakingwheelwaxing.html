Cheesemaking Wheel Waxing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Wheel 1/8
How to play: Hold down (mouse, finger, or Space) to lower the cheese wheel into the wax pot and build up its coat; release to lift it out and let the wax set while the wheel keeps turning. Watch the coating gauge — land your final thickness inside the glowing green target band. Dip too little and the rind is exposed; dip too long and wax drips off, ruining the wheel. The target band narrows and the wheel shape gets trickier to turn evenly every level!
