Press Arrow Up / Tighten or Arrow Down / Loosen (hold for faster adjusting) to move the stitch-balance needle. Once the needle rests inside the green safe zone, press Enter, Space, or Sew to lock it in. Sew while the needle is outside the zone and you'll ruin the seam, costing a life. Each round starts further off-balance with a tighter safe zone.
