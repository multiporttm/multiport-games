Three steps per door. Boring: press Space/click to stop the drill inside the alignment zone. Seating: press at the right moment to seat the bolt mechanism as the slider sweeps past the mark. Strike plate: hold Space/pointer down to tighten each screw and release inside the torque band — too loose or too tight fails the step!
