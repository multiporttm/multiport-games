Moat Digging
Level: 1
Laborer-turns: 0
Time: 0s
Hi Level: 1
Click a moat ring tile to dig it. Dig every ring tile 3 times to finish!
How to play: A castle sits at the center of the grid, surrounded by a ring of moat tiles. Click or tap a ring tile to send a laborer to dig it one level deeper (3 digs finish a tile). You have a limited number of laborer-turns and a countdown before the enemy army arrives — finish digging every ring tile before time or turns run out to complete the level and advance!
