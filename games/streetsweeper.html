Street Sweeper
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Street: 1 Time: 60 Hopper
Use Arrow Keys / WASD or drag to steer the sweeper truck. Drive over litter and leaves to clean them (watch the coverage trail) while avoiding parked cars and pedestrians. When your hopper is full, drive onto the yellow DUMP zone and click/tap it to empty. Clean each street thoroughly before time runs out — busier streets await!
