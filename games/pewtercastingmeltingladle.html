Pewter Melting Ladle
Round: 1 Batches: 0 Lives: 3 Best: 0
Hold the ladle over the flame — release when the ingot glows bright orange in the pour zone!
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) to lower the ladle into the furnace flame and heat the pewter ingot inside it. Watch the temperature gauge climb — release when it sits in the glowing green pour zone to melt a perfect batch. Release too early and the ingot is still solid; hold too long past the zone and it scorches, costing a life. Batches speed up every round — survive as many as you can!
