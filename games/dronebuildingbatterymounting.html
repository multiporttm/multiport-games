Drag the battery to the marked balance point, then tighten the strap into the green zone
Drag (or use Arrow Keys) to strap a battery pack onto the drone's frame at the correct balance point shown on the center-of-gravity diagram. Then use the Tighten/Loosen buttons (or +/−) to bring the strap tension into the green safe zone on the gauge — not too loose, not too tight. Click Secure Strap to lock it in. Balance points get trickier each round.
