Chess Piece Painting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/8
How to play: Each chess piece has one or more glowing gold detail zones (a crown, a face, a trim band). Drag your mouse or finger (or nudge the brush with the Arrow Keys and press Space to dab) across a zone to paint it in — paint fills faster near the zone's center. Stray outside the zone's outline while your brush is down and your coverage meter drains. Finish painting every zone on the piece before time runs out to move to the next, trickier piece. Run out of time or empty your coverage meter and you ruin the piece, costing a life. Lose all 3 lives and the whole set is spoiled!
