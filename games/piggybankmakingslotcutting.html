🪙 Piggy Bank Making Slot Cutting
Press on the start dot and drag along the marked curve to cut the coin slot. Stay on the line — wander off and the tool slips.
Press down on the glowing start dot, then drag (mouse or touch) along the curved slot line to the end. Staying close to the line advances your cut and keeps Tool Steadiness up; drifting far from the line drains steadiness and resets the cut. Finish a cut to score and move to a tighter, trickier slot next round.
