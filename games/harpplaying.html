Harp Playing

Pluck strings or sweep a glissando on the hit-line.

Notes scroll down toward the golden hit-line. For a single note, tap/click that string (or press keys 1-5) right as it crosses the line. For a wide cyan note spanning several strings, drag/swipe across those strings (a glissando sweep) at the right moment. Timing accuracy and streaks raise your score — tempo and note density increase each round.
