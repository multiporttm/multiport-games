🫙 Blender Making Jar Molding
Hold the furnace to heat the glass into the green zone, then release to blow and press it into the mold. Too cold and it won't shape; too hot and it cracks.
Press and hold (mouse/touch/Space) to heat the glass blank. Release when the Heat gauge needle sits inside the glowing green zone to mold a clean jar pass. Molding too cold or too hot cracks the glass and drains Jar Integrity. Empty integrity ends the run — mold as many clean jars as you can.
