Fossil Plaster Cast
Casts: 0Best: 0Mistakes: 0/5Pass: 1/2
 
Each numbered zone around the fossil must be wrapped in ascending order: click/tap each zone (or press its matching number key) from 1 up to the last, twice — a thin burlap pass, then a thick plaster pass. Wrapping out of order knocks the strip loose (a mistake); five mistakes ruins the jacket. Once both passes are done on every zone, wait for the plaster to set, then press Lift Jacket to finish.
