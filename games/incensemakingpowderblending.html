Traditional incense powder is a precise blend of aromatic bases. A recipe card shows how many scoops of sandalwood, clove and resin powder the batch needs. Tap the jars (or press 1, 2 and 3) to add scoops of each ingredient to the mixing bowl, watching the bowl fill and the running totals update. When your scoops exactly match the recipe, press Blend (or Enter) to mix the batch for points — the closer your timing and the fewer wasted scoops, the higher your score. Overshooting any ingredient spoils the batch and costs supply. Blend enough batches before your raw powder supply runs out to move on to a trickier recipe with more ingredients to balance!
