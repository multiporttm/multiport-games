Press and drag the buffing pad around the glass track in smooth circles. Keep a steady pace to polish the lens without letting it overheat.
Press down on the ring and drag (mouse or finger) around in a circle, staying on the glowing track, to buff the lens. A steady, medium pace polishes fastest and keeps things cool; dragging too fast overheats the glass, and stopping or drifting off the track lets it cool but makes no progress.
