Bow Making Finish Varnishing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Coat 1
The finished bow stave is laid out on the bench, drying racks ready, and it needs a smooth, even coat of varnish brushed down its full length. Drag the mouse, or swipe a finger, steadily along the bow's length to brush on varnish — hold Shift or tap ✨ Dip Brush first whenever the little droplet icon on your brush runs dry, or it will scrape the wood bare and gouge the finish. Brush too fast over a spot and it drips; leave a spot untouched and it stays a bare patch — three mistakes ruins the coat! Cover the entire stave evenly to seal that coat and move on to a longer bow needing more coats.
