Tap a plane to select it, then choose its altitude.
How to play: Click or tap a plane on the radar to select it, then click Low / Mid / High (or press 1, 2, 3) to assign it an altitude band. Two planes that get too close while sharing the same altitude band collide — game over! Guide a plane to the runway at Low altitude to land it safely and score points. Press Tab to cycle the selected plane.
