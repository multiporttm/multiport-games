Topiary Hedge Clipping

Drag across the overgrown sprigs to clip them to the guide line before time runs out. Don't cut below the line!
Click and drag (or touch and drag) across the shaggy green sprigs sticking up above the dashed guide line to clip them down. Every sprig you trim to or below the line raises your trim percentage — but drag your shears down into the clean hedge wall underneath the line and you will gouge it, costing you points. Clip the whole hedge to 100% before the timer runs out. Press R to restart.
