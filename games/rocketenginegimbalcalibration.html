Rocket Engine Gimbal Calibration
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Config 1/8
How to play: A steering command highlights a target zone somewhere on the gimbal plot. Drag your mouse or finger, or steer with the Arrow Keys (or tap/click to start), to move the engine nozzle crosshair into the zone and hold it there until the purple dwell meter fills — that locks in the calibration. The amber clock bar is ticking: run out before you lock it in and you lose a life. Every engine configuration shrinks the zone and speeds up the clock — calibrate all 8 to clear the engine, but lose all 3 lives and the calibration fails!
