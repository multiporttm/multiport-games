The shears glide back and forth across the printed lune pattern as the blade arm sweeps along the gauge. A gold band marks the exact point where the curved gore outline crosses the blade's path. Press Space, tap, or click ✂️ Cut the instant the blade crosses into the gold band to slice a clean strip. Cutting outside the line wastes the paper and tears the gore — three ruined strips and the printed sheet is scrapped! Finish every strip on a sheet to move to a tighter sheet with narrower gores.
