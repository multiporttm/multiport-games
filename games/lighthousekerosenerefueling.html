Lighthouse Kerosene Refueling
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
Refuel the lamp in three steps each round: hold Pump (or Space) to raise the fuel level and release right inside the glowing target band; then click the valves (or press their number keys) in the order flashed to bleed the line; finally press Light the Lamp (or Enter). Misjudge the level, bleed out of order, or run out of time and you lose a life — the target band narrows and the valve sequence grows every level.
