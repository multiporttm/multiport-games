RC Boat Test Float Trimming
Time: 30s | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold ←/→ (or A/D) or tap/hold the left and right buttons to shift trim weight and counter the rocking. Keep the waterline needle inside the green safe band on the gauge. Survive as long as you can — the water gets choppier over time. Tip too far and the boat capsizes!
