Butcher Primal Cutting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Lives: ❤️❤️❤️
Click/tap and hold on the knife, then drag along the glowing seam line to separate the primal cut. Keep the blade close to the line — wander too far off the seam and you waste meat. Release at the end of the cut to finish it. Three wasted cuts and it's over.
