Platypus Pond
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Pulses: 8 | Time: 40 | Lives: 🦫🦫🦫
Swim your platypus through the murky pond with Arrow Keys / WASD or by dragging. Click or tap anywhere to emit an electrolocation pulse — it briefly reveals nearby shrimp 🦐 and larvae 🐛 hidden in the mud. Swim onto revealed prey to eat it before it fades back into the murk. Find all the prey before your limited pulses or time run out — each round the pond gets murkier!
