Seal Quality
A pressure needle sweeps across the breath gauge while you hold a finished harmonica underwater and blow a steady test breath. A green zone marks perfectly even pressure. Press Space, tap the gauge, or click the big button the instant the needle is inside the green zone to confirm an airtight seal. Testing too early or too late lets a stream of bubbles escape and damages Seal Quality — let it run empty and the round ends! Pass enough tests to move to a tighter, faster round.
