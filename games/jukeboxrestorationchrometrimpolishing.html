Jukebox Restoration Chrome Trim Polishing
Panel: 1 Cloth: 100% Score: 0 Best: 0
Click and drag (or touch-drag) the polishing cloth back and forth over the tarnished chrome patches to buff them to a shine. Use arrow keys to move the cloth and Space to buff in place. Keep your strokes even — scrubbing one spot too long wears out the cloth faster. Shine every patch on the panel before your cloth wears through!
