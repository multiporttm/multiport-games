Tap RULE, click, or press Space when the guide reaches the next pricked hole.
How to play: A page of pricked guide holes waits to be ruled, left to right, one line at a time. A sliding guide bar sweeps down the margin toward the next hole. Press Space, click, or tap RULE exactly when the guide lines up with the hole to score a straight line and build your streak; a mistimed strike leaves a crooked, wavy line and drains your steadiness. Rule every line on the page before steadiness runs out to finish the page and move on to one with more lines, spaced tighter and sweeping faster.
