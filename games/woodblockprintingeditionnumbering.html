How to play: Prints pile onto the drying rack and each must be numbered as the next fraction in the edition run, one higher than the last. Use the + and − dial buttons, or the Up/Down arrow keys, to set the correct numerator shown on the current card, then click "Pencil & Sign" before the timer runs out. A correct, in-order number clears that print from the rack; a wrong number or a timeout spoils the sheet. Keep up as the rack fills and the timer gets shorter with every edition you complete.
