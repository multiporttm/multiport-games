Upholstery Furniture Stripping
Score: 0 Chair: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Use Arrow Keys + Space, or click/tap directly, on each section. Click the little staples first to pull them out, then click the section again to peel off the cover, then the padding, then the webbing — in that order. Ripping the cover off with staples still in costs a life. Strip every section bare before the timer runs out.
