Stained Glass Leading
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Window: 1 ♥♥♥
How to play: Each colored glass piece is marked with a number. Click or tap the pieces in ascending order (1, then 2, then 3...) to fit them into the lead channel strips and assemble the window. Use the arrow keys to move the highlight between pieces and Enter/Space to fit the highlighted one. Fit a piece out of order and a channel buckles, costing a life. Three mistakes and it is game over. Windows get more pieces and more complex layouts as you go.
