Bracelet Making Clasp Fitting

Press Space, click, or tap in the green zone to fit each clasp pin cleanly.

A clasp fitted even a hair off will slip loose on the first wear.
Watch the marker sweep across the clasp-fitting gauge. Press Space/Enter, click or tap right when it is inside the green zone to snap the tiny clasp pin into place without bending it. A miss weakens your fitting precision — let it run out and the clasp jams for good. Fit 5 pins cleanly to move to the next bracelet and a faster gauge!
