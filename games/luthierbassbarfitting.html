Luthier Bass Bar Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bar 1/10
How to play: The dashed line is the curved underside of the top plate, with a green pressure band running along its length. Drag any handle on the bass bar up or down (or press Left/Right to pick a handle and Up/Down to shape it) until every handle sits inside the green band — no gaps allowed anywhere along the bar. When the whole bar hugs the curve, click Glue & Clamp (or press Enter) to fit it. More handles and narrower bands arrive with every bar!
