Violin Bridge Fitting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Bridge 1
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger across the bridge feet (or move with the Arrow Keys and hold Space) to carve each foot's underside toward the violin top's curve, shown as a dashed line. Carve too far below the top curve and you ruin the foot, costing a life. When the gap meter is low, click Light Test (or press Enter) to check — too much light leaking through means it's not flush yet. Fit the bridge and move to a more sharply arched top.
