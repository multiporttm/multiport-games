Click a region of the mold, then time your hammer strike.
How to play: Phase 1 — Hammering: click a region of the gold sheet (or press 1-6), then click Strike! or press Space when the moving needle is inside the green zone for an even strike. Careless strikes outside the zone risk tearing the thin gold — too many tears ruins the mask. Phase 2 — Inlay: pick a gem color from the palette then click the matching recessed slot on the mask to set every gemstone correctly and finish the royal death mask.
