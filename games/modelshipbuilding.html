    Click the correct part, then click its glowing attachment point.
    How to play: The build order panel shows every part your ship needs, top to bottom. Click the part in the tray that matches the current highlighted step, then click the glowing attachment point on the ship. Get it right and the part attaches; get it wrong (wrong part or wrong point) and that step resets. Finish a ship to move to the next one — each new ship has more parts and steps to complete.
