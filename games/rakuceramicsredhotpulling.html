How to play: A pot in the raku kiln cools from white-hot toward black as the heat gauge slides left. A highlighted orange-red window marks the perfect pulling temperature. Press Space, Enter, click the canvas, tap the screen, or hit the PULL button the instant the gauge marker is inside that window to pull a perfect, thermal-shocked pot with the tongs. Pulling too early or too late costs composure. Land the target number of good pulls before composure runs out to clear the batch and face a faster kiln next round.
