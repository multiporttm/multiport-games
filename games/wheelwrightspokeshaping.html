Wheelwright Spoke Shaping
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Spoke 1
A rough-sawn oak blank is clamped in the shaving horse, waiting to become a wagon spoke. Press Space, tap, or click 🪚 Shave to start drawing the knife along the grain, and hold (press and hold, or tap and hold) to keep shaving the taper thinner — release right as the gauge reaches the true taper line, no sooner, no later. Shave too little and the spoke stays clunky and oversize; shave too much and the oak splinters along the grain — three ruined blanks and the session is wasted! Finish every spoke in a set to move to a trickier set with a narrower taper window.
