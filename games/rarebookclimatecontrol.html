Rare Book Climate Control
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Season 1/6
How to play: Each shelf shows a temperature gauge and a humidity gauge, both drifting with the seasons. Click/tap a shelf's heater, cooler, humidifier, or dehumidifier button (or select a shelf with 1-4 and a control with the Arrow Keys, confirm with Enter/Space) to nudge that reading back toward the safe green band. Any shelf left outside its safe band too long damages its books and costs a life. Later seasons swing faster and the safe bands narrow — lose all 3 lives and the collection is damaged beyond repair!
