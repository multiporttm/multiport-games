In hand: S
The rim blank is clamped flat with empty sockets waiting around its circle, each cut a little shorter or longer than its neighbor. A curved felloe blank is in your hand — its size is shown below the wheel. Use the ◀ ▶ arrow keys (or buttons, or tap a socket on the wheel) to move the highlighted socket, then press Space, Enter, or tap 🔧 Fit to drive the felloe home. Fit it into a socket of the matching size and it seats clean; force it into the wrong socket and the wood splits — three split blanks and the shop closes for the day! Ring every socket to rack up a finished wheel and move on to a wheel with more, trickier sockets.
