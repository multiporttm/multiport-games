Use arrow keys or click/tap to select a patch, then mark it if it looks thin and gappy.
How to play: A finished thatch roof is shown as a grid of straw bundles, and a few of them are laid too thin, letting light show through where rain will later leak in. Use the arrow keys to move the selection box (or click/tap a patch directly) and press Space, Enter, or the Mark Patch button to flag the selected patch as weak. Correctly flagging a weak patch scores points and slows the advancing storm meter; flagging a sound patch or leaving a weak one unflagged lets the storm meter creep closer. Mark every weak patch on the roof before the storm meter fills to pass inspection and move on to a bigger roof with more patches to check.
