Lost-Wax Casting Investment Pouring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Flask 1/8
How to play: Hold down the mouse, finger, or Space to pour liquid investment plaster into the flask around the wax model. Keep the rising level inside the green safe band on the side gauge — pour too fast and bubbles get trapped or the flask overflows; stop pouring too long and the plaster starts to set before the flask is full. Fill each flask to the top while staying in the safe band to move on. Flasks get taller and the safe band narrows every round — lose all 3 lives and the casting is scrapped!
