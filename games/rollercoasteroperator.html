Each rider needs a height check: click Board to seat tall-enough riders (locks their restraint) or Reject short ones. When your car has riders, click Dispatch (or press Space) while the gap gauge is in the green zone — dispatching in the red zone causes a collision! Keys: R reject, B board, Space dispatch. Don't let the queue overflow.
