How to play: Press and drag (or click/tap) along the dashed cut line to slice a pad off the parent cactus — stray too close to the spines (red dots) and you'll get pricked. Cut pads go into the callusing tray; wait for the bar to fill, then click the card and choose the correct soil mix to pot it and grow a new plant. Get pricked 3 times and the propagation session ends.
