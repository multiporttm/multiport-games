How to play: Each flight shows wind speed, cloud base height, and visibility. Decide whether conditions are safe by clicking/tapping GO or NO-GO (or press G for GO, N for NO-GO) before the launch window countdown bar runs out. Flight is unsafe (correct call is NO-GO) if wind is 15 kt or higher, cloud base is under 1000 ft, or visibility is under 3 mi — otherwise it's a GO. Ten flights bring closer, trickier marginal readings and a shorter window. Get it wrong, or run out of time, and you lose a life!
