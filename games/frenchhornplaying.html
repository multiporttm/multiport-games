Notes scroll toward the golden hit-line, each marked OPEN (bright tone) or STOPPED (muted tone). For an OPEN note, keep your hand out of the bell — do nothing. For a STOPPED note, press and hold the ✋ HAND-STOP button (or hold Space) right as it crosses the line to mute the tone. You're scored on timing and whether you used the right technique. Tempo and complexity increase each round.
