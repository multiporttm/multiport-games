How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your loaded brush tracking the glowing guide point as it travels the lettering stroke. Stay close to the line and move at a steady, even pace — rushing ahead or dragging behind thins or blots the stroke width. Drift too far or paint too unevenly and your ink dries up, ruining the stroke and costing a life. Finish the stroke to move to the next sign — six increasingly ornate lettering styles await. Lose all 3 lives and the sign is ruined!
