Taxidermy Fur Combing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Mount 1/5
How to play: Each matted patch on the mount shows an arrow — drag your mouse or finger (or nudge with the Arrow Keys) across the patch moving in that direction to comb the mat smooth. Comb the wrong way and the mat gets worse. Clear every patch before the timer runs out to finish the mount. Mounts grow bigger, with more patches, longer fur, and less time — lose all 3 lives and the coat is ruined!
