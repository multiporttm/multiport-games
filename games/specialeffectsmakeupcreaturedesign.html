Level 1 Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤
How to play: The concept strip shows which texture belongs on each zone of the creature. Click a zone on the face/neck (or press Tab to cycle zones) to select it, then click a palette swatch (or its number key) to apply that texture. When every zone matches the concept, press Submit Design (or Enter). Wrong submissions cost a life — later levels add more zones and less time.
