Click the problem branches to prune them
How to play: Click (or tap) each branch that's dead (brown, leafless), crossing another branch, or part of overcrowded growth (clustered thin shoots) to prune it away with your shears. Leave healthy green productive branches alone — cutting a healthy branch costs you a mistake. Prune every problem branch correctly to maximize next season's fruit yield and move to the next tree. Three mistakes ends your pruning session.
