How to play: A steamed bamboo strip rests on the former while a faint target curve shows the hoop shape you need to bend at each station along its length. Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) then drag up and down, or use the Up/Down arrow keys, to pull the strip toward the glowing target, matching the curve station by station. Bending close to the target fills your precision streak and tops up your heat budget; bending too far snaps the cane, and drifting far from the target cools the strip fast. Finish every station before the heat runs out to lock in the hoop and move on to a trickier frame shape.
