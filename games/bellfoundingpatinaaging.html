Bell Founding Patina Aging
Panel: 1 Time: 0s Score: 0 Best: 0
Move the brush with the arrow keys (or tap a patch) to select a patch of bare bronze — the number on it shows how many patina coats it still needs. Press Enter/Space to swing the wetness needle, then press Enter/Space again when it is in the green zone to lay down a clean coat. Too few coats leaves it dull; brushing a finished patch again etches and ruins it. Bring every patch to exactly the right coat count before the solution dries!
