You are the head judge at the county fair butter sculpture tent. Each entry rolls in under the show lights with a short list of traits — read them fast. Award 🔵 Blue only to an entry that is both sharply detailed and perfectly symmetrical with no melt damage. Award 🔴 Red to a solid entry with just one small flaw. Award ⚪ White to a rough but finished entry with two or more flaws. Award 🚫 DQ (disqualify) to any entry that is severely melted or structurally collapsed. Use the number keys 1-4, tap, or click the matching button before the clock runs out. A wrong call or a timeout costs a life — three bad calls and the fair is over!
