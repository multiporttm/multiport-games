Solar Panel Wiring
Score: 0 | Hi: 0Lives: ❤❤❤Level: 1Time: 0
Click (or select with Arrow keys + Space) the gap between two neighboring panels to toggle a series wire joining them into one string; separate strings combine in parallel. Make every string the same length so their voltages match, then hit Submit (or Enter) when your combined voltage and current reach the target shown. Mismatched string lengths cause a short circuit!
