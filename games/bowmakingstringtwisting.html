❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Strand 1
Two bundles of plant fiber are laid out on the twisting frame, and a strong bowstring needs each loop twisted in the exact over-under order shown on the frame — get the pattern wrong and the ply comes loose. Watch the strands demonstrate their twist pattern, then repeat it yourself using the Left/Right arrow keys or by tapping ⟲ Under / Over ⟳ in the same order. Match the whole pattern correctly to lay up that length of string; get a step wrong and a loop slips loose, costing a life — lose all three and the string is ruined! Each completed length adds a longer, trickier twist pattern to remember.
