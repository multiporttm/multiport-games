Watch the sensor trace. When it spikes solid red — a real critical failure — press Space or hit the ABORT button immediately. Flickering yellow blips are false alarms: triggering abort on one costs a life, and so does missing a real red spike in time. Later tests mix in faster, trickier look-alike alarms.
