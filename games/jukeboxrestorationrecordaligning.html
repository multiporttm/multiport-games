Jukebox Restoration Record Aligning
Record: 1 Stage: center Score: 0 Best: 0
Drag the vinyl record (or use arrow keys) so its spindle hole lines up with the center pin, then release to drop it. Once seated, line up the swinging tonearm with the lead-in groove and press Space (or tap the tonearm) to drop the needle at the right moment. A clean center and a clean drop both score big — miss too often and the stack jams!
