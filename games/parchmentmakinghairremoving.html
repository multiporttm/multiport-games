Use Up/Down or drag to line up with a tuft, then hold SCRAPE or Space to shave it off.
How to play: Loose tufts of hair drift from right to left toward the scraping line on the limed hide. Move the scraper up and down with the Up/Down arrow keys, a mouse drag, or a touch drag so it lines up with the tuft's height. Hold Space, click, or the SCRAPE button while a tuft is at the line to shave it off cleanly; keep holding past the tuft or scrape with nothing there and you gouge a hole, draining your hide's health. Clear enough tufts before health runs out to finish the pass and move on to a faster, thicker hide.
