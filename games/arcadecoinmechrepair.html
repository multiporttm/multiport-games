How to play: First tune the mech: press 1/2 or click a row to select Gate Width or Sensor Sensitivity, drag its slider (or use Up/Down Arrow) until the readout sits in the highlighted spec zone, then press Enter or LOCK IN — locking outside spec costs a life. Once locked, coins drop through the mech: press Left Arrow/REJECT for slugs and fakes, Right Arrow/ACCEPT for real coins, before each one falls past — guess wrong or too slow and you lose a life. Five machines await, each with trickier counterfeits and a tighter spec window.
