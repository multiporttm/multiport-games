Halloween Candy Run
Candy: 0
Hi: 0

Use Arrow Keys / WASD to move (up/down to dodge, the street scrolls automatically), or drag up/down on touch. Run past houses and grab the candy 🍬 that appears on the sidewalk, but dodge jack-o'-lanterns 🎃 and spooky decorations 👻 — hitting one costs a life. Collect as much candy as you can before the curfew timer hits zero or you run out of lives!
