Aquarium Exhibit Design
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Pick one Species, one Substrate, one Décor and one Plant row by clicking/tapping a card in each row, or use Left/Right Arrows to move and Up/Down to switch rows, then press/tap Open Exhibit (or Space/Enter). All four choices must share the same water-parameter zone, and the species must not prey on or outcompete anything chosen. Mistakes cost a life — three lives, and the options get more numerous each level.
