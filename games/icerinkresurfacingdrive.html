Ice Rink Resurfacing Drive
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Period 1
How to play: Steer the Zamboni with the Arrow Keys / WASD (or drag with mouse/finger) in overlapping passes across the rink, laying fresh ice as you go. Cover the whole sheet before the clock hits zero to finish the period. Clipping a wall or driving over the same fresh ice too much costs a life. Each period the rink grows and the clock gets tighter — lose all 3 lives and resurfacing is a bust!
