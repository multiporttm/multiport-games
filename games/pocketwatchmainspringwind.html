Pocket Watch Mainspring Wind
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Watch 1/6
How to play: Press and hold Space, the Hold to Wind button, or tap/hold the canvas to turn the winding key. The tension needle has momentum — it keeps climbing a little after you let go, so release early! When the needle sits inside the glowing safe zone, press Enter or click Lock In to set the mainspring. Lock outside the zone and the spring is under- or over-tensioned — you lose a life. Let the needle hit the top and it snaps — an instant life lost. Calibrate all 6 pocket watches, each with a narrower safe zone and a touchier key, before you run out of lives.
