Tiffany Lamp Making Soldering Joints
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Joint 1
A soldering iron hovers over a copper-foiled joint, and the heat meter rises while you hold it down and cools when you let go. Press and hold Space, press and hold 🔥 Hold Iron, or press and hold on the canvas to melt the solder wire into a smooth, domed bead right inside the glowing heat zone, then release. Release too soon and the joint stays cold and weak; hold too long past the zone and you scorch the glass — either mistake costs a life. Three ruined joints and the lamp frame falls apart! Finish every joint on a seam to move on to a tighter, hotter-running heat zone.
