Taxi Driver Navigation
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
How to play: Steer your taxi with Left/Right Arrow (or A/D), or the on-screen ◀ ▶ buttons, to change lanes. Dodge other traffic and closed-off lanes (marked with cones) while racing toward each passenger's destination marker. Reach it to score and pick up a new fare; crash and you lose a life. Traffic thickens and speed increases every level.
