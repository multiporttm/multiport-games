Jigsaw Puzzle Box Assembly
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Box 1/10
How to play: Watch each flap on the flattened box blank glow in the order it needs to fold — then repeat that exact order by clicking/tapping the flaps, or by pressing the matching number keys (1-9, 0 for 10). Press Space or click/tap to start and to restart. Fold a flap out of turn and it springs back open, costing a life. Boxes gain more flaps and change shape — classic, hexagonal, then triangular — as you progress. Lose all 3 lives and assembly stops!
