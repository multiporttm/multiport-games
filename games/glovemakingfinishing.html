Glove Making Finishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Glove 1/8
How to play: A finished leather glove lies on the bench, divided into small patches — sweep your cloth across it by dragging with mouse or finger, or glide it with the Arrow Keys, to condition every patch. Each patch has its own gauge: sweeping over it fills the gauge toward the glowing green safe zone, and the patch turns a healthy warm gold once it rests inside that band. Keep sweeping past the safe zone, though, and the gauge overfills — the patch darkens into an over-conditioned burgundy stain and a life is lost. Press Space to finish once every patch glows gold inside its safe zone — missed pale patches or any darkened stain will fail the glove. Clear all 3 lives' worth of mistakes to move on — every new glove is larger with more patches to track, so a controlled, even hand is the mark of a true finisher!
