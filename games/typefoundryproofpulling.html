Typefoundry Proof Pulling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Line 1/8
How to play: Stage 1 — Inking: drag the brayer (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys) back and forth across the composed type to roll ink over every letter, keeping the gauge's needle inside the green safe band — too little ink and spots stay dry, too much and it floods. Stage 2 — Pressing: once evenly inked, press and hold (or hold Space) to lower the proof sheet, keeping pressure steady and even across the whole line to pull a clean proof. Miss spots or smudge it and you lose a life. Lines get longer and the margin for error shrinks each round.
