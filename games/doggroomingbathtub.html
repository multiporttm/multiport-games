Dog Grooming Bathtub
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Drag over the dog's coat to scrub shampoo (circular motions work best) until every patch is soapy, then press R or tap the Rinse button to switch to the sprayer and drag again to rinse it all clean. Finish each dog before its patience meter empties. Three lives — more dogs join the queue and patience runs out faster every level.
