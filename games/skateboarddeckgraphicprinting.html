How to play: Drag the heat-transfer graphic (mouse, finger, or Arrow Keys) until it lines up with the faint dashed template outline on the deck. Once it's close, hold Space / click-and-hold / press-and-hold to run the heat press — keep the pressure needle in the green safe band until the transfer bar fills. Press while misaligned too long and the graphic scorches, costing a life. Graphics get more intricate and alignment gets tighter every round — complete all 8 to finish the production run!
