Harpsichord Building Keyboard Balancing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Key 1
A lead balance weight slides back and forth along the underside of each key as the gauge sweeps across the scale below. A gold band marks the exact pivot point where the key sits level and responds with even touch-weight. Press Space, tap, or click ⚖️ Set Weight the instant the slider crosses into the gold band to lock the weight in place. Setting it outside the band throws the key off balance and wears out a felt bushing — lose three and the keyboard frame has to be scrapped! Balance every key in a keyboard to move on to a keyboard with a narrower tolerance.
