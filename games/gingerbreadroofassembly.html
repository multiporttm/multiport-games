Gingerbread Roof Assembly
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1/8
How to play: A diagram in the corner shows the target angle for this roof panel. Use Left/Right Arrow Keys (or drag left/right on the panel) to lean it to match the target angle, then hold it there to let the icing glue set. A balance gauge tips toward red the longer you're off-angle, and if it maxes out the panel slides off and you lose a life. Get the gauge filling green by matching the angle and hold steady until the panel locks in place — then move on to the next, trickier roof shape. Lose all 3 lives and the roof assembly collapses!
