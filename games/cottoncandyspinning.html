Cotton Candy Spinning
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Hold Left/Right Arrow, or drag your finger/mouse in a circle around the spinning web, to sweep the cone through the sugar at a smooth, steady speed. Too slow and no fluff sticks; too fast and the web tears, costing a life. Fill the fluff meter before the timer runs out to serve each customer. Three lives — customers get fussier and the web tears more easily with every level.
