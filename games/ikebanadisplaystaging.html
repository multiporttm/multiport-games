How to play: A ghost preview flashes the target composition at the start of each round. Tap a tray piece to select it, then tap an alcove cell to place it — asymmetric balance means leaving most cells empty ("ma"). Click Stage It when you are done. Matching most placements scores big; miss too many and the display is rejected. Number keys 1-9 also select cells, letter keys select tray pieces.
