Ceramic Glaze Dipping
Score: 0 Shelf: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Press Space / Click / Tap
Press Space, click, or tap once to plunge the piece into the glaze bucket, and press again to lift it out. Release while the rising marker is inside the green zone for even coverage — too early leaves bare spots, too late causes drips and pooling. Clear a kiln shelf of pieces each round; the zone narrows and the dip speeds up every round. 3 ruined pieces and it's over.
