Tarot Illustration Varnishing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Card 1
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to sweep the varnish brush over the illustration. Each cell has a thickness gauge: too little and it stays dull and unfinished, too much and it pools into a sticky blotch — keep passing over every cell until its gauge sits in the glowing green target band, then move on before it pools. Finish the whole card within the time shown to coat it; let too many cells pool or run out the clock and you lose a life. The illustration grows larger and the safe band narrower each round.
