🔮 Kaleidoscope Making Mirror Tiling
Drag around the barrel to rotate each triangular mirror shard, then tap Lock the instant it sits inside the glowing slot.
Drag your finger or mouse anywhere on the barrel to rotate the mirror shard around its center. Watch for the glowing green slot on the rim — when the shard's point sits inside it, tap "Lock Tile" (or press Space/Enter) to fit it cleanly. A tile drifts on its own while you're not dragging, so keep correcting it. Locking outside the slot cracks a shard and drains Tile Integrity; let it run out and the kaleidoscope is ruined.
