Plumbing Snake
Drain: 1 Score: 0 Time: 45 Hi: 0
Hold ADVANCE (or Up Arrow) to feed the drain snake forward through the pipe. At each junction, steer Left/Right (or Arrow keys) toward the open path — pick wrong and the snake jams, costing time. When the head reaches the clog, hold BREAK (or Down Arrow) steadily to grind through it — jerking the snake while breaking makes it kink and get stuck! Clear each drain before time runs out to move on to a longer, more winding pipe.
