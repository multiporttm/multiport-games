🐴 Saddle Making Leather Tooling
Drag the stamping swivel along the glowing guide line to tool a swirling pattern into the saddle leather. Stay on the line or the hide gets scuffed.
Press and drag (mouse or finger) the stamping tool along the glowing swirl pattern from the start dot to the end dot. Staying inside the guide band fills the pattern and scores points; drifting off the line scuffs the hide and drains Hide Condition. Finish a pattern to advance rounds — each round adds a tighter, wigglier swirl.
