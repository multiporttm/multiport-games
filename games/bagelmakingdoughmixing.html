Bagel Making: Dough Mixing
Batch: 1 | Hi: 0
Hold Space, Click, or Tap to knead the dough. Keep the elasticity needle inside the glowing safe zone to build up the batch's score. Knead too hard too long and you will overmix (toughen it); let go too long and it stays crumbly (undermixed). Fill the batch meter before you run out of mixes!
