Dice Making Pip Drilling
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Face 1
A die blank is clamped under the drill press, and the bit plunges up and down over each pip mark while a depth needle sweeps across the gauge, hunting for the exact depth that leaves every pip looking identical and never breaking through the far wall. Press Space, tap, or click 🔩 Drill the instant the needle sweeps into the glowing target band to punch that pip hole to perfect depth. Drill too shallow and the pip looks faint, too deep and you blow out the opposite face — either mistake costs a life, and three ruined holes scraps the whole die! Finish every pip on a face to flip to the next face, with a narrower target band each time.
