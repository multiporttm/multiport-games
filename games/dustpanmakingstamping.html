🧹 Dustpan Making Stamping
Hold to charge the stamping press, then let go the instant the pressure needle sits in the green zone to punch a clean dustpan blank from the sheet.
Press and hold (click, tap, or hold Space/Enter) to charge the stamping press — the pressure needle climbs while you hold it. Release exactly when the needle is inside the green zone for a clean stamp. Release too early, too late, or let the press over-charge and the blank is ruined, draining your Press Integrity. Each clean stamp speeds up the press and shrinks the target zone.
