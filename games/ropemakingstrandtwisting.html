Keep the needle inside the moving band to twist a tight, even lay.
How to play: Three loose fiber strands run into a hand-cranked twisting frame at the left of the screen, and a tension gauge in the middle shows your needle against a drifting target band. Move the needle up and down with the up and down arrow keys, W and S, the on-screen buttons, or by dragging with the mouse or your finger, trying to keep it inside the band as it wanders. While the needle sits inside the band, the strands wind together into a tight, even lay shown spooling onto the reel at the right in a warm golden color; stray outside the band and the lay turns loose and kinked, shown duller and fraying, which drags your quality down for as long as you are off target. Your score each round is built from how much rope you wound and how good your average tension quality was, so smooth corrections beat frantic jabs. Each new reel spins faster and the band moves more erratically, so see how many reels of well-laid rope you can wind before your tension control runs out.
