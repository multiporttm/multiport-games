Basket Weaving Tightening Rows
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ●●● Round 1 Row 1/6
Sweep over each loose woven row to tighten it. Hold Space, mouse button, or your finger to build tension, then release right as the needle sits inside the green safe zone on the gauge. Release too early and the row stays loose; too late and the basket warps. Work through more rows, with a narrower safe zone, each round!
