Rainforest Misting
Time: 0sScore: 0Hi: 0
Press Start to open the exhibit
Four exhibit zones each need their humidity kept in the green ideal range for their orchids and ferns. Click/tap a zone or press 1-4 to fire its misting nozzle for a burst of moisture (nozzles need a moment to recharge). Heat waves and visitor foot traffic dry zones out faster — let any zone stay too dry or too soggy for too long and the exhibit's health meter drops. Survive as long as you can!
