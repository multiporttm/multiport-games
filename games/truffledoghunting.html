Truffle Dog Hunting
Score: 0 Season: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Move your dog through the forest with Arrow Keys / WASD or tap an adjacent tile. When the dog sniffs or scratches, a truffle is buried nearby — stand on the glowing tile and press Space (or tap the dog's own tile) to dig. Watch out for rival hunters racing to the same spot, and don't dig empty ground — you only have 3 lives before the season ends!
