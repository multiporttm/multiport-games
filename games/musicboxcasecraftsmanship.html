Glue the glowing piece next, in the right build order
Left/Right or A/D select a piece · Space/Tap glue it in place
Fit and glue a music box's wooden case panels and inlay decoration together in the correct build order before the glue sets. Use Left/Right or A/D to pick a piece and Space or Tap to glue it into place. Gluing a piece out of order weakens the joint and costs a life. Case designs grow more ornate, with more panels and inlay pieces, as you level up.
