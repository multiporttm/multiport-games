Kilt Making Buckle Attaching
Round: 1 Attached: 0 Score: 0 Best: 0
Lives: 3
Buckles slide down three lanes toward matching colored waist tabs at the bottom. Click or tap a lane, or use the 1, 2, 3 keys (or Left/Right to pick a lane and Space to grab), to snap the clamp shut the instant a buckle reaches the tab in its own lane. Grab the right-colored buckle in time to attach it; miss or grab the wrong lane and you lose a life. Attach enough buckles to clear the round before your three lives run out.
