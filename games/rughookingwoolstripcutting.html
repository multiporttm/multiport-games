Rug Hooking Wool Strip Cutting
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Batch 1
A strip of hand-dyed wool feeds through the cutter as the blade carriage slides back and forth across the width gauge. A highlighted band marks the exact strip width this batch calls for. Press Space, tap, or click ✂️ Squeeze Shears the instant the blade is inside the band to cut a clean, even strip. Cut outside the band and you waste good wool — three bad strips ruins the whole batch! Cut every strip in a batch to move on to a trickier width.
