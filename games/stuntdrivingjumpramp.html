Stunt Driving Jump Ramp
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Jump 1/8
How to play: A speed needle sweeps left and right along the gauge. Press Space, click, or tap the instant the needle sits inside the glowing landing zone to gun the engine off the ramp. Nail the speed and the stunt car arcs over the gap to land safely on the far ramp. Launch too slow and you fall short; too fast and you overshoot. Every jump widens the gap and narrows the safe landing zone — lose all 3 lives and the stunt is over!
