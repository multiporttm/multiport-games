Pick a paint swatch from the palette (click, tap, or press number keys), then click a faded patch on the cabinet art to repaint it. Match the original color closely to restore the panel — bad matches cost a life. Use Tab to move between patches and Enter to apply your selected color. Panels get bigger and colors trickier with every level!
