Weaving Finishing Hemming
Lives: 3 Level: 1 Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Press Space, click, or tap right as each stitch mark crosses the needle line
Stitch marks drift toward the needle line along the raw fabric edge. Press Space, click, or tap exactly when a mark reaches the line to fold and hem it. A late or missed stitch lets the edge fray — fill the fray meter and a thread gives way, costing a life. Hem the whole edge to move on to longer edges and trickier, faster-fraying fabric weights!
