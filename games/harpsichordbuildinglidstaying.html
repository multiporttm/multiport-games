Harpsichord Building Lid Staying
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Lid 1
The lid rises and falls on its hinge as the angle gauge sweeps across the scale below. A gold band marks the exact open angle where the carved stay notch lines up with the pin and holds the lid safely without strain. Press Space, tap, or click 📐 Lock Stay the instant the gauge crosses into the gold band to drop the stay into its notch. Locking it outside the band leaves the lid propped crooked and splits the stay — three split stays and the lid has to come off for repair! Lock every stay on a lid to move on to a lid with a narrower safe angle.
