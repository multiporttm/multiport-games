Hold the crank to reel in ribbon, but keep the tension needle in the green zone.
How to play: A spool of old, loose ribbon needs winding onto the empty takeup spool at a steady tension. Hold the Hold to Crank button (or hold Space, or the Up arrow / W), to reel the ribbon in — the tension needle on the gauge rises the harder and longer you crank. Keep the needle inside the green safe zone: cranking too hard for too long pushes it into the red zone and the ribbon stretches and frays, while letting go for too long lets the needle fall and the ribbon goes slack and tangles. Use Ease Off (Down arrow / S) to relax the crank quickly, or Crank Hard (the on-screen button, or hold Shift) for a burst of speed when you are behind. Watch for knots sliding through the feed — they flash a warning just before they reach the spool, so ease off briefly as each one passes to avoid a jam. Respool the entire ribbon before it snaps from damage to post a high score.
