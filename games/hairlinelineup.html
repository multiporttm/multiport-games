Keep the clippers tracking the dashed hairline guide as it scrolls by. Use Arrow Up/Down, or drag/tap vertically on the canvas, to follow the edge in short careful strokes. Drifting off the line fills the slip meter — fill it and you lose a life. Finish each full hairline to step back and check it; the edge gets sharper and the tolerance narrower every client.
