🧵 Ironing Board Making Fabric Stretching
Two clamps pull the cover fabric taut across the board. Tap the LEFT or RIGHT lane the instant its marker sits in the green zone to lock that clamp. Keep both sides even or the fabric tears.
Two tension gauges move independently, one for each clamp stretching the fabric cover. Tap (or click) the left half of the frame when the LEFT marker is in its green zone, and the right half when the RIGHT marker is in its zone, or press A/D (left/right arrow keys also work) at the right moment. Landing both evenly scores big; missing a zone or leaving a clamp loose too long damages Fabric Integrity — let it empty and the cover tears.
