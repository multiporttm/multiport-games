Find the matching piece
A half-built lamp shade spins slowly on the soldering form, with one empty cell glowing white-hot where the next piece of copper-foiled glass needs to go. Study the shape and color shown above the empty cell, then click, tap, or press 1-5 to pick the matching glass piece from the tray before the solder underneath cools and hardens. Pick the wrong piece and it will not fit the cell, cracking off and costing you a life — three cracked pieces and the shade collapses! Fill every cell to finish the shade and move on to a bigger one with more lookalike pieces in the tray.
