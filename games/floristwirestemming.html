How to play: Step 1 — press Space / Click / Tap when the sliding wire lines up with the stem's threading mark. Step 2 — hold Space / mouse-down / touch to wrap tape, keeping the rising tension needle inside the green zone. Nail both steps to finish the stem. Flower types get trickier (thinner wires, narrower zones) as you go. 3 lives — good luck!
