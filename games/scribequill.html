Scribe Quill
Letter: 1/8   Accuracy: 100%   Ink:
Press and hold (mouse or touch) on the glowing dot at the start of the stroke, then drag your quill along the guide path to the end to trace the letter. Staying close to the path preserves ink and accuracy; drifting away wastes ink fast. Trace all letters on the page before the inkwell runs dry!
