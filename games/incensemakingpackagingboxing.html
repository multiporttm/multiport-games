Finished incense bundles roll off the conveyor belt in different scent colors and must be sorted into the matching box before they hit the floor. Slide the sorting tray left and right with the arrow keys, A/D, by dragging, or by tapping either side of the canvas on touch devices, to catch each bundle as it falls. Catching the right-colored bundle delivers it to its box for points and speeds up your combo; missing a bundle or catching the wrong color counts as a miss. Five misses ends the shift — catch and sort as many bundles as you can first!
