Tarot Illustration Inking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Design 1/10
How to play: A faint pencil sketch glows with the guide line still to ink. Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to sweep your brush pen along the moving glow point and lay down permanent ink. The sketch fades a little every moment the brush strays off the line — let the fade meter empty and the sketch is lost, costing a life. Finish inking before it fades to move to the next card. Designs grow more intricate and the fade quickens each round — lose all 3 lives and the sketchbook is ruined!
