🎸 Fret Polishing
Drag the polishing cloth along each glowing fret wire from end to end. Stay inside the narrow track - sliding off scratches the neck finish.
Drag the polishing cloth (mouse or touch) along the highlighted fret from one end to the other, staying inside the narrow glowing track. Reach the far end to buff that fret to a mirror shine and move to the next. Sliding off the track scratches the neck and drains Neck Finish - empty it and the guitar neck is ruined.
