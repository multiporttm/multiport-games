Smooth every wrinkle before you run out of moves!
Move the smoother with Arrow Keys / WASD and press Space to press out a wrinkle, or simply tap/click any bumpy patch directly. Press out every wrinkle on the basting table before your moves run out — waste moves on already-smooth cells and you will run dry before the quilt is done. Quilts grow larger and bumpier each round!
