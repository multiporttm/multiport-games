Domino Making Weight Balancing
Round: 1 Basket Left: 14 Score: 0 Best: 0
Click or tap a basket tile then click the left or right pan to load it. Press Z to load the next tile left and X to load it right. Lock the scale (or press Enter) once both pans read the target weight.
Every finished domino tile has to be checked on the brass balance scale before it ships. A basket of tiles with different weights sits beside the scale, and a target weight is shown above the pans. Click a basket tile and then click the left or right pan (or use Z and X as quick keys for the next tile) to load tiles onto each side. Both pans need to reach the exact target weight, and using fewer tiles earns a bigger bonus. Once both sides read the target, press Lock Scale (or Enter) to seal the check. Overloading a pan past the target ruins that tile's balance test and wastes basket stock, so plan your loads carefully before your basket runs dry!
