Lifeguard Rescue Tube Response
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Rescue 1
How to play: Use Arrow Keys/WASD to swim, or hold a finger/mouse button anywhere on the water to swim toward that point. Reach the struggling swimmer (the red ring shows your tube reach) then press Space or tap Loop Tube to attach it. Once attached, swim back to the sand before your teal fatigue bar or their red fatigue bar runs out. Every successful rescue sends the next swimmer farther out and more panicked!
