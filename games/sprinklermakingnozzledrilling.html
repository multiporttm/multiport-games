Marked spots appear on the sprinkler head with a shrinking ring. Tap or click each one before its ring closes to drill a clean hole. Miss or let one close and the plate takes damage!
Drill marks appear on the sprinkler head plate, each shrinking inside a closing ring. Tap or click a mark while its ring is still open to drill a clean, precise hole and score points — the smaller the ring when you hit it, the bigger the bonus. Letting a ring fully close, or clicking the wrong spot, damages the plate's integrity; run out and the plate is scrapped. Holes come faster and the rings shrink quicker each round.
