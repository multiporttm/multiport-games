Sword Polishing
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●● Stone: Coarse
Mouse/Touch: drag along the blade following the stroke arrow to polish. Keyboard: hold Left/Right Arrows to stroke the stone along the blade, Up/Down Arrows to move to a new section. Cover every section evenly with each stone before the stone wears out (watch the wear meter) to advance to the next, finer grit. Rushing or missing sections costs a life. Finish all grits to reveal the mirror polish and hamon line. Three lives — more, finer grits are added each level.
