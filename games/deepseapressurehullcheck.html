Deep Sea Pressure Hull Check
Depth: 1m Lives: 3 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Repair sections showing EARLY WARNING stress before they turn critical!
Safe — leave alone Early warning — repair! Critical — too late
How to play: Each hull section shows a rising stress percentage. Click (or move the keyboard cursor with Arrow Keys and press Enter/Space) on sections glowing yellow (early warning) to repair them. Repairing a safe green section wastes a move, and letting a section turn red (critical) costs a life. Clear each depth's warnings before stress overwhelms the hull to dive deeper!
