Find the leak: one pipe segment on the main line sprays water — click/tap it to diagnose it. Close valves: click/tap the two valves on either side of the leak to shut them (or use ←/→ to move the cursor and Space to toggle). Repair: click/tap the leak again once both valves are closed. Reopen: click/tap both valves again to restore flow. Closing the wrong valve costs a life, and any fish tank left cut off too long will drain dry. Three lives — more pipes and faster drains arrive each level.
