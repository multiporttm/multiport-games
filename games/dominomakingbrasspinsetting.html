Use Left/Right (or drag) to slide the punch over the glowing open hole, then press Set (or Space/tap) to drive the brass pin home. The punch also drifts on its own — time your tap for when it's centered.
Traditional domino tiles are held together by a brass pin driven through the spine where bone or ivory meets ebony. A row of drilled holes runs along the spine, and a punch arm drifts back and forth above it. Steer the punch with the arrow keys, by dragging, or with the on-screen buttons, and press Set (or Space, or tap the spine) the instant it sits centered over the glowing open hole to drive a brass pin flush and true. A centered strike scores big and advances to the next hole; an off-center tap bends the pin and burns through your limited supply of brass. The punch drifts faster and the holes get tighter as you complete each spine, so steady hands and good timing are essential before you run out of pins.
