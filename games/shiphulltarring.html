Drag across the hull (mouse or finger) to brush on hot tar in overlapping strokes, or use the Arrow Keys to steer the brush and hold Space to paint. Cover the required percentage of the hull before the tar-cooling clock runs out — the hull gets bigger and the clock gets shorter every round.
