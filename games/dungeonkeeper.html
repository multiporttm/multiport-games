Prisoners

Tap a prisoner, then tap a cell to assign them.
How to play: Tap (or drag) a prisoner from the queue onto a cell to assign them. Each cell has a capacity — don't overfill it — and rival factions (red/blue/green) must never share a cell. Spend your limited guard points on cells holding risky prisoners to boost patrol coverage and stop escapes. When every prisoner is placed, click Lock In before time runs out to see if your dungeon holds!
