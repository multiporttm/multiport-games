The knife sweeps back and forth over the comb. Press Space or Tap the instant the blade lines up with the glowing green grid line to make a clean cut. Cutting off-line crushes the cells and costs a life. Finish every cut on the sheet before the honey pools — the grid gets finer and the knife moves faster each level.
