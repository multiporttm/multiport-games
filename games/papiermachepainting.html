Papier-Mâché Painting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Piece 1/8 Time: 30
How to play: Drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to sweep the brush across every part of the dried piece. Each patch fills with paint the longer your brush covers it — watch the gauge below: the glowing green band is the safe coverage zone. Too little paint leaves a missed patch; lingering too long on one spot makes it drip. Get the whole piece evenly into the safe zone before time runs out. Shapes get more intricate every round — lose all 3 lives and the paint job is ruined!
