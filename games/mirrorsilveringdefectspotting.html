Move the inspection crosshair (mouse, finger, or Arrow keys) across the mirror sheet and click/tap or press Space to mark every defect — black spots, scratches, and hazy patches hiding in the silvering. Tap Sweep Light to briefly flash raking light across the whole sheet and reveal faint defects. Clicking clean glass costs a penalty, so look carefully before you tap!
