Coffee Bean Sorting
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Click/Tap the defective beans (broken, bug-bitten, or under-ripe) to pull them off the belt before they cross the red inspection line. Leave the good beans rolling! Use Left/Right Arrows to select a bean and Space to pick it. The belt speeds up every round — you have 3 lives.
