Ankh Crafting
Hammering: A marker sweeps across the bar — tap, click, or press Space the instant it's inside the glowing zone to strike the gold true. Land 10 good strikes before you run out of attempts. Engraving: Drag your finger, mouse, or use Arrow Keys (press Enter to grip the tool) to trace the dotted ankh outline closely from start to finish — the steadier your hand, the finer the engraving.
