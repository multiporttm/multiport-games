Epoxy Resin Leveling
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Mold 1
Use Left/Right Arrows (or the tilt buttons) to gently rock the table and shift the resin surface. Keep the bubble-level gauge needle inside the glowing safe zone as long as possible before the cure timer runs out. Tilt too far past either edge and the resin spills over the lip — game over! Molds get bigger and sloshier each round.
