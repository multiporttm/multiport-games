Snow Fence Installation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Line 1/6
How to play: Drag/tap along the boundary (or use Left/Right Arrow) to position the next fence section, and Up/Down Arrow (or drag vertically) to angle it against the wind arrow. Press Space or tap Place to drive it in when the spacing and angle are right. Good placements score points; bad ones cost a life. Place every section before the storm timer runs out — spacing requirements and the clock get tighter each line!
