Bee Pollen Harvesting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Round 1/5
How to play: Press Up/Down Arrow (or drag up/down on the canvas) to adjust the pollen trap's tension dial. When a bee reaches the entrance, matching tension scrapes her pollen baskets for points. Too tight and bees queue up waiting — let the queue overflow and you lose a life; too loose and you miss the pollen but bees pass freely. Traffic gets busier every round, 3 lives — good luck, keeper!
