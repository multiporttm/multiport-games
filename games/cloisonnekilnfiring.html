Cloisonné Kiln Firing
Round: 1 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Hold Up Arrow / tap Heat to raise the kiln temperature, and Down Arrow / tap Cool to lower it. Keep the glowing needle inside the safe orange band of the firing curve as it scrolls — drift above it to scorch the enamel, or stay too far below to let it crack. Survive the whole schedule to finish the piece. Longer, trickier schedules await each round.
