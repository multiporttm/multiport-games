Orchard Irrigation Setup
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Orchard 1
Select a line, place it, click a placed line to rotate it.
How to play: Click a drip line type in the toolbar, then click an empty cell to place it. Click a placed line of the same type to rotate it, or select Remove to pick one back up. Connect the blue water source to every green tree using your limited lines, then click Test Lines to check for leaks and dry spots — a failed test costs a life. Clear the orchard before you run out of lives to move on to a bigger layout. Keyboard: 1-4 select line type, R remove, arrow keys move the cursor, Space/Enter act on the cursor cell, F tests the lines.
