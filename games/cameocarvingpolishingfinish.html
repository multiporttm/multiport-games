Piece: 1 Shine: 0% Score: 0 Best: 0
Buffing pressure:
Drag the cloth (mouse or finger) in small circles across the shell cameo relief to buff it. Use the pressure slider (or Up/Down arrow keys) to set how hard you press — too light and shine builds slowly, too hard too long and the delicate carving cracks. Polish every facet past the shine target without a single crack to finish the piece. Each new cameo is more intricate!
