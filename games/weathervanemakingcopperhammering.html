A disc of copper glows hot on the anvil while the heat bar drains and a hammer indicator sweeps across a gauge beside it, hunting for the glowing target band where the blow lands flush and even. Press Space, tap, or click 🔨 Hammer the instant the indicator sits inside the target band to shape the dome true. Strike too soon or too late and the metal dents crooked, costing you a life — three bad dents and the copper cools stiff and cracks! Finish every strike on a dome before the heat runs out to move on to a bigger, trickier dome.
