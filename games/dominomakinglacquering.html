🖤 Domino Making Lacquering
Hold to lower the domino into the lacquer vat, release to lift it. Keep the dip depth inside the glowing coating band for a steady beat to seal a flawless gloss.
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space/Enter) to lower the domino into the lacquer vat; release to let it rise. Keep the depth marker inside the glowing coating band — hold it there long enough to seal a flawless coat. Dipping too shallow leaves bare wood, dipping too deep causes drips, and either drains your Gloss Quality meter; let it empty and the batch is ruined.
