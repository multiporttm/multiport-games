How to play: Press and hold (mouse or finger) on the glowing starting dot of a detail path, then drag your brush along the guide line to the end dot without drifting off it — the paint is wet and dries fast, so keep your stroke smooth and on-track. Finishing a line before it dries paints it in cleanly; drifting too far off the glowing path or letting the paint dry mid-stroke smears it and costs a life. Paint every detail line on the model to finish it and move on to a more intricate model — lose all 3 lives and the model gets scrapped!
