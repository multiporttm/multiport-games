✨ Teacup Making: Glaze Dipping
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
How to play: The teacup bobs up and down above a vat of glaze while a marker rises and falls along the depth gauge beside it. Click / tap the cup or the Start button, or press Space / Enter, exactly as the marker passes through the glowing green zone to dunk and lift the cup for an even glaze coat. Miss the depth and a patchy, dripping coat damages your coating quality meter; empty it and the glaze run ruins the piece for good. Land 5 clean dips to finish a round — the marker moves faster and the safe zone narrows every round after.
