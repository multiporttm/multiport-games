Quill Pen Making Inkwell Pairing
❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Tray 1
The finishing bench has a tray of face-down tiles hiding finished quills and their matching inkwells, mixed up after the delivery cart jolted over cobblestones, and every quill needs to be reunited with the one inkwell whose ink color was mixed just for it. Use the mouse, Arrow keys + Enter, or taps to flip two tiles at a time: flip a quill and an inkwell that belong together and the pair locks in place with a satisfying clink, but flip two that do not match and both flip back face-down, costing you a flip. You only get a limited number of mismatched flips before the tray is swept away unfinished — match every pair before your flips run out to move on to a bigger tray with more pairs.
