A long square acrylic rod is clamped in the miter jig, and a marker line sweeps back and forth across the blade gauge as the saw blade bounces, hunting for the exact mark that will produce a perfectly cube-shaped blank. Press Space, tap, or click 🪚 Cut the instant the sweeping blade lines up inside the glowing target zone to slice off a clean, square blank. Cut too early or too late and the blank comes out as an uneven rectangle, costing you a life — three bad cuts and the rod is ruined! Finish every blank on a rod to move on to a longer rod with a narrower, trickier target zone.
