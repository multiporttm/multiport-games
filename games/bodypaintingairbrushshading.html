Sweep the airbrush over the silhouette until every panel is smoothly shaded, not pale and not overblown.
How to play: A stylized mannequin silhouette stands on your easel, divided into panels waiting for smooth gradient shading. Hold the airbrush with the mouse or your finger, or move it with the arrow keys or WASD, sweeping it back and forth across every panel so the fine mist slowly builds up tone. Each panel needs a few careful passes to reach a good feathered gradient shown in soft teal, but hover too briefly and a panel stays pale and flat, while lingering too long pools the paint into an overblown dark blotch that costs you polish. Watch the paint-reservoir bar drain at the top — once it runs dry, the figure is judged on how many panels landed in the good shading range. Shade mannequin after mannequin, each with a shorter reservoir, to build the highest score you can before the paint can runs dry mid-sweep.
