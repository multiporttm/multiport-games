Sandstorm Survival
Distance: 0m Shelters: 0 Stamina
How to play: Move your traveler with Arrow Keys / WASD or by dragging on mobile, fleeing the sandstorm wall closing in from the left. Running drains stamina (regenerates when still); reach a rock outcrop 🪨 and stay near it briefly to shelter and recover, reducing the storm's reach. Visibility drops sharply inside the storm. Survive as far as you can before the storm overwhelms you!
