Organ Reed Pipe Adjustment
Score: 0 | Hi: 0
How to play: Drag the reed dot with your mouse or finger, or nudge it with Arrow keys / WASD, to adjust the tuning wire (left/right = pitch) and the reed curvature (up/down = tone quality) at the same time. Guide the dot into the glowing target box, then press Space / tap SET to lock the adjustment before the timer runs out. The target box shrinks and starts drifting as reed types get trickier every round!
