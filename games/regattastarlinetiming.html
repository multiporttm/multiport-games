Regatta Start Line Timing
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Start 1/10
How to play: Press Space / Up Arrow (or Tap) to accelerate toward the starting line, and release to slow down. The countdown clock ticks toward the horn — approach the line so you cross it at full speed at the exact instant the horn sounds. Cross early and you're recalled (a life lost); arrive late or too slow and you lose ground. Nail the start to gain the best position! Rounds get tighter on timing and speed every start — lose all 3 lives and the regatta ends.
