Match the striped pattern shown on the left! Pick a color, then click Pour Layer and release at the right moment to fill up to the marked line without overflowing. Wait for the layer to partially freeze (watch the frost bar) before pouring the next one, or the colors will blend together. Once all layers are poured, click Insert Stick and then Freeze to finish.
