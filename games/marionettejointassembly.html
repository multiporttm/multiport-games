Marionette Joint Assembly
Level: 1 Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ♥ ♥ ♥
First pick a joint type from the palette — Hinge or String — then Click/Tap the connection point that needs it. With a keyboard, press H or S to pick a joint type, use Arrow Keys to move the cursor between points, and Enter to apply. Get every joint right, then watch the range-of-motion test before the next, trickier limb!
