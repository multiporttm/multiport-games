Hold the glue brush and move up/down to match the glowing target bead as it travels along the rib.
How to play: A paper panel waits over the bamboo rib while a faint target line shows the glue bead thickness you need at each point along the seam. Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) then drag up and down, or use the Up/Down arrow keys, to move the brush toward the glowing target, matching the bead point by point. Gluing close to the target fills your precision streak and tops up your paste supply; laying on too much soaks through the paper, and staying too thin dries the seam fast. Finish every point before the paste runs dry to seal the panel and move on to a trickier rib pattern.
