Air Show Formation
Show: 60s | Tightness: 0% | Hi: 0%
How to play: Use Arrow Keys / WASD, or drag/tap anywhere on the display, to steer your jet (the white triangle) and hold the target slot just behind and left of the lead jet (the blue diamond), marked by the dashed circle. The lead flies a full scripted routine of turns and passes — stay inside the circle to keep your formation score climbing. Drift too far off-screen and the show ends early!
