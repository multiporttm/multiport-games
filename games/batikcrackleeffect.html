Crack Density
Drag across the wax-coated fabric to flex and crumple it, cracking fine lines into the wax. Keep the Crack Density gauge inside the green safe zone, then press Space (or tap the Lock In button) to finish the cloth. Press too hard or too long and you will over-crack and ruin the resist pattern. Each new cloth needs a trickier target density.
