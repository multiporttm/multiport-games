Pewter Mold Assembly
Level: 1 Placed: 0/4 Time: 45 Best: 0
Click a piece, then click its matching slot. Press R to rotate the selected piece.
Click a mold piece from the tray at the bottom to select it, then click the clamp slot on the mold block above with the matching silhouette to lock it in. Some pieces need to be rotated first — select a piece and press R or the Rotate button (or the Up arrow key) until its outline matches the slot exactly before placing it. Bolt every slot shut before the timer runs out to advance to a harder mold with more pieces!
