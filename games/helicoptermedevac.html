Helicopter Medevac
Mission: 1 Lives: ●●● Score: 0 | Hi: 0
How to play: Use Arrow Keys/WASD to thrust the helicopter, or click-drag/touch-drag to steer toward your finger. Fly to the flashing landing zone and hover slowly over it to winch up the patient — watch out for the wind and the obstacles. Once aboard, race to the hospital pad and hover there before the critical timer runs out. Crashing or missing the timer costs a life.
