How to play: A target flow rate line drifts up and down the tank's gauge. Drag your mouse or finger up/down on the canvas (or use Arrow Up/Down, or tap to start) to move your valve handle onto the line and keep it there. Drift above it too long and tank pressure climbs; drift below it and cryogenic boil-off eats your fuel — either one drains your stability meter, and an empty meter costs a life. Hold steady until the tank fills to finish the sequence. The target moves faster and the safe band narrows every sequence — lose all 3 lives and the fueling is scrubbed!
