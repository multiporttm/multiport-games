How to play: A new bisque piece waits to go onto the kiln shelf. Move its highlighted position with the arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen arrows, or by dragging on the grid; rotate it with R or the Rotate button; set it down with Enter, Space, the Place button, or a tap. A placement only works if every cell is on the shelf, empty, and not touching another piece on any side — raku pieces need an airflow gap to fire evenly. A bad placement costs kiln-master confidence. Load the target number of pieces before confidence runs out to seal the kiln and move to a bigger shelf.
