Sailboat Winch Cranking
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Line 1/10
How to play: Crank the winch handle by alternately pressing Left/Right Arrow (or tapping the left/right halves of the canvas) in a steady rhythm to bring in the line. Line tension rises as it comes in — press G (or the Shift Gear button) to switch to low gear once tension gets high, or you'll overload and strip the winch, losing a life. Switch too early and you crank too slowly. Fill the progress bar to finish the line before time runs out — lines get heavier and tension builds faster each round. Lose all 3 lives and the job's done!
