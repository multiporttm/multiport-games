🧰 Toolbox Making Tray Sliding
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Round 1
How to play: A marker sweeps back and forth along the rail. Press Space/Enter, or tap/click the canvas, the instant the marker is inside the green zone to slide the tray in smoothly. A clean slide earns points and fills your round; a bad slide bruises your slide smoothness meter. Empty the meter and the run ends. Every 5 clean slides the rail speeds up and the sweet zone tightens — how many rounds can you finish?
