Hold to charge the chisel, release in the green zone.
Each highlighted section of rough stone needs a chisel strike. Press and hold Space, click, or touch to swing the chisel — the power gauge fills as you hold. Release inside the green zone to carve it away cleanly. Release too early (gauge still low) and the stone barely chips; release too late (gauge in the red) and the stone cracks. Too many cracks and the block shatters — carve every section to reveal the gargoyle!
