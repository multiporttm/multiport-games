Shape the cam lobe to match the target motion graph
Use the Left/Right arrow keys or the on-screen Segment buttons to select a lobe segment on the cam, and the Up/Down arrow keys or Raise/Lower buttons to set its radius. Match the solid follower graph to the dashed target graph for every segment to finish the rotation. You can also click or tap a segment on the cam directly to select it. Three timeouts end the run — later rounds add trickier dwell-rise-fall motion profiles.
