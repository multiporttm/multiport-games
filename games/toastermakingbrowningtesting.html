🍞 Toaster Making Browning Testing
Every toaster model needs its heating coils calibrated against a target shade. Stop the test slice the instant its colour matches the swatch.
Watch the test slice darken and lighten as it toasts. Press (click, tap, or Space/Enter) exactly when the slice's shade matches the target swatch on the right to log a good calibration. Good matches raise your score and speed up the next slice; bad matches burn the batch and drain your Batch Consistency meter — let it empty and testing is shut down.
