🍵 Teacup Making: Handle Attaching
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
How to play: A small clay handle hovers beside the cup while a marker slides back and forth along the pressure gauge below it. Click / tap the cup or the Start button, or press Space / Enter, exactly as the marker passes through the glowing green zone to score the clay and press the handle on cleanly. Miss the timing and a weak, crooked joint damages your bond quality meter; empty it and the handle snaps off for good. Land 5 clean attachments to finish a round — the marker sweeps faster and the safe zone narrows every round after.
