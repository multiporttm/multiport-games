Carpet Cleaning
Round 1
Score: 0
Time: 50s
Hi: 0
 
How to play: Click each stain on the carpet, then choose the matching cleaning solution from the popup (Enzyme Cleaner for organic stains, Solvent for oil/grease, Oxidizer for wine/juice/ink, Detergent for mud/dirt). Picking the wrong solution sets the stain permanently — it can no longer be removed this round. Once every stain is treated, click-and-drag the shampoo machine across the whole carpet in overlapping passes until the coverage heatmap shows full coverage. Finish before time runs out to move to a bigger carpet with trickier stains!
