A fresh bead of wood glue has been worked into a wobbly chair joint, and the clamp handle sweeps back and forth across the pressure gauge, hunting for the glowing sweet-spot band where squeeze-out is even and the joint pulls perfectly tight. Press Space, tap, or click 🗜️ Clamp Joint the instant the needle sits inside the target band to lock the joint true.
