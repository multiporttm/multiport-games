Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) to pump air behind the diaphragm film and inflate it. Release the moment the outer ring enters the glowing target band to lock in a perfectly tensioned diaphragm. Release too early and it stays loose; hold too long and it stretches past the band and tears, costing a life. Lock in diaphragms to raise your score as the target band shrinks and the pump speeds up each round.
