Firefighter Hose
Fires Out: 0 | Lives: 3 | Hi: 0
Move your mouse or finger to aim the hose and hold down / touch to spray water on burning windows. Keep the stream on a fire for a couple seconds to put it out — but watch your water tank, which refills only when you're not spraying. Fires left too long spread to neighboring windows and cost you a life if a section fully burns.
