Cross-Stitch Stitch Tensioning
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Row 1
Watch the tension needle swing across the gauge. Press Space, click, or tap exactly when it is inside the green safe zone to pull that stitch snug. Too early or too late puckers the fabric. Keep a steady rhythm across the whole row — rows get longer and the safe zone narrower each round.
