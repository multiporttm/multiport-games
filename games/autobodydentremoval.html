Auto Body Dent Removal
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Panel 1/8
How to play: A glowing high spot drifts back and forth across the dent — steer your pick with the Arrow Keys or by dragging your mouse/finger to track it. Hold Space (or keep your finger/mouse down) to push while the pick is over the hot zone and the dent slowly flattens. Push while off target and you risk cracking the paint — fill the crack meter and you lose a life. Flatten the panel fully to move on. Hot spots move faster and the safe zone narrows with every panel — lose all 3 lives and the job is ruined!
