Wax Seal Stamping
Score: 0 Level: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Press and hold (Space, click, or touch) to melt the wax — watch the meter and release inside the glowing gold zone for the right consistency. Release too early and the wax is too hard; too late and it's too runny. Once melted, do the same to press the stamp: hold, then release inside the zone for a crisp seal. Miss either step and you lose a life. The zone shrinks and the meter speeds up every level.
