Watch the sonar pings to figure out where the contact really is. Move the targeting reticle with your mouse / touch drag or Arrow Keys, then press Space or click right after a ping to lock a firing solution on the contact's true position. Lock it enough times before it closes to collision range or drifts off the scope — contacts get faster and noisier each round.
