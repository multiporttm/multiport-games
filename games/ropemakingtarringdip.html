Rope Making Tarring Dip
Batches Tarred: 0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Hold the feed key to push rope through the hot tar — not too fast, not too slow.
How to play: A length of rope runs through a trough of simmering pine tar on a feed wheel, and your job is to control how fast each marked segment of rope passes through. Hold down the spacebar or the on-screen "Hold to Feed" button to push the rope forward through the trough, and release to let it drift back, watching the heat gauge that fills while a segment sits in the tar. Segments that pass through too quickly come out pale and bone-dry — they will rot at sea — while segments left sitting too long scorch black and brittle, so aim to keep each segment's gauge inside the glossy dark-brown target band stamped on the gauge before it clears the trough. Each segment that comes out in the target band locks in a good tar and adds to your score; too many pale or scorched segments ruin the batch early. Clear a full batch of segments before the tar trough runs dry to bank a big bonus and move on to the next batch, which runs a little hotter and faster than the last.
