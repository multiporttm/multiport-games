Jog the hull left/right to slip it through the neck, then pull it down.
How to play: A folded ship hull dangles on two pull-strings above the bottle's narrow neck. Use ◀ ▶ (or arrow keys, or drag) to jog it sideways between the neck walls without touching them, and ▼ (or Down arrow) to lower it. Once it clears the neck and rests inside the bottle, click "Pull Strings Taut" before your slack meter runs out to snap the hull open and score the ship. Touching a wall costs slack; run out and the strings go slack for good, ending the round.
