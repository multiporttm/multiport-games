Marionette Control Bar Balancing
Score: 0Level: 1❤❤❤Hi: 0
Use ←/→ (or A/D, or drag left-right on the stage) to tilt the control bar and step the marionette left/right; use ↑/↓ (or W/S) to raise an arm cue. Match each cue arrow the moment it reaches the stage line. Jerky or late moves fill the tangle meter — let it max out and you lose a life. 3 lives, and the choreography speeds up every level!
