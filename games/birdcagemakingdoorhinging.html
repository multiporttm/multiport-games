Hinge pins light up one at a time on the little cage door - tap (or click) each glowing pin before its ring burns down to drive it home. Hitting a pin while it glows raises your score and fills the Hinge Set meter; tapping the wrong pin or letting one burn out damages the door and builds up Rattle. Keep your reflexes sharp as pins flash faster each set to set a new best score.
