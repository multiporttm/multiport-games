Shoe Sole Stitching
Score: 0 Shoe: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Punch holes appear one at a time along the welt in a glowing ring. Click/tap it (or press Space) right as the ring shrinks to the target size to pull a clean, even stitch. Too early or too late loosens tension and costs a life. Keep up a full lap before the welt runs out of holes.
