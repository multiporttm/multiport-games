Forge Hammer Striking
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Press Space, click, or tap the anvil to strike the metal exactly when the moving marker crosses the glowing strike zone. Good hits shape the metal and build heat; misses waste heat. Let the metal cool to zero and it goes back in the forge, costing a life. The zone narrows and the marker speeds up every level. Three lives.
