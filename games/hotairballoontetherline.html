Hot Air Balloon Tether Line
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Stage 1/5
How to play: Wind gusts randomly push the tethered balloon's line slack toward drifting away or sinking too low. Hold Arrow Up or the Reel In button to pull slack back toward center; hold Arrow Down or the Let Out button to push it the other way. Keep the balloon marker inside the green safe band for the whole stage timer to advance. If it reaches either red extreme, you lose a life! Five stages bring stronger, more frequent gusts.
