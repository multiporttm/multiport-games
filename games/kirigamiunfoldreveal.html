How to play: The small canvas shows a sheet folded a certain way with a shape snipped out of the folded wedge. Picture how the paper looks once fully unfolded, then click (or use number keys 1-4 + Enter, or Tab/Arrow Keys + Enter) the matching pattern among the lookalike options before the timer runs out. A correct pick scores points and speeds things up; a wrong pick or a timeout costs a life. Fold counts and cuts get trickier every round — lose all 3 lives and it's game over!
