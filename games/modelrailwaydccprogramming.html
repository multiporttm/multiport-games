How to play: Check the reference sheet on the left for the CV (configuration variable) number and value each row needs, then type that CV then its value on the keypad (digits 0-9 on screen or keyboard), separating them with the "/" shown, and press Enter to lock it in. Fix a typo with Backspace. Lock in every row correctly before the programming window timer runs out to flash the decoder and move on to a locomotive with more settings to configure!
