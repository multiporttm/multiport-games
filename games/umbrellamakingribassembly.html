Umbrella Making Rib Assembly
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Frame 1/8
How to play: Each numbered joint on the left (a stretcher socket) has one matching numbered joint on the right (a rib pin). Click or tap a joint on the left, then click or tap the joint on the right with the same number to lock that rib into the frame. You can also use Tab to cycle the highlighted joint and Enter / Space to select it with the keyboard. A mismatched pair springs apart and costs a life. Connect every rib correctly to finish the frame — frames grow more intricate, with more ribs and stretchers, every round.
