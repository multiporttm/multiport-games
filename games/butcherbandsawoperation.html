Butcher Band Saw Operation
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 | Lv 1 | ❤❤❤
Hold Space / Up Arrow or press and hold the canvas to push the cut into the band saw — release to ease off. Keep the feed-speed needle inside the glowing safe zone the instant each automatic cut lands, so every slice comes out at the marked thickness. Three uneven slices and the shift is over.
