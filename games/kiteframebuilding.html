Kite Frame Building
Level: 1 Score: 0 Lives: 3 Hi: 0
Click (or use ← → + Enter) to pick a spar from the tray, then click the matching frame slot (or press its number key) to lock it in. Once every slot is filled, press Space or click Lock Tension when the gauge needle is in the green zone to finish the frame. Wrong spars or a mistimed lock cost a life — build through all the frame shapes before your lives run out!
