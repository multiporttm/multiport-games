How to play: A glowing bead travels along the curl pattern traced on the wig — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep the heated iron tip inside the amber tolerance tube around it. Drift too far off the pattern, or hold the iron still in one spot too long, and the fibers start to scorch, draining your sheen meter — lose it all and the style is ruined, costing a life. Every finished wig unlocks a more intricate curl pattern, with a faster bead and a tighter tube. Lose all 3 lives and the styling session ends!
