Click/tap and drag (or use Arrow Keys) to sweep the grinder over the anvil face, wearing down the red high spots. Press Enter or tap the Check with Straightedge button to check your progress — it only passes once the whole face reads flat and smooth. Grinding the same spot too long gouges it, costing a life.
