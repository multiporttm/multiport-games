📀 Gramophone Making: Needle Fitting
Score: 0 Round 1 Best: 0
How to play: A clamp slider sweeps across the depth gauge beneath the sound box as the steel needle is lowered toward its seat. Click / tap the gauge, or press Space / Enter, right as the slider passes through the glowing green zone to seat the needle at exactly the right depth. Miss and your fit quality meter drops; empty it and the needle is bent for good. Land 5 clean fittings to finish a round — the slider speeds up and the sweet zone narrows every round after.
