Saloon Brawl
Troublemakers: 20 | Score: 0 | Hi: 0
Use Up/Down Arrow (or the lane buttons) to switch between 3 lanes. Press Space / Punch to knock out a patron next to you. Press F / Throw to hurl a chair or bottle at anyone in your lane (limited supply — it refills over time). Clear all the troublemakers before your health runs out!
