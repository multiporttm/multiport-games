Cut the leather
Drag the blade along the dashed guide line to cut each leather piece (or press Enter for an assisted cut), click the numbered stitch points in ascending order to sew them together, then pick the right hardware chip and click its marked spot to attach it. Misaligned cuts and wrong order cost points — each saddle adds more pieces and steps.
