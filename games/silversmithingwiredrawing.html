Hold and drag the wire tip steadily through the draw-plate hole. Keep your pulling speed inside the bright zone — too slow and it cools stiff, too fast and it snaps.
Press and hold (mouse, finger, or Space) and drag steadily to the right to draw the wire through the plate. Keep your drag speed inside the glowing sweet-spot band on the heat gauge — drag too fast and the wire snaps, too slow and it seizes. Clear each plate to advance to a thinner gauge. Three ruined pulls and it's game over!
