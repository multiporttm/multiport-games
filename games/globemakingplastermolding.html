❤️❤️❤️ Score: 0 | Hi: 0 Mold 1
Wet plaster pours into a rotating hemisphere mold, rising and falling in the measuring flask as you work the funnel. A gold line marks the exact fill level this half-shell needs before it is set aside to cure. Press Space, tap, or click 🥛 Stop Pour the instant the level crosses the target to seal the pour. Pour too much and the shell overflows and cracks; stop too early and it is too thin — three ruined shells and the batch of plaster is wasted! Seal every shell in a batch to move on to a tighter pour.
