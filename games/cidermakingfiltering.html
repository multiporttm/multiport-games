Cider Making Filtering
Filtered: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Batch 1
How to play: Press and hold (or press Space / Up Arrow) to tip the jug and pour cider; release to slow the pour. Keep the needle inside the glowing green safe zone on the flow gauge as the batch bar fills. Pour too slow and the cloth clogs with pulp; pour too fast and the filter overflows, spilling precious cider and costing a life. Fill the batch bar to finish — batches get bigger and the safe zone narrows each round!
