Barrel Charring Process
Score: 0 Barrel: 1 Lives: ❤❤❤ Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrow (or drag) to rotate the barrel over the flame, and hold Space or the 🔥 button to char the section under the flame. Get every section up to the target char level — shown by the ring — without torching any one section black. An evenly-charred barrel wins; a burnt section costs a life!
