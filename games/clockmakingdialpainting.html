Clockmaking Dial Painting
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Dial 1
Press and drag your brush along the glowing guide path to paint each numeral onto the dial. Stay inside the thin guide line and keep moving — the wet paint bar drains while you trace and a stroke left unfinished too long will dry and ruin that numeral. More numerals and finer guide lines appear on each new dial.
