A finished weathervane is balanced on a swaying ladder high over the ridge, and the spindle slides back and forth across the roof bracket while the wind rocks the ladder beneath you. Press Space, tap, or click 🔨 Set Spindle the instant the spindle sits inside the glowing bracket socket to seat it plumb and true. Drop it too far left or right of the socket and it clatters off the ridge, costing you a life — miss three times and you climb down empty-handed! Seat every spindle on a roof to move up to a steeper pitch with a narrower socket.
