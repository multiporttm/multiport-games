Skateboard Deck Shaping
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Shape 1/10
How to play: A glowing bit travels the target outline of the deck shape — drag your mouse or finger (or steer with the Arrow Keys) to keep your router bit tracking inside the orange safe zone around it. Wander outside it and your accuracy meter drains; empty it and you cut outside the line, costing a life. Trace the whole outline to finish the shape and move to the next one. Outlines get more intricate and the safe zone narrows every round — lose all 3 lives and the blank is ruined!
