🎩 Hat Stand Making Base Weighting
Score: 0 Base 1 Best: 0
Each hat stand base ships with an uneven casting. Tap "+ Left" or "+ Right" (or press A / D) to drop small calibration weights on that pan and tip the balance beam level. When the tilt needle sits inside the green centre band, tap "Confirm Balance" before the timer runs out. A level base scores big and speeds up the next one; confirming off-balance or running out of time cracks your Base Stability.
