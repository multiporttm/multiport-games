Holding the plate by its corner, you lower it edge-first into the silver nitrate bath — it sinks slowly in, then rises back out on its own, over and over, a crystal frost of silver halides spreading across the dipped surface. A gold band marks the fully-sensitized depth where the frost is thick and even. Press Space, tap, or click 🧪 Lift Clear the instant the plate's depth enters the gold band to pull it out perfectly sensitized. Lift too early and the coating stays thin and patchy; lift too late and the silver bath eats through the collodion — three ruined plates and the bath runs dry! Sensitize every plate in a session to move on to a fussier, faster bath.
