Dog Blow Dry Station
Score: 0 Level: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
How to play: Use ←/→ (or click/tap a section) to aim the dryer at a wet patch of coat, then hold Space (or hold your click/tap) to blow it dry and fluff it up. Dry every section before the patience meter empties! Watch out — if too much coat stays wet too long, the dog shakes and soaks the sections again. Three lost rounds and the dog hops off the table. Dogs get wetter and more impatient each level.
