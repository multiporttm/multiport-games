Fossil Digging
Actions: 40 Fossils: 0/0 Score: 0 Hi: 0
Click a square to dig. Golden tints hint at fossils below — go Light near strong tints!
How to play: Each grid square hides rock layers, and some hide a fossil underneath. A subtle golden tint on a square hints a fossil is near — the tint grows stronger as fewer layers remain above it. Choose your tool — Light Pick safely clears one layer, Heavy Pick clears two layers per action but will crush a fossil if used when only one layer remains above it. Click/tap a square to dig with your selected tool, or use Arrow keys to move the cursor, L/H to switch tools, and Space/Enter to dig. Uncover every fossil safely before you run out of dig actions!
