Subway Platform Crowd Control
Score: 0 Round: 1 Hi: 0 Lives: ●●●
Drag (mouse or touch) each passenger dot along the platform to an open zone, spreading the crowd evenly between the yellow doors before the timer runs out. Too many passengers bunched in one zone, or anyone left standing in a door zone when the train leaves, costs a life. Use Arrow keys to move the selected passenger and Tab/click to select one. Clear enough rounds to rack up points — crowds grow and the clock shrinks every round. Three lives.
