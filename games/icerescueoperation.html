Ice Rescue Operation
Rescued: 0/6 Floe: Hi: 0
Use Left/Right Arrows (or touch left/right side) to fight wind drift and fly your helicopter over a stranded person or seal. Hold Down Arrow / Space (or touch-hold) to lower the rescue basket and pick them up, then release to raise the basket and fly to the green SAFE zone to drop them off. Rescue as many as you can before the cracking ice floe breaks apart completely!
