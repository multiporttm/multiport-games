⏳ Hourglass Making Sand Pouring
Press and hold to open the funnel. Watch the rising sand and release right as it reaches the marked band to measure a perfect bulb.
Press and hold (mouse, touch, or Space/Enter) to open the funnel and pour sand into the glass bulb below. The fill level keeps rising while you hold. Release exactly when the level sits inside the glowing target band — too early or too late ruins the measure. Each bulb speeds up the pour and narrows the band.
