Each log line shows the miles since that item was last done and the interval it's due every. Click/tap (or press 1-4) to select exactly the items that are overdue (miles since ≥ interval), then press Enter or tap Clear Cab to send it back out. Picking the wrong set — missing an overdue item or flagging one that isn't due — costs a life. Three lives — more items appear and the clock tightens every level.
