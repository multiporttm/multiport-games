Raise and lower the main curtain at exactly the right musical cues for act breaks and curtain calls. As a cue mark reaches the playhead, press Up Arrow or tap RAISE for an open cue, or press Down Arrow or tap LOWER for a close cue. Cue sequences get faster and trickier as the show runs on. Three missed or wrong cues and the show is pulled!
