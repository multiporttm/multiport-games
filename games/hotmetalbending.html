Hold Space, an Arrow Key, or Tap-and-Hold the bar to bend the glowing hot metal around the jig. Release inside the highlighted target zone — bend too little and it cools stiff, bend too far and it cracks. Zones get tighter and the metal cools faster each round. 3 lives.
