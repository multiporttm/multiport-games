Raise/lower the lid with Up/Down, W/S, drag, or touch to track the target band.
How to play: Smoke billows from the sawdust can around the red-hot pot. The target smoke-density band drifts slowly up and down the gauge. Raise the lid for more oxygen (less smoke) or lower it for less oxygen (more smoke) using the Up/Down arrow keys, W/S, the on-screen buttons, or by dragging vertically on the canvas, keeping the smoke level inside the drifting band. Drifting outside the band drains your reduction-control meter; staying inside it refills the cycle-complete meter. Hold the band for the whole cycle to pull a perfectly crackle-glazed pot and move to a longer, trickier cycle.
