Drag the knife handle down through the fillet in one motion!
Grab the knife handle above the fillet and drag it straight down through the fish in one smooth motion (mouse, finger, or arrow keys + Enter). Release inside the highlighted target band on the thickness gauge, and keep the stroke steady — wobbling side to side ruins an otherwise good slice. Cut through the whole fillet to finish the round, then face thinner targets and tighter tolerance!
