Agility Course Run
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Level 1
How to play: Obstacles scroll toward your dog on the handler line. Press the matching cue right as the obstacle crosses the line: ↑ Jump for a bar, ↓ Duck for a tunnel, and alternate ← → for weave poles. Arrow keys or the on-screen buttons both work. Knock a bar or miss the timing and you lose a life — 3 misses ends the run. Speed and obstacle density increase every level, so stay sharp!
