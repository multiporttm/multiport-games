Snowmobile Racer
Lap: 1/3 | Place: 1st | Best: --
Hold Up Arrow/W (or the GAS button) to accelerate, and use Left/Right Arrow or A/D (or ◀ ▶) to switch lanes. Dodge the snow moguls and rocks, hit the yellow jump ramps for a speed boost, and race 2 AI opponents across 3 laps to the finish line first!
