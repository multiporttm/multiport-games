Click/drag a tray piece onto the matching slot outline on the sheet. Tab/arrow keys cycle pieces, Space drops onto the highlighted slot.
A reference pattern shows where petals (pink), leaves (green) and thread pieces (tan) belong on your wet pulp sheet. Drag each tray piece onto its matching outlined slot — shape and color both have to match, so a petal won't fit a leaf's slot. Once every slot is filled correctly, press the pulp veil to seal everything in and finish the sheet. Placing a piece on the wrong slot wastes it; waste three pieces and the sheet is ruined. Later sheets pack in more, smaller slots!
