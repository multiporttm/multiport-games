The script runs over the segment's air time. The lowest-priority story is always highlighted first — press Down (or Trim) to shave 5 seconds off it, or C (or Cut) to drop it entirely, working strictly in priority order. Once the remaining time matches the target exactly, press Enter (or Lock Script) before the clock runs out.
