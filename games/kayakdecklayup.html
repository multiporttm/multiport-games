Kayak Deck Layup
Score: 0 | Hi: 0 ❤❤❤ Deck 1/7
How to play: Each numbered panel on the deck diagram must be laid in order — click or tap the panel showing the next number in the sequence. Click the wrong panel and you waste a layer (lose a life). Once a sheet is placed, air bubbles appear on it — drag or click across every bubble to smooth it flat before the timer bar runs out, or it wrinkles and costs a life. Clear all panels in order to finish the deck. Decks get more panels and the smoothing timer gets shorter every round — lose all 3 lives and the layup is scrapped!
