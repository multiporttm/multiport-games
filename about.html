About Multiport Games

Last updated: August 2026

Multiport Games is an independently run site with one goal: free, instant browser games — no downloads, no signup required, and no malware. Just click a game and play.

Our story

Before Multiport Games existed, a lot of my friends were playing games on a sketchy gaming site — the kind with intrusive ads and no real safety behind it. I tried talking them out of using it, and for a while it worked. But without anywhere else to go, they eventually drifted right back, because they just wanted somewhere to play games.

So I decided to build that somewhere myself: a site that's actually safe, with no malware and no shady ads, that they could use instead. That project grew into Multiport Games — now home to 103+ free games.

Where did the name "Multiport" come from?

"Multi" is for multiple games — the site was always meant to hold way more than just one or two. "Port" is for multiple products — the plan from the start was for Multiport to grow beyond just games someday. Put together: Multiport.

What we offer

103+ games spanning arcade, puzzle, action, racing, sports, skill, and education categories, all playable directly in your browser on desktop, tablet, or phone. New games and features are added regularly — check the 📋 What's New button on the homepage for the latest changes.

How the site is run

Multiport Games is a small, independently operated project — not a large studio or corporation. It's actively maintained, with bugs fixed and improvements shipped on an ongoing basis. The site is hosted on Cloudflare for speed, reliability, and security.

Privacy and safety first

The site runs entirely over HTTPS, uses no ad trackers, and doesn't require an account to play any game. An optional account exists only for things like saving achievements — it's never required.
